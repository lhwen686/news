四区巩膜镜提高不规则角膜的视觉性能

Four-zone scleral lens improves visual performance for irregular corneas

深度解析

本文深入探讨了四区巩膜镜在改善不规则角膜患者视觉表现方面的显著效果。核心专业概念在于巩膜镜的工作机制：作为一种大直径硬性透气性接触镜，它支撑于巩膜上，弓形跨越角膜，在镜片与角膜间形成充满生理盐水的空间。这个液体层能有效填充角膜不规则处，形成光滑均匀的光学表面，从而消除由角膜畸变引起的视力模糊和散光，显著改善患者的最佳矫正视力。文中采用的logMAR是一种国际通用的视力计量单位，数值越低视力越好，0.30 logMAR的改善在临床上具有重要意义。 这项前瞻性、多中心临床试验为巩膜镜在眼科学领域提供了强有力证据。其行业影响深远：它为传统矫正方式无效的复杂角膜病变患者提供了安全有效的选择，显著提升生活质量。研究结果，特别是在中国人群中的高成功率和舒适度，将推动巩膜镜在全球（尤其是亚洲市场）的普及和临床应用。这不仅将刺激相关医疗器械的研发生产，也将促进眼科验光和配镜服务专业化水平的提升，为眼科医疗行业带来新的增长点和技术突破。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: For adults with irregular corneas, a four-zone scleral lens demonstrates high effectiveness in improving visual performance, according to a study published in the September issue of Eye Discovery. Qi Tan, from Rayzon Medical Technology Co. Ltd. in Changsha, China, and colleagues conducted a prospective, multisite trial involving Chinese adults ages 18 to 65 with irregular corneas. Participants were fitted with a four-zone scleral lens; 138 participants were enrolled, and seven withdrew during the study period. The researchers found that among the remaining participants, after one month of scleral lens wear, best-corrected visual acuity improved by at least 0.20 logMAR in 128 participants, corresponding to a success rate of 97.7%; the median improvement was 0.30 logMAR. To achieve an optimal fit, an average of 1.5 lenses per eye was required. During the three-month follow-up period, no significant adverse events were reported. Most participants adhered to a regular lens-wearing regimen, with 72.5% and 83.9%, respectively, wearing lenses for at least eight hours per day and at least five days per week during the three-month period. In 90% of eyes, a high level of comfort was reported. "In conclusion, this prospective, multisite clinical trial provides robust evidence that scleral lenses are highly effective in improving visual performance in Chinese patients with irregular corneas, with minimal adverse effects on ocular health over a three-month period," the authors write. One author disclosed employment by Rayzon Medical Technology Co. Ltd., which funded the study. More information Qi Tan et al, Clinical outcomes of Chinese eyes with irregular corneas managed with a scleral lens, Eye Discovery (2026).
