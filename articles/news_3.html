一种药物如何可降低高危剖宫产中的严重出血

How one drug could lower dangerous bleeding in high-risk cesarean births

Giving tranexamic acid to women with placenta previa (when the placenta covers the cervical opening) undergoing cesarean birth leads to a significant yet modest reduction in severe bleeding after delivery with no evidence of an increase in serious adverse events, finds a trial from China published by The BMJ .

Tranexamic acid is widely used to prevent or reduce heavy bleeding usually after surgery or trauma. It works by inhibiting blood clot breakdown and is recommended for the treatment of severe bleeding after childbirth (postpartum hemorrhage).

But high quality evidence on its prophylactic use to prevent postpartum hemorrhage in high-risk women remains scarce.

To address this gap, researchers in China set out to examine the effect of tranexamic acid in women with placenta previa, a group at high risk of severe bleeding.

The trial included 1,694 pregnant women with placenta previa who were scheduled for cesarean delivery at 24 maternity units across China between July 2023 and March 2025.

Participants received prophylactic oxytocin—standard treatment to reduce blood loss after delivery—and were randomly assigned to receive either intravenous tranexamic acid (845 women) or placebo (849 women) over 10 minutes, starting within five minutes of umbilical cord clamping.

The main outcome measure was postpartum hemorrhage, defined as blood loss of at least 1000 mL or red blood cell transfusion within two days after delivery. Serious adverse events including blood clots, seizures, acute kidney or liver injury, and maternal death, were also recorded.

The results show that prophylactic tranexamic acid reduced the rate of postpartum hemorrhage by 15%, from 35.1% to 29.7% compared with placebo. This means that for every 19 women receiving prophylactic tranexamic acid, one case of postpartum hemorrhage would be prevented.

Rates of serious adverse effects were similar between the two groups.

The researchers acknowledge various limitations, including that the findings are specific to women with placenta previa receiving prophylactic oxytocin and therefore may not apply to other obstetric populations. However, this was a well-designed trial and results were consistent after further analyses, suggesting that the findings are robust.

As such, they conclude, "In a high risk population—specifically, women with placenta previa undergoing cesarean delivery—prophylactic tranexamic acid leads to a statistically significant but modest reduction in the incidence of postpartum hemorrhage."

"Future studies in diverse international settings are warranted to validate these results and to identify specific patient subgroups most likely to benefit from prophylactic use of tranexamic acid," they add.

In a linked editorial, UK researchers point out that this modest reduction in bleeding understates the impact, particularly in women at high risk of harm from bleeding, for whom even modest relative risk reductions translate into worthwhile benefits.

The focus should now shift from whether tranexamic acid reduces bleeding to how it is used to maximize patient benefit, they say, noting that in non-obstetric surgery, tranexamic acid is given before incision, but in cesarean section trials it is delayed until after cord clamping to avoid placental transfer.

They recommend evaluating pre-incision administration for cesarean section, while carefully monitoring maternal and neonatal outcomes.

一项由《英国医学杂志》(The BMJ) 发表的中国试验发现，对患有前置胎盘（指胎盘覆盖宫颈内口的情况）并接受剖宫产的产妇施用氨甲环酸，可显著但适度地减少产后严重出血，且没有证据表明严重不良事件有所增加。

氨甲环酸广泛用于预防或减少通常在手术或创伤后发生的大出血。它通过抑制纤溶过程发挥作用，并被推荐用于治疗产后大出血（产后出血）。

然而，关于其在产后出血高危产妇中预防性使用的优质证据仍然稀缺。

为了弥补这一空白，中国的研究人员着手评估氨甲环酸在前置胎盘产妇中的效果，这类产妇产后严重出血风险较高。

该试验纳入了1694名患有前置胎盘并计划在2023年7月至2025年3月期间在中国24家产科单位进行剖宫产的孕妇。

受试者接受了预防性催产素（一种减少产后失血的标准治疗），并在脐带钳夹后五分钟内开始，随机分配接受静脉注射氨甲环酸（845名产妇）或安慰剂（849名产妇），输注时间为10分钟。

主要结局指标是产后出血，定义为产后两天内失血量至少1000毫升或需要输注红细胞。严重不良事件，包括血栓形成、癫痫发作、急性肾或肝损伤以及孕产妇死亡，也一并记录。

结果显示，与安慰剂组相比，预防性氨甲环酸将产后出血的发生率降低了15%，从35.1%降至29.7%。这意味着每19名接受预防性氨甲环酸治疗的产妇中，即可预防一例产后出血。

两组间严重不良反应的发生率相似。

研究人员承认存在各种局限性，包括这些发现特异性地针对接受预防性催产素治疗的前置胎盘产妇，因此可能不适用于其他产科人群。然而，这是一项精心设计的试验，并且在进一步分析后结果仍然一致，表明研究结果具有稳健性。

因此，他们总结道：“在剖宫产的高危产妇（特别是前置胎盘产妇）中，预防性氨甲环酸可导致产后出血发生率统计学上显著但适度的降低。”

他们补充说：“未来有必要在不同的国际背景下进行研究，以验证这些结果，并确定最有可能从预防性使用氨甲环酸中获益的特定患者亚组。”

在一篇相关社论中，英国研究人员指出，这种适度的出血减少低估了其影响，尤其是在因出血而面临高风险的产妇中，对她们而言，即使是适度的相对风险降低，也能转化为有价值的益处。

他们表示，现在焦点应该从氨甲环酸是否能减少出血，转向如何使用它以最大限度地提高患者获益。他们指出，在非产科手术中，氨甲环酸通常在切口前给药，但在剖宫产试验中则推迟到脐带钳夹后给药，以避免胎盘转运。

他们建议评估剖宫产术前给药的方式，同时密切监测母婴结局。
