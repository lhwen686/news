Original Text
In ALIENTO, astegolimab every 2 weeks was associated with a lower annual rate of exacerbations versus placebo in patients with COPD and a history of frequent exacerbations. In ARNASA, these findings did not meet statistical significance. Together, these findings suggest a role for targeting the ST2/IL-33 pathway to reduce the frequency of COPD exacerbations in patients with limited treatment options.
