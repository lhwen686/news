Poorly validated research antibodies linked to millions of wasted samples

深度解析

这项研究揭示了抗体验证普遍不足的严重性及其在生物医学研究中的深远影响。抗体作为检测、定量和分离生物样本中特定蛋白质的关键试剂，其特异性是科研可信度的基石。如果抗体未能识别预期靶点或识别了非预期靶点，那么基于这些抗体产生的研究数据将不可靠，直接导致实验结果的不可重复性和错误的科学结论。从医学机制层面看，这可能误导疾病发病机制的理解、药物靶点的发现以及诊断方法的开发，最终影响临床实践和患者治疗效果。例如，一个基于未经验证抗体得出的药物靶点可能导致药物研发失败或产生意外副作用。从行业影响来看，这种普遍的验证失败不仅造成全球数百万动物和人体组织样本的浪费，引发严重的伦理问题（如患者知情同意的捐赠被浪费），还导致巨额科研经费的消耗，阻碍了医学科学的进步。研究强调，当前的市场激励机制未能充分奖励高质量抗体，出版商和资助者缺乏明确的验证要求，以及问题所有权的模糊，共同构成了系统性障碍。专家小组提出的解决方案，如强制性机构培训、专项验证预算和出版商报告要求，旨在建立一个更严格、负责任的科研生态系统。这些措施若能有效实施，将显著提升研究结果的可靠性和可重复性，加速创新药物和诊断工具的开发，从而推动整个生物医药产业的健康发展。

英文原文

Original
A widespread failure to validate research antibodies before use is estimated to lead to the avoidable waste of millions of animal and human tissue samples worldwide, and an international expert panel has reached consensus on how to address the problem, according to two studies published October 6th in the open access journal PLOS Biology by Harvinder Virk of the University of Leicester, UK, and colleagues. Antibodies are critical reagents that enable researchers to detect, quantify, and isolate specific proteins in biological samples. However, research antibodies do not always bind their intended targets, or may bind additional unintended targets. Studies have suggested that many antibodies do not bind as advertised. This lack of specificity can misdirect biomedical research across fields. In the first study, Virk and first author Michael Biddle combined data from focus groups (12 researchers), a survey (107 researchers), and an analysis of 785 publications linked to antibodies that had failed rigorous, knockout-controlled testing. Among 760 publications where validation status could be determined, only 120 (15.8%) presented any validation evidence, despite 72.0% of surveyed researchers reporting having used at least one recommended validation method. The papers lacking antibody validation used a minimum of 8,064 animal samples and 4,424 human tissue samples. Extrapolating, the researchers estimate that millions of animal and human tissue samples have been consumed globally without adequate validation. "This study provides, to our knowledge, the first systematic quantification of biological sample waste attributable to the use of poorly performing antibodies without context-specific validation," the researchers say. In the second study, Virk and first author Katherine Blades convened 32 international experts-including researchers, publishers, funders, antibody manufacturers and institutional leaders-for a two-round Delphi consensus exercise to rate proposed reforms for antibody validation. The panel agreed that 15 actions, including institutional training, dedicated validation budgets in grant applications, and publisher reporting requirements, were both effective and feasible for implementation by 2030; a further 15 actions were judged effective but of uncertain feasibility. Participants pointed to diffuse ownership of the problem, and market incentives that fail to reward antibody quality as key barriers. "The findings lend themselves to a program of targeted stakeholder consultation," the authors say. "To support this, we have prepared separate documents for each stakeholder group - publishers, funders, institutions, and manufacturers - presenting consensus recommendations alongside implementation options derived from the panel's qualitative feedback. These present options rather than prescriptions, recognizing that the optimal approach will vary across organizations, countries, and contexts." Harvinder Virk (corresponding author on both papers) says, "Around ten years ago I discovered that data I had submitted in a grant application relied on an antibody that did not detect its intended target. The data included staining of bronchial biopsies from patients who had given informed consent. They would not have expected their donation to be wasted. That has driven this work ever since." Michael Biddle (first author, "Inadequate antibody validation places substantial numbers of animal and human tissue samples at risk of waste") adds, "Researchers told us they validate their antibodies: 72% reported using at least one recommended method. But only 120 of the 760 papers we could assess showed any validation evidence. The other 640 studies reported at least 8,064 animal and 4,424 human tissue samples used with antibodies that had failed independent testing; we describe these samples as at risk of waste. In the clearest cases - where the antibody has since been withdrawn from sale, so the work cannot be reproduced at all - scaling to the commercial antibody market gives a lower-bound global estimate of 4 to 7 million animal samples and 6 to 11 million human tissue samples." Katherine Blades (first author, "Actionable solutions to address antibody validation failures") states, "What struck us was the level of agreement once researchers, funders, publishers, institutions and manufacturers were brought into the same process. The panel reached consensus on 15 actions that are both effective and achievable by 2030. Progress is held back not by disagreement about what to do, but because no single group owns the problem - so everyone waits for someone else to move first." Harvinder Virk notes, "Together these papers show the problem has significant impact with ethical dimensions, and is solvable. Since completing this work, we have embedded champions for better practice in 14 UK research institutions, and the University of Leicester has made antibody validation training mandatory for its bioscience postgraduate researchers. We are working with two national funders - the NC3Rs and Cancer Research UK - on implementation solutions, and have built free tools publishers can use to protect the integrity of what they publish."

广泛未能在使用前验证研究抗体，估计导致全球数百万动物和人体组织样本的本可避免的浪费。根据英国莱斯特大学的Harvinder Virk及其同事于10月6日发表在开放获取期刊PLOS Biology上的两项研究，一个国际专家小组已就如何解决该问题达成共识。 抗体是关键试剂，使研究人员能够在生物样本中检测、定量和分离特定蛋白质。然而，研究抗体并非总是结合其预期的靶点，或者可能结合额外的非预期靶点。研究表明，许多抗体的结合情况与宣传不符。这种特异性不足可能会误导跨领域的生物医学研究。 在第一项研究中，Virk和第一作者Michael Biddle结合了焦点小组（12名研究人员）、一项调查（107名研究人员）以及对785篇与未能通过严谨的基因敲除对照测试的抗体相关的出版物的分析数据。在760篇可确定验证状态的出版物中，尽管72.0%的受访研究人员表示至少使用了一种推荐的验证方法，但只有120篇（15.8%）提供了任何验证证据。缺乏抗体验证的论文使用了至少8,064个动物样本和4,424个人体组织样本。研究人员推断，全球数百万动物和人体组织样本在未进行充分验证的情况下被消耗。 研究人员表示：“据我们所知，这项研究首次系统性地量化了因使用了性能不佳且缺乏特定情境验证的抗体而导致的生物样本浪费。” 在第二项研究中，Virk和第一作者Katherine Blades召集了32位国际专家——包括研究人员、出版商、资助者、抗体制造商和机构领导者——进行了一项两轮德尔菲共识练习，以评估抗体验证的拟议改革措施。专家小组一致认为，15项行动，包括机构培训、在拨款申请中设立专门的验证预算以及出版商报告要求，到2030年实施既有效又可行；另有15项行动被认为有效但可行性不确定。与会者指出，问题的所有权分散以及未能奖励抗体质量的市场激励机制是关键障碍。 作者们表示：“这些发现有助于开展有针对性的利益相关者咨询计划。为此，我们为每个利益相关者群体——出版商、资助者、机构和制造商——准备了单独的文件，提出了共识性建议以及基于专家小组定性反馈的实施方案。这些文件是方案而非规定，认识到最佳方法将因组织、国家和具体情境而异。” Harvinder Virk（两篇论文的通讯作者）说：“大约十年前，我发现我在一份拨款申请中提交的数据依赖于一种未能检测到其预期靶点的抗体。这些数据包括对已签署知情同意书的患者支气管活检样本进行染色。他们不会期望自己的捐赠被浪费。从那时起，这便推动了我的这项工作。” Michael Biddle（第一作者，《不充分的抗体验证使大量动物和人体组织样本面临浪费的风险》）补充说：“研究人员告诉我们他们会验证抗体：72%的人报告使用至少一种推荐方法。但在我们能评估的760篇论文中，只有120篇显示了任何验证证据。其他640项研究报告了至少8,064个动物样本和4,424个人体组织样本，这些样本是使用未能通过独立测试的抗体。我们称这些样本有浪费风险。在最明确的案例中——即抗体已被停售，因此工作根本无法重现——推广到商业抗体市场，给出的全球最低估算为400万到700万个动物样本和600万到1100万个人体组织样本。” Katherine Blades（第一作者，《解决抗体验证失败的可行方案》）指出：“令我们震惊的是，一旦研究人员、资助者、出版商、机构和制造商被纳入同一流程，所达成的共识水平。专家小组就15项到2030年既有效又可实现的行动达成共识。进展的阻碍并非在于对做什么存在分歧，而是因为没有一个单一群体拥有这个问题——所以每个人都在等待别人先行动。” Harvinder Virk指出：“这些论文共同表明，这个问题具有重要的影响，涉及伦理层面，而且是可解决的。完成这项工作以来，我们已在14个英国研究机构设立了推广最佳实践的倡导者，莱斯特大学已将抗体验证培训列为其生物科学研究生研究人员的强制性要求。我们正与两个国家资助机构——NC3Rs和英国癌症研究中心——合作，研究实施解决方案，并开发了出版商可以用来保护其出版物完整性的免费工具。”
