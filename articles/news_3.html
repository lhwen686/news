深度解析

本研究揭示了tegoprubart作为一种新型抗CD40L抗体疗法，在肾移植免疫抑制治疗领域展现出巨大潜力。其核心专业概念在于作用机制与现有标准疗法的差异。他克莫司（tacrolimus）作为钙调磷酸酶抑制剂（CNI），通过抑制T淋巴细胞的活化来发挥免疫抑制作用，但其伴随严重的肾毒性、高血压、糖尿病等全身性副作用，长期使用对移植物功能和患者生活质量均构成威胁。相比之下，tegoprubart靶向CD40L通路。CD40L是T细胞表面的一种关键分子，与抗原提呈细胞（APC）上的CD40结合后，是T细胞活化、B细胞分化和抗体产生的重要共刺激信号，在同种异体移植物排斥反应中扮演核心角色。通过阻断CD40L，tegoprubart能够有效抑制免疫细胞间的通讯与活化，从而预防排斥反应，且避免了CNI的广谱毒性。这一机制差异解释了tegoprubart在保护肾功能、改善患者生活质量方面的优越性。其行业影响深远，有望取代他克莫司，成为移植免疫抑制的新标准，显著改善移植患者的长期预后和生活质量，并可能扩大移植适应症人群，具有颠覆性的临床价值。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: CC0 Public Domain The phase 2 BESTOW clinical trial showed that tegoprubart (a novel anti-CD40L antibody therapy) was better at preserving kidney function than tacrolimus (the current standard of care) in immunosuppressive regimens in adults receiving their first kidney transplant. Kidney graft function stabilized after the first month of treatment and remained higher in tegoprubart-treated patients than in tacrolimus-treated patients at each reported time point, with a statistically significant kidney function benefit at 18 months. A recent analysis of BESTOW data found that tegoprubart also caused greater improvements in patient-reported health-related quality of life compared with tacrolimus in study participants. The findings will be presented at ASN Kidney Week 2026, Oct. 22–25. Of 127 treated patients, 107 (51 taking tegoprubart and 56 taking tacrolimus) completed study treatment to week 52. Health-related quality of life improvements from baseline were observed at week 52 across all five subscales with tegoprubart, and four of five subscales with tacrolimus. Improvements were greater with tegoprubart than tacrolimus on a mental component summary, and patients on tegoprubart also had a numerically greater reduction in total symptom score from baseline. "Treatment today with tacrolimus helps prevent organ rejection but at a high cost to patients, with side effects such as kidney toxicity, hypertension, diabetes, tremors and brain fog. Tegoprubart, which works by targeting the CD40L pathway that is central to how immune cells communicate, has the potential to replace tacrolimus and become the new standard of care immunosuppression therapy across all transplant types, not just kidney transplantation," said corresponding author Andrew Adams, M.D., Ph.D., of the University of Minnesota Medical School. "These latest BESTOW trial results are encouraging that we may soon have a new medication that can prevent rejection without the side effects of tacrolimus and transform the broader transplant immunosuppression treatment landscape."
