Acute kidney injury up for older patients, men with OD-involved hospitalization

深度解析

这篇新闻深入揭示了药物过量与急性肾损伤（AKI）之间的复杂关联，突显了药物滥用除了致命后果外，也带来了严重的非致命性并发症。从医学机制来看，药物过量可直接导致肾脏毒性，例如某些药物本身就具有肾毒性。更常见的是，过量可能引起横纹肌溶解，导致肌红蛋白阻塞肾小管，引发急性肾小管坏死。此外，药物过量导致的休克、低血压会造成肾脏灌注不足，进而引发或加重AKI。电解质紊乱也是一个重要因素。 在行业影响方面，该研究为公共卫生策略提供了关键数据。它强调了药物过量预防不仅要关注死亡率，更要重视减少如AKI等严重发病率。对于临床实践而言，医护人员需提高对药物过量患者AKI风险的警惕性，特别是考虑到老年男性是高风险群体，而年轻患者则可能经历更严重的病程。早期识别和干预对保护肾功能至关重要。医疗系统应考虑加强住院后肾脏护理的连续性，以防AKI进展为慢性肾脏病，这会增加长期的医疗负担和患者生活质量风险。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain Acute kidney injury (AKI) occurs more often among older patients and men with drug overdose–involved hospitalizations, while severe inpatient courses disproportionately affect younger patients, according to research published in the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report. Noting that overdose mortality has declined recently in the United States, Sola Han, Pharm.D., Ph.D., from the CDC in Atlanta, and colleagues examined whether serious, nonfatal complications, including AKI, have followed similar trends. The researchers found that AKI was documented in 34.5% of 1,297,847 drug overdose–involved hospitalizations. The percentage of patients with AKI was 28.2%, 37.4%, 29.4% and 38.1% among those aged 24 years and younger, those aged 65 years and older, women and men, respectively. Among patients with a drug overdose–involved hospitalization, AKI increased during 2016 to 2022 (annual percentage change [APC], 2.69%) and decreased during 2022 to 2025 (APC, −5.11%). Overall, 60.9% of patients with a drug overdose–involved hospitalization and AKI experienced a severe inpatient course, including 68.1%, 56.1%, 61.9% and 60.4% of those 24 years and younger, those 65 years and older, women and men, respectively. There was an increase in the percentage of drug overdose–involved hospitalizations with a severe inpatient course during 2016 to 2023, followed by a decrease during 2023 to 2025 (APCs, 1.82% and −2.89%, respectively). "Public health efforts focusing on preventing drug overdose to reduce subsequent AKI risk, promoting early recognition of kidney injury, and improving continuity of kidney care after hospitalization might reduce the occurrence of drug overdose–involved AKI," the authors write. Publication details Sola Han et al., Acute Kidney Injury Among Patients with Drug Overdose–Involved Hospitalizations — United States, 2016–2025, MMWR. Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report (2026).
