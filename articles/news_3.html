医生警告：将电子设备置于裸露皮肤可因热损伤致毁容。

Resting electronic device on bare skin risks disfigurement due to heat, doctors warn

深度解析

火激性红斑（Erythema ab igne, EAI）是一种由长期、反复暴露于低度热源所引起的慢性热损伤性皮肤病。其病理机制涉及真皮乳头层血管扩张、表皮基底层黑色素细胞活性增加及炎症反应，临床表现为特征性的网状红斑或色素沉着，常伴有轻度萎缩和毛细血管扩张，即文中所述的“渔网状”皮疹和皮肤变色。该病例凸显了EAI在儿科人群中日益增长的发生率，尤其是由笔记本电脑等电子设备直接接触皮肤所致，这与历史上热水袋和电热垫引起的成人病例形成鲜明对比。此现象对公共卫生和临床实践具有多重影响。首先，医生在诊断儿童不明原因的皮疹时，需将长期使用电子设备的习惯纳入鉴别诊断范畴，以避免误诊或延误治疗。其次，这警示了电子设备制造商在产品设计中应更重视散热性能和用户皮肤接触安全性，尤其考虑到儿童的生理特点。长期不消除热源不仅导致持续性色素沉着，甚至有极低的恶性转化风险，通常为鳞状细胞癌。因此，加强公众教育，推广安全使用电子设备的习惯，对于预防儿童EAI及其潜在并发症至关重要。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Adam Sondel from Pexels Balancing an electronic device on bare skin risks disfigurement, doctors warn in the journal BMJ Case Reports, after treating a child with skin discoloration and a "fishnet" rash—hallmarks of erythema ab igne—caused by repeated exposure to the low-grade heat from a laptop. Although historically more common in adults as a result of hot-water bottle and heating pad use, the condition is becoming increasingly common in children due to the prevalence of electronic devices, they highlight. Their patient, a preteen boy, presented with an abdominal rash and what appeared to be spontaneous bruising, which he had had for a week. Neither he nor his parents were able to think of a likely trigger. He was initially suspected of having a liver injury following minor trauma. But his blood test results and abdominal scans were normal, and he was discharged. But two weeks later, he returned to the hospital. The rash had progressed and become more tender, with areas of skin peeling. On further questioning, the boy revealed that he was homeschooled and routinely used a laptop for educational activities. He explained that he rested his laptop on his abdomen for up to 8 hours a day, often while charging it. He was diagnosed with erythema ab igne and advised to stop resting his laptop on his body. A phone consultation with his father several months later indicated that the condition had gradually improved and that the rash had disappeared. "The increasing use of electronic devices, particularly during homeschooling, has emerged as an under-recognized cause of [erythema ab igne] in pediatric populations," the report authors point out. Usually, the condition clears up when the heat source is removed, but "rarely, chronic exposure may result in persistent pigmentation or, in longstanding cases, malignant transformation," they warn.
