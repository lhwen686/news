Firearm violence is the leading cause of death among U.S. youth. A new study from Boston Children's Hospital found that youth-perpetrated firearm homicides increased by 41% between 2018 and 2022. Stronger firearm laws were associated with lower homicide rates, with states in the strongest law quintile having a 66% lower homicide rate compared with those in the weakest quintile.

This underscores the importance of comprehensive firearm legislation for decreasing youth perpetrated firearm homicide. Findings from the study were presented during the Pediatric Academic Societies (PAS) 2026 Meeting, taking place April 24–27 in Boston.

Prior research has focused on youth as victims, with less known about youth as perpetrators. Understanding how firearm laws shape youth-perpetrated homicides is key to prevention.

"Firearm violence is now the leading cause of death for children in the U.S., yet we know far less about youth as perpetrators than as victims," said Ayesha Dholakia, fellow at Boston Children's Hospital and lead author of the study.

"Our study shows that states with stronger firearm laws have substantially lower rates of youth-perpetrated firearm homicide, underscoring the role of policy environments in prevention and the need to better understand who is driving youth firearm violence--and why."

Of 94,863 homicides, 37,858 (39.9%) recorded perpetrator age. Of these, 12,501 (33.0%) involved youth perpetrators, of which 10,263 (82.1%) involved firearms. After excluding 11 low data capture states, there were 9,167 youth-perpetrated firearm homicides involving 8,524 unique youth perpetrators.

Five-year average state-level rates ranged from 0.3 to 10.3 per 100,000 youth (median 1.8, IQR 1.3–3.7). Perpetrators had a median age of 20 years (IQR 18–22) and were majority male (93.2%) and of Black race (64.5%). Victims had a median age of 24 years (IQR 19–33) and were majority male (85.9%) and of Black race (62.5%).

枪支暴力是美国青少年死亡的首要原因。波士顿儿童医院的一项新研究发现，在2018年至2022年间，由青少年实施的枪支凶杀案增加了41%。更严格的枪支法律与较低的凶杀率相关，法律最严格的五分之一州相比法律最宽松的五分之一州，凶杀率降低了66%。

这强调了全面枪支立法对于减少青少年实施的枪支凶杀案的重要性。该研究的发现已在2026年4月24日至27日在波士顿举行的儿科学术学会（PAS）会议上公布。

此前研究主要关注作为受害者的青少年，而对于作为施暴者的青少年知之甚少。了解枪支法律如何影响青少年实施的凶杀案是预防的关键。

波士顿儿童医院研究员兼该研究主要作者Ayesha Dholakia表示：“枪支暴力现在是美国儿童死亡的首要原因，但我们对作为施暴者的青少年了解远少于作为受害者的青少年。”

“我们的研究表明，枪支法律更严格的州，青少年实施的枪支凶杀案发生率显著降低，这强调了政策环境在预防中的作用，以及需要更好地理解谁在助推青少年枪支暴力以及原因。”

在94,863起凶杀案中，有37,858起（39.9%）记录了施暴者的年龄。其中，12,501起（33.0%）涉及青少年施暴者，而这其中10,263起（82.1%）涉及枪支。在排除11个数据捕获率低的州后，共有9,167起由青少年实施的枪支凶杀案，涉及8,524名独特的青少年施暴者。

五年平均州级发生率在每10万青少年0.3至10.3之间（中位数为1.8，四分位距为1.3–3.7）。施暴者的中位年龄为20岁（四分位距为18–22岁），绝大多数为男性（93.2%）和黑人（64.5%）。受害者的中位年龄为24岁（四分位距为19–33岁），绝大多数为男性（85.9%）和黑人（62.5%）。
