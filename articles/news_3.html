深度解析

这篇研究聚焦于乳腺癌筛查中的多模态人工智能（AI）整合，旨在克服单一影像模态的固有局限。文章核心概念在于通过深度学习框架，协同利用超声（US）、乳腺X线摄影（DM）和数字乳腺断层合成（DBT）的互补诊断信息。传统DM易受致密乳腺组织重叠影响，US虽对软组织敏感却依赖操作者且对微钙化灶不足，DBT则增加阅片负荷。该研究采用并行分支深度学习架构，针对不同模态数据进行特异性处理，并通过特征层融合（技术原理），结合US提供的病灶形态特征（如回声、边缘）与DBT捕捉的结构扭曲、微钙化灶（医学机制），生成更精准的乳腺层面风险评估。 其行业影响深远。通过显著提高诊断特异性，该AI模型能有效减少假阳性召回，降低不必要的后续检查，从而减轻患者焦虑并优化医疗资源配置。尤其对于致密型乳腺或低可疑度病灶，其优势有望支持放射科医生更精确地进行分诊。这标志着AI在医学影像领域从辅助单一模态判读向多源信息深度融合的演进，预示着未来AI将作为整合性工具，提升乳腺癌早期筛查的效率和准确性，推动精准医疗的临床落地。大规模前瞻性验证将是其广泛应用的关键。

英文原文

Original
Breast cancer screening must balance diagnostic accuracy with workflow throughput. DM remains the traditional cornerstone, but its two-dimensional projection creates tissue-overlap noise, particularly in dense breasts, leading to false positives and obscured lesions. Ultrasound (US) is widely used as an adjunct because it is sensitive to soft-tissue masses in dense parenchyma, yet it is operator-dependent and less sensitive to microcalcifications. DBT improves lesion visibility and reduces recall, but generates large volumetric datasets that increase reading time and cognitive workload. AI has shown strong performance within single modalities, but most models cannot cross-verify complementary evidence across imaging streams. Given these challenges, in-depth research is needed on multimodal AI frameworks that integrate US, DM, and DBT for breast-level risk triage. Researchers at Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China, and collaborating institutions report the findings (DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag023) in Precision Clinical Medicine in 2026 (Volume 9, Issue 3). The team developed and validated a parallel-branch deep learning framework for breast-level risk classification using paired US, DM, and DBT examinations. Models were trained on 2,187 breasts and evaluated in an internal validation cohort of 632 breasts and an independent pathology-confirmed cohort of 500 breasts, with histopathology serving as the reference standard. The study compared six prespecified model configurations. The study compared three single-modality models—US, DM, and DBT—and three dual-modality models: US-DM, DM-DBT, and US-DBT. The US-DBT model achieved the highest observed area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) in both validation cohorts: 0.944 (95% confidence interval [CI], 0.926-0.963) internally and 0.934 (95% CI, 0.913-0.955) in the pathology-confirmed cohort. In the pathology-confirmed cohort, its specificity reached 0.955 (95% CI, 0.927-0.975), and its positive predictive value (PPV) was 0.958 (95% CI, 0.931-0.977), while sensitivity was 0.850 (95% CI, 0.807-0.887) and did not differ significantly from the main comparator models. Performance remained favorable in dense breasts, lesions smaller than 2 cm, and lower-suspicion Breast Imaging Reporting and Data System (BI-RADS) strata. The architecture used modality-specific branches, a modified 2.5D ResNet18 with grouped convolutions for DBT, a Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM), feature-level fusion, and a multilayer perceptron (MLP) classifier. Each model produced a breast-level probability score using a prespecified 0.50 threshold. The approach is designed for breast-level, not participant-level, output. The authors said the findings point to a practical role for complementary imaging. They said US contributes lesion-level information such as echogenicity, margins, posterior acoustic features, and internal structure, while DBT captures structural distortion, spiculation, and microcalcifications. Together, these data streams may help radiologists refine positive or discordant breast imaging findings. They emphasized that the model is not intended as a stand-alone screening or diagnostic system and that prospective, multicenter validation is needed before clinical implementation. The most consistent benefit, they said, was improved specificity without a significant loss of sensitivity. Clinically, a US-DBT adjunct could help prioritize cases that warrant further diagnostic evaluation and reduce unnecessary escalation caused by false-positive findings, especially in dense breasts, small lesions, and lower-suspicion BI-RADS categories. By converting complementary sonographic and tomosynthesis information into a unified breast-level risk estimate, the approach may support radiologist-led triage and more selective work-up. However, the authors caution that the retrospective, single-center design limits generalizability. Broader implementation will depend on external validation across institutions, imaging platforms, and patient populations, as well as prospective testing of real-time screening workflows and clinician trust. If validated, such a tool could complement, rather than replace, routine assessment and reduce avoidable anxiety. Source: West China Hospital of Sichuan University Journal reference: Tan, Y., et al. (2026). Artificial intelligence-based multimodal integration of ultrasound and digital breast tomosynthesis for breast-level risk classification. Precision Clinical Medicine. 乳腺癌筛查必须在诊断准确性与工作流程吞吐量之间取得平衡。乳腺X线摄影（DM）仍然是传统的基石，但其二维投影会产生组织重叠噪声，尤其是在致密型乳腺中，从而导致假阳性结果和病灶被遮蔽。超声（US）作为辅助手段被广泛使用，因为它对致密型乳腺中的软组织肿块敏感，但它具有操作者依赖性，且对微钙化灶的敏感性较低。数字乳腺断层合成（DBT）改善了病灶可见性并减少了召回率，但会生成大量体积数据，从而增加了阅片时间和认知负荷。人工智能（AI）在单一影像模态中表现出强大性能，但大多数模型无法在不同影像流之间交叉验证互补证据。鉴于这些挑战，需要对整合超声、乳腺X线摄影和数字乳腺断层合成的多模态人工智能框架进行深入研究，以实现乳腺层面的风险分级。 中山大学中山纪念医院（中国广州）及合作机构的研究人员在2026年《精准临床医学》（第9卷，第3期）上报告了这些发现（DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag023）。该团队开发并验证了一个基于并行分支深度学习框架，利用配对的超声、乳腺X线摄影和数字乳腺断层合成检查数据进行乳腺层面的风险分类。模型在2187例乳腺数据上进行训练，并在包含632例乳腺的内部验证队列和包含500例经病理证实乳腺的独立队列中进行评估，其中组织病理学作为参考标准。该研究比较了六种预设的模型配置。 该研究比较了三种单模态模型：超声、乳腺X线摄影和数字乳腺断层合成，以及三种双模态模型：超声-乳腺X线摄影、乳腺X线摄影-数字乳腺断层合成和超声-数字乳腺断层合成。超声-数字乳腺断层合成模型在两个验证队列中均获得了最高的受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUC）：内部验证队列为0.944（95%置信区间[CI]，0.926-0.963），病理证实队列为0.934（95% CI，0.913-0.955）。在病理证实队列中，其特异性达到0.955（95% CI，0.927-0.975），阳性预测值（PPV）为0.958（95% CI，0.931-0.977），而敏感性为0.850（95% CI，0.807-0.887），与主要比较模型无显著差异。在致密型乳腺、小于2厘米的病灶以及较低可疑度乳腺影像报告和数据系统（BI-RADS）分级中，模型性能依然良好。该架构采用了模态特异性分支，其中数字乳腺断层合成使用了带分组卷积的改良2.5D ResNet18，还包括卷积块注意力模块（CBAM）、特征层融合和一个多层感知器（MLP）分类器。每个模型使用预设的0.50阈值生成一个乳腺层面的概率评分。该方法设计用于乳腺层面的输出，而非受检者层面的输出。 作者表示，这些发现指出了互补影像在临床实践中的实际作用。他们指出，超声提供了病灶层面的信息，例如回声、边缘、后方声学特征和内部结构，而数字乳腺断层合成则捕获结构扭曲、毛刺状和微钙化灶。这些数据流相结合，可以帮助放射科医生精确评估阳性或不一致的乳腺影像学发现。他们强调，该模型并非旨在作为独立的筛查或诊断系统，在临床实施之前，需要进行前瞻性、多中心验证。他们表示，最显著的益处是提高了特异性，而没有显著的敏感性损失。 在临床上，超声-数字乳腺断层合成辅助工具可以帮助优先处理需要进一步诊断评估的病例，并减少由假阳性结果引起的非必要升级，尤其是在致密型乳腺、小病灶和较低可疑度BI-RADS类别中。通过将互补的超声和断层合成信息转化为统一的乳腺层面风险评估，该方法可以支持放射科医生主导的分诊和更具选择性的检查。然而，作者提醒，回顾性、单中心设计限制了其普遍适用性。更广泛的实施将取决于跨机构、影像平台和患者群体的外部验证，以及对实时筛查工作流程和临床医生信任度的前瞻性测试。如果获得验证，这种工具可以补充而非取代常规评估，并减少可避免的焦虑。 来源：四川大学华西医院 期刊参考文献：Tan, Y., 等人 (2026).
