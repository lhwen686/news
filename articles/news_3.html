基于人工智能的监测揭示服用GLP-1受体激动剂减肥人群的蛋白质缺乏

AI-based monitoring reveals protein deficiencies in people taking GLP-1 receptor agonists for weight loss

💡 AI 深度解析 (AI Insight)

这篇医学新闻揭示了新一代GLP-1受体激动剂（GLP-1 RAs），如司美格鲁肽和替西帕肽，在真实世界应用中面临的严峻挑战。这些药物通过模拟GLP-1和GIP等肠道激素，显著增加饱腹感、减少食欲，从而有效降低卡路里摄入并促成体重减轻。然而，核心专业概念在于，这种药理学诱导的食欲抑制可能导致普遍的营养不足，尤其是蛋白质摄入量极低（0.6克/公斤/天），并增加漏餐现象。这引发了对伴随肌肉流失的担忧，而肌肉是维持代谢健康和长期体重管理的关键。鉴于肥胖症患者和积极减重期间对蛋白质的需求本身就更高，这种不足对患者的长期健康构成潜在风险，可能导致肌少症及其他微量营养素缺乏症。该研究对行业的影响深远，它强调了在GLP-1 RA治疗肥胖时，必须整合主动的营养监测和个性化饮食指导，包括定制蛋白质摄入目标和阻力运动，以实现超越体重减轻的全面健康管理。此外，人工智能驱动的营养追踪技术为实时、精确地评估饮食模式提供了创新方案，有效克服了传统方法的局限性，预示着未来临床实践中数据驱动型个性化干预措施的重要性。

Adults with overweight or obesity taking the new generation of weight loss drugs semaglutide and tirzepatide tend to eat significantly less, leaving them vulnerable to nutritional deficiencies, according to one of the first real-world studies to examine dietary behavior in people using glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists and dual GLP-1/glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) dual agonists RAs (collectively referred to as GLP-1 RAs) with the help of an AI-powered nutritional tracking app.

The study, being presented at the European Congress on Obesity (ECO) in Istanbul, Turkey (12–15 May), is by Dr. Valentina Vinelli from the metabolic health research team, led by Dr. Rebecca De Lorenzo and Professor Patrizia Rovere Querini at IRCCS San Raffaele Hospital and Vita-Salute San Raffaele University in Milan, Italy, in collaboration with the Robin Health team.

"While GLP-1 RAs produce impressive weight loss, the question of whether meaningful muscle loss accompanies fat loss with these therapies is still hotly debated. What remains universally agreed, however, is that protecting muscle mass should be a central goal of any weight-loss program," said lead author Dr. Vinelli.

"Muscle health depends on sufficient protein intake and regular physical activity, especially resistance exercise. For people using GLP-1 RAs, reduced appetite can make it harder to meet protein needs, making what you eat matter more than ever, especially given that protein requirements tend to be higher in people with obesity and during active weight loss.

"Protein requirements should always be personalized, expressed as grams per kilogram of adjusted body weight, rather than treated as a one-size-fits-all target."

How GLP-1 drugs change appetite

GLP1-RAs such as semaglutide (sold under the brand names Ozempic/Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound) mimic hormones the body already produces, GLP-1 and GLP-1/GIP respectively, that are released in response to eating, increasing a feeling of fullness and reducing food cravings.

GLP-1 RAs have been shown to reduce calorie intake by between 16–39%, making them a powerful tool for the treatment of obesity. However, little research has examined their impact on diet quality or the intake of key macro- and micronutrients, such as protein, fiber, vitamins, iron and calcium, needed to maintain good health.

Inadequate micronutrient intake can put people at risk of a range of conditions, from hair loss, fatigue, and slow wound healing to an impaired immune system and osteoporosis.

"While GLP-1 RAs medications are transforming obesity care, we still know very little about how they affect eating patterns and nutrient intake," explained Dr. Vinelli. "What's more, many people taking GLP-1 RAs receive little or no systematic guidance on diet quality, protein intake, or micronutrient adequacy."

Traditional dietary assessment tools such as food questionnaires and diaries are limited by recall bias. Recent advances in AI and digital health offer promising solutions, allowing users of mobile apps to record and analyze their eating habits in real time.

Inside the AI app-based study

In response to these trends, researchers analyzed 5,741 days of data from 332 adults with overweight or obesity (who gave their consent) using a mobile dietary tracking app between July 2025 and February 2026.

They compared total energy intake, macronutrient intake, weight-adjusted protein adequacy, meal patterns, and weight loss between 116 GLP-1 RA users (using either semaglutide or tirzepatide) and 216 non-users (average BMI 31.3 vs. 30.6 kg/m², 52.5 vs. 50.0 years, 72% vs. 63% female, respectively).

Of the 116 GLP-1 RA users in the study, 87 logged at least one injection in the app. Of those, 62 were using tirzepatide and 25 were using semaglutide. The remaining 29 were identified as GLP-1 users through their profile but did not log individual injections. It is important to note that the dose data reflects what users chose to log in the app.

Of the 25 semaglutide users who logged their injections, 10 (40%) recorded a maximum dose of 0.25 mg (the starting dose), four (16%) recorded 0.5 mg, six (24%) recorded 1.0 mg, two (8%) recorded 1.7 mg, and three (12%) recorded 2.4 mg (the maintenance dose).

Of the 62 tirzepatide users who logged their injections, 12 (19%) recorded a maximum dose of 2.5 mg (the starting dose), 21 (34%) recorded 5.0 mg, 10 (16%) recorded 7.5 mg, 17 (27%) recorded 10.0 mg, and two (3%) recorded 12.5 mg.

The Robin Health mobile app includes image recognition (from a photo of a meal, the app analyzes ingredients and calculates calories, carbohydrates, proteins, and fats), barcode scanning, as well as text and voice logging, providing real-time macronutrient intake estimates.

What the researchers discovered about intake

The analysis found that participants using GLP-1 RAs medications reported significantly lower total energy intake compared with non-users (average 1,102 vs. 1,281 kcal/day) as well as significantly lower intake of macronutrients, including protein (53.8 vs. 62.0 g/day), carbohydrates (128 vs. 143 g/day), and fat (39.7 vs. 45.7 g/day).

Despite lower absolute intake, macronutrient distribution (the proportion of daily calories derived from macronutrients) was nearly identical (protein 20% vs. 19%; carbohydrate 47% vs. 46%; fat 33% vs. 34%), indicating a proportional reduction in food intake rather than changes in diet quality.

However, weight-adjusted daily protein intake was critically low in GLP-1 RA users (0.6 g/kg/day), with the majority (88%) falling below the Italian national recommendation for protein intake (of 0.9 g/kg/day). In non-users, protein consumption was also below this recommendation at 0.7 g/kg/day, with 69% below the national recommendation, showing that inadequate protein consumption is a concern regardless of the weight loss strategy employed, but more of a concern in those using GLP-1-RAs.

According to the most recent Italian national food consumption survey (IV SCAI, 2017 to 2020, published in Nutrients 2025), the average Italian adult consumes 1.1 to 1.2 g/kg/day of protein, well above the LARN recommendation of 0.9 g/kg/day. Only about 7% of the general Italian population falls below this threshold.

Moreover, GLP-1 RA users were much more likely to skip breakfast (31% vs. 16% of days), lunch (31% vs. 18%) and dinner (40% vs. 30%) than non-users—reducing opportunities for adequate protein distribution across the day.

Weight loss outcomes and real-world use

As expected, among the subset of 177 participants (76 GLP-1 RA users and 101 non-users) with longitudinal weight data, GLP-1 RA users experienced significantly greater weight loss than non-users (average −2.2 kg, −2.5% vs. −0.2 kg, −0.2%), with nearly a quarter (24%, 18 participants) achieving clinically meaningful weight loss (at least 5% body weight loss) compared with just 3% (three participants) of non-users.

The authors explain that the modest weight loss seen in the GLP-1 group reflects the real-world conditions of the study.

The study did not independently verify or control drug adherence; medication use was self-reported through the app, and it is possible that some users continued taking their medication without logging it. Most users tracked their weight for only a few weeks (median duration between first and last weight measurements across the group was 22 days), and the majority of those who did log their injections appeared to still be in the dose titration phase rather than on full maintenance doses.

However, among the 13 users who tracked weight for at least three months, the average weight lost was 7.3%, on course towards the 10% plus weight loss seen at 12 months in randomized clinical trials.

Why nutritional support still matters

The authors say, "Real-world GLP-1 RA use in adults with overweight or obesity is associated with widespread protein inadequacy and increased meal skipping, underscoring the urgent need for proactive nutritional monitoring and personalized dietary guidance to be integrated alongside obesity treatment to support long-term health beyond weight loss," said Dr. Vinelli.

"In our future work, we will look beyond protein to explore how GLP-1 RAs affect the intake of other key nutrients and examine how real-time mobile apps could help people stay on track with healthy eating and lifestyle habits throughout their journey."

The authors acknowledge several limitations to the study, including the small sample size and its descriptive nature, which precludes causal inference and warrants confirmation in larger studies.

In addition, dietary data were self-reported via app-based logging and may underestimate true intake, although AI-assisted multimodal capture likely reduces recall bias compared to traditional dietary assessment methods.

根据首批旨在借助人工智能营养追踪应用，考察使用胰高血糖素样肽-1受体激动剂（GLP-1受体激动剂）以及GLP-1/葡萄糖依赖性促胰岛素多肽（GIP）双重激动剂（统称为GLP-1受体激动剂）人群饮食行为的真实世界研究之一，服用新一代减肥药物司美格鲁肽和替西帕肽的超重或肥胖成年人，食量往往显著减少，从而使他们容易出现营养缺乏。

该研究由意大利米兰IRCCS圣拉斐尔医院和Vita-Salute圣拉斐尔大学代谢健康研究团队的瓦伦蒂娜·维内利博士（Dr. Valentina Vinelli）主导，该团队由丽贝卡·德·洛伦佐博士（Dr. Rebecca De Lorenzo）和帕特里齐亚·罗维尔·奎里尼教授（Professor Patrizia Rovere Querini）领导，并与Robin Health团队合作，在土耳其伊斯坦布尔举行的欧洲肥胖大会（ECO）（5月12日至15日）上发表。

“虽然GLP-1受体激动剂能带来显著的体重减轻，但这些疗法在减脂的同时是否伴随有意义的肌肉流失，仍是一个激烈争论的问题。然而，普遍认同的是，保护肌肉质量应是任何减肥计划的核心目标，”该研究的主要作者维内利博士说。

“肌肉健康取决于充足的蛋白质摄入和规律的体育活动，尤其是阻力训练。对于使用GLP-1受体激动剂的人群来说，食欲下降可能使其更难满足蛋白质需求，这使得饮食内容比以往任何时候都更加重要，特别是考虑到肥胖人群和积极减重期间的蛋白质需求往往更高。”

“蛋白质需求应始终个性化，以每公斤调整后体重的克数表示，而不是作为一刀切的目标。”

GLP-1药物如何改变食欲

司美格鲁肽（商品名Ozempic/Wegovy）和替西帕肽（商品名Mounjaro/Zepbound）等GLP-1受体激动剂模拟身体自身产生的激素，分别是GLP-1和GLP-1/GIP，这些激素在进食后释放，增加饱腹感并减少对食物的渴望。

GLP-1受体激动剂已被证明可将卡路里摄入量降低16-39%，使其成为治疗肥胖的有力工具。然而，很少有研究考察它们对饮食质量或维持健康所需的关键宏量和微量营养素（如蛋白质、纤维、维生素、铁和钙）摄入量的影响。

微量营养素摄入不足可能使人们面临一系列疾病的风险，从脱发、疲劳、伤口愈合缓慢到免疫系统受损和骨质疏松症。

“尽管GLP-1受体激动剂正在改变肥胖症的治疗方式，但我们对其如何影响饮食模式和营养摄入仍然知之甚少，”维内利博士解释道。“此外，许多服用GLP-1受体激动剂的人几乎没有或根本没有获得关于饮食质量、蛋白质摄入量或微量营养素充足性的系统性指导。”

传统的饮食评估工具，如食物问卷和日记，受回忆偏差的限制。人工智能和数字健康领域的最新进展提供了有前景的解决方案，允许移动应用程序用户实时记录和分析他们的饮食习惯。

AI应用研究的内部细节

针对这些趋势，研究人员分析了2025年7月至2026年2月期间，332名超重或肥胖成年人（已同意参与）使用一款移动饮食追踪应用所产生的5,741天数据。

他们比较了116名GLP-1受体激动剂使用者（服用司美格鲁肽或替西帕肽）与216名非使用者之间的总能量摄入、宏量营养素摄入、体重调整后的蛋白质充足性、饮食模式和体重减轻情况（平均BMI分别为31.3 vs. 30.6 kg/m²，年龄分别为52.5 vs. 50.0岁，女性比例分别为72% vs. 63%）。

在这项研究的116名GLP-1受体激动剂使用者中，有87人在应用程序中至少记录了一次注射。其中，62人使用替西帕肽，25人使用司美格鲁肽。其余29人通过其个人资料被识别为GLP-1使用者，但未记录单次注射。值得注意的是，剂量数据反映了用户选择在应用程序中记录的内容。

在25名记录了注射的司美格鲁肽使用者中，10人（40%）记录了0.25毫克的最大剂量（起始剂量），4人（16%）记录了0.5毫克，6人（24%）记录了1.0毫克，2人（8%）记录了1.7毫克，3人（12%）记录了2.4毫克（维持剂量）。

在62名记录了注射的替西帕肽使用者中，12人（19%）记录了2.5毫克的最大剂量（起始剂量），21人（34%）记录了5.0毫克，10人（16%）记录了7.5毫克，17人（27%）记录了10.0毫克，2人（3%）记录了12.5毫克。

Robin Health移动应用包括图像识别（通过餐点照片分析成分并计算卡路里、碳水化合物、蛋白质和脂肪）、条形码扫描以及文本和语音记录功能，提供实时的宏量营养素摄入估算。

研究人员关于摄入量的发现

分析发现，与非使用者相比，使用GLP-1受体激动剂药物的参与者报告的总能量摄入显著较低（平均1,102 vs. 1,281 千卡/天），宏量营养素摄入量也显著较低，包括蛋白质（53.8 vs. 62.0 克/天）、碳水化合物（128 vs. 143 克/天）和脂肪（39.7 vs. 45.7 克/天）。

尽管绝对摄入量较低，但宏量营养素分布（每日卡路里来自宏量营养素的比例）几乎相同（蛋白质20% vs. 19%；碳水化合物47% vs. 46%；脂肪33% vs. 34%），这表明食物摄入量呈比例下降，而非饮食质量发生改变。

然而，GLP-1受体激动剂使用者的体重调整后每日蛋白质摄入量极低（0.6 克/公斤/天），其中大多数（88%）低于意大利国家蛋白质摄入推荐量（0.9 克/公斤/天）。非使用者的蛋白质摄入量也低于此推荐量，为0.7 克/公斤/天，其中69%低于国家推荐量，这表明无论采用何种减肥策略，蛋白质摄入不足都是一个问题，但在GLP-1受体激动剂使用者中更令人担忧。

根据最新的意大利全国食物消费调查（IV SCAI，2017年至2020年，2025年发表于《营养素》期刊），意大利成年人的平均蛋白质摄入量为1.1至1.2 克/公斤/天，远高于LARN推荐的0.9 克/公斤/天。只有约7%的意大利普通民众低于此阈值。

此外，与非使用者相比，GLP-1受体激动剂使用者更有可能不吃早餐（31% vs. 16%的天数）、午餐（31% vs. 18%）和晚餐（40% vs. 30%）——这减少了全天蛋白质充分分布的机会。

减重结果与真实世界应用

正如预期，在具有纵向体重数据的177名参与者子集中（76名GLP-1受体激动剂使用者和101名非使用者），GLP-1受体激动剂使用者比非使用者经历了显著更大的体重减轻（平均-2.2公斤，-2.5% vs. -0.2公斤，-0.2%），其中近四分之一（24%，18名参与者）实现了临床上有意义的体重减轻（至少5%的体重减轻），而非使用者中仅有3%（3名参与者）达到此目标。

作者解释说，GLP-1组观察到的适度体重减轻反映了该研究的真实世界条件。

该研究并未独立核实或控制药物依从性；药物使用是通过应用程序自行报告的，并且一些使用者可能在未记录的情况下继续服药。大多数使用者仅追踪了数周的体重（整个组首次和最后一次体重测量之间的中位持续时间为22天），并且大多数记录了注射的用户似乎仍处于剂量滴定阶段，而非完全维持剂量。

然而，在至少追踪了三个月的13名使用者中，平均体重减轻了7.3%，这与随机临床试验中12个月时观察到的10%以上体重减轻趋势一致。

为何营养支持依然重要

作者表示：“维内利博士说，‘超重或肥胖成年人在真实世界中使用GLP-1受体激动剂与普遍的蛋白质摄入不足和餐次遗漏增加相关，这凸显了将积极的营养监测和个性化饮食指导与肥胖治疗相结合的紧迫性，以支持超越体重减轻的长期健康。’”

“在我们未来的工作中，我们将超越蛋白质，探索GLP-1受体激动剂如何影响其他关键营养素的摄入，并研究实时移动应用程序如何帮助人们在整个减重过程中保持健康的饮食和生活习惯。”

作者承认该研究存在一些局限性，包括样本量小和描述性性质，这排除了因果推断，需要在大规模研究中进行证实。

此外，饮食数据通过基于应用程序的记录自行报告，可能低估真实摄入量，尽管人工智能辅助的多模式捕获与传统饮食评估方法相比，可能减少回忆偏差。
