深度解析

这篇新闻深度揭示了注意缺陷多动障碍（ADHD）远超其字面含义，并非仅是简单的运动过度，而是一种影响个体终生身心健康的广泛系统性神经发育障碍。文章强调了ADHD在童年期的学业与社交困境，青春期的物质滥用风险，以及成年期的身心健康恶化。核心专业概念在于，研究正从宏观行为表现（如时间感知障碍、独特的呼吸模式）深入到微观神经生理机制（如脑部铁含量降低、特定脑区受影响），并探索其“神经特征”。这些发现提示ADHD可能涉及更复杂的神经递质失衡、脑结构与功能异常，以及潜在的代谢问题。 这些突破性研究对医疗行业具有深远影响。诊断标准或需拓宽，未来可能整合生物标志物（如脑铁水平）和生理指标（如呼吸模式），实现更客观、早期的诊断。治疗策略将更精准和多元化，不再局限于单一兴奋剂药物，而是根据个体神经生理异常，考虑整合行为疗法、生理调节乃至代谢干预。文章还揭示了ADHD药物对共病（如酒精使用障碍）的潜在益处，为联合治疗提供了新思路。理解ADHD的系统性本质将推动多学科合作，从基因、神经影像到临床行为学，共同探索更全面、有效的干预措施，从而显著改善患者的长期预后和生活质量。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain New attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) research shows the condition influences mental and physical health across the life span. The findings were presented at Neuroscience 2026, the annual meeting of the Society for Neuroscience. Approximately one in nine children in the U.S. has received a diagnosis of ADHD, making it one of the most common neurodevelopmental disorders of childhood. An ADHD diagnosis is not only predictive of poorer outcomes in educational achievement and socialization in childhood, but is also associated with increased risk for substance use and abuse in adolescence and worse mental and physical health in adulthood. Researchers are discovering how the disorder affects a range of behaviors, such as breathing, time perception and substance use, and identifying neural signatures that may give rise to them. New findings show that: Young adults with ADHD show a unique breathing pattern that differentiates them from people without ADHD and changes with the use of stimulant medication (Timna Soroka, Weizmann Institute of Science) Individuals with ADHD experience chronic time pressure, or a persistent sense of running behind (Kelly Carrasco, California State University, Fresno) Brain scans of children and adolescents with ADHD show lower brain iron levels compared to controls and identify specific brain regions affected over time (Jonathan Ahern, University of California, San Diego) ADHD medication could help reduce problem drinking in young adults with both ADHD and alcohol use disorder (Amanda Elton, University of Florida) "It is becoming clear that ADHD is a broader systemic condition affecting a host of brain and body variables beyond just motor hyperactivity," says event moderator Chandan Vaidya, Ph.D., a cognitive neuroscientist at Georgetown University who studies how regulatory behavior goes awry in disorders such as ADHD. "Together, these new findings tackle timely topics and showcase the diversity of areas where work is needed to better understand ADHD and ultimately improve diagnosis and treatment."
