Scientists pinpoint key hotspot driving tau propagation in dementia

深度解析

这项研究深入揭示了神经退行性tau蛋白病变（如阿尔茨海默病和额颞叶痴呆）中，tau蛋白错误折叠及病理性传播的核心分子机制。此前，tau蛋白如何精确地从正常构象转化为致病性错误折叠并持续聚集，一直是未解之谜。本研究的关键突破在于识别了tau蛋白疏水区域内的一个“显性热点”，并阐明了其独特的水合作用模式。研究发现，水分子在这些疏水热点周围形成动态的“帽状”结构以维持稳定。当两个tau蛋白分子或一个新tau分子与现有错误折叠原纤维末端相遇时，这些“帽状”水分子被释放。正是这种水分子的释放提供了能量，驱动了两个热点间的精确结合，促使新加入的tau蛋白发生匹配性错误折叠，从而实现病理性tau原纤维的延伸和病理传播。这种精确的结合与对齐是维持聚集持续发展的关键。 这一机制的阐明对神经退行性疾病的诊断和治疗具有深远影响。首先，该“热点”可作为潜在的生物标志物，有望开发出早期、特异性的tau蛋白病变诊断工具。其次，该热点成为明确的药物靶点，为理性设计小分子或生物制剂提供了基础，以阻断正常tau蛋白被诱导错误折叠，从而阻止神经原纤维缠结的形成和疾病进展。这为阿尔茨海默病等疾病的治疗提供了新的干预策略。最后，理解此机制也为合成疾病模拟的tau原纤维提供了理论依据，对于建立更准确的体外和体内疾病模型至关重要，将加速药物筛选和基础研究，推动治疗方案的开发。

英文原文

Original
Diseases such as Alzheimer's and frontotemporal dementia are considered neurodegenerative tauopathies, in which tau proteins in the brain misfold and lead to cognitive decline. Until now, it has been unclear how misfolded tau recruits other seemingly healthy tau and causes it to misfold and clump. A new study led by Northwestern University researchers, in collaboration with researchers with the University of California, Santa Barbara, found that tau vulnerable to misfolding carries a single dominant hotspot. That site binds the matching site on a neighboring tau, or on a tau at the end of an existing fibril, and the incoming tau then misfolds to match. The hotspot is hydrated by water that is wrapping over and protecting a hydrophobic site, and releasing that water is what pulls the two together. This finding could help scientists diagnose tauopathies, block misfolded tau from converting other normal tau to misfolded shapes, and design therapeutic solutions in the future. The study will publish October 16 in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. Once you know that aggregation-prone tau proteins always have a hotspot that is first attracted to the same site of tau that is stacked to fibrils, then you know there's always some part of the protein that you can target to neutralize. If you know where to block its action of misfolding, you could block it to prevent it from propagating." Songi Han, senior author on the study, Northwestern University Songi Han is a Mark and Nancy Ratner Professor of Chemistry at Northwestern's Weinberg College of Arts and Sciences and the vice chair of the American Chemical Society's physical chemistry division. When it comes to understanding why they misfold and propagate their misfolded shape, tau proteins have been a black box for researchers. Unlike other proteins, tau protein monomers are not structured. They stack together, however, in perfect register to form long fibrils, with every tau molecule adopting the same shape upon attaching to the growing fibril end. How do thousands of noodle-like, unstructured molecules know to find each other, recreate that same misfolded shape and stack in perfect alignment, like a tower of poker chips thousands high? This is where Han and her team stepped in. "The proteins have to misfold and neatly stack to fibrils to elongate while propagating the same misfolded shape, and the only way that this process is forever is if the same site of the protein stacks to the same," Han said. "If you have a tiny mismatch in the alignment of the tau shapes that stack to the fibril end, the structure will eventually stop growing." The chemistry of tau proteins dictates the architecture of the fibrils they build, with each misfolded tau stacking on top of the one beneath it to hold the fibril together. These tau fibrils build up inside neurons as neurofibrillary tangles, disrupt communication among neurons and, when the cell dies and the misfolded tau proteins are released, they can "infect" other tau proteins that are floating around and waiting to be used. Like all proteins in a cell, tau is surrounded by water. But that water does not interact with every part of the protein in the same way. Some regions readily attract and interact with water. But other regions are hydrophobic, meaning they tend to repel water. Han and her team found unusual water structuring around hotspots within tau's hydrophobic regions. At these spots, water forms a cap reminiscent of ice, except that the water molecules stay dynamic. These caps mark where the two tau molecules will meet. "You have a certain site, and water cannot quite coat it because it doesn't like water. But that part still needs to be protected, because if you don't, then the protein crashes out and is no longer available," Han said. "What water tends to do is build a stabilizing cap around it and basically these water caps attract each other." When two hotspots meet, the capping water is released, and the energy gained by freeing it locks them together. This ensures the tau proteins will line up the same way, every time. When the team capped the hotspot with a chemical tag, the fibrils stopped growing, compared to when they capped two other sites away from the hotspot that did not stop fibril growth. Now that researchers understand how tau continues to misfold new tau and where this interaction begins, scientists can focus their efforts on blocking that hotspot before the fibrils recruit more tau and spreads to other neurons. "We might have found a shortcut for the pharmacist or biochemist because instead of searching for everything, we can say, 'Look for that hotspot. That's the spot that matters,'" Han said. "We are trying to give it the principle of how it works so that others can find solutions better, faster or more rationally." Understanding how tau works also could allow researchers to create synthetic tau fibrils that mimic the various tauopathy diseases, and to develop diagnostic and therapeutic agents. Researchers currently do not have good models for neurodegenerative disease research. "In order to cure a disease, one needs to be able to recreate it," Han said. "There's a real problem that disease-mimicking tau fibrils cannot be synthetically made easily. It's really difficult to try to do any development with human patient tissue." To synthetically make a protein, researchers need to understand how the protein works. This study could be a critical step in creating synthetic proteins, and not necessarily just tau proteins, for future medical research. This study was supported by the National Institutes of Health (award numbers R01AG05605 and R35GM136411), the Tau Consortium of the Rainwater Charitable Fund and the German Research Foundation. The W. M. Keck Foundation supported the ongoing experimental and computational method developments for the tau shape propagation study. Computational modeling was supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF-ANR MCB/PHY 2423885).

阿尔茨海默病和额颞叶痴呆等疾病被认为是神经退行性tau蛋白病变，其中大脑中的tau蛋白错误折叠，导致认知能力下降。迄今为止，错误折叠的tau蛋白如何招募其他看似健康的tau蛋白并导致其错误折叠和聚集，一直尚不清楚。 西北大学研究人员与加州大学圣巴巴拉分校的研究人员合作，主导的一项新研究发现，易于错误折叠的tau蛋白带有一个单一的显性热点。该位点与相邻tau蛋白上或现有原纤维末端的tau蛋白上的匹配位点结合，随后进入的tau蛋白会错误折叠以匹配。该热点由包裹并保护疏水位点水合形成，而释放这些水正是将两者拉在一起的原因。这一发现可能有助于科学家诊断tau蛋白病变，阻止错误折叠的tau蛋白将其他正常tau蛋白转化为错误折叠的形状，并在未来设计治疗方案。 该研究将于10月16日发表在《美国国家科学院院刊》上。 “一旦我们知道易于聚集的tau蛋白总是带有一个热点，该热点首先被堆叠到原纤维上的tau蛋白的相同位点所吸引，那么我们就知道总有蛋白质的某个部分可以作为靶点进行中和。如果我们知道在哪里阻止其错误折叠的作用，就可以阻止其传播。”——宋义·韩，该研究的资深作者，西北大学。 宋义·韩是西北大学温伯格文理学院的马克和南希·拉特纳化学教授，也是美国化学学会物理化学分会的副主席。 对于理解tau蛋白为何错误折叠并传播其错误折叠的形状，tau蛋白一直对研究人员来说是一个“黑箱”。与其他蛋白质不同，tau蛋白单体是非结构化的。然而，它们以完美的序列堆叠在一起，形成长长的原纤维，每个tau分子在附着到生长的原纤维末端时都采用相同的形状。数千个面条状、非结构化的分子是如何知道彼此找到，再现相同的错误折叠形状，并像数千张高筹码塔一样完美对齐堆叠的呢？这就是韩教授和她的团队介入的地方。 “蛋白质必须错误折叠并整齐地堆叠成原纤维以延伸，同时传播相同的错误折叠形状，而这个过程得以持续的唯一方法是蛋白质的同一位点与相同位点堆叠在一起，”韩教授说，“如果在堆叠到原纤维末端的tau形状对齐上出现微小不匹配，结构最终将停止生长。” tau蛋白的化学性质决定了它们所构建的原纤维结构，每个错误折叠的tau蛋白堆叠在其下方的一个上，以将原纤维固定在一起。这些tau原纤维在神经元内部积累形成神经原纤维缠结，扰乱神经元之间的通讯，当细胞死亡并释放出错误折叠的tau蛋白时，它们可以“感染”周围漂浮并等待使用的其他tau蛋白。 像细胞中所有蛋白质一样，tau蛋白被水包围。但水与蛋白质的每个部分相互作用的方式不同。有些区域很容易吸引水并与之相互作用。但其他区域是疏水性的，这意味着它们倾向于排斥水。韩教授和她的团队在tau蛋白疏水区域内的热点周围发现了不寻常的水结构。在这些位点，水形成了一个类似于冰的“帽状”结构，但水分子保持动态。这些帽状结构标志着两个tau分子将相遇的位置。 “你有一个特定的位点，水不能完全覆盖它，因为它不喜欢水。但那个部分仍然需要被保护，因为如果不保护，蛋白质就会沉淀出来，不再可用，”韩教授说，“水往往会围绕它建立一个稳定的帽状结构，而这些水帽基本上会相互吸引。” 当两个热点相遇时，帽状水被释放，通过释放水获得的能量将它们锁定在一起。这确保了tau蛋白每次都会以相同的方式排列。当团队用化学标记封闭该热点时，原纤维停止生长，而当他们封闭远离热点的另外两个位点时，原纤维并未停止生长。既然研究人员了解了tau蛋白如何持续错误折叠新的tau蛋白以及这种相互作用的起点，科学家们就可以集中精力，在原纤维招募更多tau蛋白并扩散到其他神经元之前，阻断该热点。 “我们可能为药剂师或生物化学家找到了一条捷径，因为我们不需要寻找所有东西，我们可以说，‘寻找那个热点。那才是关键所在，’”韩教授说，“我们正试图提供它的工作原理，以便其他人能够更好、更快或更理性地找到解决方案。” 了解tau蛋白的工作原理还可以使研究人员能够创建模拟各种tau蛋白病变的合成tau原纤维，并开发诊断和治疗药物。研究人员目前缺乏良好的神经退行性疾病研究模型。“为了治愈一种疾病，需要能够重现它，”韩教授说，“疾病模拟的tau原纤维无法轻易合成，这是一个真正的问题。试图用人类患者组织进行任何开发都非常困难。” 要合成一种蛋白质，研究人员需要了解其工作原理。这项研究可能是未来医学研究中创建合成蛋白质（不仅仅是tau蛋白）的关键一步。 这项研究得到了美国国立卫生研究院（资助号R01AG05605和R35GM136411）、雨水慈善基金的tau联盟以及德国研究基金会的支持。W. M. Keck基金会支持了tau形状传播研究的持续实验和计算方法开发。计算建模得到了美国国家科学基金会（NSF-ANR MCB/PHY 2423885）的支持。
