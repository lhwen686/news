Functional decline precedes cardiovascular disease events by many years

深度解析

这篇研究深入揭示了心血管疾病（CVD）的发生并非突发事件，而是由一系列在临床显性事件发生前数年甚至十年就开始的功能性衰退所预示。文章的核心专业概念在于“功能性衰老标志物”的提出，包括衰弱（frailty）、内在能力（intrinsic capacity）下降以及身体健康相关生活质量（QOL）的恶化。衰弱是一种与年龄相关的多系统生理储备和抗应激能力下降的状态，而内在能力则综合反映了个体所有身心能力。这些指标的早期变化，被认为是“渐进性多系统衰老”的最终体现，意味着人体的多个生理系统在CVD发生前已开始协同性地、累积性地功能减退。 这项研究的重大行业影响在于，它为CVD的早期风险评估和预防提供了新的视角和机会。传统的CVD筛查主要关注血压、血脂等危险因素，而本研究提示，通过评估衰弱程度、内在能力和QOL等功能性指标，可以更早识别出高危人群。这延长了“机会窗口”，促使医疗系统开发更前瞻性的临床评估工具和干预策略。例如，可以针对这些早期功能衰退的患者推行个性化的运动、营养和生活方式干预，甚至开发针对衰老相关机制的新型药物，从而延缓CVD的发生发展，显著改善老年人群的健康结局和生活质量，对公共卫生和制药行业均具有深远意义。

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Functional decline begins many years before clinically apparent cardiovascular disease (CVD) events, according to a study published online Sept. 23 in JAMA Cardiology. Aung Zaw Zaw Phyo, Ph.D., from Monash University in Melbourne, Australia, and colleagues examined how functional performance trajectories change in the years preceding a CVD event. The analysis included data from 12,015 participants (2,403 cases and 9,612 matched controls) in the Aspirin in Reducing Events in the Elderly cohort. The researchers found that individuals who experienced a CVD event had consistently worse profiles up to a decade beforehand, including higher frailty, lower intrinsic capacity and poorer physical health-related quality of life (QOL), compared with controls. In the five years preceding the event, these differences widened markedly. Over time, cases showed faster deterioration, with greater increases in frailty (Frailty Index: β = 0.78; Fried phenotype frailty: β = 0.05) and steeper declines in intrinsic capacity (β = −0.34) and physical health-related QOL (β = −0.55). Similar patterns were seen across components of the CVD composite, with the exception that divergence occurred earlier for heart failure and fatal coronary heart disease (≥10 years before the event) than for myocardial infarction or stroke. "Results of this nested case-control study show that declines in functional aging markers were prominent before clinically apparent CVD, suggesting that they may represent the culmination of progressive multisystem aging and indicating a prolonged window of opportunity for closer clinical evaluation and early intervention," the authors write. Publication details Aung Zaw Zaw Phyo et al, Multidimensional Aging Trajectories Preceding Cardiovascular Events, JAMA Cardiology (2026).
