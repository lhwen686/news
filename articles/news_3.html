本研究深入探讨了神经发育障碍（如注意力缺陷多动障碍ADHD和自闭症）对青少年性与生殖健康服务利用模式的影响，揭示了核心专业概念在临床实践中的重要性。ADHD患者群体的冲动控制和决策能力可能受神经生物学因素影响，使其面临更高风险的性行为。因此，他们更频繁地利用青少年诊所，获得避孕和性传播疾病预防等干预措施，体现了这些诊所在公共卫生层面的积极作用。相反，自闭症青少年因其社交沟通障碍、环境适应困难及感觉处理差异，在寻求面对面医疗服务时面临显著障碍，导致就诊率较低。数字化医疗咨询的出现，通过提供非面对面、更可控的互动方式，有效降低了自闭症青少年接触医疗服务的门槛，弥合了这一差距。这凸显了技术创新在提升医疗可及性和公平性方面的巨大潜力。该研究对医疗行业具有深远影响：首先，它呼吁医疗服务提供者需针对神经发育障碍群体的具体需求，制定更具包容性和个性化的服务策略，尤其要加强对自闭症群体的初次接触支持。其次，加速数字化医疗工具的开发和整合，不仅能改善特定群体的就医体验，也能优化整体医疗资源配置。最终，这有助于实现更广泛的健康公平目标，确保所有青少年都能获得所需的性与生殖健康支持。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Tima Miroshnichenko from Pexels Young people with ADHD are more likely to visit youth clinics than their peers, while autistic individuals are less likely to do so. This is shown by a new study from Karolinska Institutet published in the journal Autism Research. The results also suggest that digital health care consultations could help reduce these disparities. The study covered more than 450,000 young people ages 12–22 in Stockholm County. The researchers examined their use of sexual and reproductive health care at youth clinics between 2018 and 2022. In total, nearly 500,000 visits were recorded, more than 90% of which took place in person at the clinics. People with ADHD benefit from youth clinics The results show clear differences among groups. Young people with ADHD used health care services at youth clinics to a greater extent than their peers without a diagnosis. For example, about half of girls and young women with ADHD had attended an in-person visit, compared with around one-third of their peers without a diagnosis. In contrast, lower levels of use were observed among autistic youth, particularly among young men, whose likelihood of seeking in-person care was approximately half that of their peers without autism. Among young women with ADHD, contraception use was also higher, including long-acting reversible contraceptive methods such as the coil/IUD and the contraceptive implant. "Previous research shows that young people with ADHD, as a group, may engage in riskier sexual behavior," says Lovisa Hellsten, PhD student at the Department of Global Public Health, Karolinska Institutet. "It is therefore encouraging to see that they are making use of the preventive and health-promoting care offered by youth clinics." Digital care may reduce barriers According to Hellsten, differences in sexual behavior are likely to explain some of the results, but it may also be the case that some people refrain from seeking care. "An interesting finding was that the differences between the groups were smaller when it came to digital care, such as chat and video consultations," she says. "This may suggest that such channels of contact lower the threshold for certain groups, such as autistic youth." The study also shows that young autistic women who had made an initial visit used health care services to roughly the same extent as their peers without a diagnosis. "This indicates that taking that first step to make contact can be a significant barrier," says Hellsten. "We hope that the results will contribute to better health care planning so that more young people will receive the right support." The study is based on register data, which makes it possible to see who has had contact with health care services, but not exactly why they sought care or which needs went unmet. Furthermore, the results relate to a period when digital health care was still in its early stages, which may affect how they should be interpreted today. The study is part of a larger research project evaluating the digitization of youth clinics and was carried out in collaboration with the Center for Epidemiology and Community Medicine (CES), Region Stockholm. Publication details Lovisa Hellsten et al, Sexual and Reproductive Healthcare Among Youth With Autism and ADHD : A Population‐Based Study From Sweden, Autism Research (2026). DOI: 10.1002/aur.70306 Journal information: Autism Research
