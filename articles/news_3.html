该文章详细描述了巴西在2026年报告的首例猴痘死亡病例以及病例数量的增加，但卫生官员并未将其定性为疫情暴发。这在流行病学中是一个关键区别：病例激增是指发病率上升，而疫情暴发则意味着需要更积极公共卫生干预的、不受控制的持续社区传播。猴痘由正痘病毒属病毒引起，其临床表现为发热、肌肉疼痛和特征性的水疱脓疱样（疖样）皮肤病变。它既可以由动物传播给人类，也可通过密切的身体接触在人际间传播，这对公共卫生构成了重大挑战。 巴西政府加强监测措施并采购疫苗的策略，体现了旨在遏制局部传播的积极公共卫生应对。将当前病例数与2022年的历史高峰进行比较，是评估疫情阈值的重要框架。世界卫生组织此前将猴痘列为国际关注的突发公共卫生事件，凸显了其在全球快速蔓延的潜力，这要求各国和国际社会建立强大的流行病学监测系统、快速诊断能力以及公平获取疫苗和抗病毒药物等干预措施。此次事件突显了管理新发和再发传染病所需的持续警惕性，以及公共卫生决策的动态性质。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain Brazil has logged its first mpox death of 2026, but officials are not treating the surge in cases as an outbreak, according to a public health institute. Brazil has recorded 1,009 confirmed infections with the virus this year, according to a report published Friday by the Fiocruz Institute, which is affiliated with the health ministry. The death was in Rio de Janeiro, where positive cases between September and November doubled compared to those recorded during the rest of the year. The government has stepped up surveillance measures and purchased 25,000 new doses of an mpox vaccine, but is not currently treating the rise in cases as an outbreak because the number remains "significantly lower than the historic peak experienced in 2022," Fiocruz said. Mpox, which is caused by a virus from the same family as the smallpox virus, causes fever, muscle aches and large boil-like skin lesions, and can be deadly. It can be transmitted to humans by animals but can also be passed between people through close physical contact. In 2022 and 2024, the World Health Organization declared mpox a public health emergency of international concern due to its rapid spread in certain countries. The majority of cases in Brazil this year have been recorded in Sao Paulo and Rio de Janeiro—two of the three most populous states in the country of 214 million people.
