Women with Parkinson's disease may be more vulnerable to Alzheimer's-related changes in the brain than men, according to new research presented at the European Academy of Neurology (EAN) Congress 2026. Parkinson's disease and Alzheimer's disease frequently co-occur in older adults, yet sex differences in Alzheimer's-related pathology among people with Parkinson's disease remain underexplored.

To address this knowledge gap, researchers from Mayo Clinic Arizona analyzed data from 230 autopsy-confirmed Parkinson's disease cases enrolled in the Arizona Study of Aging and Neurodegenerative Disorders and Brain and Body Donation Program. Participants underwent annual clinical assessments during life and comprehensive neuropathological examinations after death.

Women showed heavier plaque buildup

The study found that women with Parkinson's disease had significantly greater amyloid plaque burden, a hallmark feature of Alzheimer's disease, than men. Female participants had higher mean cortical total plaque scores than males (6.5/15 versus 4.9/15; p=0.045) and greater neuritic plaque density (1.7/3 versus 1.3/3; p=0.035).

More than half of female participants (56.8%) had a high plaque burden compared with males (39.7%; p=0.015). Women remained more than twice as likely as men to have a high amyloid plaque burden even after adjusting for age at death and APOE ε4, a major genetic risk factor for Alzheimer's disease (OR 2.18; 95% CI 1.17–4.06; p=0.014).

Lead author Dr. Erika Driver-Dunckley of Mayo Clinic Arizona said, "These findings suggest that women may be more susceptible to amyloid-driven pathology in the context of Parkinson's disease, echoing patterns observed in clinically diagnosed and pathologically confirmed Alzheimer's disease subjects."

Pathology outpaced cognitive differences

Despite the greater amyloid plaque burden observed in women, the study found no significant differences between men and women in rates of Alzheimer's dementia or performance on cognitive testing.

Driver-Dunckley explained, "Men and women with Parkinson's disease had similar rates of Alzheimer's dementia and similar results on cognitive testing. However, women showed a higher amyloid plaque burden compared with men."

The findings raise important questions about the relationship between brain pathology and cognitive outcomes, as well as the biological mechanisms underlying the observed sex differences.

"The greater severity of amyloid plaque pathology in women should have an effect on the onset and severity of cognitive impairment, but our study did not find this," said Driver-Dunckley. "It may be that a larger study would pick up cognitive differences related to the increased plaque load."

A clue that needs confirming

While the reasons for the observed sex difference remain unclear, previous studies have also reported more severe Alzheimer's-related pathology and cognitive impairment in women with Alzheimer's disease without Parkinson's disease, suggesting a possible biological susceptibility that warrants deeper investigation.

"Our findings highlight the need for further research into sex differences in Parkinson's disease and Alzheimer's-related pathology," concluded Driver-Dunckley. "An important next step will be to confirm these findings in additional large clinicopathological studies and better understand the biological mechanisms that may underlie these differences."

根据2026年欧洲神经病学学会（EAN）大会上公布的新研究，帕金森病女性患者可能比男性患者更容易受到阿尔茨海默病相关脑部改变的影响。帕金森病和阿尔茨海默病常在老年人中并存，但帕金森病患者中阿尔茨海默病相关病理学方面的性别差异仍未得到充分探究。

为弥补这一知识空白，亚利桑那州梅奥诊所的研究人员分析了来自230例经尸检证实的帕金森病病例的数据，这些病例均参与了亚利桑那州衰老与神经退行性疾病研究及大脑和遗体捐赠项目。参与者生前接受年度临床评估，死后接受全面的神经病理学检查。

女性显示出更严重的斑块积聚

研究发现，帕金森病女性患者的淀粉样斑块负荷显著高于男性，淀粉样斑块是阿尔茨海默病的标志性特征。女性参与者的平均皮层总斑块评分高于男性（6.5/15 vs 4.9/15；p=0.045），神经炎斑块密度也更高（1.7/3 vs 1.3/3；p=0.035）。

超过半数女性参与者（56.8%）具有高斑块负荷，而男性为（39.7%；p=0.015）。即使在调整了死亡年龄和APOE ε4（阿尔茨海默病的主要遗传风险因素）后，女性拥有高淀粉样斑块负荷的可能性仍然是男性的两倍多（OR 2.18；95% CI 1.17–4.06；p=0.014）。

亚利桑那州梅奥诊所的首席作者Erika Driver-Dunckley博士表示：“这些发现表明，在帕金森病的背景下，女性可能更容易受到淀粉样蛋白驱动的病理学影响，这与在临床诊断和病理学证实的阿尔茨海默病患者中观察到的模式相呼应。”

病理学变化超越了认知差异

尽管在女性中观察到更高的淀粉样斑块负荷，但研究发现，男女在阿尔茨海默病痴呆发生率或认知测试表现上没有显著差异。

Driver-Dunckley解释说：“帕金森病男性和女性患者在阿尔茨海默病痴呆发生率和认知测试结果上相似。然而，女性显示出比男性更高的淀粉样斑块负荷。”

这些发现引发了关于脑部病理学与认知结局之间关系以及观察到的性别差异背后生物学机制的重要问题。

Driver-Dunckley说：“女性淀粉样斑块病理学程度的加重应该对认知障碍的发生和严重程度产生影响，但我们的研究并未发现这一点。可能需要更大规模的研究才能发现与斑块负荷增加相关的认知差异。”

一个有待证实的线索

尽管观察到的性别差异的原因仍不清楚，但之前的研究也报告称，未患帕金森病的阿尔茨海默病女性患者表现出更严重的阿尔茨海默病相关病理学和认知障碍，这表明可能存在一种需要深入探究的生物学易感性。

Driver-Dunckley总结道：“我们的发现强调了需要进一步研究帕金森病和阿尔茨海默病相关病理学中的性别差异。下一步重要工作将是在更多大规模临床病理学研究中证实这些发现，并更好地理解这些差异背后的生物学机制。”
