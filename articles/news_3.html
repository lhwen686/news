更好的心血管健康与膀胱过度活动症呈负相关

Better cardiovascular health inversely linked to overactive bladder

深度解析

本研究揭示了心血管健康（CVH）与膀胱过度活动症（OAB）之间存在重要的非线性负相关，其中CVH通过“生命八要素”（LE8）评分量化。这一发现强调了较佳的心脏健康能显著降低OAB风险，暗示两者可能共享病理生理机制。LE8评分涵盖饮食、体力活动、尼古丁暴露、睡眠、身体质量指数、血脂、血糖和血压等指标，全面反映个体的心血管代谢状态。这种关联部分通过全身性炎症和血浆渗透压介导。慢性全身性炎症可直接影响膀胱神经功能和平滑肌活动，而血浆渗透压的紊乱则影响膀胱感觉和急迫性。值得注意的是，身体质量指数和睡眠健康是LE8中对泌尿系统结局贡献最大的因素。 从行业角度来看，这项研究倡导一种新的范式：将OAB预防融入心血管疾病的一级预防框架。对于LE8评分低于73分的个体，可采取针对性的生活方式干预（如体重管理、改善睡眠卫生），以期对心血管代谢和泌尿系统健康产生双重益处。这对于公共卫生政策制定、临床诊疗指南的修订以及全人医疗模式的设计具有深远影响，有助于推动慢性病管理的整合而非碎片化治疗。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Better cardiovascular health (CVH) is nonlinearly and inversely associated with overactive bladder (OAB), according to a study published in the August issue of the Annals of Medicine & Surgery. Min Yan, from Changsha Medical University, and Xiaowei Jiang, M.D., from Xiangya Hospital in Changsha, both in China, conducted a cross-sectional study involving 6,042 participants from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey to examine the association between CVH, measured using the Life's Essential 8 (LE8) score, and the presence and severity of OAB. The researchers observed a pronounced inverse gradient between LE8 scores and OAB outcomes. Participants with high CVH had significantly lower odds of OAB than the low-CVH group in fully adjusted models (odds ratio, 0.59). A 13% reduction in the odds of OAB was seen with each 10-point increase in LE8 score (odds ratio, 0.87). Threshold effects were seen at LE8 scores around 73 for OAB and urgency urinary incontinence in nonlinear analyses, beyond which the protective associations plateaued. Systematic inflammation mediated 4.8% of the association, and plasma osmolality mediated 12.8%, in causal mediation analyses. Of the LE8 components, the strongest contributors to urological outcomes were body mass index and sleep health. "From a public health and clinical perspective, our results advocate for the integration of OAB prevention into the framework of primary cardiovascular disease prevention," the authors write. "Specifically, for individuals with LE8 scores below 73, targeted interventions to improve CVH may confer dual benefits for both cardiometabolic and urological outcomes." More information Min Yan et al, A retrospective cross-sectional study on the nonlinear association between cardiovascular health and overactive bladder in a national population-based cohort, Annals of Medicine & Surgery (2026).
