大多数肥胖症药物并未改善生活质量或心脏健康，分析表明

Most obesity drugs do not improve quality of life or heart health, analysis indicates

Despite substantial weight loss, most obesity drugs such as Wegovy and Mounjaro do not meaningfully improve quality of life, and few show cardiovascular benefits at one year, according to an analysis of the latest evidence published by The BMJ.

More weight loss is also generally accompanied by greater harms, including stomach and bowel symptoms, fatigue and loss of lean (muscle) mass, and improvements are not sustained after stopping treatment.

Several drugs for adults with overweight or obesity produce substantial weight loss, but most have not been compared directly in head-to-head trials, leaving uncertainty about the broader balance of benefits and harms.

To address this, researchers searched scientific databases for randomized controlled trials comparing one or more drugs with lifestyle changes, placebo or another drug.

They found 262 eligible trials involving 99,791 participants (average age 49; 63% female; average BMI 35) that evaluated 19 currently available and emerging obesity drugs with follow-up ranging from 12 to 172 weeks.

Benefits included changes in body weight, fat mass and quality of life, while potential harms included changes in lean mass, gastrointestinal adverse events, gallbladder-related disorders and fatigue.

The trials were of varying quality, but the researchers were able to assess the certainty of evidence using the recognized GRADE system.

Big losses, bigger trade-offs

Compared with lifestyle changes alone, the largest weight loss after one year was with tirzepatide (14.9%) and CagriSema (14.8%), followed by oral semaglutide (10.9%), orforglipron (9.9%), subcutaneous semaglutide (9.8%) and phentermine-topiramate (8.1%).

Emerging drugs—including retatrutide, ecnoglutide and mazdutide—showed large effects on weight loss but are supported by low- or very low-certainty evidence.

Greater weight loss was consistently accompanied by higher rates of side effects and treatment discontinuation, which the authors say indicates a clear benefit-harm trade-off.

Cardiac gains remain limited

Tirzepatide reduced fat mass the most (by 25.7%) but also lean mass the most (8.3%). Subcutaneous semaglutide was the only drug associated with a reduced risk of death from any cause (19%), heart attack (28%) and heart failure (57%). Tirzepatide also reduced heart failure risk by 51%.

No drug convincingly reduced kidney failure or showed clinically important improvements in quality of life.

Gaps in the longer view

The authors acknowledge that most trials had relatively short follow-up, limiting conclusions about long-term safety, quality of life and effects on heart and kidney health. In addition, evidence for several newer drugs was sparse and of low certainty, and trial populations may not fully represent real-world patients.

However, they say this review provides a comprehensive and up-to-date comparison of currently available and emerging obesity drugs across a broad set of outcomes important to patients, clinicians and policymakers.

They conclude: "Treatment decisions for obesity should be individualized, balancing expected benefits, harms, treatment burden, costs, availability, and patient preferences."

This study represents an important step in providing comparative information to inform patient-clinician discussions about obesity drugs in this rapidly evolving landscape of treatment options, researchers say in a linked editorial.

And they suggest future studies that incorporate individual characteristics, as well as long-term outcomes such as mortality, should provide additional data to inform individualized decision-making.

根据《英国医学杂志》（The BMJ）发表的最新证据分析，尽管能带来显著的体重减轻，但大多数肥胖症药物，如Wegovy和Mounjaro，并未显著改善生活质量，且在一年内显示出心血管益处的药物寥寥无几。

更多的体重减轻通常伴随着更大的危害，包括胃肠道症状、疲劳和瘦（肌肉）体量的流失，并且停药后改善效果无法持续。

针对超重或肥胖成年人的几种药物能产生显著的体重减轻，但大多数尚未在头对头试验中进行直接比较，这使得人们对更广泛的益处与危害平衡存在不确定性。

为了解决这一问题，研究人员在科学数据库中检索了比较一种或多种药物与生活方式改变、安慰剂或另一种药物的随机对照试验。

他们共发现了262项符合条件的试验，涉及99,791名参与者（平均年龄49岁；63%为女性；平均BMI 35），这些试验评估了19种当前可用和新兴的肥胖症药物，随访期从12周到172周不等。

益处包括体重、脂肪量和生活质量的改变，而潜在危害包括瘦体量改变、胃肠道不良事件、胆囊相关疾病和疲劳。

这些试验质量各异，但研究人员能够使用公认的GRADE系统评估证据的确定性。

显著减重，更大权衡

与单独的生活方式改变相比，一年后体重减轻幅度最大的是替西帕肽（14.9%）和CagriSema（14.8%），其次是口服司美格鲁肽（10.9%）、orforglipron（9.9%）、皮下注射司美格鲁肽（9.8%）和苯丁胺-托吡酯（8.1%）。

新兴药物——包括瑞他鲁肽、ecnoglutide和马祖肽——显示出显著的减重效果，但其证据确定性较低或非常低。

较大的体重减轻始终伴随着较高的副作用发生率和治疗中止率，作者称这表明了明显的益处与危害之间的权衡。

心脏获益仍然有限

替西帕肽在减少脂肪量方面效果最显著（减少25.7%），但同时减少瘦体量也最多（8.3%）。皮下注射司美格鲁肽是唯一与全因死亡风险（降低19%）、心脏病发作风险（降低28%）和心力衰竭风险（降低57%）降低相关的药物。替西帕肽也使心力衰竭风险降低了51%。

没有药物能令人信服地降低肾衰竭风险，也未能显示出生活质量方面具有临床重要性的改善。

长期视角的空白

作者承认，大多数试验的随访期相对较短，限制了对长期安全性、生活质量以及对心脏和肾脏健康影响的结论。此外，几种较新药物的证据稀少且确定性低，且试验人群可能无法完全代表真实世界的患者。

然而，他们表示，这项综述提供了对当前可用和新兴肥胖症药物的全面且最新的比较，涵盖了对患者、临床医生和政策制定者而言重要的广泛结局指标。

他们总结道：“肥胖症的治疗决策应个体化，平衡预期益处、危害、治疗负担、费用、可及性以及患者偏好。”

研究人员在一篇相关社论中指出，这项研究是提供比较信息的重要一步，有助于在迅速发展的治疗方案格局中，促进患者与临床医生之间关于肥胖症药物的讨论。

他们建议，未来的研究应纳入个体特征以及死亡率等长期结局，以提供更多数据来指导个体化决策。
