深度解析

本文深刻阐述了职场焦虑作为一种普遍的心理健康挑战。从临床医学角度，职场焦虑并非简单的情绪波动，而是严重的心理应激反应，其核心机制涉及下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺轴的激活，导致交感神经兴奋，引发心悸、出汗、颤抖等躯体化症状，并伴有认知功能障碍，如专注力下降、对任务的强迫性关注。若这些症状持续六个月以上且显著影响职业功能，则需临床诊断并进行专业干预，包括心理治疗（如认知行为疗法）和必要的药物管理。行业层面，高达60%的职场焦虑发生率，以及人工智能带来的“时刻待命”压力和对工作保障的担忧，共同构成了对员工福祉的严峻挑战。这不仅导致生产力损耗，也增加了企业的人力资本风险。现有员工援助计划（EAP）面临服务可及性不足及等候时间长的困境，凸显了企业在优化精神卫生服务、构建支持性工作环境方面的战略紧迫性。通过将心理健康支持融入企业文化与福利体系，组织才能有效应对职场应激源，促进可持续的员工健康与绩效。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain Workplace stress and anxiety. Who hasn't experienced it? However, if that anxiety is so strong that it hurts your performance or lingers for months, you might have a problem, one expert says. For many workers, the strain isn't easing up. About 1 in 3 employees in a recent global survey said they're "merely surviving," and 1 in 4 said their mental health had gotten worse over the past year. Half said they had a hard time staying focused or engaged at work. Those numbers come from the 2026 State of Workforce Mental Health report by Lyra Health, a company that provides mental health benefits to employers. It surveyed 7,500 workers in six countries, including the United States, in late 2025. Dr. Asim Shah, executive vice chair of psychiatry and behavioral sciences at Baylor College of Medicine in Houston, explains what can trigger workplace anxiety, how to manage it and when to seek professional help. "The goal is not to suffer and not let your anxiety affect work performance," he said. "About 60% of people experience workplace anxiety. It is common, which means you can do something about it." Surly co-workers, a demanding supervisor, small, annoying tasks or overwhelming major projects can cause workplace anxiety. Bigger worries play a part, too. Money problems were the most common source of ongoing stress in the Lyra survey (57%). Heavier workloads (35%) and fear of losing a job (23%) came next. Artificial intelligence is adding another layer. Nearly half of the workers surveyed said AI at work made them feel they always had to be available (46%), and a similar share said it made them anxious about job security (44%). If you have workplace stress or anxiety, you might notice signs like: Feeling overly anxious or nervous Experiencing excessive sweating or trembling Obsessing about a task Having a lack of interest in your work "The anxiety becomes so overwhelming that you are unable to focus and concentrate," Shah noted in a Baylor news release. "These feelings can impair your daily work functions." If that continues for more than six months, Shah said it might be time to seek professional help. "Some employers might offer an employee assistance program where you can go for help," he said. "You can also start talking to a therapist through psychotherapy or look into medication management for your workplace anxiety." Getting that help isn't always easy, though. Among survey respondents dealing with more complex mental health needs, 40% said they had trouble getting care through their employee assistance program (EAP), and more than half ran into long waitlists. Leaving for a new job won't necessarily solve the problem either, Shah added, offering several suggestions to manage it and help reduce stress. Break your tasks into small pieces. Set small goals throughout the week and congratulate yourself for reaching them. Express your emotions. Journal how you feel. Talk, laugh or cry with someone you confide in. Start healthy habits and do things you enjoy. Work within your limits. Set boundaries with your workload. © 2026 HealthDay. All rights reserved.
