Scientists develop lab-grown heart tissue model for heart failure

深度解析

本研究在心血管医学领域取得了重要进展，成功开发了人诱导多能干细胞（hiPSCs）源性射血分数保留型心力衰竭（HFpEF）人工心脏组织（hEHT）模型。HFpEF是一种全球性、预后不良且治疗选择有限的疾病，其特征是心脏舒张功能障碍。研究团队利用hiPSCs的强大分化能力，结合先进的3D组织工程技术，在体外成功构建了一个能够模拟HFpEF病理生理特征的精准模型，包括舒张功能受损、分子遗传标志物改变（如NT-proBNP、NPPB mRNA水平升高，钙离子转运相关基因表达降低，纤维化和炎症增加）。此模型的建立极大地克服了传统动物模型和活检样本在HFpEF研究中的局限性。基于此模型，研究人员首次在人类组织水平上证实了SGLT2i恩格列净能部分逆转HFpEF的病理进程，并通过转录组学分析揭示其作用机制涉及抗炎效应以及通过内皮细胞（ECs）介导的eNOS-NO-cGMP-PKG信号通路的调节，改善离子平衡。这项研究不仅为HFpEF的病理生理学研究提供了全新的工具，更为新药筛选、疗效评估和个体化医疗提供了坚实的基础，有望加速开发针对HFpEF的有效治疗策略。

英文原文

Original
Scientists study the underlying causes of a disease or test new potential drugs in animal models or cells obtained from human biopsy specimens before clinical trials. However, disease models in animals do not mimic the actual human disease, and biopsy specimens are hard to access for research purposes. Lab-grown tissues, engineered from stem cells reprogrammed from human cells, called human induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs), are fast emerging as a more reliable and accessible model in medical research. A study led by Professor Shugo Tohyama and Senior Assistant Professor Hidenori Tani from the Department of Clinical Regenerative Medicine, Fujita Health University, Japan, has focused on developing robust lab models of cardiomyocytes (CMs), the heart muscle cells. Years of pioneering research have led to the development of high-quality CMs from hiPSCs in a petri dish. "During efforts to adjust the concentrations of glucose and fatty acids in the culture medium to promote heart tissue maturation, we discovered that excessive fatty acids adversely affected the dilation capacity of the heart tissues," explains Dr. Tani, the first author of the study, regarding this breakthrough. "Around the same time, a study reported that the administration of high levels of fatty acids and nitric oxide synthase (NOS) inhibitors in mice-induced heart failure (HF) with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF), which led to the conception of this study." The study, which resulted in the development of human engineered heart tissue (hEHT) model of HFpEF, was made available online on July 16, 2026, and was published in Volume 33, Issue 8 of the journal Cell Stem Cell on August 6, 2026. Following its publication, the study was immediately selected as a featured paper in the Preview section of Cell Stem Cell, further highlighting the significance of these findings. HFpEF is a defect in the relaxation (diastole) of the heart with normal contraction (systole). It affects more than 30 million people globally but has few effective treatment options and, therefore, poor outcomes. Prof. Tohyama and his team induced hiPSC-CMs from readily available hiPSCs, created mature 3D heart tissue using collagen derived from porcine hearts-which the lab had previously developed-and cultured the tissues in vitro using a medium containing high fatty acid levels and L-NG-nitroarginine methyl ester (L-NAME), an NOS inhibitor. Supporting cells like epicardial cells, endothelial cells (ECs), and macrophages were also added to successfully create an HFpEF-hEHT that structurally and functionally mimicked diastolic HF with normal systole. Molecular and genetic markers of HFpEF, like increased NT-proBNP and NPPB mRNA levels, reduced expression of key genes related to Ca2+ transport (RYR2, SERCA2A, and PLN), increased fibrosis-related genes, and a shift to the stiffer, less pliable type collagen and TITIN, were demonstrated. Increased inflammation was also seen. Using the newly developed HFpEF model, the researchers then went on to test the effectiveness of six drugs, which are currently being used or explored in research for treatment of HFpEF. Empagliflozin, a sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor (SGLT2i), an anti-diabetic medication, was the only drug that could partially prevent the development of diastolic HF. The combined angiotensin receptor/neprilysin inhibitor, soluble guanylate cyclase activator, phosphodiesterase 5 inhibitor, and nicotinamide phosphoribosyltransferase protein activator had no effect on hEHT relaxation time. On the other hand, a hyperpolarization-activated cyclic nucleotide-gated channel inhibitor worsened the disease. Transcriptomic analysis revealed that SGLT2i had anti-inflammatory effects and partially prevented the increase in inflammatory molecules like IL-1β and IL-6 in HFpEF-hEHT. Also, the presence of ECs was required for SGLT2i to exert its effects. Further studies on the role of supporting cells revealed that the preventive effect of SGLT2i was exerted through ECs. Inhibition of the eNOS-NO-cGMP-PKG signaling pathway in ECs was found to be the main pathological mechanism of HFpEF. SGLT2i was found to exert its anti-inflammatory effects by partially removing this inhibition, and by improving the removal of Na+ and Ca2+ ions from the cells through their exchanger channels, thereby preventing their accumulation in the tissues. "The human HFpEF model established in this study can be applied to uncovering the pathophysiology of HFpEF and to research on aging," says Prof. Tohyama. "Furthermore, it is expected to serve as a research foundation that will contribute to the development of new therapeutic agents, the evaluation of drug efficacy, and personalized medicine for individual patients, paving the way for further research in the future," Prof. Tohyama adds.

在临床试验之前，科学家们会在动物模型或从人体活检样本中获得的细胞中研究疾病的根本原因或测试新的潜在药物。然而，动物疾病模型并不能完全模拟真实的人类疾病，而且活检样本难以用于研究目的。由人类细胞重编程为干细胞，即人诱导多能干细胞（hiPSCs）培养而成的实验室培育组织，正迅速成为医学研究中一种更可靠、更易获取的模型。 日本藤田医科大学临床再生医学系的远山周吾教授和谷英典高级助理教授领导的一项研究，专注于开发心肌细胞（CMs）——即心肌细胞——的稳健实验室模型。经过多年的开创性研究，他们已成功地在培养皿中利用hiPSCs培养出高质量的心肌细胞。 该研究的第一作者谷博士解释了这一突破：“在努力调整培养基中葡萄糖和脂肪酸浓度以促进心肌组织成熟的过程中，我们发现过多的脂肪酸会对心肌组织的舒张能力产生不利影响。大约在同一时间，一项研究报告称，在小鼠体内施用高水平脂肪酸和一氧化氮合酶（NOS）抑制剂会诱导射血分数保留型心力衰竭（HFpEF），这促成了本研究的构思。” 这项研究最终成功开发了HFpEF的人工心脏组织（hEHT）模型，于2026年7月16日在线发布，并于2026年8月6日发表在《细胞干细胞》杂志第33卷第8期。论文发表后，立即被选为《细胞干细胞》“预览”部分的专题论文，进一步突显了这些发现的重要性。 HFpEF是一种心脏舒张功能障碍，而收缩功能（收缩期）正常。它影响全球超过3000万人，但目前治疗选择很少，预后不佳。远山教授及其团队从易于获取的hiPSCs中诱导产生hiPSC-CMs，利用该实验室先前开发的源自猪心脏的胶原蛋白构建了成熟的3D心脏组织，并将其置于含有高脂肪酸和L-NG-硝基精氨酸甲酯（L-NAME）（一种NOS抑制剂）的培养基中进行体外培养。同时，还加入了心外膜细胞、内皮细胞（ECs）和巨噬细胞等支持细胞，成功构建了一个HFpEF-hEHT模型，该模型在结构和功能上均模拟了舒张性心力衰竭但收缩功能正常的状况。研究结果表明HFpEF的分子和遗传标志物存在，如NT-proBNP和NPPB mRNA水平升高，与Ca2+转运相关的关键基因（RYR2、SERCA2A和PLN）表达降低，纤维化相关基因增加，以及胶原蛋白和肌联蛋白（TITIN）向更硬、弹性更小的类型转变。此外，还观察到炎症反应增加。 利用新开发的HFpEF模型，研究人员随后测试了六种目前正在用于或研究中用于治疗HFpEF的药物的有效性。恩格列净（Empagliflozin），一种钠-葡萄糖协同转运蛋白2抑制剂（SGLT2i）和抗糖尿病药物，是唯一能部分阻止舒张性心力衰竭发展的药物。血管紧张素受体/脑啡肽酶抑制剂复合制剂、可溶性鸟苷酸环化酶激活剂、磷酸二酯酶5抑制剂以及烟酰胺磷酸核糖转移酶蛋白激活剂对hEHT的舒张时间均无影响。另一方面，一种超极化激活环核苷酸门控通道抑制剂反而加重了疾病。 转录组学分析显示，SGLT2i具有抗炎作用，并能部分阻止HFpEF-hEHT中炎性分子如IL-1β和IL-6的增加。此外，SGLT2i发挥作用需要内皮细胞（ECs）的存在。对支持细胞作用的进一步研究表明，SGLT2i的预防作用是通过ECs介导的。研究发现，ECs中eNOS-NO-cGMP-PKG信号通路的抑制是HFpEF的主要病理机制。SGLT2i通过部分解除这种抑制，并改善细胞通过其交换通道对Na+和Ca2+离子的清除，从而阻止这些离子在组织中的积累，发挥其抗炎作用。 “本研究建立的人类HFpEF模型可应用于揭示HFpEF的病理生理学以及衰老研究，”远山教授说。“此外，它有望成为一个研究基础，有助于新治疗药物的开发、药物疗效评估以及针对个体患者的个性化医疗，为未来的进一步研究铺平道路，”远山教授补充道。
