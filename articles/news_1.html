深度解析

该文章聚焦于美国夏季暴发的环孢子虫病，这是一种由凯氏环孢子虫（Cyclospora cayetanensis）引起的胃肠道寄生虫感染。核心医学机制在于，这种微观单细胞寄生虫通过被其具有抵抗力的卵囊（oocysts）污染的食物（尤其是新鲜农产品）或水传播。卵囊在特定温湿度下成熟后，被摄入人体并在肠道内引起感染，导致严重的、水样腹泻及其他胃肠道症状。值得注意的是，人体的免疫系统状态是疾病严重程度的关键决定因素。免疫功能健全的成年人通常表现出自限性症状，而免疫功能受损人群（如移植受者、化疗患者、艾滋病患者）以及免疫系统尚未发育成熟的婴幼儿和免疫防御功能减退的老年人，则面临更高的感染风险、更严重的临床表现和更持久的病程。 这种疾病的行业影响是多方面的。在公共卫生领域，它凸显了疾病监测（如CDC的数据跟踪）、疫情调查和源头追溯的重要性。对于临床医学实践，医生需对高危人群保持高度的临床怀疑，以便及时诊断和管理。同时，食品安全行业面临巨大挑战，需加强农产品供应链的卫生管理和质量控制，以预防污染。消费者教育关于农产品安全和良好手卫生习惯也成为预防此类食源性疾病的关键策略。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Sora Shimazaki from Pexels People across the United States are taking a closer look at their lettuce and leafy greens this summer because of an outbreak of cyclosporiasis, a gastrointestinal illness whose most notable symptom is severe, watery diarrhea. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, there are 1,645 confirmed cases across 34 states, with more than 5,100 additional cases requiring further analysis. The illness has also affected New York state, with reports in New York City. The illness can affect anyone, but who is most at risk? We spoke with Azadeh Nasuhidehnavi, an infectious disease expert and assistant professor of pharmaceutical sciences at Binghamton University, State University of New York, about what causes the illness, who is likely to be hit the hardest and how to stay safe. What causes cyclosporiasis? Nasuhidehnavi: Cyclosporiasis is caused by a microscopic, single-cell parasite called Cyclospora cayetanensis. It spreads through food (it has been linked to fresh produce) or water contaminated with the parasite's oocysts, the resistant microscopic form of the parasite that survives outside the body and causes infection when ingested. Because oocysts need certain temperatures and moisture to mature, high numbers of infections occur mostly during spring and summer. What are the symptoms of cyclosporiasis? Symptoms include watery diarrhea (often explosive), cramping, nausea, fatigue and occasionally low-grade fever. They typically appear 7–10 days after exposure and last around two weeks if untreated. Does cyclosporiasis affect some people more severely than others? Not everyone gets equally sick, and a growing body of research points to a few consistent risk factors that explain why. The most important determinant is the status of an individual's immune system. For most healthy adults, cyclosporiasis is unpleasant but self-limited. Immunocompromised groups, including transplant recipients, cancer patients on chemotherapy, dialysis patients and people living with HIV/AIDS, face substantially higher infection rates and more severe, prolonged illness. Is age a risk factor? Yes. Infants and young children, whose immune systems are still developing, and older adults, whose immune defenses naturally wane, tend to experience more severe or prolonged symptoms than healthy young adults. This is consistent with patterns seen in related parasitic gut infections like cryptosporidiosis, where children under 5 years old make up the large majority of cases in several recent outbreaks. What should people at risk do to stay safe? For people who are immunocompromised, very young or older, cyclosporiasis can be far more serious. Health officials are urging clinicians to maintain a high index of suspicion in these groups as the outbreak investigation continues. Produce safety and handwashing remain the front line of prevention while the source is tracked down. Key medical concepts CyclosporiasisDiarrhea, watery Who's behind this story?
