多种慢性疾病增加妊娠风险，研究发现

Multiple long-term conditions raise pregnancy risks, study finds

深度解析

本研究揭示了“妊娠期多种慢性疾病”（Multiple Long-Term Conditions, MLTCs in pregnancy）这一核心临床概念的深远影响。它将MLTCs明确定义为独立的产前风险指标，强调了女性在怀孕前若合并两种或以上慢性身心疾病，其流产、严重恶心呕吐、静脉血栓栓塞、子痫前期、胎盘早剥、妊娠期糖尿病以及焦虑抑郁等多种不良妊娠结局的风险将显著升高，且风险随疾病数量增加而攀升。这挑战了当前产科护理多围绕单一疾病诊疗的模式。 从医学机制上看，多种慢性疾病可能通过协同作用或独立作用，加剧孕期生理负担，影响母体和胎儿的稳态。例如，慢性炎症、内分泌失调、心血管功能受损或精神压力增加，均可导致妊娠并发症风险升高。 行业影响方面，该研究为医疗政策和临床实践提供了关键证据。它呼吁彻底重构现有产科护理路径，从“以疾病为中心”转向“以患者为中心”的整合性护理模式。这意味着在产科初次预约时就应系统识别MLTCs女性，并实施跨学科的综合评估和管理，包括产科、初级保健和精神健康服务的协同。尤其，围产期精神健康支持被提升为紧急优先事项。此外，研究结果也凸显了解决医疗保健不平等和改善现有精神健康支持的迫切性，并为未来关于MLTCs组合风险和长期母婴结局的研究指明了方向，有助于实现更精准和个性化的妊娠期管理。

英文原文

Original
Women who begin pregnancy with two or more pre-existing, long-term physical or mental health conditions have 20% higher risk of miscarriage, 69% higher risk of severe nausea and vomiting, and around four times the risk of anxiety and depression, compared with those with no long-term conditions. The new research, co-led by King's College London, also found those with multiple long-term conditions had more than double the risk of venous thromboembolism (where a blood clot forms inside a vein, such as in deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism), and 42% higher risk of pre-eclampsia (a pregnancy complication that causes high blood pressure) compared to those without long-term conditions. Risks rose with each additional condition. For women with three or more conditions, the risk of venous thromboembolism was more than three-and-a-half times that of women with no long-term health conditions. The new study, published today in The Lancet Public Health, compared antenatal outcomes in women with and without multiple long-term conditions. The study, led by researchers at King's College London, Queen's University Belfast, Bristol NHS Foundation Trust, the University of Birmingham, Swansea University and the University of St Andrews, is the largest of its kind – analyzing data from pregnancies across all four UK nations. Around one in five pregnant women in the UK live with multiple long-term conditions, yet the combined impact of these conditions on pregnancy is poorly understood. In an observational study, researchers from the MuM-PreDiCT consortium analyzed over 2.2 million pregnancies and birth events recorded between 2000 and 2022 in five datasets spanning England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Compared to women with no long-term conditions, women with multiple long-term conditions also had a 32% higher risk of placental abruption, where the placenta separates from the womb before birth, and a 26% higher risk of gestational diabetes. Dr Steven Wambua, Research Fellow in Health Data Science at King's College London and joint first author, said: "Maternity care is still largely organized around single health conditions, but one in five women now enters pregnancy with two or more. By harmonising five datasets covering all four UK nations, we could show consistently and across a much broader range of outcomes than before, that these women face higher risks and that risk climbs with every additional condition." Maternity care pathways vary considerably, but where they exist, they are often organized around individual conditions. The authors say their findings highlight a need to restructure these pathways to address the complex needs of women living with multiple conditions. Ngawai Moss, who has lived experience of pregnancy with long-term health conditions and is a Patient and Public Involvement co-investigator on MuM-PreDiCT, said: "I shouldn't have to join all the dots myself. I don't always know what I need, what questions to ask or who I need to speak to. Every pregnancy is different, and I need my care to work around me, not the other way around." Professor Krishnarajah Nirantharakumar, Clinical Professor of Public Health and Health Data Science at King's College London, MuM-PreDiCT principal investigator and joint senior author, said: "These findings make a strong case for recognizing multiple long-term conditions as a marker of antenatal risk in its own right. That means identifying these women at maternity booking, assessing physical and mental health needs together, and joining up obstetric, primary care and mental health services around them. The near four-fold risk of antenatal anxiety and depression is particularly striking, and points to perinatal mental health support as an urgent priority." Our results help define the urgent clinical challenges facing both women entering pregnancy with multiple long-term conditions, and clinicians caring for them across the UK. Supporting recommendations from recent national maternity and neonatal investigation reports, there is a critical need to address healthcare inequalities, and improve support for women with pre-existing mental health conditions. These findings highlight the pressing need to restructure existing maternity services to improve antenatal outcomes." Dr. Kelly-Ann Eastwood, Bristol NHS Foundation Trust and Queen's University Belfast, joint senior author The authors note that, as an observational study using routinely collected health records, some conditions and outcomes may be under-recorded or inconsistently recorded. The study is funded by the UKRI Strategic Priorities Fund and the Department of Health and Social Care via the National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR), and delivered by the Medical Research Council (MRC). The MuM-PReDiCT consortium was established with the aim of improving care for pregnant women with multiple long-term health conditions. Further work within MuM-PreDiCT will examine birth and child outcomes and identify which combinations of conditions carry the greatest risk.

与那些没有长期健康状况的女性相比，以两种或以上既往存在的慢性身体或精神健康状况开始怀孕的女性，其流产风险高出20%，严重恶心和呕吐风险高出69%，焦虑和抑郁风险则高出约四倍。 这项由伦敦国王学院共同主导的新研究还发现，与没有长期健康状况的女性相比，患有多种慢性疾病的女性静脉血栓栓塞（指血管内形成血凝块，如深静脉血栓和肺栓塞）的风险高出一倍以上，子痫前期（一种引起高血压的妊娠并发症）的风险高出42%。风险随着每增加一种疾病而升高。对于患有三种或以上疾病的女性，其静脉血栓栓塞的风险是没有慢性健康状况女性的三倍半以上。 这项于今日发表在《柳叶刀·公共卫生》上的新研究，比较了患有和未患有多种慢性疾病的女性的产前结局。该研究由伦敦国王学院、贝尔法斯特女王大学、布里斯托尔NHS基金会信托、伯明翰大学、斯旺西大学和圣安德鲁斯大学的研究人员领导，是同类研究中规模最大的，分析了英国全部四个国家的妊娠数据。 英国约有五分之一的孕妇患有多种慢性疾病，然而这些疾病对妊娠的综合影响却知之甚少。在一项观察性研究中，MuM-PreDiCT联盟的研究人员分析了2000年至2022年间在涵盖英格兰、苏格兰、威尔士和北爱尔兰的五个数据集中记录的超过220万例妊娠和分娩事件。 与没有慢性疾病的女性相比，患有多种慢性疾病的女性胎盘早剥（胎盘在分娩前从子宫壁分离）的风险也高出32%，妊娠期糖尿病的风险高出26%。 伦敦国王学院健康数据科学研究员、共同第一作者史蒂文·旺布阿博士（Dr Steven Wambua）表示：“产科护理仍然主要围绕单一健康状况进行组织，但目前每五名女性中就有一名以两种或以上疾病开始妊娠。通过整合覆盖英国全部四个国家的五个数据集，我们能够以前所未有的广泛结局范围，持续地表明这些女性面临更高的风险，且风险随着每增加一种疾病而攀升。” 产科护理路径差异很大，但即便存在，也通常是围绕个体疾病组织的。作者表示，他们的研究结果强调需要重组这些路径，以解决患有多种疾病女性的复杂需求。 Ngawai Moss女士本人有过患有慢性健康状况下怀孕的经历，并且是MuM-PreDiCT项目的患者和公众参与联合研究员。她表示：“我不应该自己把所有点连接起来。我并非总是知道自己需要什么、该问什么问题或该与谁交谈。每次怀孕都不同，我需要我的护理围绕我展开，而不是反过来。” 伦敦国王学院公共卫生与健康数据科学临床教授、MuM-PreDiCT首席研究员兼共同资深作者克里希纳拉贾·尼兰萨拉库马尔教授（Professor Krishnarajah Nirantharakumar）表示：“这些发现有力地证明，应将多种慢性疾病本身视为一个产前风险指标。这意味着在产科预约时就应识别这些女性，同时评估其身心健康需求，并将产科、初级保健和精神健康服务围绕她们整合起来。产前焦虑和抑郁风险近四倍的增长尤为惊人，这表明围产期精神健康支持是一个紧迫的优先事项。” “我们的研究结果有助于明确英国各地患有多种慢性疾病的孕妇以及照护她们的临床医生所面临的紧迫临床挑战。为支持近期国家妇幼和新生儿调查报告中的建议，迫切需要解决医疗保健不平等问题，并改善对患有既往精神健康状况女性的支持。这些发现强调了重组现有产科服务以改善产前结局的紧迫性。”——布里斯托尔NHS基金会信托和贝尔法斯特女王大学共同资深作者凯利-安·伊斯特伍德博士（Dr. Kelly-Ann Eastwood）。 作者指出，作为一项利用常规收集的健康记录进行的观察性研究，某些状况和结局可能存在记录不足或记录不一致的情况。 该研究由英国研究与创新战略优先基金和卫生和社会关怀部通过国家健康与护理研究所（NIHR）资助，并由医学研究委员会（MRC）执行。 MuM-PreDiCT联盟的成立旨在改善对患有多种慢性健康状况孕妇的护理。MuM-PreDiCT内部的进一步工作将检查分娩和儿童结局，并确定哪些疾病组合带来的风险最大。
