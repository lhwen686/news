Targeting EGFR signaling to reduce mucus overproduction in lung diseases

深度解析

本篇综述深入探讨了表皮生长因子受体（EGFR）信号通路在气道黏液高分泌中的核心作用及其作为治疗靶点的潜力。EGFR作为一种受体酪氨酸激酶，在被配体（如EGF、TGF-α）或氧化应激激活后，会启动下游的Ras/Raf/MAPK/ERK信号级联，并活化转录因子Sp1，从而上调黏蛋白MUC5AC和MUC2的表达。同时，EGFR激活还会抑制转录因子FOXA2，促进杯状细胞增生（GCH），这些共同导致了气道内黏液的过量生成。当前针对慢性气道疾病（如COPD、哮喘、CF）的治疗方案主要侧重于症状缓解，而EGFR靶向策略旨在从根本上抑制黏液的产生，有望实现疾病修饰。在技术原理上，单克隆抗体（MABs）通过阻断EGFR的细胞外结构域发挥作用，而酪氨酸激酶抑制剂（TKIs）则通过竞争受体酪氨酸激酶位点的ATP来抑制其活性。为克服全身给药TKIs的脱靶效应和毒性，吸入或气道选择性给药，特别是利用纳米颗粒和抗体片段平台，正成为重要的研究方向。这将有助于将药物精准递送至肺部，最大程度减少全身不良反应。尽管EGFR靶向治疗前景广阔，但仍需严谨的临床试验来证明其在减少患者急性加重和改善生存率方面的有效性与安全性，方能将其从有潜力的靶点转化为常规临床实践。

英文原文

Original
Airway mucus is part of innate lung defense, but when production overwhelms clearance, it obstructs airflow and worsens disease. About half of people with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) show mucus hypersecretion, and affected patients face a mortality risk about 3.5 times higher than those without it. In asthma, mucus plugs can contribute to fatal airway obstruction. Current options—bronchodilators, hypertonic saline, and mucolytics—mainly relieve symptoms and can cause cardiac, visual, urinary, and metabolic adverse effects. No specific therapy reliably controls mucus overproduction. The review therefore examines whether blocking a convergent inflammatory pathway can reduce mucus without merely treating cough and sputum. Given these challenges, deeper research into safer, more selective epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR)-targeted approaches is needed. The review, from researchers in the Department of Respiratory Medicine at Children's Hospital of Chongqing Medical University and affiliated laboratories in Chongqing, China, was accepted on 10 May 2025 and published (DOI: 10.1002/pdi3.70007) in Pediatric Discovery (2026 issue). It examines how EGFR signaling promotes goblet cell hyperplasia (GCH) and mucin production and reviews monoclonal antibodies (MABs), tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs), and nanoparticle-based inhibitors in preclinical and clinical studies. The article is open access and synthesizes mechanistic, animal-model, and human trial evidence, with attention to asthma, COPD, cystic fibrosis (CF), and viral infection. The review details how EGFR, a receptor tyrosine kinase also known as ErbB1/HER1, is activated by ligands such as epidermal growth factor (EGF) and transforming growth factor-alpha (TGF-α), or by oxidative stress in a ligand-independent manner. Activation converges on Ras/Raf/mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK)/extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) signaling and the transcription factor Sp1, promoting mucin 5AC (MUC5AC) and mucin 2 (MUC2) transcription; EGFR activation can also suppress the transcription factor FOXA2 and drive GCH. Sp1 further boosts EGF and TGF-α, creating positive feedback. In preclinical models, an anti-EGFR single-chain antibody fragment displayed on ferritin nanoparticles eased asthma features and reduced GCH and mucus. TKIs including gefitinib, erlotinib, and afatinib reduced MUC5AC and mucus in asthma, COPD, acrolein, and viral-infection models; erlotinib restored claudin 1, a barrier protein whose loss accompanies GCH. MABs block the extracellular domain, whereas TKIs compete with adenosine triphosphate (ATP) at the receptor's tyrosine kinase site. A retrospective lung-cancer study suggested gefitinib and afatinib can inhibit mucus hypersecretion before tumor shrinkage. Human evidence remains limited: inhaled BIBW 2948 was poorly tolerated in COPD, while the downstream inhibitor Bio11006 reached phase IIb. The authors emphasize that efficacy must be balanced against skin, gastrointestinal, hepatic, and pulmonary toxicity. The authors said the central opportunity is to turn a shared inflammatory convergence point into a selective airway therapy. They said EGFR blockade appears to reduce GCH and mucin production across diverse triggers, but they cautioned that existing TKIs carry off-target effects and that no successful EGFR-TKI trial has yet been reported in human asthma or COPD. They said future work should prioritize low off-target molecules, optimized delivery devices, and clinical trials that measure both mucus outcomes and systemic safety in patients. Clinically, EGFR-targeted drugs could complement bronchodilators and mucolytics by reducing the source of excess mucus rather than only clearing it. The most plausible near-term path is inhaled or airway-selective delivery, which may limit skin, gastrointestinal, hepatic, and pulmonary toxicity associated with systemic TKIs. Patients with severe asthma, COPD with mucus plugs, CF, or virus-driven exacerbations might benefit most, but trials will need to measure mucin burden, lung function, exacerbations, and adverse events. Nanoparticle and antibody-fragment platforms could improve targeting, yet regulators and clinicians will need robust evidence that mucus reduction translates into fewer exacerbations and better survival. The review's implication is clear: EGFR is a promising target, not yet a proven routine treatment. Source: Chinese Academy of Sciences Journal reference: Zhang, Y., et al. (2025). Recent Progress in Treating Airway Mucus Hypersecretion by Targeting the Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor Signaling Pathway. Pediatric Discovery. DOI: 10.1002/pdi3.70007. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/pdi3.70007

气道黏液是肺部先天防御的一部分，但当其生成量超过清除能力时，就会阻塞气流并加重疾病。约半数慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）患者表现出黏液高分泌，受影响患者的死亡风险比无此症状的患者高约3.5倍。在哮喘中，黏液栓可导致致命性气道阻塞。目前的治疗方案——支气管扩张剂、高渗盐水和黏液溶解剂——主要缓解症状，并可能引起心脏、视觉、泌尿和代谢方面的不良反应。目前尚无特定的疗法能够可靠地控制黏液过量生成。因此，这篇综述旨在探讨阻断一个趋同的炎症通路是否能在不单纯治疗咳嗽和咳痰的情况下减少黏液。鉴于这些挑战，需要更深入地研究更安全、更具选择性的表皮生长因子受体（EGFR）靶向方法。 这篇综述由重庆医科大学附属儿童医院呼吸内科研究人员及重庆相关实验室撰写，于2025年5月10日被接收，并发表于《儿科发现》（2026年刊期）（DOI: 10.1002/pdi3.70007）。文章探讨了EGFR信号如何促进杯状细胞增生（GCH）和黏蛋白生成，并回顾了单克隆抗体（MABs）、酪氨酸激酶抑制剂（TKIs）以及基于纳米颗粒的抑制剂在临床前和临床研究中的应用。该文章为开放获取，综合了机制研究、动物模型和人体试验的证据，并重点关注了哮喘、COPD、囊性纤维化（CF）和病毒感染。 该综述详细阐述了EGFR（一种受体酪氨酸激酶，也称为ErbB1/HER1）如何通过表皮生长因子（EGF）和转化生长因子-α（TGF-α）等配体激活，或通过氧化应激以非配体依赖方式激活。其激活汇聚于Ras/Raf/丝裂原活化蛋白激酶（MAPK）/细胞外信号调节激酶（ERK）信号通路和转录因子Sp1，从而促进黏蛋白5AC（MUC5AC）和黏蛋白2（MUC2）的转录；EGFR激活还可以抑制转录因子FOXA2并驱动杯状细胞增生（GCH）。Sp1进一步增强EGF和TGF-α的表达，形成正反馈。在临床前模型中，一种展示在铁蛋白纳米颗粒上的抗EGFR单链抗体片段缓解了哮喘特征并减少了GCH和黏液。包括吉非替尼、厄洛替尼和阿法替尼在内的TKIs在哮喘、COPD、丙烯醛和病毒感染模型中均减少了MUC5AC和黏液；厄洛替尼恢复了claudin 1的表达，这是一种屏障蛋白，其缺失伴随GCH。单克隆抗体（MABs）阻断EGFR的细胞外结构域，而TKIs则在受体的酪氨酸激酶位点与三磷酸腺苷（ATP）竞争。一项回顾性肺癌研究表明，吉非替尼和阿法替尼在肿瘤缩小前就能抑制黏液高分泌。人体证据仍有限：吸入型BIBW 2948在COPD患者中耐受性差，而下游抑制剂Bio11006已进入IIb期临床试验。作者强调，疗效必须与皮肤、胃肠道、肝脏和肺部毒性进行权衡。 作者表示，核心的机遇在于将一个共同的炎症趋同点转化为选择性气道疗法。他们指出，EGFR阻断似乎能够减少多种触发因素导致的GCH和黏蛋白生成，但他们也警告说，现有TKIs存在脱靶效应，并且目前尚未在人类哮喘或COPD中报告成功的EGFR-TKI临床试验。他们认为，未来的工作应优先开发低脱靶效应的分子、优化给药装置，并开展在患者中同时衡量黏液结果和全身安全性的临床试验。 在临床上，EGFR靶向药物可以通过减少过量黏液的来源而非仅仅清除黏液，来补充支气管扩张剂和黏液溶解剂的作用。最可行的近期途径是吸入或气道选择性给药，这可能限制与全身性TKIs相关的皮肤、胃肠道、肝脏和肺部毒性。严重哮喘、伴有黏液栓的COPD、囊性纤维化（CF）或病毒驱动的急性加重患者可能受益最大，但临床试验需要测量黏蛋白负荷、肺功能、急性加重事件和不良事件。纳米颗粒和抗体片段平台可以改善靶向性，然而，监管机构和临床医生将需要有力的证据证明黏液减少能转化为更少的急性加重事件和更好的生存率。该综述的启示是明确的：EGFR是一个有前景的靶点，但尚未成为成熟的常规治疗方案。
