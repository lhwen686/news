血液蛋白生物标志物可预测超高龄老年人未来残疾风险

Blood protein biomarkers predict future disability risk in very old adults

深度解析

本研究的核心专业概念在于识别预测高龄人群未来残疾的生物标志物。Beta-2微球蛋白（B2M）和胱抑素C是两种关键的血液蛋白。B2M是细胞表面MHC I类分子的一部分，其水平升高通常提示肾功能受损或免疫系统激活，尤其与“炎症性衰老”（inflammaging）这一慢性低度炎症状态密切相关。炎症性衰老是公认的驱动老年人功能衰退和多种慢性病的因素。胱抑素C则是一种半胱氨酸蛋白酶抑制剂，广泛用于评估肾小球滤过率（GFR），通常被认为是比血清肌酐更敏感的肾功能指标。研究发现，这两种生物标志物水平升高独立预测了高龄人群（尤其是80岁以上）的残疾风险，且在不同人群中得到了验证。 这些发现具有深远的行业影响。首先，它们为临床实践提供了新的工具，可能通过常规血液检测实现对高风险老年人的早期识别和风险分层。其次，通过早期识别，医疗系统可以从被动治疗转向主动预防，及时实施针对性的干预措施，如个性化运动方案、营养支持和康复治疗，从而延缓残疾的发生和发展。这将有助于提升老年人的生活质量，延长独立生活时间，并有效减轻全球日益增长的长期护理系统的经济和社会负担。此外，对炎症性衰老和肾功能受损在残疾进程中作用的深入理解，也可能为未来开发靶向性治疗或预防策略提供新的方向。

英文原文

Original
Japan has one of the world's fastest-aging populations, but maintaining independence into very old age remains a growing public health challenge. Nearly 60% of Japanese adults aged 85 years and older already receive support through the national Long-Term Care Insurance system, highlighting the need to identify people at risk before disability develops. Although routine blood tests can detect many age-related diseases, reliable biomarkers that predict future disability in the oldest-old have remained elusive. Identifying such markers could enable earlier interventions that help older adults remain active and independent for longer. Addressing this challenge, a research team led by Associate Professor Yusuke Osawa from the Graduate School of Health Management, Keio University, Japan, along with Professor Yasumichi Arai from the Faculty of Nursing and Medical Care at the same university, and Dr. Luigi Ferrucci, Scientific Director of the Translational Gerontology Branch, National Institute on Aging, National Institutes of Health, USA, analyzed blood samples from community-dwelling adults aged 85–89 years using an unbiased, data-driven screen of circulating proteins. Using machine learning and multivariable statistical analyses, they evaluated 29 plasma proteins for associations with future disability and mortality before independently validating the findings in the Italian Invecchiare in Chianti (InCHIANTI) aging study. The study was published online, in the journal GeroScience on June 27, 2026. The discovery analysis included 230 disability-free participants from the Kawasaki Aging Well-being Project (KAWP), who were followed for approximately 4.5 years. Higher levels of beta-2-microglobulin (B2M) and cystatin C were consistently associated with a greater likelihood of developing disability. Each increase in these biomarkers was associated with significantly higher disability risk (hazard ratio 1.35 for B2M and 1.42 for cystatin C), even after accounting for age, sex, kidney function, lifestyle factors, and other potential confounders. To determine whether the findings extended beyond one population, the researchers examined participants in the InCHIANTI study, which followed older adults in Italy for up to 15 years. Elevated B2M and cystatin C levels were again associated with greater disability risk, particularly among participants aged 80 years and older. In contrast, proteins initially linked with mortality-including epidermal growth factor and interferon gamma-induced protein 10-were not replicated, indicating that the disability-related biomarkers were the study's most consistent findings. "Because B2M and cystatin C are already measurable using standard clinical assays, they have the potential to become practical tools for identifying older adults who may benefit from preventive support," says Dr. Osawa. The two proteins also provide important biological insights. Both are associated with kidney function, while B2M additionally reflects chronic low-grade inflammation, or "inflammaging," which is increasingly recognized as a driver of age-related decline. Together, these processes may contribute to the gradual loss of physical function that ultimately leads to dependence on long-term care. "Our findings suggest that preserving healthy aging requires attention not only to diseases but also to the biological processes that precede disability," explains Prof. Arai. "Earlier identification of people at higher risk could create opportunities for timely interventions such as exercise, nutritional support, and rehabilitation before irreversible decline occurs." Overall, the study identifies B2M and cystatin C as promising blood-based biomarkers of future disability in very old adults. Their validation across Japanese and Italian cohorts supports their potential use in earlier risk stratification in rapidly aging societies. Such an approach may help shift healthcare from reactive treatment toward proactive prevention, enabling more older adults to maintain their independence while reducing the growing burden on long-term care systems worldwide.

日本是世界上老龄化速度最快的国家之一，但保持独立生活直至高龄仍是一个日益严峻的公共卫生挑战。近60%的85岁及以上日本成年人已经通过国家长期护理保险系统获得支持，这凸显了在残疾发生前识别高风险人群的必要性。尽管常规血液检查可以检测出许多与年龄相关的疾病，但能够预测高龄人群未来残疾的可靠生物标志物仍然难以捉摸。识别此类标志物可以实现早期干预，帮助老年人更长时间地保持活跃和独立。 为应对这一挑战，由日本庆应义塾大学健康管理研究生院的尾泽佑介副教授、同校护理与医疗学院的荒井康道教授以及美国国立卫生研究院国家老龄化研究所转化老年病学分部科学主任Luigi Ferrucci博士领导的一个研究团队，使用一种无偏见、数据驱动的循环蛋白筛选方法，分析了居住在社区的85-89岁成年人的血样。他们利用机器学习和多变量统计分析，评估了29种血浆蛋白与未来残疾和死亡率的关联，随后在意大利Invecchiare in Chianti (InCHIANTI) 老龄化研究中独立验证了这些发现。该研究于2026年6月27日在线发表在《GeroScience》杂志上。 这项发现性分析纳入了来自川崎健康老龄化项目（KAWP）的230名无残疾参与者，并对其进行了大约4.5年的随访。β-2微球蛋白（B2M）和胱抑素C水平较高与发生残疾的可能性增加持续相关。即使在考虑了年龄、性别、肾功能、生活方式因素和其他潜在混杂因素后，这些生物标志物每增加一个单位，残疾风险仍显著升高（B2M的风险比为1.35，胱抑素C的风险比为1.42）。 为确定这些发现是否适用于其他人群，研究人员审查了InCHIANTI研究的参与者，该研究对意大利老年人进行了长达15年的随访。升高的B2M和胱抑素C水平再次与更大的残疾风险相关，尤其是在80岁及以上的参与者中。相比之下，最初与死亡率相关的蛋白质——包括表皮生长因子和干扰素伽马诱导蛋白10——未得到重复验证，这表明与残疾相关的生物标志物是该研究中最一致的发现。 “由于B2M和胱抑素C已经可以使用标准临床检测方法测量，它们有可能成为识别可能受益于预防性支持的老年人的实用工具，”尾泽博士说。 这两种蛋白质也提供了重要的生物学见解。它们都与肾功能相关，而B2M还反映了慢性低度炎症，即“炎症性衰老（inflammaging）”，这被越来越多地认为是年龄相关性功能衰退的驱动因素。这些过程共同可能导致身体功能逐渐丧失，最终导致对长期护理的依赖。 “我们的研究结果表明，维持健康老龄化不仅需要关注疾病，还需要关注残疾发生前的生物学过程，”荒井教授解释说。“更早地识别高风险人群可以为及时干预创造机会，例如在不可逆转的衰退发生之前进行锻炼、营养支持和康复。” 总的来说，这项研究将B2M和胱抑素C确定为预测高龄成年人未来残疾的有前景的血液生物标志物。它们在日本和意大利队列中的验证支持了它们在快速老龄化社会中用于早期风险分层的潜力。这种方法可能有助于将医疗保健从被动治疗转向主动预防，使更多老年人保持独立，同时减轻全球长期护理系统日益增长的负担。
