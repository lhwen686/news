Daily oral GLP-1 medication linked to similar weight loss across three menopausal stages

深度解析

本研究揭示了口服小分子非肽类GLP-1受体激动剂orforglipron在不同更年期阶段女性体重管理中的显著疗效。GLP-1（胰高血糖素样肽-1）是一种肠道激素，通过葡萄糖依赖性地刺激胰岛素分泌、抑制胰高血糖素释放、延缓胃排空并增加饱腹感来调节血糖和食欲。Orforglipron作为GLP-1受体激动剂，通过模拟内源性GLP-1的作用，有效促进体重减轻。特别值得关注的是，更年期雌激素水平下降常加剧肥胖风险，此药物能在此特殊人群中显示出一致的减重效果，弥补了临床上的一个空白。其口服给药的便利性相较于目前主流的注射型GLP-1受体激动剂，有望显著提高患者依从性和可及性，对全球肥胖及2型糖尿病市场具有颠覆性潜力。这不仅拓展了GLP-1类药物的应用场景，也为女性特定生理阶段的体重管理提供了高效且便捷的新选择，预示着该领域新一轮的市场竞争和治疗范式的转变。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain New analyses to be presented at the annual meeting of the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD) in Milan, Italy, Sept. 28–Oct. 2, show that orforglipron, a daily oral medication for weight loss and type 2 diabetes, was associated with similar weight loss among women of different ages and at different life stages: before, during and after menopause. The study is by Dr. Hunter Thomas Hoffman of Eli Lilly and Company in Indianapolis, the manufacturer of orforglipron, and colleagues. Obesity, a chronic, progressive disease, is exacerbated by estrogen deficiency during menopause. Orforglipron, an oral small-molecule, nonpeptide GLP-1 receptor agonist, was associated with significant body weight (BW) reductions in the ATTAIN-1 and ATTAIN-2 studies. ATTAIN-1 examined people with overweight or obesity but not diabetes; ATTAIN-2 examined people with both conditions. This additional analysis evaluated orforglipron's effect on BW in women by menopausal stage. Female participants with obesity (BMI ≥30 kg/m²) or overweight (BMI ≥27 kg/m²) and at least one weight-related medical condition in ATTAIN-1 and ATTAIN-2 were analyzed by menopausal status: premenopausal, perimenopausal or postmenopausal. The number of women in each category receiving orforglipron or placebo ranged from 142 to 225. Researchers assessed changes in BW, waist circumference (WC) and waist-to-height ratio, as well as the proportion achieving BW reduction thresholds of ≥5%, ≥10%, ≥15% and ≥20%, vs. placebo (PBO) at 72 weeks. Women given orforglipron had significant and similar body weight reductions of up to 14% across all three menopausal stages in both ATTAIN-1 and ATTAIN-2. Significant reductions in waist circumference of up to 12.5 cm (4.9 inches) were observed across subgroups receiving orforglipron in both studies. Up to 83% of women receiving orforglipron lost at least 5% of their body weight, vs. 30% receiving placebo. More participants treated with orforglipron than with placebo moved to lower waist-to-height ratio categories.
