这篇研究揭示了化疗后存活的卵巢癌细胞通过释放果糖作为关键信号分子，促进肿瘤扩散的机制。核心专业概念在于，化疗未能杀死的癌细胞并非生物惰性，而是持续活跃并分泌信号分子，其中果糖被识别为驱动癌细胞转移的关键信使。果糖的作用机制在于其能抑制邻近细胞内的胆固醇合成。胆固醇在细胞间粘附中扮演“生物胶水”的角色，其水平降低会削弱细胞间的连接，从而使癌细胞更容易脱离原发灶并发生转移，这一过程正是癌症致死的主要原因。 这项发现对肿瘤学领域具有深远影响。首先，它提出了通过简单的饮食干预（如限制果糖摄入）来辅助抑制癌症进展的可能性，为患者提供了一种可控的、非药物的抗癌策略。其次，研究揭示了果糖与胆固醇代谢之间的联系，以及他汀类药物（降胆固醇药物）可能对细胞粘附产生的负面影响。这引发了对化疗与他汀类药物联合用药安全性和有效性的重新审视，尤其是在常服用他汀类药物的绝经后卵巢癌患者群体中，强调了进行临床试验以优化治疗方案的必要性。此外，研究机制有望推广至胰腺癌、结肠癌、肝癌等其他躯干内扩散的癌症，为更广泛的抗转移治疗提供新的靶点和思路。

英文原文

Original
A new study from The Wistar Institute has uncovered an unexpected link between fructose - a common dietary sugar - and the spread of an aggressive form of ovarian cancer. Published in Nature Aging, the study found that cancer cells not killed by chemotherapy send signals to neighboring tumor cells, helping them become more capable of spreading. The researchers identified fructose as a key messenger in this process, revealing a previously unrecognized way that treatment-surviving cancer cells may promote the spread of cancer. "Some cancer cells that survive chemotherapy aren't dividing anymore, but they're still biologically active," said Aidan Cole, Ph.D., a postdoctoral fellow in the lab of Katherine Aird, Ph.D., at The Wistar Institute and first author on the study. "Instead, they continue to release molecules that send signals to nearby cells. Our study is among the first to show that a nutrient-in this case, fructose-can act as one of those signals." Ovarian cancer is treated almost universally with platinum-based chemotherapy, and many patients respond well at first. However, the disease recurs in most patients and almost always spreads through the abdominal cavity. That spread, called metastasis, accounts for roughly 90% of deaths from the disease. Prior research has suggested that cancer cells not killed by chemotherapy play a role in recurrence in part through the ability of these cells to release a complex mix of signaling molecules. Cole and his colleagues began their research by designing a unique experiment: they collected the molecules released by chemotherapy-surviving cells and found these factors alone could significantly increase the spread of cancer cells. "As far as we know, this is the first time anyone has shown, in a preclinical model rather than just a dish, that it's the molecules these cells release - not the cells themselves - that drive the cancer's spread," said Cole. That finding sent the team looking for what was being released that caused the cancer cells to spread. Fructose, it turned out, was being made by the surviving cells and sent as the signal for increased spread. Even more interesting, they found that without chemotherapy treatment, consuming the high levels of fructose found in sugary drinks can also signal cancer to spread. The fructose finding is especially compelling because it raises the possibility that simple dietary changes could help shape how cancer progresses. This is particularly important given the prevalence of fructose consumption in the United States, with high fructose corn syrup accounting for ~8-20% of the daily caloric intake in some individuals. Unlike many cancer risk factors outside of patient control, fructose consumption can be modified by dietary choices. While the effectiveness of limiting fructose intake hasn't yet been tested directly in patients, the study raises the possibility that nutrition could influence cancer progression in previously unrecognized ways. The researchers next aimed to understand why fructose increases cancer cell spread. Through a variety of large-scale analytical techniques, including the use of a CRISPR screen, they discovered that fructose suppresses cholesterol within the neighboring cells. Cholesterol is important for cells to stick to each other like a biological glue, so decreased cholesterol allows cells to more easily escape and spread. The finding that fructose lowers cholesterol production has important clinical implications. Statins, which are taken by 39 million people in the United States, lower cholesterol production. The team found that statins alone decreased the glue between cells to promote escape. The research team is now examining whether these medications could be interfering with the effects of chemotherapy - though they stress this isn't a reason for patients to stop taking them. We haven't tested this effect in patients yet, but it raises questions about combining cholesterol-lowering drugs with chemotherapy, especially since ovarian cancer is most common in postmenopausal women who are often already on statins." Katherine Aird, Ph.D., professor and co-leader of the Molecular and Cellular Oncogenesis Program in the Ellen and Ronald Caplan Cancer Center, The Wistar Institute, and senior author of the study The researchers are also looking into how this mechanism might extend beyond ovarian cancer. "We think other cancers that spread within the torso - pancreatic, colon, liver - could behave similarly. We can't call it universal yet, but we think the effects are not just limited to ovarian cancer," said Aird. She and Cole have already started designing follow-up experiments to test the reproducibility of their findings in a variety of other cancer types.

威斯塔研究所的一项新研究揭示了果糖（一种常见的膳食糖）与侵袭性卵巢癌扩散之间意想不到的联系。这项发表在《自然·衰老》杂志上的研究发现，未被化疗杀死的癌细胞会向邻近的肿瘤细胞发送信号，帮助它们获得更强的扩散能力。研究人员将果糖确定为这一过程中的关键信使，揭示了治疗后存活的癌细胞可能促进癌症扩散的一种先前未被认识的方式。 威斯塔研究所凯瑟琳·艾尔德博士实验室的博士后研究员、该研究的第一作者艾丹·科尔博士说：“一些在化疗中存活下来的癌细胞不再分裂，但它们仍然具有生物活性。相反，它们继续释放分子，向附近的细胞发送信号。我们的研究首次表明，一种营养物质——在这种情况下是果糖——可以作为这些信号之一。” 卵巢癌几乎普遍采用铂类化疗，许多患者最初反应良好。然而，大多数患者的疾病会复发，并且几乎总是通过腹腔扩散。这种扩散，称为转移，约占该疾病死亡人数的90%。 先前的研究表明，未被化疗杀死的癌细胞在复发中发挥作用，部分原因在于这些细胞能够释放复杂的信号分子混合物。科尔和他的同事们通过设计一项独特的实验开始了他们的研究：他们收集了化疗存活细胞释放的分子，发现这些因子本身就能显著增加癌细胞的扩散。 科尔说：“据我们所知，这是首次有人在临床前模型而非仅仅培养皿中证明，是这些细胞释放的分子——而非细胞本身——驱动了癌症的扩散。” 这一发现促使团队寻找导致癌细胞扩散的释放物。结果发现，果糖是由存活细胞产生并作为促进扩散的信号发送的。更有趣的是，他们发现，即使没有化疗，摄入含糖饮料中高水平的果糖也能发出癌症扩散的信号。果糖的发现尤其引人注目，因为它提出了通过简单的饮食改变可能有助于影响癌症进展的可能性。考虑到美国果糖消费的普遍性，这一点尤为重要，某些个体的高果糖玉米糖浆摄入量约占每日卡路里摄入的8-20%。与许多患者无法控制的癌症风险因素不同，果糖的摄入量可以通过饮食选择来改变。尽管限制果糖摄入的有效性尚未在患者中直接测试，但该研究提出了营养可能以先前未被认识的方式影响癌症进展的可能性。 接下来，研究人员旨在理解果糖为何会增加癌细胞的扩散。通过各种大规模分析技术，包括使用CRISPR筛选，他们发现果糖会抑制邻近细胞内的胆固醇。胆固醇对于细胞像生物胶水一样相互粘附很重要，因此胆固醇减少会使细胞更容易脱离并扩散。 果糖降低胆固醇生产的发现具有重要的临床意义。他汀类药物在美国有3900万人服用，它们能降低胆固醇的产生。团队发现，他汀类药物本身会减少细胞间的粘附力，从而促进细胞逃逸。研究团队目前正在研究这些药物是否可能干扰化疗的效果——尽管他们强调这并非患者停药的理由。 “我们尚未在患者中测试这种效果，但这引发了关于将降胆固醇药物与化疗结合使用的疑问，尤其考虑到卵巢癌在绝经后女性中最为常见，而她们通常已经在使用他汀类药物。”威斯塔研究所艾伦和罗纳德·卡普兰癌症中心分子和细胞肿瘤发生学项目的教授兼联合负责人、该研究的资深作者凯瑟琳·艾尔德博士说。 研究人员还在探讨这一机制如何可能超越卵巢癌范围。 艾尔德说：“我们认为其他在躯干内扩散的癌症——胰腺癌、结肠癌、肝癌——也可能表现出类似的行为。我们还不能称之为普遍现象，但我们认为这些影响不仅限于卵巢癌。” 她和科尔已经开始设计后续实验，以在多种其他癌症类型中测试其发现的重现性。
