Previously missed DNA mutation pattern in colorectal cancer identified

深度解析

这项研究在癌症基因组学领域取得了重要进展，通过识别结直肠癌中一种此前未被充分认识的独特突变特征，深化了我们对肿瘤演进机制的理解。核心概念“突变特征”指的是DNA损伤或复制错误在基因组中留下的特异性印记，这些印记能揭示导致癌症发生的生物学或环境因素。此前，该模式被误解为已知突变组合，但新研究揭示其独立性，并与肿瘤发展后期出现的DNA小片段插入和缺失紧密相关，暗示了DNA复制和修复过程在肿瘤发生发展中可能扮演的关键角色。此发现具有显著的行业影响。首先，它为结直肠癌的病因学提供了新的线索，有助于更精准地理解其分子机制。其次，这种独特的突变特征有望成为潜在的生物标志物，用于肿瘤的早期诊断、风险分层和预后评估，从而指导临床决策。例如，识别出具有该特征的患者，可能意味着需要针对特定的DNA修复途径进行靶向治疗。这推动了癌症精准医疗的发展，通过对患者基因组特征的深入解析，实现个体化的治疗策略，最终改善患者的治疗效果和生存率。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: CC0 Public Domain Researchers from the University of California San Diego and collaborators have identified a previously overlooked pattern of DNA mutations in colorectal cancer. Scientists use these patterns, known as mutational signatures, to investigate the biological and environmental processes that have damaged DNA during cancer development. While the previously identified pattern had been detected in large cancer-genome data sets before, researchers had interpreted it as a combination of other, already known mutation patterns. When the researchers took a closer look, they found that the pattern was not associated with the known processes to which it had been attributed. They also found the same pattern in three independent colorectal cancer data sets, together comprising 2,616 tumors, providing evidence that it is a genuine and recurring feature of some colorectal cancers. The paper is published in the journal Nature Communications. A distinct signal emerges The researchers found that the mutation pattern appears relatively late as colorectal tumors develop. This suggests that something may change during tumor development that causes cancer cells to accumulate a new type of DNA damage or copying error. The researchers also found that tumors with the newly recognized pattern had more small insertions and deletions in their DNA. Because insertions and deletions can arise during DNA replication and repair, this finding provides an additional clue that the newly recognized pattern may be related to these processes. Clues to tumor evolution More broadly, the study addresses one of the fundamental challenges in cancer genomics: determining what unusual mutational patterns can reveal about the processes that shape cancer development. The work was completed by Team Mutographs. Mutographs seeks to address the critical question of the relationship between mutational signatures and cancer development. Publication details Mariya Kazachkova et al, Identification and validation of a previously missed mutational signature in colorectal cancer, Nature Communications (2026).
