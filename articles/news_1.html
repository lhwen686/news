Blood pressure drug losartan boosts metabolism by stimulating brown fat

深度解析

这篇新闻报道揭示了降压药氯沙坦在棕色脂肪组织（BAT）活化中的新潜力及其对代谢和精神健康的深远影响。核心机制在于，棕色脂肪能通过产热燃烧卡路里，与储存能量的白色脂肪功能相反。研究首次发现氯沙坦，一种常用的血管紧张素受体阻滞剂（ARB），能直接增强BAT的代谢活性，且其对BAT的影响与降血压的分子机制不同，这意味着未来可能开发出选择性活化BAT而不影响血压的新疗法。此外，该研究还创新性地将BAT与精神疾病患病率的降低联系起来，暗示了代谢健康与精神健康之间的关联。从行业影响来看，氯沙坦的药物再利用前景广阔，因为它是一种已上市、安全性明确的药物，可加速其在治疗肥胖、糖尿病、心血管疾病甚至精神疾病方面的应用。这项研究为开发新型代谢和精神疾病疗法提供了全新的靶点和思路，可能催生基于BAT活化的创新药物或干预策略。

英文原文

Original
Losartan, a drug commonly prescribed for high blood pressure, boosts brown adipose tissue or "brown fat" activity and enhances its metabolism, according to a preclinical study by Weill Cornell Medicine, Rockefeller University and Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center researchers. The findings suggest that repurposing losartan—an angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB) with a well-known safety profile—may potentially offer a therapeutic approach to stimulate brown fat, which has been linked to improved cardiovascular health. Unlike normal white fat, which stores energy in the body, brown fat burns calories to generate heat. The study also reported, for the first time, that brown fat is associated with reduced prevalence of psychiatric diseases. The study, published Oct. 14 in Nature, analyzed PET/CT scans that were performed as part of cancer surveillance for over 100,000 patients. The researchers focused on the scans that detected brown fat, which is located in six regions of the body, including the neck, armpit and around organs like the heart and kidneys. The current study builds on discoveries by senior author Dr. Paul Cohen, Albert Resnick, M.D. Associate Professor and physician at The Rockefeller University. His lab found that people with brown fat had significantly lower odds of cardiometabolic diseases, including coronary artery disease and type 2 diabetes. We only looked for a few diagnostic codes in that study, mainly focusing on cardiovascular disease since that's what this tissue has historically been associated with. The scope of this new analysis is far more expansive. We doubled our number of patients and considered more than 500 codes, which allowed us to unearth many new insights." Dr. Paul Cohen, Rockefeller University First author Dr. Nicolás Gómez-Banoy, an endocrinologist and instructor in clinical investigation in Dr. Cohen's lab and a fellow in medicine in the Comprehensive Weight Control Center and Division of Endocrinology, Diabetes and Metabolism at Weill Cornell, quantified the total activity of brown fat in each patient, as well as the activity in each of the six anatomical regions. Dr. Gómez-Banoy found that, in addition to having brown fat, the location of this tissue was important, since higher brown fat activity in certain regions seemed to have greater influence on specific diseases. "For example, the axillary, or armpit, area was linked to a decreased risk of high blood pressure compared to other regions," said Dr. Gómez-Banoy, who is also a graduate of the Tri-I Medical Research Track (MRT) program. "But overall, patients with brown fat have better glycemic parameters, lipid profiles and echocardiographic indices, as well as lower inflammatory markers." Further analyses revealed that patients taking ARBs had more brown fat in their scans. To investigate this relationship, the researchers tested how losartan affected cultured brown fat cells and observed that the drug directly increased metabolic activity in the cells. They also learned that losartan used different molecular mechanisms to impact brown fat metabolism versus to lower blood pressure. "The fact that different molecular pathways mediate its different effects is advantageous, since it means we could potentially isolate them in future therapies," Dr. Gómez-Banoy said. "That way you would be able to stimulate brown fat in people with normal blood pressure." Healthy mice treated with losartan also showed many indicators of enhanced metabolism, such as increased energy expenditure, oxygen consumption and carbon dioxide production, while maintaining a steady body weight. The team also uncovered links to mental health, including a lower incidence of anxiety and depression in patients with brown fat. "We know that metabolically healthy people tend to have a lower incidence of psychiatric diseases, so it could be that having brown fat makes you more metabolically healthy, and this correlates with fewer psychiatric issues," Dr. Gómez-Banoy said. Conversely, they found that SSRIs, commonly prescribed for depression, had a dampening effect on brown fat activity. The findings open several new research paths, such as exploring drugs beyond losartan, studying the genetics of people with higher brown fat levels and understanding the connection to anxiety and depression. "There were plenty of other medications associated with brown fat activity in our large cohort," Dr. Cohen said. "This is just a start."

AI Translation
根据威尔康奈尔医学院、洛克菲勒大学和纪念斯隆凯特琳癌症中心研究人员的一项临床前研究，一种常用于治疗高血压的药物——氯沙坦——能促进棕色脂肪组织（即“棕色脂肪”）的活性并增强其代谢。研究结果表明，重新利用氯沙沙坦这种具有良好安全性的血管紧张素受体阻滞剂（ARB），可能为刺激棕色脂肪提供一种治疗方法，而棕色脂肪已被证实与改善心血管健康有关。与储存能量的白色脂肪不同，棕色脂肪通过燃烧卡路里来产热。该研究还首次报告，棕色脂肪与精神疾病患病率的降低有关。 这项研究于10月14日发表在《自然》杂志上，分析了超过10万名患者作为癌症监测一部分进行的PET/CT扫描。研究人员重点关注了检测到棕色脂肪的扫描，棕色脂肪位于身体的六个区域，包括颈部、腋下以及心脏和肾脏等器官周围。 目前这项研究建立在资深作者保罗·科恩博士（Paul Cohen）——洛克菲勒大学阿尔伯特·雷斯尼克医学副教授兼医师——的发现基础之上。他的实验室发现，拥有棕色脂肪的人患心脏代谢疾病（包括冠状动脉疾病和2型糖尿病）的几率显著降低。 “我们当时那项研究只查找了几个诊断代码，主要集中在心血管疾病，因为这种组织历来与心血管疾病相关。这项新分析的范围要广阔得多。我们使患者数量翻倍，并考虑了500多个代码，这使我们能够挖掘出许多新见解。”洛克菲勒大学的保罗·科恩博士说。 第一作者尼古拉斯·戈麦斯-巴诺伊博士（Nicolás Gómez-Banoy）是科恩博士实验室的内分泌学家和临床研究讲师，也是威尔康奈尔大学综合体重控制中心和内分泌学、糖尿病与代谢科的医学研究员。他量化了每位患者棕色脂肪的总活性，以及六个解剖区域中每个区域的活性。 戈麦斯-巴诺伊博士发现，除了拥有棕色脂肪外，这种组织的位置也很重要，因为某些区域较高的棕色脂肪活性似乎对特定疾病有更大的影响。 “例如，与身体其他区域相比，腋窝区域与高血压风险降低有关，”戈麦斯-巴诺伊博士说，他也是Tri-I医学研究项目（MRT）的毕业生。“但总体而言，拥有棕色脂肪的患者具有更好的血糖参数、血脂谱和超声心动图指标，以及更低的炎症标志物。” 进一步的分析显示，服用血管紧张素受体阻滞剂（ARBs）的患者在扫描中显示出更多的棕色脂肪。为了探究这种关系，研究人员测试了氯沙坦如何影响培养的棕色脂肪细胞，并观察到该药物直接增加了细胞的代谢活性。他们还了解到，氯沙坦通过不同的分子机制影响棕色脂肪代谢，这与它降低血压的机制不同。 “不同分子通路介导其不同效应这一事实是有利的，因为这意味着我们未来可能在疗法中将它们分离出来，”戈麦斯-巴诺伊博士说。“那样你就可以在血压正常的人群中刺激棕色脂肪。” 用氯沙坦治疗的健康小鼠也显示出许多代谢增强的指标，例如能量消耗、氧气消耗和二氧化碳生成增加，同时体重保持稳定。 研究团队还揭示了与心理健康的关联，包括拥有棕色脂肪的患者焦虑和抑郁症发病率较低。“我们知道代谢健康的人往往精神疾病发病率较低，因此拥有棕色脂肪可能使你更代谢健康，这与较少的精神问题相关，”戈麦斯-巴诺伊博士说。相反，他们发现常用于治疗抑郁症的选择性5-羟色胺再摄取抑制剂（SSRIs）对棕色脂肪活性有抑制作用。 这些发现开辟了几条新的研究路径，例如探索氯沙坦以外的药物，研究棕色脂肪水平较高人群的遗传学，以及理解与焦虑和抑郁症的联系。 “在我们的庞大队列中，还有许多其他药物与棕色脂肪活性相关，”科恩博士说。“这只是一个开始。”
