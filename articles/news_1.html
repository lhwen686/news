insights from industry Dr. Dagmara Lewandowska Product Manager for NGS INTEGRA Biosciences

In this interview, Dr. Dagmara Lewandowska, Product Manager for NGS at INTEGRA Biosciences, talks to NewsMedical about innovations in magnetic bead-based extraction and genomics automation.

Please introduce MAGFLO™ NGS magnetic beads.

INTEGRA is built on decades of liquid-handling expertise, combining MAGFLO NGS magnetic beads for size selection in NGS library prep with protocols that can be performed on the MAG module.

MAGFLO NGS beads incorporate three key features that set them apart from alternatives. Firstly, the beads remain stable at room temperature for 12 months, whereas magnetic beads from other suppliers typically need to be kept in the fridge and equilibrated to room temperature before use to ensure consistent liquid handling parameters.

Since MAGFLO NGS beads are room temperature stable, labs can avoid equilibration delays and reduce variability in their bead-to-sample ratios. Our beads are also RNase free, so they can be used to handle RNA, and QC verified, meaning every batch is qualified for use in size-selection protocols before it's released for sale. MAGFLO NGS beads are therefore a cost-effective, 3-in-1 magnetic bead solution.

Explain how MAGFLO NGS beads integrate with liquid handling solutions in a genomics workflow.

MAGFLO NGS beads were designed to pair seamlessly with INTEGRA’s range of pipetting instruments, which can be configured based on specific workflow requirements. They are also compatible with our MAG and HEATMAG modules for fast bead collection with minimal loss and bead carryover.

Unlike traditional ring magnets, which create a ring pellet around the well that requires precise pipette handling and upright tip alignment to avoid disturbing the beads, the MAG modules conveniently collect the pellet in one corner of the well by vertically moving the magnetic rods. This enables easier, more reliable pipetting without bead loss, leading to more reproducible results.

The HEATMAG module goes one step further by combining this innovative magnetic separation with heating capabilities, providing the perfect set-up for heated lysis during nucleic acid extraction, or for optimizing elution conditions to increase yields.

These modules can be used as standalone units for manual processing, or combined with a variety of INTEGRA pipetting platforms for semi- or fully automated workflows. For example, pairing a MAG or HEATMAG with a MINI 96, VIAFLO 96, or VIAFLO 384 pipetting platform will enable users to simultaneously process entire 96 or 384 well plates – or 24 tubes at a time – significantly boosting lab productivity.

When used in combination with the ASSIST PLUS pipetting robot and a VIAFLO or VOYAGER multichannel electronic pipette, the MAG modules provide fully walk-away magnetic bead-based purification without the need for costly grippers and robotic arms. The D-ONE single channel pipetting module can also be mounted onto the ASSIST PLUS for normalization and pooling after library prep.

From the outset, INTEGRA developed MAGFLO NGS beads to offer customers a tested and calibrated end-to-end package of instrumentation and reagents for NGS, ready to use, all from one trusted supplier.

What are the main workflow challenges that MAGFLO NGS beads and MAG modules are designed to address in NGS library prep?

Firstly, reproducibility. Automating sample transfer, for instance, on the ASSIST PLUS with a MAG module, improves liquid handling consistency compared with manual methods, leading to more reproducible results. Parameters such as incubation times and magnet height can be quickly programmed using our VIALAB software to ensure liquid is handled in exactly the same way each time.

As I mentioned before, another major challenge our beads address is the need to equilibrate refrigerated beads to room temperature before use to maintain consistent pipetting parameters and bead-to-sample ratios. MAGFLO NGS beads are stable at room temperature, so they do not need to be refrigerated and equilibrated, saving cold chain storage space and preparation time.

Another benefit of our magnetic bead solutions is that the MAG module magnets move up and down, while the plate itself stays in place. This means the modules have a compact footprint, saving valuable deck space and making the modules easier to handle and integrate with other platforms.

What applications can magnetic beads be used in? How does using integrated liquid handling solutions help to scale NGS workflows?

Magnetic beads are typically used for NGS library prep, PCR clean-up, and genomic DNA extraction. Labs commonly adopt magnetic beads to scale up their throughput, improve result reproducibility, and gain more walk-away time. Some groups would also like to move away from traditional spin column extraction and introduce more automation, bypassing the centrifugation step and avoiding tedious manual processes. To support these workflows, we launched the Clean Blood and Tissue DNA Kit (CleanNA) specifically to cover an extraction step, which can be automated on an INTEGRA benchtop device equipped with a MAG module for walk-away extractions.

Describe a typical NGS workflow from start to finish and explain how different liquid handling products can be used in each step.

INTEGRA’s solutions support every stage of low to medium throughput NGS workflows. Following sample collection, the first step is extraction and isolation, including aliquoting/re-formatting and nucleic acid extraction, which can be performed using the ASSIST PLUS, VOYAGER, and HEATMAG module.

Next comes library preparation, including fragmentation, size selection/clean-up, and NGS library generation. The SWITCH hybrid pipette can be used for fragmentation, and the ASSIST PLUS and VOYAGER pipettes can also be used with the MAG module for the remainder of the tasks involved in stage 2. The MIRO CANVAS can be used for assay miniaturization.

Finally, there is sequencing ready, from quantification and normalization to pooling. The ASSIST PLUS can be mounted with the VOYAGER for quantification, and with the D-ONE single channel pipetting module for normalization and pooling. After this, the sample should be ready for sequencing.

What feedback have customers provided regarding the MAGFLO NGS beads so far?

The feedback we’ve received has been very positive. Customers are pleased with the high quality of our beads and find them to be comparable to the current industry gold standard. They also note that the beads yield highly reproducible sequencing results and appreciate the ability to quickly prepare samples in an automated, hands-free manner.

A third-party lab has performed beta testing on the MAGFLO NGS beads, using them for size selection and library clean-up. Liquid handling was carried out using an ASSIST PLUS paired with a 12 channel VIAFLO electronic pipette, and the workflow's magnetic separation steps were performed twice: once using a standard magnetic plate and once using a MAG module.

The lab found this set-up to be extremely versatile, as it could be applied across numerous labware formats and adapted to specific protocols without extensive programming skills, thanks to intuitive user interfaces and pipette automation software. It was much easier for the team to adjust and optimize the workflow than the more complex programming often required for full workflow automation, providing full control while enhancing time efficiency. The lab also discovered that automating certain time-consuming components of NGS library preparation accelerated repetitive liquid handling tasks, giving staff more walk-away time and improving sample-processing capacity.

Crucially, the combination of MAGFLO NGS magnetic beads with the MAG module achieved the tightest size selection and the least size variation of all the options trialed in this beta test. In addition, the fragments produced were, on average, closest to the target size of 750 bp across all tested set-ups. The workflow also produced the highest library concentration and the least variation in concentration of all trialed combinations, demonstrating its reproducibility. Sequencing results showed that the set-up also produced more reads and less variation between read values than the lab’s existing method, and results were more reproducible and less variable than manual NGS library preparation and size selection.

Looking ahead, how do you see solutions such as MAGFLO NGS beads and liquid handling automation shaping the future of genomics workflow design?

INTEGRA always endeavors to innovate and develop high-quality alternatives to existing solutions, providing added value to customers. Our magnetic bead kits meet this brief, as they are compatible with and verified for use with our liquid-handling instruments, enabling convenient and accurate magnetic bead extraction workflows. We are excited to establish ourselves further in the growing NGS market and continue meeting the needs of our customers in the genomics space through further innovations.

We took a major step in this regard by acquiring Miroculus, the developer of MIRO CANVAS, a microfluidics-based, fully walk-away library prep instrument, in 2022. To further support high molecular weight (HMW) sequencing workflows, we launched an innovative in-tip DNA shearing application using the SWITCH hybrid pipette. The SWITCH combines manual pipetting with electronic repeat dispensing in a single device, and now also includes reproducible HMW DNA shearing capabilities, so users do not need an additional dedicated platform. We plan to build on this foundation by continually investing in other solutions and reagents for the diverse sequencing technologies and genomics workflows that are constantly emerging.

在此次采访中，INTEGRA Biosciences公司NGS产品经理Dagmara Lewandowska博士向NewsMedical杂志讲述了磁珠法核酸提取和基因组学自动化领域的创新。

请介绍一下MAGFLO™ NGS磁珠。

INTEGRA公司在液体处理方面拥有数十年的专业经验，将MAGFLO NGS磁珠用于NGS文库制备中的大小选择，并结合可在MAG模块上执行的实验方案。

MAGFLO NGS磁珠具有三个关键特性，使其与同类产品区别开来。首先，这些磁珠在室温下可稳定保存12个月，而其他供应商的磁珠通常需要冷藏，并在使用前平衡至室温，以确保液体处理参数的一致性。

由于MAGFLO NGS磁珠在室温下稳定，实验室可以避免平衡延迟，并减少磁珠与样本比例的变异性。我们的磁珠也经过RNase（核糖核酸酶）去除处理，因此可用于处理RNA，并经过QC（质量控制）验证，这意味着每一批产品在上市销售前都经过了大小选择方案的合格验证。因此，MAGFLO NGS磁珠是一种经济高效的三合一磁珠解决方案。

请解释MAGFLO NGS磁珠如何与基因组学工作流程中的液体处理解决方案相结合。

MAGFLO NGS磁珠设计旨在与INTEGRA系列移液仪器无缝配合，这些仪器可根据特定的工作流程要求进行配置。它们还与我们的MAG和HEATMAG模块兼容，可实现快速磁珠收集，同时最大限度地减少损失和磁珠残留。

与传统环形磁铁不同，传统磁铁会在孔板周围形成环状沉淀，需要精确的移液操作和垂直的吸头对准以避免扰动磁珠，而MAG模块通过垂直移动磁棒，方便地将沉淀物集中在孔板的一个角落。这使得移液操作更简便、更可靠，减少磁珠损失，从而获得更可重现的结果。

HEATMAG模块更进一步，将这种创新的磁分离技术与加热功能结合起来，为核酸提取过程中的加热裂解或优化洗脱条件以提高产量提供了完美的设置。

这些模块既可作为独立单元用于手动处理，也可与INTEGRA的各种移液平台结合使用，实现半自动化或全自动化工作流程。例如，将MAG或HEATMAG与MINI 96、VIAFLO 96或VIAFLO 384移液平台搭配使用，用户将能够同时处理整个96孔或384孔板——或一次处理24个试管——显著提高实验室生产力。

当与ASSIST PLUS移液机器人以及VIAFLO或VOYAGER多通道电动移液器结合使用时，MAG模块可提供完全无人值守的磁珠法纯化，无需昂贵的夹具和机械臂。D-ONE单通道移液模块也可安装到ASSIST PLUS上，用于文库制备后的标准化和混样。

从一开始，INTEGRA就开发了MAGFLO NGS磁珠，旨在为客户提供一套经过测试和校准的NGS仪器和试剂端到端解决方案，即插即用，所有产品均来自一个值得信赖的供应商。

MAGFLO NGS磁珠和MAG模块旨在解决NGS文库制备中的哪些主要工作流程挑战？

首先是可重现性。例如，在ASSIST PLUS上使用MAG模块自动化样本转移，与手动方法相比，提高了液体处理的一致性，从而获得更可重现的结果。通过我们的VIALAB软件可以快速编程孵育时间、磁铁高度等参数，以确保每次液体处理方式完全相同。

正如我之前提到的，我们的磁珠解决的另一个主要挑战是，在使用前需要将冷藏磁珠平衡至室温，以保持一致的移液参数和磁珠与样本的比例。MAGFLO NGS磁珠在室温下稳定，因此无需冷藏和平衡，节省了冷链存储空间和准备时间。

我们的磁珠解决方案的另一个优势是MAG模块的磁铁上下移动，而板本身保持不动。这意味着模块占用的空间紧凑，节省了宝贵的实验台空间，并使模块更易于操作和与其他平台集成。

磁珠可用于哪些应用？使用集成液体处理解决方案如何帮助扩展NGS工作流程？

磁珠通常用于NGS文库制备、PCR产物纯化和基因组DNA提取。实验室通常采用磁珠来提高通量、改善结果可重现性并获得更多无人值守时间。一些研究团队也希望摆脱传统的离心柱提取方法，引入更多自动化，从而避免离心步骤，避免繁琐的手动过程。为了支持这些工作流程，我们专门推出了Clean Blood and Tissue DNA Kit (CleanNA)来涵盖提取步骤，该步骤可以在配备MAG模块的INTEGRA台式设备上实现自动化，从而实现无人值守提取。

请描述一个典型的NGS工作流程从开始到结束，并解释不同液体处理产品在每个步骤中的应用。

INTEGRA的解决方案支持中低通量NGS工作流程的每个阶段。样本采集后，第一步是提取和分离，包括分装/重排和核酸提取，这可以使用ASSIST PLUS、VOYAGER和HEATMAG模块来完成。

接下来是文库制备，包括片段化、大小选择/纯化和NGS文库生成。SWITCH混合移液器可用于片段化，ASSIST PLUS和VOYAGER移液器也可与MAG模块配合使用，完成第二阶段的其余任务。MIRO CANVAS可用于分析小型化。

最后是测序准备，从定量和标准化到混样。ASSIST PLUS可安装VOYAGER进行定量，安装D-ONE单通道移液模块进行标准化和混样。在此之后，样本即可进行测序。

客户对MAGFLO NGS磁珠迄今为止提供了哪些反馈？

我们收到的反馈非常积极。客户对我们磁珠的高质量感到满意，并认为它们与当前的行业黄金标准相当。他们还指出，这些磁珠产生了高度可重现的测序结果，并赞赏能够以自动化、免手动的方式快速准备样本。

一家第三方实验室对MAGFLO NGS磁珠进行了Beta测试，将其用于大小选择和文库纯化。液体处理使用ASSIST PLUS与12通道VIAFLO电动移液器配合进行，工作流程的磁分离步骤执行了两次：一次使用标准磁板，一次使用MAG模块。

该实验室发现这种设置极其通用，因为它适用于多种实验室耗材格式，并且可以根据特定方案进行调整，而无需 extensive 编程技能，这得益于直观的用户界面和移液器自动化软件。团队调整和优化工作流程比通常需要复杂编程才能实现完全工作流程自动化要容易得多，提供了完全控制，同时提高了时间效率。该实验室还发现，自动化NGS文库制备中某些耗时组件加速了重复性液体处理任务，为工作人员提供了更多无人值守时间，并提高了样本处理能力。

至关重要的是，MAGFLO NGS磁珠与MAG模块的结合，在本次Beta测试中所有试用选项中，实现了最精准的大小选择和最小的大小变异。此外，所产生的片段平均值最接近所有测试设置中的目标尺寸750 bp。该工作流程还产生了所有试用组合中最高的文库浓度和最小的浓度变异，证明了其可重现性。测序结果表明，该设置也产生了比实验室现有方法更多的读数和更小的读数值变异，并且结果比手动NGS文库制备和大小选择更具可重现性和更低的变异性。

展望未来，您如何看待MAGFLO NGS磁珠和液体处理自动化等解决方案塑造基因组学工作流程设计的未来？
