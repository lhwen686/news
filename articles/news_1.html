Cedars-Sinai Health Sciences University investigators have identified a drug-like compound that prevents two proteins from working together to promote the growth of colorectal and liver cancer. Findings from the preclinical study were published in Cell Death & Disease.

Investigators focused on the proteins GIT1 and MAT2B, which are found at unusually high levels in these cancers. The proteins partner to form a "scaffolding" that drives cancer growth. The team discovered that a compound called C3 targets GIT1 and disrupts this structure. The disruption slows cancer cell growth and triggers cancer cell death, which helps prevent the cancer from spreading.

Colorectal cancer is among the most diagnosed cancers, and its spread to the liver is a common, serious, and life-threatening complication.

"We were pleased to find a promising drug candidate that attacks colon and liver cancers simultaneously," said Shelly Lu, MD, the Women's Guild Chair in Gastroenterology, director of the Karsh Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology at Cedars-Sinai, and the study's corresponding author. "And because GIT1 is overactive in many cancers, this discovery could have broad and powerful applications in cancer treatment."

The next step is to refine the compound to improve its effectiveness.

In related research in the Journal of Experimental & Clinical Cancer Research, Lu, a member of Cedars-Sinai Cancer, and Cedars-Sinai investigators recently uncovered another way colorectal cancer spreads.

They found that cancer cells release two forms of an enzyme called MATα2 that work together to weaken the liver's natural defenses, which helps cancer spread to the liver and survive there. The findings suggest that blocking this process could offer another promising future treatment approach.

薛达西奈健康科学大学的研究人员已经识别出一种类药物化合物，该化合物能阻止两种蛋白质协同促进结直肠癌和肝癌的生长。这项临床前研究的发现已发表在《细胞死亡与疾病》（Cell Death & Disease）杂志上。

研究人员关注了蛋白质GIT1和MAT2B，这两种蛋白质在这些癌症中异常高表达。这两种蛋白质协同形成一个“支架”，驱动癌症生长。研究团队发现，一种名为C3的化合物靶向GIT1，并破坏了这一结构。这种破坏作用能减缓癌细胞生长，并诱导癌细胞死亡，从而有助于阻止癌症扩散。

结直肠癌是诊断率最高的癌症之一，其向肝脏的扩散是一种常见、严重且危及生命的并发症。

“我们很高兴能找到一种有前景的候选药物，能够同时对抗结肠癌和肝癌，”薛达西奈胃肠病学女性协会主席、胃肠病学和肝脏病学卡什部门主任、该研究的通讯作者Shelly Lu医学博士表示。“而且由于GIT1在许多癌症中都过度活跃，这项发现可能在癌症治疗中具有广泛而强大的应用。”

下一步是改进该化合物以提高其有效性。

在《实验与临床癌症研究》（Journal of Experimental & Clinical Cancer Research）杂志上发表的相关研究中，薛达西奈癌症中心成员Lu及薛达西奈的研究人员最近揭示了结直肠癌扩散的另一种途径。

他们发现癌细胞释放两种形式的酶MATα2，这两种酶协同作用削弱肝脏的天然防御能力，从而帮助癌细胞扩散到肝脏并在那里存活。这些发现表明，阻断这一过程可能提供另一种有前景的未来治疗方法。
