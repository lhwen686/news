Two new TB vaccines prove safe but fall short on broad protection in India trial

Two new vaccines to prevent tuberculosis (TB) are safe for use in adults and children, but they do not offer protection against all forms of TB, finds a large trial from India published by The BMJ.

TB remains a major global public health concern. In 2023, an estimated 10.8 million people worldwide were reported to have TB and the rate of new cases increased by 4.6%, highlighting the growing scale of the problem. BCG is currently the only licensed vaccine against TB. Yet although it is effective against severe forms of TB in young children, it does not offer protection for adolescents and adults.

New trial of candidate TB vaccines

To address this gap, researchers in India conducted a large trial to evaluate whether two new TB vaccines (VPM1002 and Immuvac) can protect against all forms of tuberculosis (pulmonary and extrapulmonary), prevent latent (dormant) infection, and generate an immune response against the TB bacterium.

The study enrolled 12,717 household contacts (aged 6 years and older) of recently diagnosed TB patients across 18 sites in six Indian states between July 2019 and December 2020.

Participants were randomly allocated to receive a first dose of either VPM1002, Immuvac, or a placebo (4,239 in each group) and were followed up for 38 months. A second dose was administered to 11,829 participants one month later. A total of 12,295 participants (96.7% of those enrolled) completed 38 months of follow-up.

What the vaccines did and didn't do

While neither vaccine offered general protection against TB or prevented latent TB infection, both demonstrated an ability to prevent the progression to active TB in those who developed latent TB.

The researchers found that although both vaccines did not show effectiveness against all TB and pulmonary TB (PTB), one of the vaccines, VPM1002, showed effectiveness (50.4%) against extrapulmonary TB (EPTB) across all age groups, including those aged 36–60 years (79.5%).

These findings suggest a potentially significant public health benefit, because extrapulmonary TB, which affects organs beyond the lungs, is often associated with a higher risk of mortality than pulmonary TB.

Stronger protection seen in children

A promising key finding was the protection seen against TB in children, whereby VPM1002 provided protection against all TB, PTB and EPTB in the 6 to under 14 year age group, while Immuvac provided protection against EPTB only in the 6 to under 10 year age group.

However, neither vaccine protected children and adults who were underweight. This suggests that nutritional support may be needed along with vaccination, especially for younger children, report the authors.

Both vaccines were found to be safe and induced an immune response.

Limitations and next research steps

The researchers acknowledge that the COVID-19 pandemic affected the study, leading to the exclusion of some participants who missed the second dose and sometimes delayed follow-ups. Furthermore, the findings may not apply in other countries or ethnicities.

Nevertheless, this was a large, well-designed study that reflects a real world scenario because it included both children and adults, regardless of pre-existing conditions like diabetes and risk factors, report the authors. Further research on commonly targeted high-risk groups for TB could be undertaken, they conclude.

根据《英国医学杂志》（The BMJ）发表的一项来自印度的大规模试验发现，两种预防结核病（TB）的新疫苗对成人和儿童使用安全，但它们不能提供针对所有形式结核病的保护。

结核病仍然是全球主要的公共卫生问题。2023年，全球估计有1080万人报告患有结核病，新发病例率增加了4.6%，凸显了该问题日益扩大的规模。卡介苗（BCG）是目前唯一获得许可的结核病疫苗。然而，尽管它对幼儿的严重结核病有效，但对青少年和成人却没有保护作用。

结核病候选疫苗的新试验

为弥补这一空白，印度研究人员进行了一项大规模试验，旨在评估两种新型结核病疫苗（VPM1002和Immuvac）是否能预防所有形式的结核病（肺结核和肺外结核），预防潜伏性（休眠性）感染，并对结核杆菌产生免疫应答。

该研究于2019年7月至2020年12月期间，在印度六个邦的18个地点招募了12,717名近期确诊结核病患者的家庭接触者（6岁及以上）。

参与者被随机分配接受第一剂VPM1002、Immuvac或安慰剂（每组4,239人），并随访38个月。一个月后，11,829名参与者接种了第二剂。共有12,295名参与者（占招募总数的96.7%）完成了38个月的随访。

疫苗的作用与局限

虽然这两种疫苗均未提供针对结核病的一般性保护或预防潜伏性结核病感染，但它们都显示出在已发展为潜伏性结核病的人群中，能够预防疾病进展为活动性结核病。

研究人员发现，尽管这两种疫苗对所有结核病和肺结核（PTB）均未显示出有效性，但其中一种疫苗VPM1002对所有年龄组的肺外结核（EPTB）显示出有效性（50.4%），其中包括36-60岁人群（79.5%）。

这些发现预示着潜在的重大公共卫生效益，因为肺外结核影响肺部以外的器官，通常比肺结核伴随更高的死亡风险。

儿童中观察到更强的保护作用

一个有希望的关键发现是儿童中观察到的对结核病的保护作用：VPM1002在6岁至14岁以下年龄组中对所有结核病、肺结核和肺外结核提供了保护，而Immuvac仅在6岁至10岁以下年龄组中对肺外结核提供了保护。

然而，这两种疫苗均未能保护体重过轻的儿童和成人。作者报告称，这表明除了疫苗接种外，可能还需要营养支持，特别是对年幼儿童。

研究发现这两种疫苗均安全并能诱导免疫应答。

局限性与下一步研究

研究人员承认，COVID-19大流行影响了这项研究，导致一些错过了第二剂和有时延迟随访的参与者被排除在外。此外，研究结果可能不适用于其他国家或种族。

尽管如此，作者报告称，这是一项大型、精心设计的试验，反映了真实的临床情景，因为它包括了儿童和成人，无论其是否有糖尿病等既往疾病和风险因素。他们总结道，可以对结核病常见的、高风险目标群体进行进一步研究。
