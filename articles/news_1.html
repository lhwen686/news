研究发现改善溃疡性结肠炎治疗的新靶点

Study identifies new target to improve ulcerative colitis treatments

深度解析

本研究深入揭示了溃疡性结肠炎（UC）发病机制中的一个关键轴线：KARAP-mTOR-线粒体自噬-IL-10。核心专业概念在于巨噬细胞的线粒体质量控制。在UC患者中，KARAP水平升高，导致巨噬细胞线粒体功能受损，表现为活性氧增加、能量代谢效率下降。这种损伤进而抑制了IL-10这种关键抗炎和屏障修复细胞因子的产生。研究发现，KARAP缺失能增强巨噬细胞的线粒体自噬，有效清除受损线粒体，优化线粒体能量代谢，从而提高IL-10的生成，最终促进上皮屏障的修复。 这一发现对UC的行业影响深远。它将KARAP确立为UC治疗的一个潜在的、巨噬细胞靶向的新靶点，特别适用于那些需要超越症状控制、实现深层黏膜愈合的患者。未来的药物研发方向可能包括开发KARAP阻断抗体、小分子抑制剂或局部递送制剂，以在炎症肠道局部发挥作用，同时最大程度减少全身免疫副作用。此外，线粒体质量控制可作为衡量UC治疗效果和疾病进展的新型生物标志物。尽管目前仍处于临床前阶段，但该研究为理解UC病理生理学提供了重要的机制性路线图，并指明了精准医疗策略的潜在方向。

英文原文

Original
Ulcerative colitis (UC) is a chronic, relapsing inflammatory bowel disease in which persistent mucosal inflammation damages the epithelial barrier. Intestinal macrophages help maintain homeostasis, but inflammatory pressure can shift them toward damaging phenotypes. IL-10 is a key repair signal: defects in IL-10 or its receptor can cause severe early-onset enterocolitis, and macrophage-derived IL-10 supports barrier restitution. Macrophage function depends on mitochondrial energy metabolism and quality control, and impaired mitophagy can accumulate damaged mitochondria, raise reactive oxygen species (ROS), and amplify inflammation. KARAP (killer cell activating receptor-associated protein) has been linked to immune-mediated diseases, yet how it controls macrophage mitochondrial fitness and IL-10 in UC remained unclear. Given these challenges, in-depth research is needed to define how KARAP regulates macrophage mitochondrial quality control and IL-10 production in UC. Published (DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag027) in Precision Clinical Medicine on 25 September 2026, this study from researchers at the Affiliated Hospital of Jining Medical University and Jining Medical University examined human UC samples and a DSS-induced mouse colitis model. The team combined Tyrobp−/− mice (KARAP-deficient), macrophage RNA sequencing, mitochondrial and mitophagy assays, and macrophage-epithelial cocultures with functional barrier readouts to test whether KARAP controls barrier repair through IL-10. The work links patient observations to mechanistic experiments. In patient peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs), TYROBP mRNA was higher in UC than in healthy controls and correlated positively with C-reactive protein (CRP) and erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR). KARAP was especially high in CD14⁺ (cluster of differentiation 14-positive) monocytes/macrophages and in inflamed colonic tissue, where it colocalized with F4/80⁺ macrophages. In DSS-treated mice, KARAP deficiency reduced weight loss, colon shortening, inflammatory infiltration, and serum interleukin-6 (IL-6), interleukin-1β (IL-1β), and tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α), while increasing tight-junction proteins zonula occludens-1 (ZO-1) and Occludin. Macrophage RNA sequencing showed upregulated cytokine pathways; interleukin-22 (IL-22) rose, but IL-10 showed the strongest difference. Tyrobp−/− macrophages produced more IL-10. Mitochondrial mass was unchanged, yet MitoSOX signals fell, membrane potential was better preserved, and adenosine triphosphate (ATP) and nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD⁺) levels rose. Seahorse analysis showed higher basal respiration, maximal respiration, and spare respiratory capacity. Mitophagy was enhanced, with increased microtubule-associated protein 1 light chain 3 alpha/beta-II (LC3A/B-II), reduced P62, increased PTEN-induced kinase 1 (PINK1), and lower AKT (protein kinase B)/mTOR phosphorylation. The mTOR activator MHY1485 reversed these mitochondrial and IL-10 benefits. In cocultures, Tyrobp−/− macrophages increased epithelial ZO-1/Occludin and transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER) and reduced permeability; anti-IL-10 antibody abolished these effects. These data connect clinical KARAP elevation to macrophage metabolic and repair defects. The authors said the findings place KARAP at a practical crossroads between immune signaling and macrophage metabolism. Guangxi Zhou, a corresponding author, said blocking KARAP may help macrophages keep healthier mitochondria, produce more IL-10, and support epithelial repair during colitis. They cautioned that the evidence is preclinical and that human studies will be needed to confirm safety and efficacy. They said the next step is to test whether this pathway can be targeted selectively in inflamed gut tissue without broadly disrupting immune defense. The study suggests KARAP could become a macrophage-directed target for UC, especially for patients who need better mucosal healing beyond symptom control. A KARAP-mTOR-mitophagy-IL-10 axis may also help explain why some inflamed guts fail to restore the epithelial barrier. Practical applications could include KARAP-blocking antibodies, small molecules, or locally delivered agents designed to act in the inflamed colon while limiting systemic immune effects. Before clinical translation, researchers will need human tissue validation, dose and safety studies, and trials measuring endoscopic healing, barrier function, and durable remission. For now, the findings offer a mechanistic roadmap rather than a ready-made therapy. They also point to mitochondrial quality control as a measurable readout for future trials.

AI Translation
溃疡性结肠炎 (UC) 是一种慢性复发性炎症性肠病，其中持续性黏膜炎症损害上皮屏障。肠道巨噬细胞有助于维持稳态，但炎症压力可使其转向有害表型。IL-10 是关键的修复信号：IL-10 或其受体的缺陷可导致严重的早发性肠结肠炎，而巨噬细胞衍生的 IL-10 支持屏障恢复。巨噬细胞功能依赖于线粒体能量代谢和质量控制，线粒体自噬受损可导致受损线粒体积累，增加活性氧 (ROS)，并放大炎症。KARAP (杀伤细胞激活受体相关蛋白) 已被证实与免疫介导疾病相关，但其如何在 UC 中控制巨噬细胞线粒体健康和 IL-10 仍不清楚。鉴于这些挑战，需要深入研究以阐明 KARAP 如何调节 UC 中巨噬细胞线粒体质量控制和 IL-10 产生。 这项研究由济宁医学院附属医院和济宁医学院的研究人员于 2026 年 9 月 25 日发表在《精准临床医学》杂志 (DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag027) 上，检查了人类 UC 样本和 DSS 诱导的小鼠结肠炎模型。该团队结合了 Tyrobp−/− 小鼠 (KARAP 缺陷型)、巨噬细胞 RNA 测序、线粒体和线粒体自噬测定以及带有功能性屏障读数的巨噬细胞-上皮细胞共培养模型，以测试 KARAP 是否通过 IL-10 控制屏障修复。该研究将患者观察与机制实验联系起来。 在患者外周血单核细胞 (PBMC) 中，UC 患者的 TYROBP mRNA 水平高于健康对照组，并与 C 反应蛋白 (CRP) 和红细胞沉降率 (ESR) 呈正相关。KARAP 在 CD14⁺ (分化簇 14 阳性) 单核细胞/巨噬细胞和炎症结肠组织中特别高，并与 F4/80⁺ 巨噬细胞共定位。在 DSS 处理的小鼠中，KARAP 缺陷减轻了体重减轻、结肠缩短、炎症浸润以及血清白细胞介素-6 (IL-6)、白细胞介素-1β (IL-1β) 和肿瘤坏死因子-α (TNF-α) 水平，同时增加了紧密连接蛋白 zonula occludens-1 (ZO-1) 和 Occludin。巨噬细胞 RNA 测序显示细胞因子通路上调；白细胞介素-22 (IL-22) 升高，但 IL-10 显示出最显著的差异。 Tyrobp−/− 巨噬细胞产生更多 IL-10。线粒体质量未改变，但 MitoSOX 信号下降，膜电位得到更好维持，三磷酸腺苷 (ATP) 和烟酰胺腺嘌呤二核苷酸 (NAD⁺) 水平升高。Seahorse 分析显示基础呼吸、最大呼吸和呼吸储备能力更高。线粒体自噬增强，表现为微管相关蛋白 1 轻链 3 alpha/beta-II (LC3A/B-II) 增加、P62 减少、PTEN 诱导激酶 1 (PINK1) 增加以及 AKT (蛋白激酶 B)/mTOR 磷酸化水平降低。mTOR 激活剂 MHY1485 逆转了这些线粒体和 IL-10 的益处。在共培养中，Tyrobp−/− 巨噬细胞增加了上皮 ZO-1/Occludin 和跨上皮电阻 (TEER)，并降低了渗透性；抗 IL-10 抗体消除了这些作用。这些数据将临床 KARAP 升高与巨噬细胞代谢和修复缺陷联系起来。 作者表示，这些发现将 KARAP 置于免疫信号传导和巨噬细胞代谢之间的实用交叉点。通讯作者周广西表示，阻断 KARAP 可能有助于巨噬细胞维持更健康的线粒体，产生更多 IL-10，并在结肠炎期间支持上皮修复。他们警告说，现有证据仍处于临床前阶段，需要进行人体研究来确认安全性和有效性。他们表示下一步是测试该通路是否可以在炎症肠道组织中选择性靶向，而不会广泛破坏免疫防御。 该研究表明 KARAP 有望成为 UC 的巨噬细胞靶向治疗目标，特别是对于那些除了控制症状外还需要更好黏膜愈合的患者。KARAP-mTOR-线粒体自噬-IL-10 轴也可能有助于解释为何某些炎症肠道无法恢复上皮屏障。实际应用可能包括 KARAP 阻断抗体、小分子药物或局部递送的制剂，旨在作用于炎症结肠同时限制全身性免疫效应。在临床转化之前，研究人员需要进行人体组织验证、剂量和安全性研究，以及衡量内镜愈合、屏障功能和持久缓解的临床试验。目前，这些发现提供了一个机制路线图，而非现成的疗法。它们也指出线粒体质量控制可作为未来试验的可测量指标。
