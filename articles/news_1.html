2023年至2024年医学影像周转时间增加

Imaging turnaround time increased from 2023 to 2024

深度解析

该研究深入揭示了美国门诊放射学报告周转时间持续延长的严峻趋势。影像学检查周转时间，即从影像采集到最终放射科医师报告解读所耗费的时间，是衡量诊断效率和患者护理质量的关键指标。其显著延长，尤其是在2021至2024年间高达177%的增幅，直接预示着多层面的临床和行业挑战。 在临床层面，周转时间的延长可能导致诊断延迟，尤其对于急危重症患者，这可能贻误最佳治疗时机，影响患者预后，甚至增加发病率和死亡率。同时，患者等待报告的焦虑感会加剧，降低医疗服务满意度。对于转诊医生而言，报告的迟滞阻碍了其及时制定诊疗计划和管理患者病程，影响了整体医疗流程的顺畅性。 从行业角度看，这一趋势凸显了放射科医生人力资源短缺的日益严峻。报告指出，放射科医生已供不应求，而影像检查需求却不断增长，这种供需失衡对现有和未来放射科医生队伍造成巨大压力。这不仅考验着医疗系统的承载能力，也催生了对创新解决方案的迫切需求，例如人工智能辅助诊断系统和工作流优化工具，以提高解读效率并缓解人力压力。此外，研究结果也对医疗政策制定者提出了警示，需重新审视医生培养、招聘和留用策略，以确保诊断放射学的可持续发展和高质量医疗服务的可及性。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Imaging turnaround times continued to increase across modalities through 2024, according to a study published online Aug. 3 in Journal of the American College of Radiology. Alexandra R. Drake, M.P.H., from the Harvey L. Neiman Health Policy Institute in Reston, Va., and colleagues updated national outpatient radiologic report turnaround time trends, using data from a 5% sample of Medicare fee-for-service beneficiaries from 2014 to 2024. Based on more than 2.87 million technical imaging claims matched with professional claims within 30 days, the researchers found that most claims were for radiography/fluoroscopy (XR; 59.7%), followed by computed tomography (CT; 16.0%), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI; 13.0%) and ultrasound (11.4%). Across the study period, turnaround times were 0.091 days in 2014, followed by a slight increase to 0.104 days in 2021. Thereafter, turnaround times increased each year, reaching 0.251 days in 2024. Overall, turnaround times increased 177% from 2021 to 2024, with 92.0% of the total increase occurring from 2022 to 2024 (12.3% in 2022, 46.2% in 2023 and 33.4% in 2024). Turnaround times increased from 2023 to 2024 across all imaging modalities (49.1% for ultrasound, 35.4% for XR, 11.6% for CT and 4.5% for MRI). While most imaging studies were interpreted on the same day (96.7% during the full study period), the share interpreted one or more calendar days after imaging acquisition increased from 1.97% in 2014 to 7.39% in 2024, a 3.8-fold increase. Similarly, the share of imaging studies interpreted seven or more calendar days after acquisition increased from 0.41% in 2014 to 0.87% in 2024, a 2.1-fold increase. While the increase in mean turnaround time from 2023 to 2024 was smaller than the increase from 2022 to 2023, both overall and by modality, turnaround times still increased. "With radiologists already in short supply, projected shortages in physician supply and rising imaging utilization, these results continue to raise concerns about the ability of the current and future radiologist workforce to meet the growing imaging demand in a timely manner," the authors write. Publication details Alexandra R. Drake et al, National Turnaround Time Trends for Medicare Fee-for-Service Beneficiaries: Updated Through 2024, Journal of the American College of Radiology (2026).
