深度解析

该研究深入揭示了皮肤纤维化，包括肥厚性瘢痕、瘢痕疙瘩和系统性硬化症，其核心在于骨膜素阳性（POSTN+）成纤维细胞的异常激活。文章识别出RUNX家族转录因子1（RUNX1）是这些致病性成纤维细胞中的关键上游调控因子，并通过RUNX1-ATF4-POSTN/MDK信号轴阐明了其分子机制。RUNX1通过激活ATF4增强细胞对内质网（ER）应激的适应能力并提升对铁死亡的抵抗力，这解释了纤维化环境中细胞的持久性；同时，通过MDK促进血管重塑，加剧纤维化进程。这一发现将RUNX1从单纯的细胞激活标志物提升为控制细胞存活、应激适应和微环境重塑的关键调控因子。在行业影响方面，该研究为皮肤纤维化提供了新的生物标志物和治疗靶点。鉴于转录因子难以直接成药，研究通过虚拟筛选发现小檗碱（BBR）能抑制RUNX1/POSTN相关通路，并在动物模型中显示出显著的抗瘢痕效果。此外，腺相关病毒（AAV9）介导的RUNX1基因沉默也证实了其治疗潜力。这些成果预示着针对RUNX1-ATF4-POSTN/MDK轴的新型抗纤维化药物和基因疗法的开发前景，为临床转化提供了坚实基础，尽管仍需解决细胞特异性靶向、安全性和剂量等挑战。

英文原文

Original
Skin fibrosis includes hypertrophic scar (HTS), keloid scar (KS), and systemic sclerosis (SSc). These conditions share persistent fibroblast activation, excessive extracellular matrix (ECM) deposition, and progressive skin remodeling. Single-cell studies have shown that fibroblasts are not one uniform population, but the shared pathogenic states and upstream regulators across skin fibrosis remain unclear. Periostin-positive (POSTN+) fibroblasts are emerging as an activated state in several organs, yet their role in skin fibrosis and the mechanisms that keep them pathogenic are not well defined. Fibrotic skin also imposes endoplasmic reticulum (ER) stress, oxidative stress, and proteostatic burden. Because of these challenges, further research is needed to define the upstream regulators and therapeutic vulnerabilities of POSTN+ fibroblasts in skin fibrosis. The study was conducted by researchers at the Department of Plastic Surgery, Xijing Hospital, Fourth Military Medical University, Xi'an, China, and published (DOI: 10.1093/burnst/tkag063) in Burns & Trauma on 18 September 2026. Using single-cell RNA sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, cleavage under targets and tagmentation (CUT&Tag), RNA sequencing, and animal models, the team studied RUNX family transcription factor 1 (RUNX1) in POSTN+ fibroblasts. They reported that RUNX1 silencing reduced skin fibrosis in mice, while berberine (BBR) reduced scar formation in a rabbit ear model. The researchers found a RUNX1-high POSTN+ fibroblast population that expanded in HTS and KS, with similar signals in SSc. RUNX1 directly activated ATF4 (activating transcription factor 4), POSTN (periostin), and MDK (midkine). Through ATF4, it strengthened ER stress adaptation by increasing BiP (binding immunoglobulin protein), phosphorylated PERK (protein kinase RNA-like endoplasmic reticulum kinase), and phosphorylated eIF2α (eukaryotic translation initiation factor 2 alpha). It also raised SLC7A11 (solute carrier family 7 member 11) and GPX4 (glutathione peroxidase 4) protein levels, lowered lipid reactive oxygen species (ROS), Fe²⁺, and malondialdehyde (MDA), and maintained glutathione (GSH), helping cells avoid ferroptosis. Overexpression of ATF4 partially reversed the effects of RUNX1 loss. Through MDK, these fibroblasts promoted endothelial migration, proliferation, and tube formation; recombinant MDK restored these functions after RUNX1 silencing. Virtual screening prioritized BBR, which suppressed RUNX1/POSTN-related stress adaptation and profibrotic markers. In mice, AAV9 (adeno-associated virus serotype 9)-mediated RUNX1 silencing reduced dermal thickness, collagen deposition, and POSTN, alpha-smooth muscle actin (α-SMA), ATF4, and GPX4 signals. In rabbit ear scars, BBR reduced scar thickness, collagen, and α-SMA. The authors said the findings reframe RUNX1 as more than a marker of fibroblast activation. They said it appears to sit upstream of a stress-adaptive program that helps pathogenic POSTN+ fibroblasts survive ER stress and ferroptosis while reshaping the surrounding niche. They said the RUNX1-ATF4-POSTN/MDK axis may explain why these cells persist and promote vascular changes in scars. They also said BBR's effects support this fibroblast state as a candidate therapeutic vulnerability, while cautioning that cell-specific targeting is still needed. The study points to RUNX1-high POSTN+ fibroblasts as a shared feature of HTS, KS, and SSc, with potential value as a biomarker and drug target. Because transcription factors are difficult to drug directly, the authors turned to POSTN and identified BBR as a candidate compound predicted to bind it. BBR reduced profibrotic and stress-adaptive features in hypertrophic scar fibroblasts (HTSFs) and reduced scar formation in a rabbit ear model. AAV9-mediated RUNX1 silencing also attenuated bleomycin-induced skin fibrosis and scar formation in mice. Before clinical translation, however, fibroblast-specific targeting, safety, dosing, and human validation are needed. The work supports further testing of RUNX1/POSTN-directed strategies in skin fibrosis. Source: Chinese Academy of Sciences Journal reference: Song, B., et al. (2026). RUNX1 links ATF4-associated stress adaptation to microenvironmental remodeling in POSTN+ fibroblasts during skin fibrosis. Burns & Trauma. 皮肤纤维化包括肥厚性瘢痕（HTS）、瘢痕疙瘩（KS）和系统性硬化症（SSc）。这些病症共同表现为成纤维细胞持续激活、细胞外基质（ECM）过度沉积和进行性皮肤重塑。单细胞研究表明，成纤维细胞并非单一均匀的细胞群，但皮肤纤维化中共同的致病状态和上游调控因子仍不明确。骨膜素阳性（POSTN+）成纤维细胞正作为多种器官中的一种活化状态而逐渐显现，但它们在皮肤纤维化中的作用以及维持其致病性的机制尚未明确。纤维化皮肤还会带来内质网（ER）应激、氧化应激和蛋白稳态负荷。鉴于这些挑战，需要进一步研究以明确皮肤纤维化中POSTN+成纤维细胞的上游调控因子和治疗靶点。 这项研究由中国西安第四军医大学西京医院整形外科的研究人员进行，并于2026年9月18日在《烧伤与创伤》（DOI: 10.1093/burnst/tkag063）上发表。研究团队利用单细胞RNA测序、空间转录组学、靶点切割与转座（CUT&Tag）、RNA测序和动物模型，对POSTN+成纤维细胞中的RUNX家族转录因子1（RUNX1）进行了研究。他们报告称，RUNX1沉默可减轻小鼠皮肤纤维化，而小檗碱（BBR）则可减少兔耳模型中的瘢痕形成。 研究人员发现了一个RUNX1高表达的POSTN+成纤维细胞群，该细胞群在HTS和KS中增殖，并在SSc中也显示出类似信号。RUNX1直接激活活化转录因子4（ATF4）、骨膜素（POSTN）和中间调理素（MDK）。通过ATF4，它通过增加结合免疫球蛋白蛋白（BiP）、磷酸化蛋白激酶RNA样内质网激酶（PERK）和磷酸化真核翻译起始因子2α（eIF2α）来增强内质网应激适应能力。它还提高了溶质载体家族7成员11（SLC7A11）和谷胱甘肽过氧化物酶4（GPX4）的蛋白水平，降低了脂质活性氧（ROS）、Fe²⁺和丙二醛（MDA），并维持了谷胱甘肽（GSH），从而帮助细胞避免铁死亡。ATF4的过表达部分逆转了RUNX1缺失的影响。通过MDK，这些成纤维细胞促进了内皮细胞迁移、增殖和管腔形成；在RUNX1沉默后，重组MDK恢复了这些功能。虚拟筛选优先确定了小檗碱（BBR），其抑制了RUNX1/POSTN相关的应激适应和促纤维化标志物。在小鼠中，腺相关病毒血清型9（AAV9）介导的RUNX1沉默降低了真皮厚度、胶原沉积以及POSTN、α-平滑肌肌动蛋白（α-SMA）、ATF4和GPX4信号。在兔耳瘢痕中，BBR降低了瘢痕厚度、胶原和α-SMA。 作者表示，这些发现重新定义了RUNX1，使其不仅仅是成纤维细胞激活的标志物。他们认为，RUNX1似乎位于一个应激适应程序的上游，该程序帮助致病性POSTN+成纤维细胞在内质网应激和铁死亡中存活，同时重塑周围微环境。他们还指出，RUNX1-ATF4-POSTN/MDK轴可能解释了这些细胞为何持续存在并促进瘢痕中的血管变化。此外，他们认为小檗碱的作用支持了这种成纤维细胞状态作为潜在的治疗靶点，但同时提醒仍需要进行细胞特异性靶向治疗。 该研究指出RUNX1高表达的POSTN+成纤维细胞是HTS、KS和SSc的共同特征，具有作为生物标志物和药物靶点的潜在价值。由于转录因子难以直接作为药物靶点，作者转而关注POSTN，并确定小檗碱（BBR）是一种预测能与其结合的候选化合物。小檗碱降低了肥厚性瘢痕成纤维细胞（HTSFs）的促纤维化和应激适应特征，并减少了兔耳模型中的瘢痕形成。AAV9介导的RUNX1沉默也减弱了小鼠博来霉素诱导的皮肤纤维化和瘢痕形成。然而，在临床转化之前，仍需要进行成纤维细胞特异性靶向、安全性、剂量和人体验证。这项工作支持进一步测试针对RUNX1/POSTN的皮肤纤维化治疗策略。 来源：中国科学院 期刊引用：Song, B., et al.
