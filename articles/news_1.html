深度解析

这篇新闻报道介绍了一项针对孕妇和分娩人群围产期焦虑的数字疗法RELAX的临床试验成果。其核心专业概念在于利用认知行为疗法（CBT）原理干预“反复消极思维”（RNT），从而预防低水平焦虑升级。RNT是一种跨诊断过程，被认为是焦虑和抑郁的核心症状及风险因素。RELAX通过提供结构化的在线情境，训练参与者对模棱两可的日常事件形成更积极的解读，从根本上改变了导致焦虑的认知偏差。这种自主指导的数字疗法显著降低了产前产后焦虑和抑郁水平，并有效阻断了RNT对焦虑的维持和加剧作用。 该研究的行业影响深远。首先，它解决了围产期精神健康领域一个巨大的未满足临床需求，即许多未确诊但受焦虑困扰的孕产妇缺乏及时有效的精神卫生保健支持。RELAX的数字形式极大提升了医疗服务可及性，突破了地理、时间限制和传统就医带来的病耻感。其次，作为一种早期预防性干预，它有望降低早产、低出生体重以及儿童期心理障碍等不良母婴结局的风险，减轻长期医疗负担。未来，RELAX有望整合到常规产科护理中，作为传统服务的有力补充，实现更广泛、更成本效益的母婴心理健康支持。目前向LENS4Pregnancy应用的转化，预示着该技术有望实现大规模临床应用和商业化，推动数字健康在围产期医学领域的发展。

英文原文

Original
Pregnant women or birthing people with undiagnosed anxiety could benefit from a new digital therapy, according to a clinical trial led by King's College London. Published in JAMA Network Open, the results from the REducing Levels of AnXiety (RELAX) study have shown that a self-led digital therapy significantly reduced participants' pre-and post-birth anxiety compared to participants who received usual care. The study was funded by the National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR) and delivered through the NIHR Biomedical Research Centre: Maudsley. The trial also found that, compared to usual care, the RELAX digital therapy lowered levels of depression before and after birth, as well as levels of repetitive negative thinking (RNT) - a risk factor and symptom that is central to anxiety and that the therapy targeted. Building on previous research which has shown that this digital therapy approach can be effective in helping those with high levels of anxiety, this was the first time this approach has been trialled in preventing low levels of anxiety escalating. Anxiety disorders affect 1 in 5 pregnant women or birthing people. Research has shown that this perinatal anxiety increases during pregnancy and can have adverse consequences such as increased likelihood of preterm birth, lower birth weight, reduced maternal responsiveness to infants and risk of children developing psychological disorders. Lead researcher on the study, Professor Colette Hirsch, Professor in Cognitive Clinical Psychology, King's College London and Deputy Theme Lead for Digital Therapies at NIHR BRC: Maudsley said: "We know there is a large unmet need amongst those women who experience anxiety before and after birth, but who do not have a diagnosis and who often do not receive mental healthcare support. If left untreated, this anxiety can lead to further challenges for parent and child. "Our digital therapy aims to address the symptom of repetitive negative thinking where people constantly consider the negative outcome of situations, and which has been shown to predict and maintain anxiety. This is the first study to show that a digital therapy addressing this type of thinking prevents the increase of anxiety during pregnancy. Integrating RELAX into maternity care for those with, or at risk of increasing anxiety has the potential to improve maternal mental health and by taking a digital approach we can reach a large number of people." REducing Levels of AnXiety (RELAX) is a multi-session online intervention co-created by psychologists, researchers, clinicians and people with lived and professional experience of perinatal anxiety. One study participant who received the RELAX training said: "I found that's had quite a big impact on me actually…I'm finding that I'm coping much better with the worries that I am having...So, for example, we went for our 20-week scan… I was actually quite excited about the scan. And previously, scans have been a real trigger for me." The randomized clinical trial (RCT) aimed to test whether adding RELAX to usual care prevents anxiety increasing during pregnancy and after birth for people with high repetitive negative thinking and up to moderate anxiety, compared to usual care alone. Usual care involves maternity services monitoring, potentially contact from a health visitor, and/or being offered information on self-referral to psychological services. There were 268 participants in total and 134 were allocated to the group who received RELAX training which consisted of 12 brief online RELAX sessions completed on a laptop, or tablet over four weeks and they also continued usual care. Each session involved listening to 30 scenarios. Scenarios targeted worry and rumination and provided examples of everyday ambiguous situations that could be interpreted in either a positive/neutral or negative manner. Scenarios were either pregnancy-related or based on common everyday situations which pregnant women would encounter. The intervention trained them to generate more positive interpretations, in keeping with people who do not suffer from anxiety or repetitive negative thinking. Study participants were 16 – 28 'weeks' gestation, over 18 and had reported high traits of repetitive negative thinking and up to moderate levels of anxiety. They were only accepted for the study if they had no current psychiatric diagnosis or risk of suicide. They were recruited from maternity services or the community and completed screening questionnaires on the study website and were then assessed through screening calls with the study team and measuring anxiety using the Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7(GAD-7) score. People were then randomly allocated to the RELAX sessions plus usual care or to assessments plus usual care only. 81.3% of the participants in the RELAX + usual care group completed at least 10 of the 12 sessions. The majority of participants were positive about their experience of using RELAX and said they would recommend using it to others. Pregnancy can be such a cherished and critical time, but it is also a time when everyday situations can cause worry and anxiety. As a digital therapy that can be used by pregnant and birthing people in their own homes, RELAX has the potential to prevent this anxiety escalating when it is still manageable, aligning with many of aspects of the NHS 10-year plan and providing much-needed support to this group." Professor Colette Hirsch, Professor in Cognitive Clinical Psychology, King's College London Following stakeholder and user feedback, RELAX is now being developed into an app called LENS4Pregnancy. The study team is working with people with lived experience of anxiety, during and after pregnancy, clinicians and policymakers, charities and health service providers to develop implementation plans. The RELAX study was funded by the Efficacy and Mechanism Award, a UKRI Medical Research Council (MRC) and National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR) partnership and delivered through the NIHR Biomedical Research Centre: Maudsley.

根据伦敦国王学院领导的一项临床试验，未确诊焦虑症的孕妇或分娩人群可能受益于一种新的数字疗法。 发表在《美国医学会杂志》开放网络版（JAMA Network Open）上的缓解焦虑水平（RELAX）研究结果显示，与接受常规护理的参与者相比，一项自主指导的数字疗法显著降低了参与者产前和产后的焦虑水平。这项研究由国家健康与护理研究所（NIHR）资助，并通过NIHR莫兹利生物医学研究中心（NIHR Biomedical Research Centre: Maudsley）实施。 该试验还发现，与常规护理相比，RELAX数字疗法降低了产前和产后的抑郁水平，以及反复消极思维（RNT）的水平——RNT是焦虑的核心风险因素和症状，也是该疗法所针对的目标。 这项研究是在此前已有研究表明该数字疗法方法能有效帮助高水平焦虑人群的基础上进行的，但这是首次尝试将此方法应用于预防低水平焦虑升级。 焦虑症影响着五分之一的孕妇或分娩人群。研究表明，这种围产期焦虑在怀孕期间会加剧，并可能导致不良后果，例如早产几率增加、婴儿出生体重降低、母亲对婴儿的反应性减弱以及儿童患心理障碍的风险。 该研究的首席研究员、伦敦国王学院认知临床心理学教授兼NIHR莫兹利生物医学研究中心数字疗法副主题负责人Colette Hirsch教授表示：“我们知道，许多产前和产后经历焦虑但未获诊断且常常得不到精神卫生保健支持的女性存在巨大的未满足需求。如果得不到治疗，这种焦虑可能会给父母和孩子带来进一步的挑战。” “我们的数字疗法旨在解决反复消极思维这一症状，即人们不断考虑情境的负面结果，而这已被证明可以预测并维持焦虑。这是第一项研究表明针对此类思维的数字疗法能有效预防怀孕期间焦虑的加剧。将RELAX整合到针对有焦虑风险或焦虑水平正在上升的产妇护理中，有潜力改善母亲的精神健康，并且通过数字方法，我们可以惠及大量人群。” 缓解焦虑水平（RELAX）是一个多疗程在线干预项目，由心理学家、研究人员、临床医生以及有围产期焦虑亲身经历和专业经验的人共同创建。 一位接受RELAX训练的研究参与者表示：“我发现它对我产生了相当大的影响……我发现我处理担忧的能力大大提高了……例如，我们去做了20周的超声检查……我实际上对这次检查感到非常兴奋。而以前，超声检查对我来说是一个真正的触发因素。” 这项随机临床试验（RCT）旨在检验，对于具有高度反复消极思维和中度以下焦虑的人群，与单独的常规护理相比，在常规护理中增加RELAX是否能预防怀孕期间和产后焦虑的加剧。常规护理包括产科服务监测、社区健康访视员的潜在联系，以及/或提供自我转介至心理服务的信息。 共有268名参与者，其中134人被分配到接受RELAX训练的组，该组在四周内通过笔记本电脑或平板电脑完成了12次简短的在线RELAX疗程，同时他们也继续接受常规护理。每个疗程都包括听取30种情境。这些情境旨在解决担忧和反刍思维，并提供日常生活中模棱两可的情境示例，这些情境可以被解读为积极/中性或消极。情境要么与怀孕相关，要么基于孕妇可能遇到的常见日常情境。该干预措施训练她们形成更积极的解读，使其与不受焦虑或反复消极思维困扰的人保持一致。 研究参与者为妊娠16至28周、年龄超过18岁，并报告有高度反复消极思维特质和中度以下焦虑水平的人群。他们只有在没有当前精神疾病诊断或自杀风险的情况下才被接受参加研究。他们从产科服务或社区招募，并在研究网站上完成了筛查问卷，然后通过与研究团队的筛查电话进行评估，并使用广泛性焦虑障碍量表-7（GAD-7）评分测量焦虑水平。之后，这些人被随机分配到RELAX疗程加常规护理组或仅进行评估加常规护理组。 在RELAX加常规护理组中，81.3%的参与者完成了至少12个疗程中的10个。大多数参与者对使用RELAX的体验持积极态度，并表示会向他人推荐使用。 “怀孕可能是一个珍贵而关键的时期，但同时也是日常情境可能引起担忧和焦虑的时期。作为一种可在孕妇和分娩人群家中使用的数字疗法，RELAX有潜力在焦虑尚可控时阻止其升级，这与英国国民医疗服务体系十年计划的诸多方面相契合，并为这一群体提供了急需的支持。”伦敦国王学院认知临床心理学教授Colette Hirsch教授说。 根据利益相关者和用户反馈，RELAX目前正被开发成一款名为LENS4Pregnancy的应用程序。研究团队正与有孕期和产后焦虑亲身经历的人、临床医生、政策制定者、慈善机构和卫生服务提供商合作，制定实施计划。 RELAX研究由英国研究与创新局医学研究理事会（MRC）和国家健康与护理研究所（NIHR）合作的“功效与机制奖”资助，并通过NIHR莫兹利生物医学研究中心实施。
