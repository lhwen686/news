荟萃分析表明，司美格鲁肽在65岁以上老年人中显示出良好疗效

Pooled analysis reveals semaglutide shows good efficacy in older adults aged over 65 years

这篇新闻报道的核心在于司美格鲁肽，一种胰高血糖素样肽-1（GLP-1）受体激动剂，在老年肥胖患者中的应用。GLP-1受体激动剂通过模拟人体自然产生的GLP-1激素来发挥作用，主要机制包括：延迟胃排空，增加饱腹感，从而减少食物摄入；同时，它还能刺激胰岛素分泌（仅在血糖升高时），抑制胰高血糖素分泌，从而改善血糖控制。这项研究的价值在于，它首次证实了司美格鲁肽在65岁以上这一脆弱且合并症高发的特殊群体中，依然保持了与年轻群体相似的良好减重效果和可接受的安全性。

这项研究具有重要的临床和行业影响。从临床角度看，它为老年肥胖患者提供了重要的治疗依据，有助于医生更安全、有效地管理这类患者的体重和相关心血管代谢风险。此前，由于老年人常伴随多重合并症和药物相互作用风险，其用药选择相对有限。从行业角度看，该研究进一步巩固了司美格鲁肽作为减肥药物的市场地位，并可能推动制药公司投入更多资源研发针对老年人群的特定配方或剂量调整方案，以满足日益增长的老年健康需求。同时，通过降低肥胖相关疾病的发生率，长远来看也有助于减轻全球医疗系统的负担。

A new analysis of the STEP trials carried out by semaglutide manufacturer Novo Nordisk has analyzed various trials to show the safety and efficacy of the obesity drug semaglutide in older adults (over 65 years), and found similar efficacy and safety as in the general trial populations. The study is by Prof Luca Busetto from the University of Padova in Italy and colleagues, including those from Novo Nordisk.

Individuals of advanced age with obesity represent a vulnerable group, often presenting with comorbidities and frailty, and being at risk of adverse events (AEs). Information on the use of glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists, including semaglutide, in this population, is limited; therefore, the authors decided to assess the efficacy and safety of once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg in individuals aged 65 years and over.

The analysis pooled together data from the STEP 1, 3, 4, 5, 8 and 9 trials (only in people with obesity or overweight, not diabetes, because weight loss in obesity drug trials is always lower in people with diabetes than without—thus results cannot be compared or mixed).

It included participants aged ≥65 years with a body mass index of at least 30 kg/m², or at least 27 kg/m², and at least one obesity-related complication (without diabetes) who were randomized to receive semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo. All participants received lifestyle intervention and, in STEP 3, intensive behavioral therapy.

Endpoints were assessed from baseline to week 68 and included percentage change in body weight; proportion of participants achieving categorical body weight reductions (≥10%, ≥15%, ≥20%); change in waist circumference; proportion of participants achieving waist to height ratio (WHtR) <0.53; shift in BMI category; and changes in cardiometabolic risk factors (glucose parameters, blood pressure, serum lipids and hs-CRP). Adverse events (AEs) were also assessed.

Of the total population in the selected trials (N=4523), 358 participants (8%) were aged 65 years or older and included in the analysis (semaglutide 2.4 mg, n=248; placebo, n=110), with most (90%) being aged 65–74 years (and the others 75 years and over).

Across the pooled semaglutide and placebo groups at baseline, mean age was 69 years, bodyweight was 99.0 kg, BMI was 36.6 kg/m² and waist circumference was 115 cm; 72% were female.

At week 68, there was a mean −15.4% change in body weight for semaglutide 2.4 mg vs. −5.1% for placebo, and a mean −14.3 cm vs. −6.0 cm change in waist circumference, respectively. Proportions of participants achieving body weight reduction thresholds in the semaglutide 2.4 mg vs. placebo groups were 66.5% vs. 15.5% (at least 10%), 46.8% vs. 6.4% (at least 15%) and 28.6% vs. 2.7% (at least 20%), respectively.

In the semaglutide 2.4 mg group, 11.3% achieved a WHtR <0.53, compared with 4.5% with placebo. A greater proportion of semaglutide-treated participants improved their BMI category from baseline to week 68 compared with placebo.

A BMI of <27 kg/m² (so-called healthy weight) was achieved by 27.0% of participants in the semaglutide group vs. 5.5% in the placebo group; and the proportion of elderly patients in the overweight and obesity class I, II and III categories all fell in the semaglutide group at week 68 due to the increase in participants who had reached a healthy weight.

For participants achieving both a BMI of 27 or less and a WHtR of <0.53, these values were 10.5% vs. 2.7%, respectively. Greater improvements in cardiometabolic risk factors were observed with semaglutide 2.4 mg vs. placebo, including blood pressure, blood fats, cholesterol and glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c—a measure of blood sugar control used in diagnosis of diabetes).

Proportions of participants experiencing AEs and serious AEs in the semaglutide 2.4 mg vs. placebo groups were similar for AEs overall (89.1% vs. 84.5%), but higher for semaglutide re: serious AEs—19.0% vs. 12.7%, respectively.

Constipation and dizziness rates (known side effects of this class of drug) were higher with semaglutide, while fractures and hypoglycemia were comparable to placebo, both affecting less than 1% in each group.

Dr. Busetto concludes, "In this analysis of individuals with obesity aged 65 years and older, semaglutide reduced body weight and improved cardiometabolic risk factors compared with placebo, and the safety and efficacy profile of semaglutide was consistent with that reported in the STEP program."

He adds, "In many countries, including many high-income countries, the majority of the cases of excess weight actually occur in adults aged 65 years and over, representing a major driver for obesity-related complications and an important cause of reduced quality of life and disability. Our results support the use of semaglutide in this patient group."

诺和诺德（司美格鲁肽生产商）对STEP试验进行的一项新分析，通过对多项试验的汇总分析，显示了肥胖症药物司美格鲁肽在老年人（65岁以上）中的安全性和有效性，并发现其疗效和安全性与一般试验人群相似。这项研究由意大利帕多瓦大学的卢卡·布塞托教授及其同事（包括来自诺和诺德的同事）共同完成。

患有肥胖症的高龄个体代表一个脆弱群体，他们常伴有合并症和衰弱，并且面临不良事件（AEs）的风险。关于胰高血糖素样肽-1（GLP-1）受体激动剂（包括司美格鲁肽）在此类人群中使用的信息有限；因此，作者决定评估每周一次皮下注射2.4毫克司美格鲁肽在65岁及以上个体中的有效性和安全性。

该分析汇总了STEP 1、3、4、5、8和9试验的数据（仅限于肥胖或超重而非糖尿病患者，因为肥胖症药物试验中糖尿病患者的体重减轻效果总是低于非糖尿病患者——因此结果无法进行比较或混淆）。

研究纳入了65岁及以上、体重指数（BMI）至少为30 kg/m²，或至少为27 kg/m²并伴有至少一种肥胖相关并发症（无糖尿病）的参与者，他们被随机分配接受2.4毫克司美格鲁肽或安慰剂治疗。所有参与者均接受了生活方式干预，而在STEP 3试验中则接受了强化行为疗法。

终点指标从基线评估至第68周，包括体重百分比变化；达到分类体重减轻（≥10%、≥15%、≥20%）的参与者比例；腰围变化；腰高比（WHtR）<0.53的参与者比例；BMI类别转变；以及心血管代谢风险因素（血糖参数、血压、血脂和高敏C反应蛋白）的变化。不良事件（AEs）也进行了评估。

在选定试验的总人群中（N=4523），358名参与者（占8%）年龄在65岁或以上，并被纳入分析（司美格鲁肽2.4毫克组n=248；安慰剂组n=110），其中大多数（90%）年龄在65-74岁之间（其余为75岁及以上）。

在基线时，汇总的司美格鲁肽组和安慰剂组的平均年龄为69岁，体重为99.0公斤，BMI为36.6 kg/m²，腰围为115厘米；72%为女性。

在第68周，司美格鲁肽2.4毫克组的体重平均减轻15.4%，而安慰剂组为5.1%；腰围平均分别减少14.3厘米和6.0厘米。在司美格鲁肽2.4毫克组与安慰剂组中，达到体重减轻阈值的参与者比例分别为：66.5% vs 15.5%（至少10%），46.8% vs 6.4%（至少15%），以及28.6% vs 2.7%（至少20%）。

在司美格鲁肽2.4毫克组中，11.3%的参与者达到了WHtR <0.53，而安慰剂组为4.5%。与安慰剂组相比，更多接受司美格鲁肽治疗的参与者从基线到第68周改善了他们的BMI类别。

司美格鲁肽组中有27.0%的参与者达到了BMI <27 kg/m²（即所谓的健康体重），而安慰剂组为5.5%；在第68周，司美格鲁肽组中超重以及肥胖I、II和III级类别的老年患者比例均有所下降，这得益于达到健康体重的参与者数量增加。

对于同时达到BMI ≤27和WHtR <0.53的参与者，这些值分别为10.5%和2.7%。与安慰剂相比，司美格鲁肽2.4毫克组在心血管代谢风险因素方面观察到更大的改善，包括血压、血脂、胆固醇和糖化血红蛋白（HbA1c——用于诊断糖尿病的血糖控制指标）。

司美格鲁肽2.4毫克组与安慰剂组之间，总体不良事件（AEs）的发生率相似（分别为89.1%和84.5%），但司美格鲁肽组的严重不良事件发生率较高（分别为19.0%和12.7%）。

司美格鲁肽组的便秘和头晕发生率（此类药物的已知副作用）较高，而骨折和低血糖发生率与安慰剂组相当，两组中受影响的比例均低于1%。

布塞托博士总结道：“在这项针对65岁及以上肥胖个体的分析中，与安慰剂相比，司美格鲁肽减轻了体重并改善了心血管代谢风险因素，其安全性和有效性特征与STEP项目中报告的结果一致。”

他补充说：“在包括许多高收入国家在内的许多国家，大多数超重病例实际上发生在65岁及以上的成年人中，这是肥胖相关并发症的主要驱动因素，也是生活质量下降和残疾的重要原因。我们的结果支持在这一患者群体中使用司美格鲁肽。”
