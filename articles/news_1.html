深度解析

此最新专家共识标志着夜间遗尿症（NE）管理理念的重大转变，从单一症状治疗转向以个体化、表型驱动为核心的精准医疗。其核心专业概念在于对NE病因的深刻理解，即其由夜间多尿、膀胱功能容量减小及觉醒障碍等多因素协同作用。共识通过引入单症状性（MNE）与非单症状性（NMNE）的明确分类，强调日间下尿路症状（LUTS）及合并症（如便秘、ADHD）的重要性，改变了传统仅关注夜间排尿的视角。排尿日记作为诊断基石，实现了对夜间多尿或膀胱容量减小的准确表型诊断。治疗上，对MNE推行基于表型的去氨加压素（减少夜间尿量）或遗尿报警器（建立条件反射）的一线治疗，对NMNE则强调优先处理日间症状和合并症。这一框架对行业影响深远，它促进了NE诊疗的标准化与个体化，降低了诊断门槛以实现早期干预，减少了盲目试错，提升了治疗效果和患儿生活质量。同时，明确的转诊路径加强了分级诊疗的协作，有助于克服区域差异和文化偏见等障碍，推动NE管理向更科学、高效、以家庭为中心的方向发展。

英文原文

Original
Nocturnal enuresis (NE) in school-age children is more than a nighttime inconvenience: when persistent, it can affect self-esteem, sleep, family life and social functioning. An updated expert consensus now offers a practical roadmap for recognizing and treating childhood NE, with 18 recommendations spanning diagnosis, classification, evaluation, first-line therapy, comorbidity management and referral. The framework lowers the diagnostic frequency threshold to at least one involuntary nighttime void per month for three months in children aged five years or older, and places greater emphasis on tailoring treatment to the child's clinical pattern. The goal is to help clinicians move from symptom-based treatment toward more standardized, individualized and family-centered care. Nocturnal enuresis (NE) is common in childhood and arises from multiple interacting factors, including excess nighttime urine production, reduced functional bladder capacity and difficulty waking to bladder signals. Effective treatments are available, yet underdiagnosis, inconsistent clinical practice and poor adherence continue to limit outcomes. In China, care is further complicated by regional differences in access, cultural perceptions of bedwetting and uneven coordination between primary and specialist services. Since the previous Chinese consensus was issued in 2014, international standards and the evidence base have changed substantially, while domestic clinical experience has expanded. Given these challenges, an updated, locally applicable framework was needed to align diagnosis, treatment and referral with current evidence and frontline practice. Researchers from the Department of Nephrology, Children's Hospital of Fudan University, National Children's Medical Center, together with the Chinese Cooperative Group for the Management of Pediatric NE and the Pediatric Nephrology Committee of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association, published (DOI: 10.1007/s12519-026-01051-4) an updated expert guidance in the World Journal of Pediatrics. The consensus establishes 18 recommendations for diagnosing, classifying, and managing childhood NE, emphasizing symptom-based classification, voiding diaries, individualized first-line treatment, management of daytime urinary symptoms and comorbidities, and referral pathways for refractory cases. The consensus introduces several key updates. First, it lowers the diagnostic threshold: children aged five years or older who experience at least one involuntary nighttime void per month for three months now qualify for diagnosis-a shift from the previous weekly standard that enables earlier intervention. Second, it mandates a clear clinical distinction between monosymptomatic NE (MNE), without daytime lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS), and non‑monosymptomatic NE (NMNE), where daytime symptoms such as urgency, frequency or incontinence are present. This classification drives all subsequent treatment decisions. Third, the voiding diary becomes a diagnostic cornerstone-patients must record at least two daytime charts and seven consecutive nights of fluid intake and voids, allowing clinicians to phenotype children as having nocturnal polyuria, reduced bladder capacity, or both. For MNE, the consensus specifies phenotype‑driven first‑line therapy: desmopressin for nocturnal polyuria and the enuresis alarm for reduced bladder capacity, with combination therapy for mixed types. For NMNE, management must prioritize daytime LUTS and comorbidities-especially constipation, which affects 36–80% of these children-before addressing nighttime wetting. The framework also outlines clear referral criteria: primary care may manage MNE, but non‑responders or suspected NMNE require specialist evaluation with urodynamics and lumbosacral magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). For refractory cases, defined as less than 50% improvement after three months, the consensus advises systematic re‑evaluation of adherence, diary findings and underlying causes before escalating treatment. The authors said the central message is that NE should not be managed as a single, uniform disorder. They said the updated pathway asks clinicians to identify the child's specific pattern, look for daytime symptoms and comorbidities, and match treatment to the likely underlying mechanism while keeping the family involved. They also stressed that apparent treatment failure should trigger a careful review-of adherence, voiding records and possible missed conditions-before stronger or more complex therapy is added. In this way, they said, the consensus is designed to make care both more systematic and more responsive to individual children. In practice, the recommendations could help pediatricians and primary‑care clinicians identify children who can be managed locally and those who need specialist assessment. Clearer use of voiding diaries and symptom‑based classification may reduce trial‑and‑error treatment, while earlier attention to constipation, sleep‑disordered breathing, attention‑deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and daytime urinary symptoms could improve response rates. The framework also encourages timely referral when first‑line therapy fails or NMNE is suspected, supporting better coordination across levels of care. The authors acknowledge that some recommendations reflect Chinese practice patterns and that evidence remains limited for areas such as desmopressin withdrawal strategies. Future trials and multidisciplinary care models could further refine individualized treatment.

学龄儿童的夜间遗尿症（NE）不仅仅是夜间的不便：当其持续存在时，会影响儿童的自尊、睡眠、家庭生活和社交功能。一份最新的专家共识现在为识别和治疗儿童夜间遗尿症提供了实用的路线图，其中包括涵盖诊断、分类、评估、一线治疗、合并症管理和转诊在内的18项建议。该框架将诊断频率阈值下调至五岁或以上儿童在三个月内每月至少一次非自主性夜间排尿，并更加强调根据儿童的临床模式量身定制治疗方案。其目标是帮助临床医生从基于症状的治疗转向更标准化、个体化和以家庭为中心的护理。 夜间遗尿症在儿童期常见，源于多种相互作用的因素，包括夜间尿液产生过多、膀胱功能容量减小以及难以被膀胱信号唤醒。有效的治疗方法是存在的，但诊断不足、临床实践不一致和依从性差持续限制着治疗效果。在中国，由于地区间的医疗可及性差异、对遗尿的文化观念以及初级和专科服务之间协调不力，护理工作进一步复杂化。自上一个中国共识于2014年发布以来，国际标准和证据基础已发生重大变化，国内临床经验也已积累。鉴于这些挑战，需要一个更新的、适用于本地的框架，以使诊断、治疗和转诊与当前证据和临床一线实践保持一致。 复旦大学附属儿科医院肾脏科、国家儿童医学中心的研究人员，联合中华医学会医师协会儿科肾脏病分会以及中国儿童夜间遗尿管理协作组，在《世界儿科杂志》上发表了一份更新的专家指南（DOI: 10.1007/s12519-026-01051-4）。该共识提出了18项关于儿童夜间遗尿症诊断、分类和管理的建议，强调基于症状的分类、排尿日记、个体化一线治疗、日间泌尿症状和合并症的管理以及难治性病例的转诊途径。 该共识引入了几个关键更新。首先，它降低了诊断阈值：五岁或以上儿童在三个月内每月至少一次非自主性夜间排尿即可诊断——这改变了之前每周一次的标准，从而实现早期干预。其次，它明确规定了单症状性夜间遗尿症（MNE，无日间下尿路症状 LUTS）和非单症状性夜间遗尿症（NMNE，伴有日间症状如尿急、尿频或尿失禁）之间的临床区别。这种分类指导所有后续治疗决策。第三，排尿日记成为诊断基石——患者必须记录至少两份日间排尿图和连续七个夜晚的液体摄入量及排尿量，使临床医生能够将儿童表型为夜间多尿、膀胱容量减小或两者兼有。对于MNE，共识规定了基于表型的一线治疗：夜间多尿者使用去氨加压素，膀胱容量减小者使用遗尿报警器，混合型则采用联合治疗。对于NMNE，在处理夜间遗尿之前，必须优先管理日间下尿路症状和合并症——特别是便秘，它影响着这些儿童的36%至80%。该框架还概述了明确的转诊标准：初级保健机构可管理MNE，但对治疗无反应者或疑似NMNE患者需要通过尿动力学和腰骶部磁共振成像（MRI）进行专科评估。对于难治性病例，即三个月后改善不足50%者，共识建议在升级治疗前，系统地重新评估依从性、日记记录和潜在病因。 作者表示，核心信息是夜间遗尿症不应被视为一种单一、统一的疾病进行管理。他们指出，更新后的路径要求临床医生识别儿童的具体模式，寻找日间症状和合并症，并将治疗与可能的潜在机制相匹配，同时让家庭参与其中。他们还强调，明显的治疗失败应在增加更强效或更复杂的治疗前，促使对依从性、排尿记录和可能遗漏的状况进行仔细审查。他们说，通过这种方式，共识旨在使护理既更系统化，又能更好地响应个体儿童的需求。 在实践中，这些建议可以帮助儿科医生和初级保健临床医生识别可在当地管理和需要专科评估的儿童。更清晰地使用排尿日记和基于症状的分类可能减少试错式治疗，而早期关注便秘、睡眠呼吸障碍、注意缺陷多动障碍（ADHD）和日间泌尿症状可以提高治疗反应率。该框架还鼓励在一线治疗失败或疑似NMNE时及时转诊，支持各级医疗机构之间更好的协调。作者承认，一些建议反映了中国的实践模式，并且在去氨加压素撤药策略等领域的证据仍有限。未来的试验和多学科护理模式可以进一步完善个体化治疗。
