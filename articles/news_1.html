团队在线护理与面对面护理对湿疹的改善效果相当

Equivalent improvements in eczema seen with team-based online care, in-person care

此研究对医疗行业具有深远影响。它验证了数字健康方案在慢性病管理中的可行性和可靠性，为医疗服务模式转型提供了有力证据。TCH模式的可扩展性使其能有效应对医疗资源分布不均的挑战，显著提升医疗服务覆盖和效率。对于患者，它减轻了就医负担；对于医疗系统，它优化了资源配置并可能降低运营成本，从而推动临床实践向更高效、以患者为中心的数字化方向发展，促进慢性病管理的持续性与协同性。

The online team-based connected health (TCH) model yields equivalent improvements in signs and symptoms of atopic dermatitis (AD) compared with in-person care, according to a study published in JAMA Dermatology.

April W. Armstrong, M.D., M.P.H., from the David Geffen School of Medicine at the University of California, Los Angeles, and colleagues examined whether an online TCH model provides equivalent improvements in AD severity compared with in-person care in a pragmatic, randomized clinical equivalence trial involving 300 patients with AD.

Participants from eight outpatient dermatology clinics were randomly assigned to TCH or in-person care (149 and 151 patients, respectively). The primary outcome was 12-month change in the Eczema Area and Severity Index (EASI). Changes in the Patient-Oriented Eczema Measure (POEM) and validated Investigator Global Assessment (vIGA) were secondary outcomes.

The researchers found that the difference in mean change was −0.01, 0.38 and 0.06 for the EASI, POEM and vIGA, respectively, between the two groups. All differences fell within prespecified equivalence margins.

"As health systems continue to integrate digital platforms, TCH offers a scalable model that can maintain clinical quality while addressing the everyday challenges patients and clinicians face in managing chronic diseases," the authors write.

Publication details April W. Armstrong et al, Online vs In-Person Care for Atopic Dermatitis, JAMA Dermatology (2026). DOI: 10.1001/jamadermatol.2026.1959 Journal information: JAMA Dermatology

根据发表在《美国医学会杂志·皮肤病学》上的一项研究，在线团队协作型互联健康（TCH）模式在改善特应性皮炎（AD）的体征和症状方面，与面对面护理取得了等效的疗效。

来自加州大学洛杉矶分校大卫格芬医学院的医学博士、公共卫生硕士April W. Armstrong及其同事，在一项涉及300名特应性皮炎患者的实用、随机临床等效性试验中，探讨了在线TCH模式是否能在改善AD严重程度方面与面对面护理提供等效的疗效。

来自八家皮肤科门诊的参与者被随机分配到TCH组或面对面护理组（分别为149名和151名患者）。主要结局指标是湿疹面积和严重程度指数（EASI）的12个月变化。患者导向湿疹测量（POEM）和经验证的研究者总体评估（vIGA）的变化是次要结局指标。

研究人员发现，两组之间EASI、POEM和vIGA的平均变化差异分别为-0.01、0.38和0.06。所有差异均落在预设的等效性界限内。

作者写道：“随着医疗系统不断整合数字平台，TCH提供了一个可扩展的模式，既能保持临床质量，又能解决患者和临床医生在管理慢性病方面面临的日常挑战。”
