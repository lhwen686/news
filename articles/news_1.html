深度解析

这项研究在阿尔茨海默病（AD）诊断领域取得了突破性进展，核心在于利用实时超声蛋白错误折叠循环扩增（PMCA）技术检测血浆中的β-淀粉样蛋白（Aβ）聚集“种子”活性。AD的病理生理学特征是Aβ错误折叠并形成“种子”，这些“种子”能够以朊病毒样机制诱导正常Aβ蛋白发生构象变化，进而自我增殖、聚集并损伤神经元。超声PMCA技术通过模拟并加速这一体内病理过程，尤其利用超声空化产生的局部剪切力与热能，显著促进Aβ原纤维形成与错误折叠，从而将极低浓度的Aβ寡聚体扩增至可检测水平，实现了对Aβ聚集“种子”活性的高灵敏度检测。 此项技术的行业影响深远。首先，它为AD和AD所致轻度认知障碍提供了微创、高效的早期诊断工具，相较于传统的脑脊液检测和PET扫描，其可及性和成本效益显著提升，有望实现社区和基层医疗机构的大规模筛查。这对于AD的早期干预至关重要，可在神经元损伤广泛发生前启动治疗。其次，高诊断准确性有助于精准区分AD与其他类型痴呆，避免误诊，指导临床用药。再者，作为一种客观的生物标志物，它能极大助力AD药物的临床试验，优化受试者筛选并有效评估新药疗效。这项创新预示着AD诊断范式正从侵入性向非侵入性转变，为患者带来更早、更精准的诊断和更及时的治疗。

英文原文

Original
Alzheimer's disease (AD) accounts for 60–80% of global dementia cases, with its core pathological feature being the misfolding and aggregation of β-amyloid (Aβ) proteins in the brain. These misfolded Aβ "seeds" can induce conformational changes in normal Aβ proteins in a prion-like manner, continuously propagating and amplifying, ultimately leading to neuronal damage and cognitive decline. Currently, definitive diagnosis of AD primarily relies on cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) testing or positron emission tomography (PET). However, these methods have limitations such as high invasiveness, expensive costs, or poor accessibility. Although blood biomarkers have shown promising results in recent years, research targeting the critical pathological process of Aβ aggregation seeding activity remains relatively limited. Recently, a research team led by Professor Jianping jia from Xuanwu Hospital of Capital Medical University, China, published significant findings online in the Chinese Medical Journal on June 16, 2026. The team successfully developed a novel blood-based diagnostic method using real-time ultrasonic protein misfolding cyclic amplification (PMCA) technology. This method can accurately diagnose AD and mild cognitive impairment (MCI) due to AD by detecting plasma Aβ aggregation seeding activity, achieving a diagnostic accuracy exceeding 90% in the validation cohort. The research team employed real-time ultrasonic PMCA technology, marking its first application in detecting plasma Aβ seeding activity. "Compared to traditional quaking-based methods, this ultrasonic technology can detect Aβ oligomers at concentrations as low as 1 femtomole," says Prof. Jia. This method integrates ultrasonic and fluorescence techniques, enabling real-time tracking of dynamic changes in Aβ aggregation during the amplification process and completing detection within 24 hours. The advantages of ultrasonic technology are attributed to its unique physical mechanisms: the spherical interfaces and energy generated by ultrasonic cavitation promote fibril formation, while the localized heat and shear forces induce the misfolding of normal Aβ proteins, thereby substantially enhancing amplification efficiency. To ensure reliability, the study employed a rigorous two-stage design. The discovery phase included 120 participants to establish preliminary diagnostic efficacy, while the validation phase confirmed the method's stability in an independent cohort of 429 participants (comprising cognitively normal individuals, patients with MCI due to AD, AD patients, and non-AD dementia patients). In the validation cohort, AD patients showed significantly higher plasma Aβ seeding activity compared to cognitively normal individuals and non-AD dementia patients (P < 0.001). Patients with MCI due to AD also exhibited significantly elevated seeding activity, revealing the method's capacity for early-stage identification. Furthermore, immunodepletion experiments confirmed that the detected seeding activity was indeed induced specifically by Aβ "seeds" in the plasma. Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve analysis demonstrated the excellent diagnostic performance of plasma Aβ seeding activity. The area under the ROC curve (AUC) reached 0.93 for distinguishing AD from cognitively normal individuals, 0.91 for distinguishing AD from non-AD dementia, 0.92 for distinguishing MCI due to AD from cognitively normal individuals, and 0.90 for distinguishing MCI due to AD from non-AD dementia. "The most important significance of this study lies in providing clinicians with a minimally invasive, efficient, and accurate AD diagnostic tool," says Prof. Jia. This method can facilitate large-scale screening in communities and primary healthcare facilities, promoting early detection and intervention of AD. It effectively differentiates AD from other types of dementia to avoid misdiagnosis, assesses disease severity, and monitors disease progression. Additionally, it provides an objective biomarker for subject screening and efficacy evaluation in clinical trials of AD drugs. Moving forward, the research team plans to conduct larger-scale clinical validation studies and long-term follow-ups to assess the biomarker's ability to predict disease progression. This research marks a critical transition in AD diagnosis from relying on invasive examinations to minimally invasive blood testing, which is expected to bring earlier, more accurate diagnoses and more timely treatments to patients.

阿尔茨海默病（AD）占全球痴呆症病例的60%至80%，其核心病理特征是β-淀粉样蛋白（Aβ）在脑部发生错误折叠和聚集。这些错误折叠的Aβ“种子”能够以朊病毒样方式诱导正常Aβ蛋白的构象变化，持续增殖和扩增，最终导致神经元损伤和认知能力下降。目前，AD的明确诊断主要依赖于脑脊液（CSF）检测或正电子发射断层扫描（PET）。然而，这些方法存在侵入性高、成本昂贵或可及性差等局限性。尽管血液生物标志物近年来显示出有前景的结果，但针对Aβ聚集“种子”活性这一关键病理过程的研究仍然相对有限。 最近，来自首都医科大学宣武医院的贾建平教授领导的研究团队于2026年6月16日在线发表在《中华医学杂志》上，公布了重要研究成果。该团队成功开发出一种基于血液的新型诊断方法，利用实时超声蛋白错误折叠循环扩增（PMCA）技术。该方法通过检测血浆Aβ聚集“种子”活性，能够准确诊断AD和AD所致的轻度认知障碍（MCI），在验证队列中诊断准确性超过90%。 研究团队采用了实时超声PMCA技术，这标志着该技术首次应用于检测血浆Aβ“种子”活性。贾教授表示：“与传统基于震荡的方法相比，这种超声技术能够检测浓度低至1飞摩尔的Aβ寡聚体。” 该方法整合了超声和荧光技术，可以实时追踪扩增过程中Aβ聚集的动态变化，并在24小时内完成检测。超声技术的优势归因于其独特的物理机制：超声空化产生的球形界面和能量促进原纤维形成，而局部热量和剪切力则诱导正常Aβ蛋白的错误折叠，从而显著提高扩增效率。 为确保可靠性，该研究采用了严谨的两阶段设计。发现阶段纳入了120名参与者以建立初步诊断效能，而验证阶段则在一个独立的由429名参与者组成的队列中（包括认知正常个体、AD所致MCI患者、AD患者和非AD痴呆患者）证实了该方法的稳定性。 在验证队列中，AD患者的血浆Aβ“种子”活性显著高于认知正常个体和非AD痴呆患者（P < 0.001）。AD所致MCI患者也表现出显著升高的“种子”活性，这揭示了该方法早期识别的能力。此外，免疫耗竭实验证实，所检测到的“种子”活性确实是由血浆中的Aβ“种子”特异性诱导的。 受试者工作特征（ROC）曲线分析表明，血浆Aβ“种子”活性具有出色的诊断性能。区分AD与认知正常个体的ROC曲线下面积（AUC）达到了0.93，区分AD与非AD痴呆的AUC为0.91，区分AD所致MCI与认知正常个体的AUC为0.92，区分AD所致MCI与非AD痴呆的AUC为0.90。 贾教授说：“这项研究最重要的意义在于为临床医生提供了一种微创、高效、准确的AD诊断工具。” 该方法可促进在社区和基层医疗机构进行大规模筛查，从而推动AD的早期发现和干预。它能有效区分AD与其他类型痴呆，避免误诊，评估疾病严重程度，并监测疾病进展。此外，它还为AD药物临床试验中的受试者筛选和疗效评估提供了客观的生物标志物。 展望未来，研究团队计划开展更大规模的临床验证研究和长期随访，以评估该生物标志物预测疾病进展的能力。这项研究标志着AD诊断从依赖侵入性检查向微创血液检测的关键转变，有望为患者带来更早、更准确的诊断和更及时的治疗。
