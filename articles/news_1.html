Original Text
The current global health financing emergency is not only creating a service delivery crisis;1 it threatens hard-won health research and life science ecosystems in low-income and middle-income countries (LMICs). Yet the impacts of recent aid cuts by high-income countries on national health research ecosystems have received insufficient attention, although realising health sovereignty2 requires domestic research and innovation capacity. Funding cuts for research in LMICs are already threatening national health research institutions and multicountry research networks.
