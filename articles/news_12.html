May 10 (Reuters) - Cerebras Systems is set to raise the size and price of its initial public offering as soon as Monday, as demand for the artificial intelligence ‌chipmaker's shares continues to climb, two people familiar with the matter told Reuters on Sunday.

The ‌company is considering a new IPO price range of $150-$160 a share, up from $115-$125 a share, and raising the number of shares ​marketed to 30 million from 28 million, said the sources, who asked not to be identified because the information isn't public yet.

At the top of the new range, Cerebras would raise roughly $4.8 billion, up from $3.5 billion under its original terms, though the figures remain subject to change before pricing, the people said.

The increase follows a broader ‌surge in AI adoption that has ⁠driven sharp demand for high-performance chips and turned semiconductors into a key bottleneck in the technology supply chain. Cerebras' IPO has drawn orders for more than 20 ⁠times the number of shares available, the people said, as the chipmaker looks to manage surging interest ahead of its May 13 pricing.

Cerebras did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Bloomberg News previously reported the company planned ​to raise ​the price range of the IPO to $125-$135 per share.

Sunnyvale, ​California-based Cerebras makes specialized chips for running ‌advanced AI models in a market dominated by Nvidia.Cerebras is seeing surging demand for its processors as AI labs shift from training models to deploying them. Cerebras' chips are better suited for inference, the computations that allow AI models to respond to user queries, than the GPU chips the industry has long relied on for model training.

The IPO next week would mark Cerebras' second attempt to go public - the company first ‌filed for an IPO in 2024 but pulled that plan ​last year. The offering ‌is being led by Morgan Stanley, Citigroup, Barclays and UBS Group AG. Cerebras plans for ​its shares to trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol CBRS.

路透社5月10日报道——两位知情人士周日告诉路透社，随着市场对人工智能芯片制造商Cerebras Systems股票的需求持续攀升，该公司最早将于周一上调其首次公开募股（IPO）的规模和价格。

消息人士称，该公司正考虑将IPO发行价区间从每股115-125美元上调至每股150-160美元，并将营销的股票数量从2800万股增至3000万股。消息人士要求匿名，因为该信息尚未公开。

如果按新发行价区间的上限计算，Cerebras将筹集大约48亿美元，高于其原有条款下的35亿美元，不过这些数字在定价前仍可能发生变化，知情人士说。

此次上调是在人工智能普及浪潮更广泛的背景下进行的，这股浪潮推动了对高性能芯片的强劲需求，并使半导体成为技术供应链中的关键瓶颈。知情人士称，Cerebras的IPO认购订单已超过可供发行股票数量的20倍，该芯片制造商正设法管理5月13日定价前激增的兴趣。

Cerebras未立即回应置评请求。

彭博社此前曾报道该公司计划将IPO发行价区间上调至每股125-135美元。

总部位于加利福尼亚州桑尼维尔的Cerebras公司生产用于运行高级人工智能模型的专用芯片，该市场目前由英伟达主导。随着人工智能实验室从模型训练转向部署，Cerebras的处理器需求激增。Cerebras的芯片更适合推理——即允许人工智能模型响应用户查询的计算，而非行业长期依赖于模型训练的GPU芯片。

下周的IPO将标志着Cerebras第二次尝试上市——该公司最初于2024年提交了IPO申请，但去年撤回了该计划。其与阿联酋人工智能公司G42的合作曾引起美国外国投资委员会（CFIUS）的国家安全审查，G42在2024年上半年贡献了Cerebras超过80%的收入。该委员会最终批准了这项交易。

此后，Cerebras已将全球最大的两家人工智能基础设施建设商亚马逊和OpenAI发展为客户。

根据Dealogic的数据，此次上市将是今年全球最大的一笔IPO。

此次发行由摩根士丹利、花旗集团、巴克莱和瑞银集团牵头。Cerebras计划其股票在纳斯达克全球精选市场交易，股票代码为CBRS。
