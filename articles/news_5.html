深度解析

这篇文章深入探讨了人工智能时代的核心专业概念及其广泛影响。首先是“AI主权”概念，Anthropic模型对欧洲用户的限制暴露了区域对美国和中国AI基础设施的依赖，这促使欧洲思考如何建立并拥有自己的AI模型和数据中心，以确保数据安全、技术控制及抵御地缘政治风险。其次是“数据收集装置”与“隐私”的冲突。Meredith Whittaker指出，AI系统实质上是数据收集器，其营销策略常掩盖大量数据获取的附带后果，这强调了AI功能与用户隐私间的平衡，预示着隐私优先的AI解决方案将成为市场刚需。最后，“AI代理”对经济和社会的深远影响被提出。Emad Mostaque和Mia Negru警示，当智能代理接管认知工作、机器人承担体力劳动时，可能需要重新定义所有权、工作、民主甚至社会参与模式，权力将高度集中于少数AI控制者手中。这些概念共同揭示了AI发展不仅是技术进步，更是关乎国家治理、个人权利和人类未来社会结构重塑的重大议题。

Original
At TechBBQ in Copenhagen, the conversation among investors, founders, and operators from across Europe wasn’t just about what to build with AI, but who should control it. No matter where you were during the annual Nordic conference, from onstage to cocktail hours and after-parties, the chatter kept coming back to how Europe can gain more control over the technology powering AI. That question of sovereignty — which fittingly matched this year’s theme of “Emerging from Agency” — was especially timely after Anthropic’s AI models Mythos and Fable became unavailable to users outside Europe earlier this year. The incident led many in the European ecosystem to think seriously about what it means to own the models and the infrastructure underpinning this AI wave, instead of renting that power from the U.S. and China. One startup exec told me the Mythos and Fable incident seriously disrupted his software team, while another shrugged it off. Sure, the startup exec said, relying on two other geopolitical powers for AI might one day cause Europe severe headaches, but for now, everything is still pretty much OK. “TechBBQ’s theme of agency felt particularly timely this year as the conversation shifted from what AI can do to what we’re actually willing to let it do,” Ellen de Brever, an angel investor and head of partnerships at the Novo Nordisk Foundation Cellerator, told me during the conference that wrapped up August 27. There were panels about the future of women’s health, a summit on how the Nordic and African ecosystems can collaborate on innovation and investment, and talks about how to get more growth capital into Europe. AI was an underlying theme in many of the conversations, but as de Brever pointed out, “the debates weren’t about smarter machines, but about human judgment and who gets to stay in the driver’s seat.” “The age of AI agents isn’t really a story about machines gaining agency,” she continued. “It’s a story about humans deciding what to give up.” An attendee told me that one of the most refreshing sessions at the event was the panel with Signal President Meredith Whittaker, which I moderated and focused on whether privacy and AI can coexist. Whittaker didn’t mince words, and spoke out against AI assistants and agents being integrated into operating systems, like what ChatGPT is doing with iMessage. She said this AI era is creating a “data collection apparatus” and that AI labs are using clever marketing to make people forget “about the collateral consequences” of acquiring so much data. “There is still a huge market need for privacy,” she said. “And particularly with the sovereignty concerns, we will have customers here.” Elsewhere, Emad Mostaque, co-founder of Stability AI and Intelligent Internet, sat on a panel with me about how an agentic workforce will impact the economy and what that means for personal sovereignty. “Sovereignty is the ability to resist power being exerted over you,” Mostaque said. He spoke about the concentration of power in the hands of a few AI labs and said, “Inevitably, every country will be run by AI and that “the person that controls the AI controls the country.” Mia Negru, the advocacy and engagement director for the nonprofit Life With Artificials, sent me a message after the panel about what crossed her mind as she listened. “If intelligent agents perform cognitive work and robots increasingly perform physical work, we may be approaching something much bigger than another technological revolution,” she said in the message. “We may have to rethink ownership, work, democracy, economic participation and eventually even who gets to participate in society.” But when the panels weren’t in session, people let loose at happy hours and rooftop soirées, where more lighter conversations eventually took over. Lovable, Nvidia, OpenAI, AWS Startups, and HSBC came together to host a rooftop barbecue where I spoke to founders about Denmark’s growing tech hubs. London’s Ada Ventures hosted a gathering at the cocktail bar Bird where I chatted with one health tech founder about increased innovation in the women’s brain health space. Then, there was the speakers’ dinner on an island in the middle of Copenhagen where the international press sat together and watched fire dancers. The after-party continued upstairs, where everyone was tempted to stay for one more drink until the bar tab closed. “In a room full of conversations about machines, the most memorable moments still come from the simplest thing,” de Brever said. “Human connection, face-to-face, building relationships, sharing ideas, and reminding each other of what technology can never quite replace.”

中文翻译

AI Translation
在哥本哈根的TechBBQ大会上，来自欧洲各地的投资者、创始人及运营者之间的讨论，不仅仅围绕着如何利用AI进行建设，更关注谁应该控制AI。无论你在这场年度北欧会议的何处，从台上演讲到鸡尾酒会再到派对结束后，人们的话题始终回归到欧洲如何才能更好地掌控驱动AI的技术。 主权问题——恰好与今年“从主体性中浮现”的主题相契合——在今年早些时候Anthropic的AI模型Mythos和Fable对欧洲以外的用户不可用之后，显得尤为及时。 这一事件促使许多欧洲生态系统中的人士认真思考，拥有支撑这波AI浪潮的模型和基础设施的意义，而不是从美国和中国租用这种力量。 一位初创公司高管告诉我，Mythos和Fable事件严重扰乱了他的软件团队，而另一位则不以为然。这位高管说，当然，在AI方面依赖另外两个地缘政治大国有一天可能会给欧洲带来严重的麻烦，但目前来看，一切都还算顺利。 “TechBBQ今年大会的主体性主题显得格外及时，因为讨论已从AI能做什么转向了我们实际愿意让它做什么，”诺和诺德基金会细胞加速器（Novo Nordisk Foundation Cellerator）的天使投资人兼合作主管Ellen de Brever在8月27日结束的会议期间告诉我。 会议设有关于女性健康未来的专题讨论会、关于北欧和非洲生态系统如何进行创新和投资合作的峰会，以及关于如何吸引更多增长资本进入欧洲的演讲。AI是许多讨论中的一个潜在主题，但正如de Brever所指出的，“辩论的焦点不是更智能的机器，而是人类的判断以及谁能掌握主导权。” 她继续说：“AI代理的时代并非真正关于机器获得主体性的故事。这是一个关于人类决定放弃什么的故事。” 一位与会者告诉我，大会上最令人耳目一新的环节之一是与Signal总裁Meredith Whittaker的小组讨论，该讨论由我主持，重点关注隐私和AI能否共存。Whittaker直言不讳，反对AI助手和代理集成到操作系统中，就像ChatGPT与iMessage的做法一样。 她说，这个AI时代正在创建一个“数据收集装置”，而AI实验室正在利用巧妙的营销手段，让人们忘记获取如此大量数据的“附带后果”。 “市场对隐私仍有巨大需求，”她说。“特别是考虑到主权问题，我们将在这里拥有客户。” 另一方面，Stability AI和Intelligent Internet的联合创始人Emad Mostaque与我一同参加了一个小组讨论，探讨代理劳动力将如何影响经济以及这对个人主权意味着什么。 “主权是抵制他人对你施加权力的能力，”Mostaque说。他谈到权力集中在少数AI实验室手中，并表示，“不可避免地，每个国家都将由AI运行”，而且“控制AI的人将控制国家”。 非营利组织“与人工智能共生”（Life With Artificials）的倡导和参与总监Mia Negru在小组讨论结束后给我发来一条消息，谈及她在听讲时所想到的。 她在消息中说：“如果智能代理进行认知工作，机器人越来越多地进行体力劳动，我们可能正在接近比又一次技术革命更重大的事物。我们可能需要重新思考所有权、工作、民主、经济参与，甚至最终谁能参与社会。” 然而，当小组讨论环节结束时，人们在欢乐时光和屋顶派对上放松下来，气氛也随之变得轻松。 Lovable、英伟达、OpenAI、AWS初创公司和汇丰银行共同举办了一场屋顶烧烤活动，我在那里与创始人探讨了丹麦日益发展的科技中心。 伦敦的Ada Ventures在鸡尾酒酒吧Bird举办了一次聚会，我与一位健康科技创始人聊起了女性大脑健康领域日益增长的创新。随后，在哥本哈根市中心的一个岛屿上举行了演讲者晚宴，国际媒体齐聚一堂，观赏火舞表演。派对结束后，大家又上楼继续，直到酒吧打烊，每个人都想再多喝一杯。 “在一个充满了关于机器的对话的房间里，最令人难忘的时刻仍然来自最简单的事物，”de Brever说。“面对面的人际连接、建立关系、分享想法，以及互相提醒科技永远无法完全取代的东西。”
