遗传信息有助于预测青光眼的发生和进展

Genetic information helps predict the onset and progression of glaucoma

💡 AI 深度解析 (AI Insight)

多基因风险评分（Polygenic Risk Score, PRS）是评估个体对复杂疾病（如青光眼）遗传易感性的重要工具。其核心原理在于，许多常见疾病的发生并非由单一基因突变决定，而是由基因组中众多常见遗传变异的累积效应所致。PRS通过整合这些散布在基因组各处、每个效应微小的遗传变异，计算出一个量化的风险指标，从而反映个体整体的遗传倾向。这种方法超越了传统的家族史评估，提供了更为精细和准确的风险预测。

该技术对医疗行业具有深远影响。在青光眼等疾病中，PRS能够识别出在疾病发展数十年之前就处于高风险的个体，从而实现更早期、更具靶向性的筛查。这有助于及时干预，预防或延缓不可逆的视力损伤，显著改善患者预后。此外，PRS还能预测疾病的严重程度和进展，指导医生制定个性化的治疗方案，例如为高风险患者提供更频繁的监测或更积极的治疗策略。若风险分层筛查被证明具有成本效益，PRS有望优化医疗资源配置，推动眼科学从被动治疗向主动预测和预防的模式转变，具有重要的临床应用和公共卫生价值。

Glaucoma is the leading cause of irreversible visual impairment worldwide. Because the disease often progresses without symptoms for years, many patients are diagnosed only after permanent damage to the optic nerve has already occurred. Earlier detection through targeted screening could help prevent vision loss.

A new study based on the FinnGen dataset, which includes hundreds of thousands of Finnish participants and more than 21,000 individuals with glaucoma, demonstrates that genetic information can be used to identify people at particularly high risk of developing the disease. The paper is published in the journal Ophthalmology.

The researchers calculated a polygenic risk score for each participant by combining the effects of numerous genetic variants associated with glaucoma. Based on these scores, the population could be divided into groups with markedly different lifetime risks of developing the disease.

Among individuals whose polygenic risk score ranked in the highest 1% of the population, nearly half developed glaucoma during their lifetime. In contrast, fewer than three out of every 100 individuals in the lowest 1% risk group developed the disease.

The polygenic risk score also predicted disease severity after diagnosis. Patients with a high genetic risk were more likely to require additional medications, laser treatments and glaucoma surgery than those with a low genetic risk.

"Our findings show that genetic risk information can identify individuals at increased risk of glaucoma decades before the disease develops. In the future, this could enable more targeted screening and earlier diagnosis for those who would benefit most," says ophthalmologist and adjunct professor Joni Turunen from the University of Helsinki, HUS Eye Clinic and the Folkhälsan Research Center.

Toward the use of personalized genetic risk information in health care

Traditionally, inherited susceptibility to glaucoma has been assessed based on family history. However, the study found that polygenic risk scores provide a substantially more accurate estimate of an individual's genetic risk than information on whether close relatives have developed the disease.

"A polygenic risk score combines the effects of numerous genetic variants across the genome into a single measure of genetic risk. Our study demonstrates that genetic information could significantly complement current methods for risk assessment," says Nina Mars, one of the senior authors of the study from the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland (FIMM), University of Helsinki.

"To our knowledge, this is one of the largest studies in the world to evaluate both the association of polygenic risk with glaucoma incidence and disease prognosis within the same dataset. These results provide a strong foundation for incorporating genetic risk information into future screening programs," says Eemeli Tusa, a medical student and doctoral researcher at the University of Helsinki, who conducted the study.

According to the researchers, the next important step is to investigate in prospective follow-up studies how polygenic risk information can be integrated into routine health care and whether risk-based glaucoma screening is cost-effective.

The research was carried out in collaboration among researchers from the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland (FIMM) at the University of Helsinki, the Folkhälsan Research Center and the HUS Eye Clinic.

青光眼是全球不可逆视力损伤的首要原因。由于该疾病多年来常无症状进展，许多患者在视神经已发生永久性损害后才被诊断。通过靶向筛查进行早期检测有助于预防视力丧失。

一项基于FinnGen数据集的新研究表明，遗传信息可用于识别青光眼高危人群。该数据集包含了数十万芬兰参与者，其中超过21,000名青光眼患者。该论文已发表在《眼科学》杂志上。

研究人员通过结合与青光眼相关的众多遗传变异效应，计算了每位参与者的多基因风险评分。根据这些评分，人群可被划分为终生患病风险显著不同的组别。

在多基因风险评分位列人群最高1%的个体中，近一半在有生之年患上了青光眼。相比之下，风险最低的1%组别中，每100名个体中患病者不足三人。

多基因风险评分还能预测诊断后的疾病严重程度。与遗传风险较低的患者相比，遗传风险较高的患者更可能需要额外的药物治疗、激光治疗和青光眼手术。

“我们的研究结果表明，遗传风险信息可以在疾病发生前数十年识别出青光眼风险增加的个体。未来，这能够使那些最需要的人获得更具靶向性的筛查和更早的诊断，”赫尔辛基大学、赫尔辛基大学医院眼科诊所和Folkhälsan研究中心的眼科医生兼副教授Joni Turunen表示。

迈向在医疗保健中使用个性化遗传风险信息

传统上，青光眼的遗传易感性是根据家族史进行评估的。然而，本研究发现，多基因风险评分提供了比近亲是否患病信息更准确的个体遗传风险估计。

“多基因风险评分将全基因组众多遗传变异的效应整合为一个单一的遗传风险衡量指标。我们的研究表明，遗传信息可以显著补充当前的风险评估方法，”赫尔辛基大学芬兰分子医学研究所（FIMM）的资深作者之一Nina Mars说。

“据我们所知，这是全球同类研究中规模最大的之一，在同一数据集中评估了多基因风险与青光眼发病率及疾病预后之间的关联。这些结果为将遗传风险信息纳入未来的筛查计划奠定了坚实基础，”赫尔辛基大学医学系学生兼博士研究员Eemeli Tusa表示，他主导了这项研究。

研究人员表示，下一步重要工作是在前瞻性随访研究中调查如何将多基因风险信息整合到常规医疗保健中，以及基于风险的青光眼筛查是否具有成本效益。

这项研究由赫尔辛基大学芬兰分子医学研究所（FIMM）、Folkhälsan研究中心和赫尔辛基大学医院眼科诊所的研究人员合作开展。
