婴儿看到镜中的自己能否帮助他们模仿他人？

Could seeing themselves in a mirror help babies copy others?

这一发现对早期儿童发展、神经科学研究及临床实践具有深远影响。它强调了看似简单的日常互动（如照镜子）对塑造社会感知神经基础的重要性。在临床上，这为理解和干预涉及社会认知障碍（如自闭症谱系障碍）的儿童提供了新视角，提示早期感觉运动干预可能在行为症状显现之前就已开始重塑大脑。未来研究可进一步探究延长镜像暴露时间是否能最终促进行为层面的面部模仿，以及这些早期神经塑性变化如何影响儿童长期的社会情绪发展。

A new study has assessed whether exposure to their own reflection influences the development of facial mimicry, a process associated with empathy and emotion recognition, in 4-month-old infants. The results showed that infants exposed to their own reflection showed greater increases in sensorimotor cortex activity when observing others' facial expressions, but this did not translate into increased facial copying behavior.

Mimicry, the spontaneous tendency to copy others' actions, plays an important role in facilitating social bonds, increasing empathy between strangers and promoting helping behaviors. Facial mimicry can begin early in life and is particularly relevant because it may help us recognize others' emotions.

Some authors argue that the link between "seeing" and "doing" is innate and that infants can imitate others' actions from birth. However, other studies suggest that this link may instead be shaped by sensorimotor experience throughout development.

Based on this latter hypothesis, the recent study examined whether exposure to one's own reflection can influence the development of facial mimicry in infants. The research was led by Dr. Carina de Klerk from the University of Essex (United Kingdom) and involved infants about 4 months old.

Over two weeks, one group of infants had daily contact with a small mirror embedded in a toy, while another group used the same toy without access to the mirror. Before and after this period, the infants watched videos of other babies displaying facial expressions while researchers simultaneously recorded brain activity using electroencephalography (EEG) and facial muscle activity using electromyography (EMG), allowing neural and behavioral responses to be assessed.

The results are reported in the article "Two Weeks of Mirror Exposure Enhances Sensorimotor Cortex Activation but not Facial Mimicry in 4-Month-old Infants," published in the journal Developmental Science. When observing other infants' facial actions, the infants in the mirror group demonstrated greater activation over sensorimotor regions involved in linking perception and action at post-test.

This effect was particularly evident over the right hemisphere, in an area associated with face representation, suggesting that observing one's own movements may strengthen neural circuits involved in perceiving others' actions. However, no increase in facial mimicry, as measured by EMG, was observed. Although the neural processing of others' facial actions was enhanced by sensorimotor experience, longer exposure may be required for this to translate into increased facial mimicry.

This study suggests that simple, everyday experiences, such as seeing one's own reflection, can shape the social brain from a very early age. As de Klerk notes, these findings "suggest that the neural mechanisms underlying social perception can be shaped by sensorimotor experience early in infancy, and may emerge before these changes are reflected in behavior."

一项新研究评估了接触自身镜像是否会影响 4 个月大婴儿面部模仿（一个与同理心和情绪识别相关的过程）的发展。结果显示，接触自身镜像的婴儿在观察他人面部表情时，其感觉运动皮层活动增加更为显著，但这并未转化为增加的面部复制行为。

模仿，即自发地复制他人行为的倾向，在促进社会联系、增进陌生人之间的同理心和促进助人行为方面发挥着重要作用。面部模仿可以从生命早期开始，并且特别重要，因为它可能帮助我们识别他人的情绪。

一些学者认为，“看到”与“做到”之间的联系是与生俱来的，婴儿从出生起就能模仿他人的行为。然而，其他研究表明，这种联系可能在整个发育过程中受感觉运动经验的影响而形成。

基于后一种假设，这项近期研究探讨了接触自身镜像是否能影响婴儿面部模仿的发展。该研究由英国埃塞克斯大学的 Carina de Klerk 博士领导，研究对象为约 4 个月大的婴儿。

在两周的时间里，一组婴儿每天接触一个嵌入玩具的小镜子，而另一组婴儿使用相同的玩具但无法接触到镜子。在此期间前后，研究人员让婴儿观看其他婴儿展示面部表情的视频，同时使用脑电图（EEG）记录大脑活动，并使用肌电图（EMG）记录面部肌肉活动，从而评估神经和行为反应。

研究结果发表在《发展科学》（Developmental Science）期刊上，题为“两周的镜像暴露增强了4个月大婴儿的感觉运动皮层激活，但未增强其面部模仿”（Two Weeks of Mirror Exposure Enhances Sensorimotor Cortex Activation but not Facial Mimicry in 4-Month-old Infants）。在后测中，观察其他婴儿面部动作时，镜像组的婴儿在参与连接感知与动作的感觉运动区域表现出更强的激活。

这种效应在右半球尤为明显，该区域与面部表征相关，这表明观察自己的动作可能会加强与感知他人动作相关的神经网络回路。然而，肌电图（EMG）测量显示，面部模仿行为并未增加。尽管通过感觉运动经验增强了对他人面部动作的神经处理，但可能需要更长时间的接触才能将这种增强转化为增加的面部模仿行为。

这项研究表明，简单的日常经验，例如看到自己的镜像，可以在非常早的年龄塑造社会脑。正如 de Klerk 指出的，这些发现“表明，社会感知背后的神经机制可以在婴儿早期通过感觉运动经验形成，并且这些变化可能在行为中体现出来之前就已经出现。”
