深度解析

麻疹是一种由麻疹病毒引起的急性呼吸道传染病，主要通过飞沫传播，具有极高的传染性。其核心病理机制是病毒侵犯宿主细胞并在淋巴组织和呼吸道上皮中复制，引发全身性炎症反应，导致典型症状如发热、皮疹，并可能造成免疫抑制。文章提及的肺炎和脑炎是麻疹的严重并发症。肺炎是病毒直接损伤肺部或继发细菌感染所致的肺部炎症，而脑炎则是病毒侵犯中枢神经系统或诱发自身免疫反应，引起脑部炎症和功能障碍。麻疹疫苗接种是预防该病最有效的措施，其原理是诱导机体产生特异性抗体和细胞免疫，建立对病毒的保护。当人群疫苗接种率达到足够高的水平（通常超过95%）时，可形成“群体免疫屏障”，有效阻断病毒传播，从而间接保护了未接种或无法接种疫苗的脆弱个体。然而，近年来疫苗怀疑论抬头以及对公共卫生机构的信任度下降，导致疫苗接种率滑坡，削弱了群体免疫，使得麻疹疫情卷土重来，对全球公共卫生构成严峻挑战，并加剧了医疗负担。

The Jefferson County resident died Saturday from complications associated with measles, the county coroner's office said in a statement posted on Facebook. The Washington Post, citing Coroner Greg Furlong, reported that the woman was unvaccinated. "While cases of severe illness remain uncommon, this tragedy highlights that measles should not be taken lightly," Jefferson County Chief Deputy Coroner Jason Stiver said. Two measles-related deaths were recorded in August in the state's Lancaster County, the first such deaths in the United States in 2026 and the first in Pennsylvania in 35 years. Lancaster is home to a large Amish population, a traditional agrarian people with lower vaccination rates than broader American society. The victims were unvaccinated. Measles was declared eliminated in the United States in 2000, but it has made a strong comeback in recent years amid newfound vaccine skepticism and growing distrust of health authorities. President Donald Trump's health secretary, vaccine skeptic Robert Kennedy Jr., is accused of contributing to the crisis by stoking fears about the vaccine. Nearly 3,300 confirmed measles cases have been recorded in the United States in 2026, surpassing 2025's total of 2,289 cases, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. The previous high for a year was 1991, with 9,643 cases. Early symptoms of measles include fever, cough and runny nose, followed by a rash a few days later. It can lead to more serious complications such as pneumonia and inflammation of the brain, which can cause severe lasting damage or even death.
