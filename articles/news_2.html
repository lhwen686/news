深度解析

GLP-1受体激动剂（GLP-1 RAs），如司美格鲁肽和替西帕肽，其核心专业概念在于通过模拟天然肠道激素GLP-1的作用，产生多重生理效应。在治疗暴食症中，其机制主要包括：首先，通过靶向中枢神经系统（特别是下丘脑的食欲调节区域），GLP-1 RAs能有效抑制食欲，增加饱腹感，从而减少食物摄入量和暴食冲动。其次，它们能延迟胃排空，进一步延长饱腹感，有助于控制进食节奏。此外，这些药物还可能影响大脑的奖赏和冲动控制通路，调节患者对食物的享乐性反应和冲动性进食行为。 此研究对行业影响深远。它将GLP-1 RAs的应用范围从传统的2型糖尿病和肥胖症拓展至精神疾病领域，特别是暴食症这一目前药物治疗选择有限的疾病，满足了巨大的临床未满足需求。这预示着制药公司在行为健康市场拥有巨大的增长潜力。同时，研究强调了药物治疗应与心理疗法和社会支持相结合的多模式治疗模式，推动了综合诊疗方案的发展。然而，鉴于现有研究的局限性，如高偏倚风险和制药公司资助，行业亟需更多独立、设计严谨、长期随访且针对明确诊断暴食症患者的临床试验，以确证GLP-1 RAs的长期疗效和安全性，指导未来的临床实践和医保政策，确保患者能获得有效且可负担的治疗。

英文原文

Original
Drugs commonly used for weight loss, known as GLP-1 receptor agonists, have been found to reduce the key symptoms of binge eating disorder, in a new review of evidence led by University College London (UCL) researchers. The systematic review and meta-analysis, published in eClinicalMedicine, found that weight loss drugs can reduce binge eating episodes, loss-of-control eating and emotional eating, and highlights its potential role to treat binge eating disorder as well as obesity. Binge eating disorder, where people regularly eat an excessive amount of food while feeling they have lost control, is common and highly impairing, affecting over 17 million people worldwide. But treatment options are limited and there are currently no approved medications, so there remains a need for better ways to help people living with this condition. We found evidence that weight loss drugs may help to manage some key symptoms of binge eating disorder." Dr. Ilaria Costantini, Lead Author, UCL Psychiatry In the largest study to date on the subject, the researchers pulled together evidence from 25 randomized controlled trials that took place in 12 countries on four continents, including data from 8,069 participants. The studies were testing the effects of drugs targeting the appetite-regulating hormone GLP-1 such as semaglutide (often marketed under brand names Ozempic or Wegovy), tirzepatide (also known as Mounjaro) or liraglutide. These drugs can suppress appetite by targeting the central nervous system and insulin secretion, and they can delay stomach emptying, while also potentially influencing brain processes of reward and impulse control. The researchers found that the drugs yielded benefits beyond weight loss, including reducing binge eating, loss of control eating and emotional eating. Participants also reported increased cognitive or dietary restraint (which relates to how much people intentionally limit their eating), but the researchers say more research is needed to understand this link. The study's first author, PhD candidate Izzy Emptage (UCL Psychiatry), said: "From the evidence available, we cannot say whether the increase in dietary restraint reflects a positive and helpful form of self-regulation or if it is a more dysfunctional pattern of eating. We hope that future research can clarify whether or not taking weight loss drugs might contribute to more pathological forms of eating restriction such as meal skipping." The researchers say their findings demonstrate that weight loss drugs could be an important part of treatment plans for people with binge eating disorder, alongside psychological therapies and social support. Izzy Emptage added: "Many people with binge eating disorder cannot access weight loss drugs through their public healthcare providers, so many have to seek treatment privately at considerable personal cost. "We hope that by highlighting the potential of weight loss drugs to help with binge eating symptoms, our findings will lead to further funding of larger high-quality studies in this area, to better understand how this medication could be used in practice and improve treatment options." Dr Costantini said: "One strength of our study is the involvement of a lived experience panel, who shared important insights into their views and concerns about the use of these medications for binge eating, as well as the challenges many people face in accessing treatment for binge eating disorder. Importantly, they emphasized that sustainable recovery is likely to depend not only on medication but also on psychological therapies and social support, as well as policy or community-level approaches to tackle societal norms and weight bias." The researchers note limitations of their study, as most of the trials included in the review had a high risk of bias and were funded by pharmaceutical companies, and they rarely included participants with a clinical diagnosis of binge eating disorder, which the researchers say limits the certainty of the findings. They say that robust, independently-funded randomised controlled trials with long follow-up times that include people diagnosed with binge eating disorders are still needed to clarify the potential clinical role of these drugs to help treat binge eating disorder, and to determine whether the observed short-term benefits translate into meaningful and sustained improvements. The study involved researchers in the UCL Faculties of Brain Sciences, Population Health Sciences and Medical Sciences alongside colleagues in the Universities of Exeter, Oxford, North Carolina and Karolinska Institute, and was supported by Wellcome, the Medical Research Foundation and the National Institute for Health Research (NIHR).

AI Translation
伦敦大学学院（UCL）研究人员领导的一项新证据综述发现，常用于减肥的药物，即GLP-1受体激动剂，可减轻暴食症的关键症状。 这项发表在《eClinicalMedicine》杂志上的系统性综述和荟萃分析发现，减肥药物可以减少暴食发作、失控性进食和情绪性进食，并强调了其在治疗暴食症和肥胖症方面的潜在作用。 暴食症是指患者在感觉失控的情况下，定期摄入过量食物，这是一种常见且严重损害健康的疾病，全球影响超过1700万人。然而，目前的治疗选择有限，且尚无获批的药物，因此迫切需要更好的方法来帮助罹患此病的人群。我们发现有证据表明减肥药物可能有助于管理暴食症的一些关键症状。”伦敦大学学院精神病学系首席作者伊拉里亚·科斯坦蒂尼博士表示。 在这项迄今为止规模最大的相关研究中，研究人员汇集了来自四大洲12个国家进行的25项随机对照试验的证据，其中包括8,069名参与者的数据。 这些研究旨在测试针对食欲调节激素GLP-1的药物效果，例如司美格鲁肽（常以Ozempic或Wegovy等品牌名销售）、替西帕肽（又称Mounjaro）或利拉鲁肽。 这些药物通过靶向中枢神经系统和胰岛素分泌来抑制食欲，并能延迟胃排空，同时还可能影响大脑的奖赏和冲动控制过程。 研究人员发现，这些药物除了减轻体重外，还带来了其他益处，包括减少暴食、失控性进食和情绪性进食。 参与者还报告了认知或饮食限制的增加（这与人们有意限制饮食的程度有关），但研究人员表示需要更多研究来理解这一关联。 该研究的第一作者、伦敦大学学院精神病学系的博士生伊兹·恩普塔奇表示：“根据现有证据，我们无法断定饮食限制的增加是积极有益的自我调节形式，还是一种更具功能障碍的饮食模式。我们希望未来的研究能够阐明服用减肥药物是否可能导致更病态的饮食限制形式，例如跳餐。” 研究人员表示，他们的发现表明，减肥药物与心理疗法和社会支持相结合，可能成为暴食症患者治疗计划的重要组成部分。 伊兹·恩普塔奇补充道：“许多暴食症患者无法通过公共医疗服务机构获得减肥药物，因此许多人不得不自费寻求私人治疗，个人经济负担沉重。 我们希望通过强调减肥药物在缓解暴食症状方面的潜力，我们的研究结果能够促使该领域获得更多高质量、大规模研究的资助，以更好地理解这种药物在临床实践中的应用方式并改善治疗选择。” 科斯坦蒂尼博士说：“我们研究的一个优势在于，我们邀请了一个有亲身经历的专家小组参与，他们分享了对使用这些药物治疗暴食症的看法和担忧，以及许多人在获得暴食症治疗方面面临的挑战。重要的是，他们强调可持续康复可能不仅取决于药物治疗，还需要心理疗法和社会支持，以及政策或社区层面的方法来应对社会规范和体重偏见。” 研究人员指出他们的研究存在局限性，因为综述中纳入的大多数试验都存在高偏倚风险，并且由制药公司资助，而且很少纳入有暴食症临床诊断的参与者，研究人员表示这限制了研究结果的确定性。 他们表示，仍需要进行设计严谨、独立资助的随机对照试验，这些试验应具有长期随访期，并纳入已确诊暴食症的患者，以阐明这些药物在治疗暴食症方面的潜在临床作用，并确定观察到的短期益处是否能转化为有意义且持续的改善。 这项研究由伦敦大学学院脑科学学院、人口健康科学学院和医学院的研究人员，以及埃克塞特大学、牛津大学、北卡罗来纳大学和卡罗林斯卡学院的同事共同参与，并得到了惠康基金会、医学研究基金会和国家健康研究所（NIHR）的支持。
