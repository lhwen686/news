深度解析

这项研究深入揭示了蛋白质组学在精准肿瘤学中的巨大潜力，特别是在黑色素瘤免疫疗法领域。其核心专业概念在于通过“多组学”（multi-omics）方法，整合基因组、蛋白质组和代谢组数据，构建了一个全面的黑色素瘤分子图谱。蛋白质是细胞功能执行者，其表达模式（蛋白质组学状态）直接反映了肿瘤的生物学行为和对治疗的响应性，这比单独分析基因组能提供更动态、更实时的信息。研究识别出的三种肿瘤蛋白质模式亚型——高免疫活性、高细胞增殖、高肿瘤生长与转移——精准地关联了患者对免疫检查点抑制剂（ICIs）的反应。例如，免疫富集型肿瘤对ICIs响应最佳，这符合ICIs解除免疫抑制、激活抗肿瘤免疫的机制。 这项突破性工作对行业影响深远。它为开发新型“蛋白质组学生物标志物”奠定了基础，这些标志物能够预测患者对昂贵且可能伴有严重副作用的免疫疗法的响应。这意味着临床医生可以在治疗前更准确地筛选患者，避免无益治疗造成的身体和经济负担，实现“个性化免疫疗法”。此外，识别出的蛋白质化学改变和潜在靶点将加速新药研发。一旦这些生物标志物转化为经临床验证的诊断检测（clinical assay），将彻底改变黑色素瘤的治疗决策流程，推动精准医疗向前迈进，优化医疗资源配置，并最终改善患者的预后和生活质量。

英文原文

Original
Protein patterns in melanoma tumors can predict how patients respond to immunotherapy and can be used to find promising biomarkers and drug targets, according to two new University of Pittsburgh studies. The companion studies, published today in Cancer Discovery and Clinical Cancer Research, journals of the American Association for Cancer Research, developed a molecular atlas of melanoma and method of classifying and predicting immunotherapy response based on protein patterns. Determining which patients are most likely to respond to immunotherapy before treatment begins may allow patients who are unlikely to benefit to avoid harmful side effects and prevent delays in starting better treatment options. Immune checkpoint inhibitors, a type of cancer immunotherapy, have dramatically improved melanoma patient survival in the last 15 years. But a large percentage of patients don't respond to the treatment, and biomarkers that predict treatment response remain elusive. To better understand why some patients are resistant to the treatment, researchers looked to the proteome, the entire set of proteins expressed in a tissue or organism. In 2003, we decoded the human genome. However, because proteins are the actual molecular machines that carry out the work of life, characterizing the proteomic states of tumors is critical to both understanding both the biology of tumors and identifying prognostic biomarkers. Protein atlases bridge the gap between genomic possibility and therapeutic reality." Diwakar Davar, M.D., co-senior author, associate professor of medicine at Pitt and co-leader of the Cancer Immunology and Immunotherapy Program, UPMC Hillman Cancer Center The Cancer Discovery study characterized pre-treatment melanomas from 183 patients, simultaneously analyzing 13 molecular levels, including the genome, proteome and metabolome (all the metabolites in a tissue or organism). This approach, called multi-omics, yielded a comprehensive atlas that not only provides a clearer picture of the melanoma tumor microenvironment, but also serves as a foundation for discovering new biomarkers and drugs. The research team identified chemical alterations to proteins that are associated with survival as well as hundreds of potential targets for future investigations. "To understand the proteomic landscape in melanoma, we needed two different sets of analyses," says Davar. "The primary tumor analysis linked changes in the genome to their protein consequences, revealing therapeutic vulnerabilities and protein patterns in untreated melanoma tumors. The characterization of melanoma tumors before combination immunotherapy revealed the tumor and immune properties that shape sensitivity or resistance to the treatment." The Clinical Cancer Research study used data from 208 tumors samples obtained from patients with melanoma prior to immunotherapy, through an international collaboration with contributions from all NCI-funded Melanoma SPORE programs. The research team identified three subtypes characterized by distinct protein patterns. One group had increased immune activity, one had elevated features of cell proliferation, and one had characteristics associated with tumor growth and metastasis. Each of these protein pattern groups was differentially associated with treatment response, with the immune-rich group being the most responsive. Among patients who received combination immunotherapy, subtypes were also associated with disease progression and survival, suggesting protein patterns can be used to predict how patients will respond to treatment. Researchers then trained a classifier model to use protein patterns to sort tumors into one of the three groups. This classifier, independently validated in two patient cohorts, was able to predict treatment response type and progression-free survival with a high degree of certainty. Further validation of the findings is needed, but the research lays the groundwork for developing more personalized immunotherapy that considers a patient's likely responsiveness and prognosis before treatment begins. "We currently lack robust biomarkers to predict which patients will derive benefit from combination immunotherapy. A proteomic biomarker could identify tumors most likely to respond, providing the foundation for a clinical assay to guide treatment selection," said Davar. "Once translated into a validated clinical assay, the biomarker could allow pathology laboratories across the country, from Pittsburgh to Palm Beach, to help determine whether a patient is likely to benefit from treatment."

根据匹兹堡大学的两项新研究，黑色素瘤肿瘤中的蛋白质模式可以预测患者对免疫疗法的反应，并可用于寻找有前景的生物标志物和药物靶点。 这些配套研究今天发表在美国癌症研究协会的期刊《癌症发现》(Cancer Discovery) 和《临床癌症研究》(Clinical Cancer Research) 上，它们开发了一个黑色素瘤分子图谱，以及一种基于蛋白质模式分类和预测免疫疗法反应的方法。在治疗开始前确定哪些患者最有可能对免疫疗法做出反应，可以使那些不太可能受益的患者避免有害的副作用，并防止延误启动更好的治疗方案。 在过去的15年里，免疫检查点抑制剂这种癌症免疫疗法显著提高了黑色素瘤患者的生存率。然而，很大一部分患者对治疗没有反应，而预测治疗反应的生物标志物仍然难以捉摸。为了更好地理解为什么有些患者对治疗有抵抗性，研究人员转向了蛋白质组，即组织或生物体中表达的全部蛋白质。 “2003年，我们解码了人类基因组。然而，由于蛋白质是执行生命功能的实际分子机器，表征肿瘤的蛋白质组状态对于理解肿瘤生物学和识别预后生物标志物都至关重要。蛋白质图谱弥合了基因组可能性与治疗现实之间的鸿沟。” 匹兹堡大学医学副教授、UPMC希尔曼癌症中心癌症免疫学和免疫疗法项目共同负责人、本研究的共同通讯作者Diwakar Davar医学博士说。 《癌症发现》研究表征了来自183名患者的治疗前黑色素瘤，同时分析了13个分子层面，包括基因组、蛋白质组和代谢组（组织或生物体中的所有代谢物）。这种被称为“多组学”的方法，产生了一个全面的图谱，它不仅提供了黑色素瘤肿瘤微环境更清晰的图像，而且为发现新的生物标志物和药物奠定了基础。研究团队识别出与生存相关的蛋白质化学改变，以及数百个未来研究的潜在靶点。 Davar说：“为了了解黑色素瘤中的蛋白质组学图景，我们需要两组不同的分析。” “原发肿瘤分析将基因组变化与其蛋白质后果联系起来，揭示了未经治疗的黑色素瘤肿瘤中的治疗脆弱性和蛋白质模式。联合免疫疗法前黑色素瘤肿瘤的表征揭示了决定对治疗敏感性或抵抗性的肿瘤和免疫特性。” 《临床癌症研究》研究利用了通过国际合作、由所有NCI资助的黑色素瘤SPORE项目提供的数据，分析了从208名黑色素瘤患者在免疫疗法前获得的肿瘤样本。研究团队识别出三种以不同蛋白质模式为特征的亚型。其中一组具有增加的免疫活性，一组具有升高的细胞增殖特征，另一组则具有与肿瘤生长和转移相关的特征。 这些蛋白质模式组中的每一个都与治疗反应差异性相关，其中免疫富集组的反应性最强。在接受联合免疫疗法的患者中，这些亚型也与疾病进展和生存相关，这表明蛋白质模式可以用于预测患者对治疗的反应。 研究人员随后训练了一个分类器模型，利用蛋白质模式将肿瘤分为这三组之一。这个分类器在两个患者队列中得到了独立验证，能够以高度确定性预测治疗反应类型和无进展生存期。 尽管这些发现需要进一步验证，但这项研究为开发更个性化的免疫疗法奠定了基础，这种疗法可以在治疗开始前考虑患者可能的反应性和预后。 “我们目前缺乏稳健的生物标志物来预测哪些患者将从联合免疫疗法中获益。蛋白质组学生物标志物可以识别最有可能做出反应的肿瘤，为指导治疗选择的临床检测奠定基础，”Davar说。“一旦转化为经过验证的临床检测，这种生物标志物将使全国各地的病理实验室，从匹兹堡到棕榈滩，都能够帮助确定患者是否可能从治疗中受益。”
