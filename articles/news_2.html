深度解析

该文章揭示了慢性鼻窦炎伴鼻息肉 (CRSwNP) 与焦虑症状之间存在显著的共病关系，凸显了慢性炎症状况对局部病理以外的全身性影响。其核心医学概念在于慢性炎症、免疫失调与神经精神表现之间错综复杂的相互作用。CRSwNP常伴有2型炎症，涉及嗜酸性粒细胞，血液嗜酸性粒细胞百分比升高被认为是与焦虑相关的显著因素，提示了连接CRSwNP严重程度与焦虑的潜在免疫学途径。 鼻窦鼻腔结局量表-22 (SNOT-22) 评分，作为一项患者报告结局指标，也被确认为强有力的预测因子。高SNOT-22评分（特别是>57分）独立地与焦虑相关，这表明鼻塞、流涕、面部疼痛和嗅觉减退等慢性症状负担显著影响患者的心理健康。合并哮喘作为一个预测因子，进一步强化了可能存在共同炎症通路或遗传易感性的观点，这些因素使个体易患多种慢性炎症性疾病及其相关的心理困扰。 这项研究对临床实践和行业具有重要意义。它提倡整合护理模式，鼓励耳鼻喉科医师、呼吸科医师和心理健康专业人员进行协作。对高危CRSwNP患者，常规筛查心理症状至关重要。整合心理评估和干预措施可以改善患者的整体结局和生活质量。对于制药行业，这可能刺激开发同时针对炎症通路及其神经精神后遗症的新型疗法，或开发能全面满足患者需求的联合疗法。它也强调了在评估疾病影响和指导整体护理时，患者报告结局超越客观测量的重要性。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: A considerable proportion of patients with chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) exhibit anxiety symptoms, according to a study published in the Journal of Inflammation Research. Pei-Wen Wu, M.D., from Chang Gung University in Taoyuan, Taiwan, and colleagues examined the associations between severe CRSwNP and anxiety and depression. A total of 96 adults with severe primary diffuse CRSwNP scheduled to undergo sinus surgery and 40 healthy controls were enrolled in the study. The researchers found that Beck Anxiety Inventory (BAI) scores were significantly higher among patients with CRSwNP than among controls, while no significant difference was seen in Beck Depression Inventory-II scores. Overall, 32.3% of patients with CRSwNP exhibited possible anxiety, defined as a BAI score >7. In a univariate logistic regression analysis, comorbid asthma, Sinonasal Outcome Test-22 (SNOT-22) score and blood eosinophil percentage were significant factors associated with anxiety; in a multivariate analysis, comorbid asthma and SNOT-22 remained statistically significant. The optimal cutoff value for identifying patients with possible anxiety was a SNOT-22 score >57. "These findings highlight the importance of screening for psychological symptoms and potentially integrating psychological assessments and interventions into the care of high-risk patients," the authors write. More information Pei-Wen Wu et al, Evaluation of Anxiety and Depression in Patients with Chronic Rhinosinusitis with Nasal Polyps, Journal of Inflammation Research (2026).
