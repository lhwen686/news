虎杖苷通过调节肠道免疫细胞减轻溃疡性结肠炎

Polydatin reduces ulcerative colitis by regulating intestinal immune cells

深度解析

该研究深入揭示了虎地肠溶胶囊（HDEC）的活性成分虎杖苷在治疗溃疡性结肠炎（UC）中的免疫调节机制，核心在于其对KEAP1-NFE2L2信号通路的调控。NFE2L2（核因子E2相关因子2，又称Nrf2）是细胞抵御氧化应激和炎症的关键转录因子。在正常生理条件下，KEAP1（类Kelch样ECH相关蛋白1）通过泛素化促进NFE2L2降解，抑制其活性。本研究证实，虎杖苷能够直接稳定结合KEAP1，解除其对NFE2L2的抑制作用，从而激活NFE2L2。活化的NFE2L2进入细胞核，上调抗氧化和抗炎基因的表达，显著降低细胞内活性氧（ROS）水平。 在免疫层面，UC的一个标志性特征是CD4+ T细胞稳态失衡，表现为促炎性T辅助细胞1（Th1）和T辅助细胞17（Th17）反应过度，而调节性T细胞（Treg）功能不足。虎杖苷通过激活NFE2L2，有效地抑制了Th1和Th17细胞的分化，同时促进了Treg细胞的生成和功能，从而纠正了这种免疫失衡，减轻了肠道炎症。 这一发现具有显著的行业影响。它不仅为源自中医药的虎地肠溶胶囊提供了坚实的现代药理学基础，更将虎杖苷定位为治疗UC的新型KEAP1靶向药物候选。通过阐明这一精密的分子机制，研究为UC的精准治疗开辟了新途径，并强调了将天然产物开发为具有明确靶点和作用机制药物的巨大潜力，尽管其临床应用仍需大规模人体试验验证。

英文原文

Original
Ulcerative colitis (UC) is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease in which recurrent intestinal inflammation can be difficult to control over the long term. Current treatments include 5-aminosalicylic acid (5-ASA), corticosteroids, immunomodulators, biologics, and small-molecule therapies, but drug resistance and adverse effects remain important clinical concerns. A key feature of UC is disrupted cluster of differentiation 4-positive (CD4+) T-cell homeostasis, including excessive T helper 1 (Th1) and T helper 17 (Th17) responses and insufficient regulatory activity. Hudi enteric-coated capsule (HDEC) has been used for gastrointestinal disorders, while polydatin has shown anti-inflammatory potential, yet earlier work relied mainly on chemically induced acute colitis models and left its immune mechanism unresolved. Based on these challenges, deeper investigation is needed into how HDEC and polydatin regulate intestinal T-cell immunity in chronic colitis. Researchers from the First Affiliated Hospital of Henan Medical University, Affiliated Suzhou Hospital of Nanjing Medical University, Dongfang Hospital of Beijing University of Chinese Medicine, Shanghai Tenth People's Hospital, Tongji University School of Medicine, and collaborating institutions reported (DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag018) the study in Precision Clinical Medicine on July 3, 2026. The team investigated HDEC and polydatin using immune-driven mouse colitis models, mouse and human CD4+ T cells, UC mucosal samples, transcriptomic and protein analyses, and molecular interaction assays, aiming to explain how the treatment suppresses pathogenic T-cell inflammation and restores intestinal immune balance. The researchers first established chronic colitis by transferring CD45RBhigh CD4+ T (Treg) cells into immunodeficient mice, then treated animals with HDEC or polydatin. Both interventions eased weight loss, disease activity, colon shortening, and histological damage, while reducing inflammatory Th1- and Th17-associated responses. Screening major HDEC constituents identified polydatin as the strongest inhibitor of Th1 and Th17 differentiation. A dose-ranging experiment showed that 45 mg/kg achieved efficacy comparable to 90 mg/kg, with no liver-toxicity signal in serum enzyme measurements. RNA sequencing then pointed to NFE2L2 signaling: polydatin increased NFE2L2-associated antioxidant activity and lowered intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS). When NFE2L2 was blocked pharmacologically with ML385 or reduced using ribonucleic acid interference (RNAi), polydatin largely lost its ability to suppress Th1 and Th17 cells, promote Treg differentiation, and control oxidative stress, showing that the pathway is functionally required. The mechanism was then carried into human systems. Polydatin increased NFE2L2 nuclear accumulation in CD4+ T cells from healthy donors and patients with active UC, as well as in UC intestinal mucosal samples. Finally, surface plasmon resonance (SPR), molecular docking, and molecular dynamics simulations supported direct, stable binding between polydatin and Kelch-like ECH-associated protein 1 (KEAP1), providing a molecular explanation for NFE2L2 activation. The authors said the findings connect a defined natural compound with a specific molecular target and an immune pathway that is central to intestinal inflammation. Rather than showing only that HDEC or polydatin can reduce colitis, they said the study explains how polydatin may release NFE2L2 from KEAP1 control, reduce oxidative stress, and shift CD4+ T-cell differentiation away from inflammatory Th1 and Th17 states toward a more regulatory profile. This mechanistic link, they said, helps clarify the pharmacological basis of HDEC and gives polydatin a clearer path for further translational evaluation. The findings position polydatin as a candidate for further development as a KEAP1-targeting approach to UC, while also offering a mechanistic framework for evaluating HDEC beyond traditional use. Because the work combines chronic immune-driven colitis models with human peripheral and intestinal immune cells, it provides a stronger translational bridge than acute chemical models alone. However, the study remains preclinical. The authors report that clinical sample sizes were limited, Treg stability and possible Th17-to-Treg conversion require further study, and key pharmacokinetic questions—including oral bioavailability, intestinal distribution, in vivo stability, and dose-response relationships—remain unresolved. Larger human studies and rigorous clinical trials will be needed to establish long-term safety, dosing, and therapeutic efficacy. Source: Chinese Academy of Sciences Journal reference: Wu, X., et al. 溃疡性结肠炎（UC）是一种慢性炎症性肠病，其反复发作的肠道炎症长期难以控制。目前的治疗方法包括5-氨基水杨酸（5-ASA）、皮质类固醇、免疫调节剂、生物制剂和小分子疗法，但药物耐药性和不良反应仍是重要的临床问题。UC的一个关键特征是CD4阳性（CD4+）T细胞稳态失衡，表现为过度的T辅助细胞1（Th1）和T辅助细胞17（Th17）反应以及不足的调节活性。虎地肠溶胶囊（HDEC）已用于治疗胃肠道疾病，而虎杖苷已显示出抗炎潜力，但早期的研究主要依赖于化学诱导的急性结肠炎模型，其免疫机制尚未明确。基于这些挑战，需要对HDEC和虎杖苷如何调节慢性结肠炎中的肠道T细胞免疫进行更深入的研究。 来自河南医科大学第一附属医院、南京医科大学附属苏州医院、北京中医药大学东方医院、上海市第十人民医院、同济大学医学院及合作机构的研究人员于2026年7月3日在《精准临床医学》（DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag018）上发表了这项研究。该团队利用免疫驱动的结肠炎小鼠模型、小鼠和人类CD4+ T细胞、UC黏膜样本、转录组学和蛋白质分析以及分子相互作用实验，研究了HDEC和虎杖苷，旨在阐明该治疗如何抑制致病性T细胞炎症并恢复肠道免疫平衡。 研究人员首先通过将CD45RB高表达CD4+ T细胞转移到免疫缺陷小鼠体内建立了慢性结肠炎模型，然后用HDEC或虎杖苷处理动物。两种干预措施均缓解了体重减轻、疾病活动、结肠缩短和组织学损伤，同时减少了炎症性Th1和Th17相关反应。筛选HDEC的主要成分后，发现虎杖苷是Th1和Th17分化最强的抑制剂。剂量范围实验表明，45毫克/千克的剂量达到了与90毫克/千克相当的疗效，血清酶测量未显示肝毒性信号。随后，RNA测序指向NFE2L2信号通路：虎杖苷增加了NFE2L2相关的抗氧化活性，并降低了细胞内活性氧（ROS）。当NFE2L2被ML385药理学阻断或使用核糖核酸干扰（RNAi）降低时，虎杖苷在抑制Th1和Th17细胞、促进Treg分化和控制氧化应激方面的能力大体丧失，这表明该通路在功能上是必需的。随后，该机制被引入人类系统。虎杖苷增加了来自健康供体和活动性UC患者的CD4+ T细胞以及UC肠道黏膜样本中NFE2L2的核积累。最后，表面等离子共振（SPR）、分子对接和分子动力学模拟支持了虎杖苷与类Kelch样ECH相关蛋白1（KEAP1）之间的直接稳定结合，为NFE2L2的激活提供了分子解释。 作者表示，这些发现将一种明确的天然化合物与一个特定的分子靶点以及一个对肠道炎症至关重要的免疫通路联系起来。他们指出，这项研究不仅仅是表明HDEC或虎杖苷可以减轻结肠炎，更是解释了虎杖苷如何可能通过解除KEAP1对NFE2L2的控制，减少氧化应激，并将CD4+ T细胞的分化从炎症性的Th1和Th17状态转向更具调节性的表型。他们认为，这种机制联系有助于阐明HDEC的药理学基础，并为虎杖苷的进一步转化评估提供了更明确的路径。 这些发现将虎杖苷定位为UC治疗中靶向KEAP1方法的进一步开发候选药物，同时也为超越传统用途评估HDEC提供了机制框架。由于这项工作结合了慢性免疫驱动的结肠炎模型与人外周和肠道免疫细胞，它比单纯的急性化学模型提供了更强的转化桥梁。然而，该研究仍处于临床前阶段。作者报告称，临床样本量有限，Treg稳定性以及可能的Th17向Treg转化需要进一步研究，且关键的药代动力学问题——包括口服生物利用度、肠道分布、体内稳定性和剂量-反应关系——仍未解决。建立长期安全性、剂量和治疗效果将需要更大规模的人体研究和严格的临床试验。 来源：中国科学院 期刊参考文献：Wu, X., et al.
