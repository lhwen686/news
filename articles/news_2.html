Original Text
In September, 2023, Julia Davenne was painting, as she always did, in the small studio she built at the end of her beautifully tended garden, framed by tall, wildflowers, surrounded by the dramatic landscapes of the Brecon Beacons in Wales, UK. For Davenne, who had trained at London's St Martin's School of Art and the Royal Academy of Arts, working from nature was important. Woods, water, soil, and animals took new form on her canvas, painted in watercolours, oils, or collaged onto expanses of fabric.
