深度解析

本文深入探讨了人工智能（AI）在优化复杂医疗政策实施方面的潜力，特别以美国医疗补助（Medicaid）新增的工作要求为例。核心专业概念在于AI通过数据整合和实时分析，提升政策执行的效率和公平性。其技术原理包括：利用AI连接医疗补助参保记录与工资、税务等多元公共数据，实现资格的自动化核查，从而减轻参保人提交文件的行政负担。此外，AI还能分析在线门户、呼叫中心数据，识别申请流程中的瓶颈，并通过智能聊天机器人提供个性化支持。 行业影响显著。在临床和公共卫生领域，AI的应用可以显著降低医疗补助机构的运营成本，同时保障符合条件的弱势群体持续获得医保，避免因行政障碍而中断医疗服务，这对于维护患者健康权益至关重要。政策层面，AI提供实时反馈机制，使政府能够根据实际情况动态调整和改进政策，实现更精准有效的健康管理。然而，文章也强调了AI固有的数据偏见风险，并提出必须通过“人工干预”（human in the loop）和严格监督来确保AI应用的公正性与伦理合规性，这在涉及民生福祉的医疗领域尤为关键，以防技术加剧现有社会不平等。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain Complex health care policies are often challenging to implement. That's a reality states across the country will deal with as new Medicaid work requirements are rolled out next year. The new federal rule, part of the Budget Reconciliation Act of 2025 (HR 1), requires adults enrolled under the Affordable Care Act (ACA) expansion of Medicaid eligibility to complete at least 80 hours per month of qualifying work or community engagement activities or meet exemption criteria to maintain their coverage starting Jan. 1. A new Special Communication published Aug. 7 in JAMA Health Forum suggests that artificial intelligence may enhance implementation of this policy by helping state Medicaid agencies keep eligible individuals enrolled in Medicaid. Going forward, the use of AI can help states and local governments roll out and improve other health care policies, too. "Medicaid work requirements introduce administrative complexities into an already very complex program," said Dr. Beth McGinty, professor of population health sciences at Weill Cornell and co-founding director of the Cornell Health Policy Center. "It's already very complicated to figure out how to apply for Medicaid and stay enrolled. It differs by state. Forms are confusing, and this new requirement creates additional administrative complexity for Medicaid agencies and the people who are trying to get or stay enrolled." Taking the burden off enrollees The biggest concern is that people who are working or qualify for exemptions to the rule may still lose coverage because they have difficulty submitting documentation. The law requires states to use existing databases to verify eligibility whenever possible, but in many cases, states may not be able to verify compliance or exemption status based on the information that's available to them. In that case, enrollees will need to submit documentation themselves. The Special Communication cited previous research finding that some eligible Arkansas enrollees—when presented with a similar work requirement—lost coverage due to documentation difficulties. McGinty and her co-authors Drs. Yongkang Zhang, Fei Wang and William Schpero, as well as Dr. John Ayanian of the University of Michigan, suggest states can use AI tools to take better advantage of information they already have. "For example, states can use AI to link Medicaid enrollment records with payroll and tax data or enrollment data for other public programs," said Schpero, an associate professor in population health sciences at Weill Cornell Medicine and associate center director of the Cornell Health Policy Center. "This would allow agencies to verify if someone is working or qualifies for an exemption without asking the enrollee to fill out more forms or submit more documentation." AI tools embedded in online Medicaid application portals could also be used to identify where enrollees and applicants are having the most difficulty navigating the process, he said. Today, about a quarter of state Medicaid programs are already using AI chatbots for consumer assistance, McGinty said, which could be extended to help implement the new work requirements. AI-powered digital assistants could explain requirements, answer questions and help applicants better understand the documentation they need to provide. Schpero said AI could also help agencies learn where bottlenecks are in real time by analyzing call center transcripts, help desk messages and activity on Medicaid websites. "Using AI to provide real-time learning for state Medicaid programs has promise," Schpero said. "For example, AI could mine anonymized call center transcripts to understand from week to week what challenges enrollees are facing in trying to comply with work requirements, and states could adapt their outreach and assistance programs accordingly." Of course, there are some caveats. States and jurisdictions have different levels of AI maturity and IT infrastructure, McGinty said. The authors argue that federal assistance will be vital for lower-capacity states to implement this approach. Finally, as with the use of other AI tools, there will have to be a human component for it to be successful, McGinty said. "There are biases baked into our data that AI implementation will 100% reproduce here, and so having a human in the loop and really careful monitoring and oversight of AI is needed," she said. "This cannot be a 'hand it over to the bots' solution." Publication details Emma E. McGinty et al, Closing the Health Policy Implementation Gap With Artificial Intelligence, JAMA Health Forum (2026). 本文已根据Science X的编辑流程和政策进行审阅。编辑们在确保内容可信度的同时，重点强调了以下特质： 图片来源：Unsplash/CC0 公有领域 复杂的医疗政策往往难以实施。随着新的医疗补助（Medicaid）工作要求明年开始推行，全国各州都将面临这一现实。 这项新的联邦法规是2025年预算协调法案（HR 1）的一部分，要求根据《平价医疗法案》（ACA）下的医疗补助资格扩大部分注册的成年人，必须每月完成至少80小时的合格工作或社区参与活动，或符合豁免标准，才能从1月1日起维持其医保覆盖。 8月7日发表在《美国医学会杂志·健康论坛》（JAMA Health Forum）上的一篇特别通讯指出，人工智能或许能通过帮助各州医疗补助机构确保符合条件的个人持续获得医疗补助，从而提升这项政策的实施效率。未来，人工智能的使用也可帮助各州和地方政府推行和改进其他医疗政策。 威尔康奈尔大学人口健康科学教授、康奈尔健康政策中心联合创始主任贝丝·麦金蒂博士说：“医疗补助工作要求给这个本身就非常复杂的项目带来了额外的行政复杂性。弄清楚如何申请并维持医疗补助资格已经非常复杂。各州规定不同，表格令人困惑，而这项新要求又给医疗补助机构以及试图获得或维持资格的人们带来了额外的行政复杂性。” 减轻参保人的负担 最大的担忧是，那些正在工作或符合豁免条件的人，仍可能因提交文件困难而失去医保覆盖。法律要求各州尽可能利用现有数据库来核实资格，但在许多情况下，各州可能无法根据现有信息核实遵守情况或豁免状态。在这种情况下，参保人将需要自行提交文件。这篇特别通讯引用了此前的研究，发现阿肯色州一些符合条件的参保人，在面对类似的工作要求时，因文件提交困难而失去了医保覆盖。 麦金蒂及其合著者，张永康博士、王飞博士和威廉·舍佩罗博士，以及密歇根大学的约翰·阿亚尼安博士，建议各州可以利用人工智能工具更好地利用他们已有的信息。 威尔康奈尔医学院人口健康科学副教授、康奈尔健康政策中心副主任舍佩罗说：“例如，各州可以使用人工智能将医疗补助参保记录与工资和税务数据或其他公共项目的参保数据进行关联。这将允许机构核实某人是否正在工作或符合豁免条件，而无需参保人填写更多表格或提交更多文件。” 他表示，嵌入在线医疗补助申请门户的人工智能工具也可用于识别参保人和申请人在流程中最难操作的环节。 麦金蒂说，目前约有四分之一的州医疗补助项目已在使用人工智能聊天机器人提供消费者协助，这可以扩展到帮助实施新的工作要求。人工智能驱动的数字助手可以解释要求、回答问题，并帮助申请人更好地理解他们需要提供的文件。 舍佩罗说，人工智能还可以通过分析呼叫中心记录、服务台消息和医疗补助网站上的活动，帮助机构实时了解瓶颈所在。 舍佩罗说：“利用人工智能为州医疗补助项目提供实时学习具有前景。例如，人工智能可以挖掘匿名的呼叫中心记录，每周了解参保人在尝试遵守工作要求时面临的挑战，各州可以据此调整其外展和援助项目。” 当然，也存在一些注意事项。麦金蒂表示，各州和司法管辖区在人工智能成熟度和IT基础设施方面存在差异。作者们认为，联邦援助对于能力较弱的州实施这一方法至关重要。 最后，麦金蒂说，与使用其他人工智能工具一样，要取得成功，必须有人工的参与。 她说：“我们的数据中存在固有偏见，人工智能的实施将百分之百地再现这些偏见，因此需要有人工参与其中，并对人工智能进行非常谨慎的监测和监督。这不能是一个‘交给机器人’的解决方案。” 发表详情 艾玛·E·麦金蒂等，《利用人工智能弥合健康政策实施鸿沟》，《美国医学会杂志·健康论坛》（2026）。DOI：10.1001/jamahealthforum.2026.2515 期刊信息：《美国医学会杂志·健康论坛》 关键医学概念 人工智能 本文作者 亚历山大·波尔 博士，代尔夫特大学纳米工程学博士。已发表研究员和期刊审稿人。为内容标准带来科学见解。完整资料 →
