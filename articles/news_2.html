儿童、青少年特应性皮炎心理负担更重

Children, teens have higher psychological burden of atopic dermatitis

深度解析

本研究通过系统评价和荟萃分析，揭示了特应性皮炎（AD）在儿童和青少年患者中伴发抑郁、焦虑及自杀意念等心理共病的风险显著高于成人。特应性皮炎作为一种慢性、复发性炎症性皮肤病，其病理生理机制涉及皮肤屏障功能障碍、免疫失调和神经炎症，导致剧烈瘙痒、睡眠障碍及外观改变，这些因素共同构成了患者巨大的生理和心理负担。 研究中采用的风险比（HR）和优势比（OR）是流行病学中衡量关联强度的关键指标。HR主要用于队列研究，反映在特定时间段内事件发生的相对风险；OR则常用于横断面研究，反映暴露与结局之间关联的可能性。儿童青少年患者的HR和OR值均显著高于成人，提示该年龄段AD患者的心理脆弱性更高。尽管存在研究异质性，但这一发现对临床实践具有重要指导意义。它强调了对AD患儿和青少年进行早期心理筛查和干预的必要性，倡导皮肤科医生与精神心理科医生、儿科医生等多学科协作，制定整合性管理方案，以改善患者的整体预后和生活质量。同时，这也有助于提升公众对AD非皮肤症状的认知，促进社会对患者的理解与支持。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: The psychological burden of atopic dermatitis (AD) seems strongest in children and adolescents, according to a review published online July 6 in Frontiers in Public Health. Hongli Wang, from the Beijing Institute of Chinese Medicine, and colleagues conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis to examine whether the association between AD and psychological comorbidities differs across developmental stages. Random-effects models were used to calculate pooled effect estimates (hazard ratios from cohort and case-control studies and odds ratios from cross-sectional studies). The meta-analysis included 28 cohort, cross-sectional and case-control studies. The researchers found that, compared with adults, children and adolescents with AD had numerically higher risk estimates for depression, anxiety and suicidality. For depression, the pooled hazard ratio from cohort studies was 2.16 versus 1.32 in youth versus adults. Similarly, the pooled odds ratio from cross-sectional studies was 2.79 versus 1.61 in youth versus adults. For anxiety, a similar pattern was observed. In addition, the risk of suicide was elevated in younger patients relative to adults. "Our findings suggest that children and adolescents with atopic dermatitis may have a higher risk of common psychological comorbidities such as depression and anxiety than adults, but the substantial heterogeneity across studies limits the certainty of this conclusion," the authors write. "Overall, these results should be interpreted cautiously." Publication details Hongli Wang et al, Age differences in psychological comorbidity in atopic dermatitis: a systematic review and meta-analysis, Frontiers in Public Health (2026).
