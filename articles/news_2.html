新研究发现，人tau蛋白种子在小鼠大脑中引发疾病特异性形态

Human tau seeds trigger disease-specific shapes in mouse brains, new study finds

深度解析

本研究提供了令人信服的原子层面证据，支持错误折叠的tau蛋白在神经退行性疾病中以朊病毒样机制传播。其核心机制在于，异常折叠的tau蛋白“种子”能够作为模板，诱导正常tau蛋白复制其精确的三维结构，形成疾病特异性的错误折叠纤维并持续积聚，从而驱动病理进展。研究明确区分了阿尔茨海默病（AD）的配对螺旋丝（paired helical filament）和皮质基底节变性（CBD）的特异性折叠，并发现这些不同的结构决定了病理类型和受累脑细胞（例如，AD仅影响神经元，而CBD同时影响神经元和支持性胶质细胞）。 这一发现对神经病学领域具有深远影响。首先，它为开发新型疾病特异性诊断工具奠定了基础。通过识别特定tau蛋白折叠构象，临床医生有望实现更早期、更精确的神经退行性疾病鉴别诊断。其次，在治疗方面，研究揭示了阻断tau蛋白播种作为潜在治疗靶点的可行性。未来的药物开发可以专注于抑制异常tau蛋白的形成、防止其模板诱导传播或促进已形成聚集体的清除。这为阿尔茨海默病、皮质基底节变性等tau蛋白病（tauopathies）的治疗提供了新的干预策略，有望开发出更有效的疾病修饰疗法，以应对目前缺乏有效治疗手段的临床困境。

Credit: Nature (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-11061-x Tau is a protein that occurs naturally in the brain. It is essential for proper neuron development and for how neurons communicate with each other. The same protein, however, when misshapen or misfolded, can act as a driver of neurodegenerative diseases. Abnormal buildup of tau is a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease, and scientists have also found an association between misfolded tau and more than 20 neurodegenerative diseases. The prominent prion hypothesis proposes that misfolded proteins spread by forcing normally shaped proteins to copy their exact 3D structure. To test its validity, a recent study investigated at the atomic level how misfolded tau proteins spread and multiply through the brain. Researchers injected tiny amounts of human tau seeds from Alzheimer's disease (AD) or corticobasal degeneration (CBD) into the brains of healthy mice. High-resolution microscopy that captured tau at the atomic level revealed that the mice's own tau had misfolded into exact 3D structural copies of the diseased original seeds. The human seeds vanished within a week, but not before setting off a chain reaction in which the mice's tau continued to form fibers with matching disease-specific folds and accumulate for the next nine to 12 months. The findings are published in Nature. Double-labeling immunofluorescence of AD and CBD seed-injected mice 9 mpi. Credit: Nature (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-11061-x How misfolds propagate Misfolded forms of the protein called prion protein can form long, thread-like structures that cause brain degeneration in diseases such as kuru, bovine spongiform encephalopathy and chronic wasting disease. These misfolded proteins are thought to spread through templated seeding, a process in which small amounts of prion protein filaments cause normally folded prion proteins to misfold into filaments with the same structure. Scientists have long suspected that abnormal tau spreads through the brain by forcing normal tau to copy its shape, a process called prion-like seeding. Tau is known to misfold into distinct shapes in different diseases and spread through the brain in neurodegenerative conditions. What previous studies had not shown directly was whether a misfolded tau seed makes normal tau reproduce its exact atomic structure as it spreads. To observe the process at the molecular level, the researchers started by preparing the mice's brains. They obtained donated brain tissue from deceased individuals diagnosed with either Alzheimer's or corticobasal degeneration (CBD) and extracted the tau seeds. After injecting the tau seeds into the mice's brains, the team examined the mice at set intervals from immediately after injection to nine and 12 months later, tracking tau buildup through tissue staining and protein analysis. At the 9- and 12-month marks, they collected brain tissue and isolated the newly formed mouse tau aggregates. Histological staining of wild-type mouse brains 9 months after injection with human AD tau seeds. Credit: Nature (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-11061-x Histological staining of wild-type mouse brains 9 months after injection with human CBD. Credit: Nature (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-11061-x Seeds pass on the structure High-resolution 3D images from cryo-electron microscopy revealed that the AD seeds made mouse tau adopt the exact Alzheimer's fold, known as the paired helical filament, while CBD seeds produced the exact CBD fold. These structures set off a cascade in which normal tau proteins continued to misfold in the same way. The shape of the misfolded tau also determined which brain cells were affected. AD seeds produced tau clumps only inside nerve cells, whereas CBD seeds produced clumps in both neurons and supporting glial cells, similar to the cell loss pattern seen in people with CBD. These findings provided structural evidence that distinct tau folds act as prion strains that retain their exact structural identity as they spread from cell to cell. Knowing how each tau fold forms and spreads could help researchers test treatments that aim to block tau seeding, and it could eventually lead to the development of new disease-specific diagnostic tools. Tau蛋白是一种在大脑中天然存在的蛋白质。它对神经元的正常发育以及神经元之间的相互通信至关重要。然而，当同样的蛋白质变形或错误折叠时，它会成为神经退行性疾病的驱动因素。Tau蛋白的异常积聚是阿尔茨海默病的一个标志，科学家们还在错误折叠的tau蛋白与超过20种神经退行性疾病之间发现了关联。 著名的朊病毒假说提出，错误折叠的蛋白质通过强迫正常形态的蛋白质复制其精确的三维结构来传播。为了验证其有效性，最近一项研究在原子层面调查了错误折叠的tau蛋白是如何在大脑中传播和增殖的。 研究人员将微量来自阿尔茨海默病（AD）或皮质基底节变性（CBD）患者的人tau蛋白“种子”注射到健康小鼠的大脑中。捕获原子层面tau蛋白的高分辨率显微镜显示，小鼠自身的tau蛋白已错误折叠成与患病原始“种子”精确相同的三维结构副本。这些人类“种子”在一周内消失，但在此之前已引发了连锁反应，导致小鼠的tau蛋白在接下来的9到12个月内持续形成具有匹配疾病特异性折叠的纤维并积聚。
