该研究揭示了美国成年人晒伤的高患病率及其复杂性，具有重要的临床和公共卫生意义。晒伤（Sunburn）是皮肤过度暴露于紫外线（UV）辐射后产生的急性炎症反应，其医学机制涉及紫外线对皮肤细胞（特别是角质形成细胞）DNA的直接损伤，从而激活细胞凋亡、免疫抑制和炎症级联反应，表现为红斑、疼痛甚至水疱。反复或严重的晒伤，尤其是儿童期的晒伤，会显著增加个体罹患各种皮肤癌（Skin Cancer）的风险，包括基底细胞癌、鳞状细胞癌和恶性黑色素瘤，这是因为累积的DNA损伤可能导致基因突变。

行业影响方面，该研究数据（35.1%的成年人至少一次晒伤，7.5%经历四次或更多次）突显了现有防晒策略的不足。高达55.1%的晒伤发生在使用防晒霜的情况下，这提示了防晒霜的错误使用（如涂抹不足、未及时补涂）或在特定情境（如水边活动）下其防护能力受限的问题。这促使制药和化妆品行业开发更广谱、更持久、更具防水性的新型防晒产品。同时，它也强调了公共卫生领域需要更加精准、定制化的循证防晒干预措施，包括加强公众教育，推广正确的防晒霜使用方法，以及强调除防晒霜外的综合光防护策略，如穿着防护服、戴帽、戴墨镜以及避免日照高峰期出行。对于医疗专业人员，这重申了在临床实践中对患者进行皮肤癌筛查和防晒宣教的重要性。

More than one-third of U.S. adults report at least one sunburn during the previous 12 months, according to research published in the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report.

Dawn M. Holman, M.P.H., from the CDC in Atlanta, and colleagues describe the prevalence of one or more and four or more sunburns among U.S. adults during the previous 12 months using data from the 2024 National Health Interview Survey sample adult questionnaire.

The researchers found that an estimated 88.1 million (35.1%) and 18.8 million (7.5%) U.S. adults had at least one sunburn and had four or more sunburns, respectively, during the previous 12 months.

Spending time in, on, or near the water was the most frequently reported context of the most recent sunburn among adults who experienced sunburn in the previous year (60.6%), followed by exercising, drinking alcohol, intentionally tanning, and working at their job (24.7%, 17.6%, 15.9%, and 12.9%, respectively). About half (55.1%) reported that the most recent sunburn occurred despite sunscreen use.

"Measures to adapt, tailor, and expand existing evidence-based sun safety interventions might help prevent sunburns and reduce the risk for skin cancer," the authors write.

Publication details Dawn M. Holman et al, Prevalence and Context of Sunburn Among U.S. Adults — United States, 2024, MMWR. Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report (2026). DOI: 10.15585/mmwr.mm7519a2 Journal information: Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report
