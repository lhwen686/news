Extending vaccination opportunities, involving community members alongside health care professionals in communicating about vaccines, and providing financial incentives are among the most effective ways to increase vaccine uptake, finds an analysis of international trial evidence published by The BMJ.

The researchers say the findings are important for designing, optimizing, and implementing targeted interventions to increase vaccine uptake, as they highlight the most effective components across different populations and contexts.

The global decline in vaccine uptake, alongside a rise in preventable disease outbreaks, highlights the urgent need to pinpoint and close gaps in vaccine coverage. Yet no existing reviews assess the effectiveness of individual intervention components across different populations and contexts.

To address this, researchers conducted a component-level analysis of 237 randomized controlled trials in high and upper middle income countries. Their aim was to compare the effectiveness of different content and delivery components of vaccine uptake interventions for all vaccines on the UK schedule and across all age groups.

The 237 trials involved 4,361,717 participants (40% male). Of these, 110 trials were at low risk of bias, 96 had some concerns, and 31 were at high risk.

Key findings across age groups

The results show that for children's vaccination, offering payments to cover costs (e.g., travel costs) and decision aids were beneficial, while extended vaccination opportunities and information about social factors (e.g., acting for the benefit of society) might also increase uptake.

For adolescents and young adults, beneficial effects were shown for personal delivery formats (e.g., calls, texts, emails), delivery by community members alongside health care professionals, and social factors. There was evidence of a negative effect of decision aids and some evidence of a negative effect of human versus non-human interaction.

For adults, human interaction, extended vaccination opportunities, appointment scheduling help, payments to cover costs, and motivational interviewing to encourage uptake were beneficial. Financial incentives and information on vaccine safety and effectiveness showed some evidence of benefit, while non-human interaction showed a negative effect versus no interaction.

In underserved communities who often face social, economic, and geographical barriers to care, extended vaccination opportunities, financial incentives, and providing reminders increased uptake. The effectiveness of certain components may also have been influenced by the COVID-19 pandemic.

Limitations and implications for policy

This large study, using complex analyses across diverse interventions and vaccines, provides widely generalizable findings, say the researchers, but they also acknowledge some limitations, such as limited data for some intervention components and two-thirds of the trials being US-based.

They suggest integrating these findings with local-level data to guide the implementation of effective intervention combinations, and say that consideration of cost and cost-effectiveness data "will further inform decision making around which interventions to implement given limited resources."

《英国医学杂志》（The BMJ）发表的一项国际试验证据分析发现，扩大疫苗接种机会，让社区成员与医护专业人员一同参与疫苗宣教，以及提供经济激励，是提高疫苗接种率最有效的方法之一。

研究人员表示，这些发现对于设计、优化和实施旨在提高疫苗接种率的靶向干预措施至关重要，因为它们突出了在不同人群和背景下最有效的组成部分。

全球疫苗接种率的下降，伴随着可预防疾病暴发的增多，凸显了查明并弥补疫苗覆盖缺口的紧迫性。然而，目前尚无任何现有综述评估不同人群和背景下个体干预措施组成部分的有效性。

为解决这一问题，研究人员对高收入和中高收入国家的237项随机对照试验进行了组成部分层面的分析。他们的目的是比较针对英国疫苗接种计划中所有疫苗以及所有年龄组的疫苗接种干预措施中不同内容和实施组成部分的有效性。

这237项试验共涉及4,361,717名参与者（男性占40%）。其中，110项试验偏倚风险较低，96项存在一些问题，31项偏倚风险较高。

各年龄组的关键发现

结果显示，对于儿童疫苗接种，提供费用补贴（如交通费）和决策辅助工具是有益的，而扩大疫苗接种机会和提供社会因素（例如，为社会利益着想）相关信息也可能提高接种率。

对于青少年和青年人，个人化传递形式（例如，电话、短信、电子邮件）、社区成员与医护专业人员共同提供服务以及社会因素均显示出有益效果。有证据表明决策辅助工具存在负面影响，且人际互动与非人际互动相比，也存在一定的负面影响。

对于成年人，人际互动、扩大疫苗接种机会、预约安排协助、费用补贴以及旨在鼓励接种的动机性访谈均有益。经济激励以及关于疫苗安全性与有效性的信息也显示出一定的益处，而非人际互动与无互动相比则显示出负面影响。

在常常面临社会、经济和地理就医障碍的弱势社区，扩大疫苗接种机会、经济激励以及提供提醒信息均提高了接种率。某些组成部分的有效性也可能受到COVID-19大流行的影响。

局限性与政策启示

研究人员表示，这项大型研究通过对多种干预措施和疫苗进行复杂分析，提供了广泛可推广的发现，但他们也承认存在一些局限性，例如某些干预措施组成部分的数据有限，以及三分之二的试验以美国为基础。

他们建议将这些发现与地方层面的数据相结合，以指导有效干预组合的实施，并表示对成本和成本效益数据的考量“将进一步为在资源有限的情况下选择实施哪些干预措施提供决策依据。”
