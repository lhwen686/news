Digital platform significantly reduces distress among children of divorce

Every year, thousands of Danish children experience their parents splitting up. For many, this is a major upheaval that can leave lasting marks on their well-being and daily lives. A new study conducted in collaboration with 21 Danish municipalities and the Danish Agency of Family Law shows that a digital tool developed by researchers at the University of Copenhagen can make a real difference. The tool helps children understand their emotions, put them into words and make tangible changes that improve their everyday life.

"When parents divorce, it is often the first major negative life event a child experiences. Some children struggle psychologically or develop school refusal, and many need support to get back on track," says Gert Martin Hald, psychologist and professor at the Department of Public Health and co-founder of the digital platform SES NXT.

A paper about the effects on the well-being of children of divorce is published in the journal npj Digital Medicine. A study about the effects on the level of family conflict is published in the Journal of Family Psychology.

Digital support designed for children

SES NXT is a digital intervention for children aged 3 to 17. It consists of a series of modules that children work through at their own pace, tailored to their age and individual needs. The modules include videos and exercises on topics such as coping when parents argue, understanding one's emotions, blended families and living in two homes. Children also hear other children share their own experiences of divorce.

The content is age-specific. The younger the child, the more the platform involves parents, helping to strengthen communication within the family.

"Children experience that their feelings are normalized. It is okay to feel sad or scared. At the same time, the exercises are very concrete, which many children need. They provide clear suggestions, for example, about what children can say to their parents when conflicts arise," says Hald.

The platform builds on research into divorce and child well-being. It is also informed by input from social workers and psychologists who work daily with children affected by parental conflict. This ensures that the solution addresses real needs and challenges.

Fewer problems and better social relationships

The study included 866 children and young people aged 3 to 17. Participants were randomly assigned to two groups where one group received access to SES NXT, while the control group only gained access after the study ended. Researchers measured well-being at the start of the study and again after 4 and 12 weeks.

After 12 weeks, children who used the digital self-help tool were doing significantly better than those in the control group. They reported fewer emotional problems such as sadness and worry, fewer behavioral difficulties, better social relationships, improved concentration and more prosocial behavior.

Almost half of the children using SES NXT moved from poor well-being to normal levels. In the control group, this applied to only about 1 in 10 children. Such results are unusually strong for psychological interventions. "They experience fewer problems in their daily lives, and those problems affect them less," says Hald. Children who completed more modules saw greater improvements.

The findings are supported by the separate study showing that SES NXT also reduces conflict between parents after divorce. "In divorce research, parental conflict is the holy grail. We know that it is the single most important factor in whether families go on to thrive. That is why solutions that reduce conflict between former partners are so important," says Hald.

Municipality: A highly useful tool

SES NXT is already in use in 16 Danish municipalities. Municipalities in Sweden, Norway, Iceland and Finland have also adopted the platform. In Næstved Municipality, SES NXT is used both in group-based work with children of divorce and as an individual offer.

"It is a highly useful tool," says Niels Rask, who works with divorce support for children in Næstved Municipality.

"The modules focus on children's emotions, and we find that children and parents develop a shared language that makes it easier to talk about difficult issues."

For the municipality, a key advantage is that the platform makes it possible to reach many children, including those who might not otherwise receive support.

"Our schools and childcare institutions are the first to notice when divorce affects children socially, psychologically or academically. With this tool, we can intervene quickly with support that we know improves well-being," says Rask.

Despite the positive results, Hald emphasizes that digital solutions cannot stand alone in all cases but can serve as an important supplement to existing services. "It is not some magic pill that works for everyone. Digital solutions can reach many children and reduce the need for further follow-up, but some children and families need more intensive, personal support," he says.

About SES NXT

SES NXT is a platform designed to help children and parents better manage divorce.

It was developed by SES Family, a company originating from a Ph.D. project by Søren Sander at the Department of Psychology, University of Copenhagen, in collaboration with Gert Martin Hald.

SES Family received the University of Copenhagen Innovation Award in 2019.

SES NXT is used in 16 municipalities in Denmark and 111 municipalities across Iceland, Sweden, Norway and Finland.

About 1,700 children in Denmark have used SES NXT.

每年，数千名丹麦儿童经历父母分居。对许多孩子来说，这是一次重大的变故，可能对他们的幸福感和日常生活留下持久的印记。一项与21个丹麦市政当局和丹麦家庭法局合作进行的新研究表明，由哥本哈根大学研究人员开发的数字工具可以产生实实在在的积极影响。该工具有助于儿童理解和表达自己的情感，并作出具体的改变来改善他们的日常生活。

“当父母离婚时，这往往是孩子经历的第一个重大负面生活事件。有些孩子会出现心理困扰或厌学，许多孩子需要支持才能回到正轨，”心理学家、公共卫生系教授、数字平台SES NXT的联合创始人格特·马丁·哈尔德（Gert Martin Hald）说。

一篇关于离婚儿童幸福感影响的论文发表在《npj数字医学》（npj Digital Medicine）期刊上。另一篇关于对家庭冲突水平影响的研究发表在《家庭心理学杂志》（Journal of Family Psychology）上。

专为儿童设计的数字支持

SES NXT是一种面向3至17岁儿童的数字干预工具。它由一系列模块组成，儿童可以按照自己的节奏完成，并根据他们的年龄和个人需求进行定制。这些模块包括关于父母争吵时如何应对、理解自己的情绪、重组家庭以及居住在两个家庭等主题的视频和练习。孩子们还可以听到其他孩子分享他们自己的离婚经历。

内容是针对特定年龄设计的。孩子的年龄越小，平台对父母的参与度越高，有助于加强家庭内部的沟通。

“孩子们会体验到他们的感受被正常化了。感到悲伤或害怕是可以的。同时，这些练习非常具体，这是许多孩子所需要的。它们提供了明确的建议，例如，当冲突发生时孩子可以对父母说什么，”哈尔德说。

该平台建立在关于离婚和儿童幸福感研究的基础上。它也吸收了每天与受父母冲突影响的儿童打交道的社会工作者和心理学家的意见。这确保了该解决方案能够解决实际需求和挑战。

更少的问题和更好的社交关系

这项研究包括了866名3至17岁的儿童和青少年。参与者被随机分配到两组，其中一组可以使用SES NXT，而对照组仅在研究结束后才能使用。研究人员在研究开始时以及4周和12周后再次测量了幸福感。

12周后，使用该数字自助工具的儿童的情况明显优于对照组。他们报告的情绪问题（如悲伤和担忧）更少，行为困难更少，社交关系更好，注意力更集中，亲社会行为更多。

近一半使用SES NXT的儿童从幸福感差的水平提升到正常水平。在对照组中，只有大约十分之一的儿童达到了这一效果。这样的结果对于心理干预来说异常显著。“他们日常生活中遇到的问题更少，而且这些问题对他们的影响也更小，”哈尔德说。完成更多模块的儿童取得了更大的进步。

这些发现得到了另一项独立研究的支持，该研究表明SES NXT还能减少父母离婚后的冲突。“在离婚研究中，父母冲突是‘圣杯’。我们知道，这是决定家庭能否继续兴旺发展的最重要单一因素。因此，减少前伴侣之间冲突的解决方案至关重要，”哈尔德说。

市政当局：一个非常有用的工具

SES NXT已在丹麦的16个市政当局投入使用。瑞典、挪威、冰岛和芬兰的市政当局也采用了该平台。在奈斯韦兹市政当局，SES NXT既用于针对离婚儿童的小组工作，也作为个人服务提供。

“这是一个非常有用的工具，”负责奈斯韦兹市政当局儿童离婚支持工作的尼尔斯·拉斯克（Niels Rask）说。

“这些模块侧重于孩子的情绪，我们发现孩子和父母形成了一种共同的语言，使讨论困难问题变得更容易。”

对市政当局而言，一个关键优势是该平台能够接触到许多儿童，包括那些可能无法获得其他支持的儿童。

“我们的学校和儿童保育机构是首先注意到离婚对儿童在社交、心理或学业上产生影响的地方。有了这个工具，我们就能迅速介入，提供我们知道能改善幸福感的支持，”拉斯克说。

尽管取得了积极成果，哈尔德强调数字解决方案在所有情况下都不能单独存在，但可以作为现有服务的重要补充。“它不是对每个人都有效的‘万能药’。数字解决方案可以接触到许多儿童并减少进一步跟进的需求，但有些儿童和家庭需要更密集、个性化的支持，”他说。

关于SES NXT

SES NXT是一个旨在帮助儿童和父母更好地处理离婚的平台。

它由SES Family开发，该公司源于哥本哈根大学心理学系索伦·桑德（Søren Sander）与格特·马丁·哈尔德合作的一个博士项目。

SES Family在2019年获得了哥本哈根大学创新奖。
