实施所有推荐的心力衰竭药物指南可以减少住院并降低成本

Implementing all recommended drug guidelines for heart failure could reduce hospitalizations and cut costs

Patients hospitalized with a common form of heart failure could avoid later hospital stays—and dramatically lower health care costs—if they receive the full combination of four medications currently recommended in national treatment guidelines, according to research led by UCLA Health and published in JAMA Cardiology.

Analyzing Medicare-linked data from more than 50,000 patients 65 or older with a condition called heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF), researchers found that full implementation of the recommended drug therapy could dramatically reduce rehospitalizations during the year after discharge. These results could lower hospitalization-related costs by nearly $10,000 per patient annually.

"Heart failure hospitalizations place a significant financial burden on patients, families and the broader health care system—costs that extend well beyond the initial hospital stay. Improving use of guideline-recommended heart failure therapies isn't just the right clinical move to enhance survival. It may also lower the downstream costs driven by repeat hospitalizations," said Dr. Gregg Fonarow, director of the Ahmanson-UCLA cardiomyopathy center at UCLA Health, and the paper's senior author.

"Our findings suggest that getting patients on all four key medications could deliver real value for both patients and health systems."

Randomized clinical trials have shown that therapy using four classes of medications reduces hospitalizations and improves outcomes. Together, the four drugs form the foundation of the current standard of care—called quadruple guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT)—but the full four-drug approach tends to be underused, the authors said, presenting a persistent challenge to heart failure care.

Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction is a chronic condition in which the heart cannot pump blood effectively enough to meet the body's needs. It is one of the most common reasons older adults are hospitalized in the U.S., and many patients return to the hospital repeatedly.

In their study, the researchers found that the average one-year total health care cost after hospitalization for heart failure was $41,802 per patient, with $25,172 coming from hospitalizations alone. Based on modeling, they estimated that hospitalization costs would fall by about $9,780 per patient per year with implementation of full four-drug therapy, which was associated with a 61% reduction in all-cause hospitalizations and an 87% reduction in heart failure hospitalizations.

The final analysis weighs hospitalization costs against drug costs, and in this study, some medication combinations resulted in higher net cost, especially when newer or more expensive drugs were used. But many lower-priced or generic versions produced overall savings after accounting for fewer hospitalizations. In fact, using a variety of medication pricing assumptions in their calculations, the investigators found that most scenarios provided net savings when optimal drug therapy was used.

As drug prices evolve and more medications become available in generic form, the balance between medication spending and hospitalization savings may shift even more toward net cost savings.

"Prior cost analyses often focused on individual therapies or relied on trial-based models, but in this study, we used real-world Medicare data to calculate the combined economic impact of implementing all four therapies after a heart failure hospitalization in a contemporary, real-world population," said Dr. Mohammad Keykhaei, an internal medicine resident at UCLA Health and the paper's first author.

"Our findings reinforce that patients receiving optimal therapy after hospitalization may be at reduced risk of future hospitalizations and the associated costs," he said. "For clinicians and health systems, the study highlights the importance of closing treatment gaps, improving medication access, and initiating or optimizing guideline-based therapy before and after hospital discharge."

The investigators used Medicare-linked data from the American Heart Association's Get With The Guidelines—Heart Failure registry, including 50,598 Medicare beneficiaries hospitalized with HFrEF between 2016 and 2020. They calculated actual Medicare Part A and Part B health care costs through one year after discharge. Then they used treatment-effect estimates from major clinical trials of guideline medications to model the expected reduction in hospitalizations if eligible patients received optimal therapy with all four drugs.

Finally, they combined the projected hospitalization cost reductions with estimated annual medication costs to project the net annual cost impact of optimal quadruple therapy. Depending on different drug pricing assumptions, the net annual impact ranged from $8,556 in savings to $6,347 in costs, with most scenarios resulting in net savings with optimal drug therapy.

The study estimated drug costs under three scenarios including no guideline-directed therapy, partial therapy, and optimal quadruple therapy, which uses four classes of medications: angiotensin receptor-neprilysin inhibitors (ARNIs); beta blockers; mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists (MRAs); and sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitors (SGLT2i).

"The magnitude of the projected hospitalization cost offset when the therapies were considered together was particularly noteworthy," Fonarow said. "These findings underscore the importance of ensuring patients with heart failure leave the hospital on the right medications promptly, in line with established treatment guidelines."

根据加州大学洛杉矶分校健康中心牵头并在《美国医学会杂志·心脏病学》上发表的研究，因常见心力衰竭住院的患者，如果能接受国家治疗指南目前推荐的全部四种药物联合治疗，可以避免后续住院，并显著降低医疗费用。

研究人员分析了超过5万名65岁及以上、患有射血分数降低型心力衰竭（HFrEF）患者的医保关联数据，发现全面实施推荐的药物治疗可显著减少出院后一年内的再住院率。这些结果每年可为每位患者节省近1万美元的住院相关费用。

加州大学洛杉矶分校健康中心阿姆森-加州大学洛杉矶分校心肌病中心主任、该论文的资深作者格雷格·福纳罗博士表示：“心力衰竭住院给患者、家庭和更广泛的医疗系统带来了巨大的经济负担，这些费用远远超出了最初的住院费用。提高指南推荐的心力衰竭疗法的应用，不仅是提高生存率的正确临床举措，也可能降低由反复住院带来的后续成本。”

“我们的研究结果表明，让患者接受全部四种关键药物治疗，可以为患者和医疗系统带来真正的价值。”

随机临床试验表明，使用四类药物治疗可减少住院并改善患者结局。作者指出，这四种药物共同构成了当前标准治疗的基础——被称为四联指南指导药物治疗（GDMT）——但完整的四药方案往往未被充分利用，这对心力衰竭的治疗提出了持续的挑战。

射血分数降低型心力衰竭是一种慢性疾病，患者心脏无法有效泵血以满足身体需求。它是美国老年人住院的最常见原因之一，许多患者会反复住院。

在他们的研究中，研究人员发现，心力衰竭住院后每位患者的平均一年总医疗费用为41,802美元，其中仅住院费用就占25,172美元。根据模型预测，实施完整的四药治疗方案每年可使每位患者的住院费用降低约9,780美元，这与全因住院率降低61%和心力衰竭住院率降低87%相关。

最终分析权衡了住院费用与药物费用，在这项研究中，一些药物组合导致了更高的净成本，尤其是在使用较新或更昂贵的药物时。但许多价格较低或通用版本的药物在考虑到住院次数减少后，产生了总体节约。事实上，研究人员在计算中使用了各种药物定价假设，发现大多数情况下，采用最佳药物治疗方案能带来净节约。

随着药价的演变以及更多药物以通用名形式上市，药物支出和住院节约之间的平衡可能会进一步向净成本节约倾斜。

加州大学洛杉矶分校健康中心内科住院医师、该论文的第一作者穆罕默德·凯哈伊博士表示：“以往的成本分析通常侧重于单一疗法或依赖基于试验的模型，但在这项研究中，我们使用了真实的医疗保险数据，计算了在当代真实世界人群中，心力衰竭住院后实施所有四种疗法的综合经济影响。”

他说：“我们的研究结果再次证实，住院后接受最佳治疗的患者未来再次住院的风险及其相关费用可能会降低。对于临床医生和医疗系统而言，这项研究强调了弥合治疗差距、改善药物可及性以及在出院前后启动或优化基于指南的治疗的重要性。”

研究人员使用了美国心脏协会“遵循指南—心力衰竭”注册系统中的医保关联数据，其中包括2016年至2020年期间因HFrEF住院的50,598名医保受益人。他们计算了出院后一年内实际的医疗保险A部分和B部分医疗费用。然后，他们利用主要指南药物临床试验中的治疗效果估计值，模拟了如果符合条件的患者接受四种药物的最佳治疗，预期住院率的降低情况。

最后，他们将预计的住院费用降低额与估计的年度药物费用相结合，以预测最佳四联疗法的年度净成本影响。根据不同的药物定价假设，年度净影响从节约8,556美元到增加6,347美元不等，但大多数情况下，最佳药物治疗方案带来了净节约。

该研究评估了三种情境下的药物成本：无指南指导治疗、部分治疗和最佳四联疗法。最佳四联疗法使用四类药物：血管紧张素受体脑啡肽酶抑制剂（ARNIs）、β受体阻滞剂、盐皮质激素受体拮抗剂（MRAs）和钠-葡萄糖协同转运蛋白2抑制剂（SGLT2i）。

福纳罗说：“当这些疗法综合考虑时，预计的住院费用抵消幅度特别值得注意。这些发现强调了确保心力衰竭患者出院时能及时、按照既定治疗指南接受正确药物治疗的重要性。”
