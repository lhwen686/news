New framework maps the complex link between psychological status and cardiovascular health

深度解析

这篇综述的核心贡献在于提出了“心身心血管相互作用网络（PCIN）”框架，它超越了传统的“脑-心轴”概念，旨在系统性地揭示心理状态与心血管健康之间的双向互作机制。文章深入剖析了六个系统层面的生物机制，包括下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺（HPA）轴和自主神经系统（ANS）失衡导致的神经内分泌紊乱，以及慢性炎症、血小板活化等生理病理过程。同时，在分子层面，它详细阐述了BDNF、PROK/PKR、MAPK、5-HT/5-HT2A-R信号通路及NLRP3炎症小体等关键分子机制如何介导应激、情绪与心血管疾病（CVD）之间的复杂联系。例如，NLRP3炎症小体的激活不仅与炎症性细胞死亡（焦亡）相关，更是连接心理应激与多种炎症性疾病及CVD的关键靶点。这些机制的交织网络解释了精神障碍与CVD为何频繁共存，并提示单一靶点治疗的局限性。 该研究对行业的影响是深远的。它倡导从整合医学角度重新审视心血管疾病的诊疗范式，推动将心理健康评估纳入常规心血管护理流程，尤其强调对抑郁、焦虑和慢性应激患者的关注。通过深入理解PCIN，可以开发出更精准的生物标志物，并为设计靶向多个通路的综合干预策略提供理论依据，例如结合认知行为疗法（CBT）与新型药理学制剂（如TrkB激动剂、NLRP3抑制剂）来同时改善患者的心理和心血管健康。这预示着未来个性化、多组学驱动的预防和治疗策略将成为主流，有望显著提升心血管疾病和共病精神障碍患者的预后，为制药、诊断和数字健康领域带来创新机遇。

英文原文

Original
CVD remains the leading cause of death worldwide, while the global burden of mental disorders continues to rise. Depression and anxiety are associated with myocardial infarction, stroke, heart failure, and cardiovascular mortality, and patients with CVD face a higher risk of comorbid mental disorders. Despite this bidirectional relationship, most studies have examined single diseases or single mechanisms, leaving a fragmented picture of how the brain and heart communicate. Given these challenges, in-depth research is needed on the integrated mechanisms, biomarkers, and interventions that link psychological status and cardiovascular health. The review was led by researchers at West China Hospital, Sichuan University, and West China Second University Hospital, Sichuan University, Chengdu, China. Published (DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag025) in 2026 in Precision Clinical Medicine, the article synthesizes evidence from epidemiology, neuroendocrine, autonomic, immune, and molecular studies to build the psycho-cardiovascular interaction network (PCIN) framework. It highlights how psychological factors may influence cardiovascular function through shared pathways and proposes brain-heart axis-informed strategies for assessment and treatment. The review details six system-level mechanisms: hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis imbalance, autonomic nervous system (ANS) imbalance, chronic inflammation, platelet activation, unhealthy behaviors, and sleep/circadian disruption. These processes can raise cortisol, shift sympathetic-parasympathetic balance, increase interleukin-6 (IL-6) and tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α), promote thrombosis, and worsen lifestyle and treatment adherence. At the molecular level, the authors focus on five pathways. BDNF signaling is protective when mature BDNF activates tropomyosin receptor kinase B (TrkB), but pro-BDNF can trigger injury through p75 neurotrophin receptor (p75NTR). PROK/PKR signaling shows receptor-specific effects: PKR1 is often cardioprotective, whereas PKR2 can drive hypertrophy and vascular leakage. MAPK branches, including extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK), c-Jun N-terminal kinase (JNK), and p38, link stress and inflammation to depression, cardiac fibrosis, and remodeling. 5-hydroxytryptamine (5-HT)/ 5-HT2A receptor (5-HT2A-R) signaling influences platelet aggregation, vasoconstriction, and inflammation. NOD-like receptor protein 3 (NLRP3) inflammasome activation links stress, interleukin-1β (IL-1β), interleukin-18 (IL-18), and pyroptosis with both mental disorders and CVD. The authors stress that these pathways are not isolated. They form an interconnected network in which neurotrophic, peptidergic, kinase, monoaminergic, and inflammatory signals converge, helping explain why mental and cardiovascular conditions frequently coexist and why single-target treatments may have limited effects. The authors said the PCIN is more than a new label for the brain-heart axis. They said it is a practical way to connect psychosocial phenotypes with measurable molecular signals, from BDNF and 5-HT to NLRP3. In their view, the most immediate opportunity is to bring mental health assessment into routine cardiovascular care, especially for patients with depression, anxiety, or chronic stress. Longer term, they said, the field needs rigorous trials testing whether pathway-targeted therapies can deliver dual mental and cardiovascular benefits without unacceptable side effects. Clinically, the PCIN framework supports integrated risk assessment that combines psychological, molecular, genetic, and physiological data. Mental health screening and interventions such as cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) could become part of cardiovascular management. Pharmacological strategies include selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs), 5-HT2A-R antagonists, NLRP3 inhibitors with central nervous system (CNS) penetration, and TrkB agonists such as 7,8-dihydroxyflavone. Adeno-associated virus (AAV)-mediated BDNF gene therapy and exosome delivery platforms remain experimental but promising. The authors call for multiomics studies, long-term randomized controlled trials, and individualized brain-heart axis-based prevention. Source: Chinese Academy of Sciences Journal reference: Li, Z., et al. (2026). Psycho-cardiovascular interaction network in cardiovascular health and disease: signaling pathways and molecular mechanisms. Precision Clinical Medicine. AI Translation
心血管疾病（CVD）仍然是全球主要的死亡原因，而全球精神障碍的负担持续上升。抑郁症和焦虑症与心肌梗死、中风、心力衰竭以及心血管死亡率相关，心血管疾病患者面临着更高的共病精神障碍风险。尽管存在这种双向关系，但大多数研究都集中于单一疾病或单一机制，导致大脑和心脏如何沟通的图景仍然支离破碎。鉴于这些挑战，需要对连接心理状态和心血管健康的综合机制、生物标志物和干预措施进行深入研究。 该综述由四川大学华西医院和四川大学华西第二医院的研究人员主导，于2026年发表在《精准临床医学》（DOI: 10.1093/pcmedi/pbag025）上。文章综合了流行病学、神经内分泌学、自主神经学、免疫学和分子学研究的证据，构建了心身心血管相互作用网络（PCIN）框架。它强调了心理因素如何通过共享通路影响心血管功能，并提出了基于脑-心轴的评估和治疗策略。 该综述详细阐述了六个系统层面的机制：下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺（HPA）轴失衡、自主神经系统（ANS）失衡、慢性炎症、血小板活化、不健康行为以及睡眠/昼夜节律紊乱。这些过程会升高皮质醇水平，改变交感-副交感平衡，增加白细胞介素-6（IL-6）和肿瘤坏死因子-α（TNF-α），促进血栓形成，并恶化生活方式和治疗依从性。在分子层面，作者们关注了五种通路。当成熟的脑源性神经营养因子（BDNF）激活原肌球蛋白受体激酶B（TrkB）时，BDNF信号通路具有保护作用，但其前体形式pro-BDNF可通过p75神经营养因子受体（p75NTR）引发损伤。前列腺素样肽/前列腺素样肽受体（PROK/PKR）信号通路表现出受体特异性效应：PKR1通常具有心脏保护作用，而PKR2则可能驱动肥大和血管渗漏。丝裂原活化蛋白激酶（MAPK）分支，包括细胞外信号调节激酶（ERK）、c-Jun氨基末端激酶（JNK）和p38，将应激和炎症与抑郁症、心脏纤维化和重塑联系起来。5-羟色胺（5-HT）/ 5-HT2A受体（5-HT2A-R）信号通路影响血小板聚集、血管收缩和炎症。核苷酸结合寡聚化结构域样受体蛋白3（NLRP3）炎症小体激活将应激、白细胞介素-1β（IL-1β）、白细胞介素-18（IL-18）和焦亡与精神障碍和心血管疾病联系起来。作者们强调，这些通路并非孤立存在。它们形成一个相互连接的网络，其中神经营养、肽能、激酶、单胺能和炎症信号汇聚，这有助于解释为何精神和心血管疾病频繁共存以及为何单一靶点治疗效果有限。 作者们表示，PCIN不仅仅是脑-心轴的一个新标签。他们认为，这是一种将社会心理表型与可测量的分子信号（从BDNF和5-HT到NLRP3）联系起来的实用方法。在他们看来，最直接的机会是将心理健康评估纳入常规心血管护理，特别是对于患有抑郁症、焦虑症或慢性应激的患者。他们表示，从长远来看，该领域需要严格的临床试验来测试靶向通路疗法能否在没有不可接受的副作用的情况下，同时带来精神和心血管双重益处。 在临床实践中，PCIN框架支持整合风险评估，该评估结合了心理、分子、遗传和生理数据。心理健康筛查和干预措施，如认知行为疗法（CBT）和正念减压疗法（MBSR），可以成为心血管管理的一部分。药理学策略包括选择性5-羟色胺再摄取抑制剂（SSRIs）、5-HT2A-R拮抗剂、具有中枢神经系统（CNS）渗透性的NLRP3抑制剂以及TrkB激动剂（如7,8-二羟基黄酮）。腺相关病毒（AAV）介导的BDNF基因疗法和外泌体递送平台仍处于实验阶段，但前景广阔。作者们呼吁开展多组学研究、长期随机对照试验和个体化的脑-心轴预防。 来源：中国科学院 杂志参考文献：Li, Z., et al.
