深度解析

FeNO检测和CO监测是呼吸医学领域重要的非侵入性诊断技术。FeNO，即呼出气一氧化氮分数，其检测原理基于气道炎症（特别是嗜酸性粒细胞炎症）发生时，气道上皮细胞会产生更高水平的一氧化氮。因此，FeNO值升高常作为哮喘等嗜酸性粒细胞性气道炎症性疾病的生物标志物，有助于疾病诊断、表型分类及糖皮质激素治疗效果的评估与指导。例如，在哮喘管理中，FeNO检测可帮助临床医生判断患者对吸入性糖皮质激素的依从性及其疗效，从而优化治疗方案。 另一方面，一氧化碳（CO）监测，如Smokerlyzer®设备所用技术，主要用于评估吸烟者的吸烟状态、一氧化碳暴露水平及戒烟进展。其原理是测量呼出气中CO浓度，CO是香烟燃烧的产物之一，通过呼出气CO水平可客观反映吸烟程度。这些呼吸分析技术对呼吸医学行业影响深远。它们提供了一种快速、客观、无创的评估工具，提高了呼吸系统疾病诊断的精确性和治疗的个性化。在临床研究中，这些生物标志物有助于更深入地理解疾病机制，加速新疗法开发。在公共卫生层面，CO监测支持戒烟干预，改善全民健康。贝德丰特公司在ERS大会上展示这些技术，不仅促进了学术交流与合作，更彰显了数字医疗与精准诊断在改善全球呼吸健康中的关键作用。

Original
Bedfont® Scientific Limited, a world leader in breath analysis, is excited to be attending this year's European Respiratory Society (ERS) Congress in Barcelona. With over 50 years of experience in the design and manufacture of medical breath analysis devices, Bedfont® looks to connect with professionals in healthcare and clinical research to share its innovative Fractional exhaled Nitric Oxide (FeNO) and carbon monoxide (CO) monitoring technology. Image Credit: Bedfont® Scientific Ltd. The ERS Congress is the world’s largest international meeting dedicated to respiratory medicine, bringing together approximately 20,000 clinicians, researchers, healthcare professionals and industry leaders from across the world. This year’s congress takes place in Barcelona from the 5th to the 9th of September, providing a platform for sharing the latest scientific advances, promoting collaboration and driving innovation in respiratory care. This year’s theme is “United for better breathing: partnership between patients, clinicians and researchers.” With Bedfont® looking to strengthen its commitment to respiratory clinical research, ERS provides the perfect platform to connect with industry professionals and showcase its innovations. ERS Congress is one of the most important events in the respiratory calendar, bringing together the very people driving innovation in respiratory medicine. We're excited to return to Barcelona to showcase NObreath® FeNO device and Smokerlyzer® range, while strengthening relationships with our distributor network and connecting with research teams and healthcare professionals. As Bedfont® continues to expand its presence in medical research, we're looking forward to discussing how our trusted respiratory monitoring solutions can further support studies and ultimately improve patient care around the world." Jason Smith, CEO, Bedfont® The Bedfont® team will be at Stand R.19 from the 6th to the 9th of September, where visitors can explore the NObreath® and Smokerlyzer® devices and meet the experts behind them. Joining the team is renowned respiratory nurse and clinical leader, Carol Stonham MBE, who will be on the stand to share her expertise and provide valuable clinical insight into the role of FeNO testing in respiratory care. Carol will be available alongside Bedfont's Clinical Research Partnerships Manager, Hannah Vince-Drew, to answer questions and discuss the application of FeNO testing in both clinical practice and clinical research.
