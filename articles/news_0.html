英文原文

Original
A UVA Health doctor and other leading national experts have developed important new guidelines to improve care for patients who develop sepsis, a dangerous full-body infection that kills at least 350,000 Americans every year. Taison Bell, MD, MBA, interim chair of UVA Health's Department of Medicine, and colleagues assembled the new guidelines on behalf of the Infectious Diseases Society of America and seven other professional groups. The guidelines aim to standardize and enhance infection-related practices and workflows in hospitals to ensure patients receive the best-possible sepsis care. What excites me most about this work is its focus on making high-quality sepsis care more consistent and reliable. By strengthening the systems behind diagnosis, treatment, prevention and quality improvement, we have an opportunity to make a real difference for patients while continuing to evaluate the technologies that could shape the future of sepsis care." Taison Bell, MD, MBA, part of the Divisions of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine and Infectious Diseases and International Health, University of Virginia School of Medicine Sepsis remains a leading cause of serious illness and death despite decades of efforts to improve recognition and treatment. It occurs when a person's immune response spirals out of control in response to an infection, potentially leading to organ failure and death. The new guidelines address a vast area of sepsis-related care, including diagnostic testing and pathogen detection, antimicrobial management, infection prevention and surveillance and performance measurement, as well as hospital infrastructure and organizational support. Some of the recommendations: implement rapid molecular testing of positive blood cultures; ensure immediate antibiotic therapy for patients suffering the life-threatening medical emergency known as septic shock; deploy electronic sepsis surveillance; and standardize daily toothbrushing to reduce hospital-acquired pneumonia. In addition, the guidelines highlight emerging technologies with the potential to improve sepsis care, such as next-generation diagnostics and artificial intelligence-enhanced clinical decision support. The authors caution, however, that these technologies will need further validation before they are widely deployed in hospitals. The sepsis guidelines have been published as a position paper in the Clinical Infectious Diseases journal. The guidelines were developed by representatives from the Infectious Diseases Society of America, the American College of Emergency Physicians, American Society for Microbiology, American Thoracic Society, Pediatric Infectious Diseases Society, Society of Critical Care Medicine, Society for Healthcare Epidemiology of America, Society of Hospital Medicine, Society of Infectious Diseases Pharmacists and Sepsis Alliance. Finding new ways to improve care for patients across Virginia and beyond is a major mission of UVA's new Paul and Diane Manning Institute of Biotechnology. The institute has been launched to accelerate the development of new drugs and treatments for the most complex and challenging diseases.

弗吉尼亚大学健康中心的一名医生和其他国内顶尖专家共同制定了重要的新指南，旨在改善脓毒症患者的护理。脓毒症是一种危险的全身感染，每年导致至少35万美国人死亡。 弗吉尼亚大学健康中心内科临时主任、医学博士兼工商管理硕士泰森·贝尔及其同事，代表美国感染病学会和其他七个专业团体，共同编制了这些新指南。该指南旨在规范和加强医院内与感染相关的实践和工作流程，以确保患者获得最佳的脓毒症护理。 “这项工作最让我兴奋的是，它专注于使高质量的脓毒症护理更具一致性和可靠性。通过加强诊断、治疗、预防和质量改进背后的系统，我们有机会为患者带来真正的改变，同时继续评估可能塑造未来脓毒症护理的技术。” 泰森·贝尔，医学博士兼工商管理硕士，弗吉尼亚大学医学院肺部与危重症医学科以及感染病与国际健康科成员。 尽管几十年来一直致力于改进识别和治疗，但脓毒症仍然是导致严重疾病和死亡的主要原因。当人体对感染的免疫反应失控时，就会发生脓毒症，可能导致器官衰竭和死亡。 新指南涵盖了脓毒症相关护理的广泛领域，包括诊断检测和病原体检测、抗菌药物管理、感染预防和监测、绩效评估，以及医院基础设施和组织支持。其中一些建议包括： 实施阳性血培养物的快速分子检测； 确保脓毒性休克这一危及生命的医疗急症患者能立即获得抗生素治疗； 部署电子脓毒症监测系统； 并推广每日刷牙以减少医院获得性肺炎。 此外，该指南还强调了具有改善脓毒症护理潜力的新兴技术，例如下一代诊断技术和人工智能增强型临床决策支持系统。然而，作者们也提醒，这些技术在医院广泛部署之前还需要进一步的验证。 这些脓毒症指南已作为一份立场文件发表在《临床感染病》杂志上。 该指南由以下机构的代表共同制定：美国感染病学会、美国急诊医师学会、美国微生物学会、美国胸科学会、儿科感染病学会、危重症医学会、美国医疗流行病学学会、医院内科学会、感染病药师学会和脓毒症联盟。 寻求改善弗吉尼亚州乃至更广泛地区患者护理的新方法，是弗吉尼亚大学新成立的保罗和黛安·曼宁生物技术研究所的一项重要使命。该研究所的设立旨在加速针对最复杂和最具挑战性疾病的新药物和新疗法的开发。
