机体自身的细胞间信息传递载体被研究作为下一代药物的基础

Body's own cell-to-cell messaging packets studied as the basis for next-generation medicines

文章核心聚焦于细胞外囊泡（EVs）在未来再生医学和免疫疗法中的颠覆性潜力。EVs是细胞释放的纳米级生物包裹，富含蛋白质、脂质和核酸等生物信息，在细胞间通信、组织再生和炎症调节中发挥关键作用。它们被形象地比作“微型无人机”，能够实现药物和治疗分子的靶向递送，从而提高治疗精准度并降低侵略性。源自间充质干细胞（MSCs）的EVs因其多向分化潜力而尤具临床价值。

EVs疗法大规模、标准化生产是其走向临床的关键挑战。本研究引入了核磁共振（NMR）作为过程分析技术（PAT），能实时、精确监测生物反应器中细胞培养物的生化状态，确保细胞代谢最佳并符合优良制造规范（GMP）的严格要求。NMR/PAT结合GMP是保障EVs产品质量、安全性和有效性的基石，对于其临床转化至关重要。

这项研究显著推动了再生医学发展。EVs的应用前景广阔，可用于心肌梗死后的组织修复和血管再生，以及在肿瘤治疗中调节免疫反应、减轻炎症，甚至在基因治疗中递送RNA。由于EVs为非细胞疗法，免疫原性低，排斥风险小，有望彻底改变现有疗法，开启精准医学新篇章。

The Blood and Tissue Bank is studying how to therapeutically manufacture and use a type of nanoparticles released by the body's cells to communicate with one another, in one of the most promising fields of research for the therapies of the future. New medicines and advanced therapies modify cells (immune cells), genes and tissues to treat diseases such as cancer. However, the next step is to focus on these subcellular packages loaded with biological information, known as extracellular vesicles (EVs), which perform essential functions such as tissue regeneration and the modulation of inflammation.

The Blood and Tissue Bank (BST), in collaboration with the Germans Trias i Pujol Research Institute (IGTP) and the Reus-based technology company Biosfer Teslab, is playing a leading role in the development and improvement of these therapies through the publication of research aimed at enabling the large-scale manufacture of regenerative medicine products based on these cell-to-cell "information packages," known as extracellular vesicles (EVs).

Therapies based on these nanoparticles are not yet part of routine clinical practice. Many are still under development, and work is ongoing to demonstrate their safety, the appropriate route, dose and treatment regimen, as well as their efficacy, before they can become widely used treatments.

The study coordinated by the BST represents a significant boost for the advancement of this therapeutic field and has been published in the Journal of Extracellular Vesicles (JEV). The research demonstrates the potential of a specific technology: Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) as a Process Analytical Technology (PAT) to monitor, optimize and scale up the manufacture of these extracellular vesicles (EVs). The EVs used are derived from mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), which have the unique ability to differentiate into other tissues in the body (bone, cartilage or muscle), and have great potential as the basis for new therapies in regenerative medicine and immunology.

"These nanoparticles could represent a genuine revolution in medicine. They could become a kind of microscopic drone capable of delivering medicines and regenerative molecules directly to the cells that need them, making treatments more precise and less aggressive," explained Dr. Joaquim Vives from the BST Research Department. They could be used, for example, in patients who have suffered a myocardial infarction, "by injecting these nanoparticles into the damaged heart tissue so that the healthy cells can signal and trigger revascularization and regeneration of the damaged tissue," Vives explained, with a much lower risk of rejection because they are not cell-based therapies.

NMR makes it possible to determine precisely the biochemical status of the cell culture inside the bioreactors where these vesicles are secreted. In other words, it allows researchers to assess whether cell metabolism is optimal and aligned with compliance with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) requirements, which are essential for this type of therapy.

Multiple therapeutic possibilities

The functions of these nanoparticles are wide-ranging. They act as communication elements with the immune system and are involved in processes such as tissue repair and cell growth, among others. They are currently used as highly useful diagnostic biomarkers for the early detection of diseases, as they are released by tumors and damaged tissues and can be detected directly in blood samples.

In the future, these nanoparticles could become key biological tools for the targeted delivery of drugs and therapeutic molecules to the cells that need them, as well as for modulating the immune response, reducing excessive inflammation or even enhancing the immune response against tumors. They also open the door to applications in gene therapies through RNA delivery, as well as to the repair of tissues such as the heart, bones and nervous system.

血液和组织库正在研究如何治疗性地生产和利用人体细胞释放的一种用于相互通信的纳米颗粒，这是未来疗法最有前景的研究领域之一。新药和先进疗法通过修饰细胞（免疫细胞）、基因和组织来治疗癌症等疾病。然而，下一步重点是关注这些富含生物信息的亚细胞包裹，即细胞外囊泡（EVs），它们具有组织再生和炎症调节等重要功能。

血液和组织库（BST）与德国人特里亚斯·伊·普霍尔研究所（IGTP）以及总部位于雷乌斯的科技公司 Biosfer Teslab 合作，正通过发表研究，在这些疗法的开发和改进中发挥主导作用，旨在实现基于这些细胞间“信息包”——即细胞外囊泡（EVs）——的再生医学产品的大规模生产。

基于这些纳米颗粒的疗法尚未进入常规临床实践。许多仍处于开发阶段，在它们能够成为广泛使用的治疗方法之前，还需要持续努力证明其安全性、合适的给药途径、剂量和治疗方案，以及其疗效。

由 BST 协调的这项研究为该治疗领域的发展提供了显著推动，并已发表在《细胞外囊泡杂志》（JEV）上。该研究展示了一种特定技术的潜力：核磁共振（NMR）作为一种过程分析技术（PAT），可用于监测、优化和扩大这些细胞外囊泡（EVs）的生产。所使用的 EVs 源自间充质干细胞（MSCs），MSCs 具有分化成体内其他组织（骨骼、软骨或肌肉）的独特能力，在再生医学和免疫学的新疗法中具有巨大潜力。

“这些纳米颗粒可能代表着医学领域的一场真正革命。它们可以成为一种微型无人机，能够将药物和再生分子直接输送到需要它们的细胞，从而使治疗更加精准且侵略性更小，”BST 研究部门的 Joaquim Vives 博士解释道。他举例说，它们可以用于心肌梗死患者，“通过将这些纳米颗粒注射到受损的心脏组织中，健康的细胞可以发出信号并触发受损组织的血管再生和修复，”Vives 解释说，由于它们不是基于细胞的疗法，因此排斥风险大大降低。

核磁共振（NMR）使得精确测定分泌这些囊泡的生物反应器内细胞培养物的生化状态成为可能。换句话说，它允许研究人员评估细胞代谢是否处于最佳状态，并符合优良制造规范（GMP）的要求，这对于此类疗法至关重要。

多种治疗可能性

这些纳米颗粒的功能广泛。它们充当与免疫系统沟通的要素，并参与组织修复和细胞生长等过程。它们目前被用作疾病早期诊断的非常有用的诊断生物标志物，因为它们由肿瘤和受损组织释放，可以直接在血液样本中检测到。

未来，这些纳米颗粒可能成为关键的生物工具，用于向需要的细胞靶向递送药物和治疗分子，以及调节免疫反应、减轻过度炎症或甚至增强抗肿瘤免疫反应。它们还为通过 RNA 递送进行基因治疗以及心脏、骨骼和神经系统等组织的修复开辟了应用前景。
