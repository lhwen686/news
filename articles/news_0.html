Online reviews reveal troubling side effects of insomnia drug lemborexant

深度解析

这篇文章深入探讨了新型失眠药物来波雷生（lemborexant）在真实世界中的患者体验。其核心专业概念在于来波雷生独特的作用机制：作为一种食欲素受体拮抗剂，它通过特异性阻断食欲素（一种促醒神经肽）的信号通路来促进睡眠，而非像传统安眠药那样广泛抑制中枢神经系统。这种靶向作用旨在提供更接近生理睡眠的模式。 然而，研究揭示了药物在临床试验与现实应用间的差异。虽然临床试验显示其疗效和较低的副作用发生率，但在线评论显示部分患者报告了显著的副作用，如睡眠麻痹和生动噩梦。这可能与药物导致REM睡眠（梦境睡眠）增加有关，REM睡眠期间肌肉张力消失和强烈梦境体验可能“溢出”到清醒状态。这种差异强调了药物警戒的重要性，即通过真实世界证据补充临床试验数据，以全面评估药物的安全性与耐受性。 对行业的影响是深远的：它促使制药公司和临床医生重新审视药物开发和患者管理策略。精准医疗成为关键，需根据个体患者的反应和期望调整治疗方案。此外，该研究也启发了对食欲素系统在精神疾病相关睡眠障碍中作用的进一步探索，预示着此类药物未来可能扩展到更广泛的治疗领域。

英文原文

Original
Researchers from Rutgers and other universities analyzed hundreds of online reviews on lemborexant – and learned that about 1 in 4 of the reviewers stopped or planned to stop taking the prescription medication used to treat insomnia. Insomnia – difficulty falling or staying asleep – affects about 30% of adults, according to prior sleep research. Lemborexant, approved in the U.S. in 2019, belongs to the newest class of insomnia drugs. Rather than broadly sedating the brain like many older sleeping pills, these medicines block signals from orexin, a chemical messenger that helps keep people awake. In clinical trials, lemborexant (sold as Dayvigo) helped people fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer than a placebo. Ongoing work at Rutgers Brain Health Institute is investigating the potential repurposing of orexin-based sleep medications to treat sleep disturbances in a range of psychiatric conditions, including addiction. To learn how patients experience the drug in everyday life, researchers from Rutgers Brain Health Institute, Virginia Commonwealth University and University of Sydney analyzed 260 online reviews of lemborexant, posted between 2020 and 2023. The results of the study were published in the journal Sleep Medicine. Team members classified 70 of the 260 reviewers as having stopped or planning to stop. These reviewers were less likely to say the drug had helped, yet they were about as likely as everyone else to complain of poor or broken sleep. Side effects most clearly set them apart. Across all reviewers, ratings split sharply. The most common score was 1, followed by 10. Roughly a third of reviewers said the drug meaningfully improved their sleep. "It feels like I am sleeping naturally like I did once upon a time," one person wrote online about lemborexant, rating it 10 out of 10. Another person rated the same drug a 1. "Felt paralyzed to move. Wanted to scream and could not make a sound," the reviewer noted. "Was able to touch my husband's arm and waken him. He said he never saw such terror in my eyes." That person was describing sleep paralysis, a temporary state of being awake but unable to move or speak. Sleep paralysis came up in 40% of reviews from people who stopped or planned to stop, compared with 15% of the rest. Vivid dreams or nightmares were more common, as was next-day grogginess. Many reviewers said side effects drove them to stop. "The dreams [nightmares] are VERY vivid and that's the reason I can no longer take this medication," one wrote. "What surprised us most was how unsettling some patients found certain side effects," said Abanoub J. Armanious, a pharmacoepidemiologist and neuropsychiatry researcher with the Rutgers Brain Health Institute and lead author of the study. "For these individuals, stopping the medication was not simply about it failing to improve sleep." One proposed explanation for the nightmares and sleep paralysis involves dreaming sleep, when the brain normally switches off most of the body's muscles. Blocking orexin can increase dreaming sleep, which may let vivid dreams and muscle shutdown spill into waking moments. "One of the important messages from our study is that lemborexant was reported to work very well for some people," said Morgan James, the senior author of the study, adjunct Assistant Professor at Rutgers Brain Health Institute and Horizon Fellow and senior lecturer at the Brain and Mind Centre and School of Psychology at the University of Sydney. But half of the reviewers reported little or no benefit, and two-thirds mentioned at least one side effect. About 1 in 5 brought up sleep paralysis, and nearly a third described vivid dreams or nightmares. Not every dream was unwelcome. One reviewer, who rated the drug a 9, looked forward to the "wild crazy dreams." The researchers emphasized that the numbers can't show how common any of these experiences are among all users, because people with the best or worst experiences are most likely to post. In lemborexant's two main clinical trials, which tracked everyone enrolled, fewer than 2 in 100 participants taking it had sleep paralysis, and about 1 to 3 in 100 reported nightmares or abnormal dreams. "A clinical trial can tell you how often side effects occur," Armanious said. "It is less equipped to tell you how much that side effect matters to the person living it. A side effect can be rare and still be the reason someone discontinues treatment." Most reviewers, especially those who stopped or planned to stop, had used the drug for less than a month. Longer-term users generally rated it higher, though the study cannot say why. The researchers said they hope people will know about these side effects without being alarmed. Anyone taking a prescribed sleep medication should talk with a clinician before stopping or changing it. "What really stands out is how different people's experiences with this medication can be, which highlights the importance of setting expectations and finding the right treatment for the individual," James said. This research was funded in part by the National Institute on Drug Abuse, National Institutes of Health, grant numbers DA045765 and DA063103, as well as the University of Sydney (Horizon Fellowship). The content is solely the responsibility of the authors and does not necessarily represent the official views of the National Institutes of Health.

Armanious）说，“对于这些人来说，停药不仅仅是因为它未能改善睡眠。” 关于噩梦和睡眠麻痹的一种解释涉及梦境睡眠（即REM睡眠），此时大脑通常会关闭身体大部分肌肉。阻断食欲素可能会增加梦境睡眠，这可能导致生动的梦境和肌肉关闭溢出到清醒时刻。 “我们研究的一个重要信息是，来波雷生对一些人来说效果非常好，”该研究的资深作者、罗格斯大学脑健康研究所兼职助理教授、悉尼大学脑与精神中心及心理学院地平线研究员兼高级讲师摩根·詹姆斯（Morgan James）说。 但半数评论者表示效果甚微或无效，三分之二的评论者至少提到一种副作用。大约五分之一的人提到了睡眠麻痹，近三分之一的人描述了生动的梦境或噩梦。 并非所有的梦境都是不受欢迎的。一位给药物评9分的评论者，期待着“狂野离奇的梦”。 研究人员强调，这些数字不能显示这些经历在所有用户中的普遍程度，因为体验最好或最差的人最有可能发表评论。在来波雷生的两项主要临床试验中，对所有参与者进行了跟踪，服用该药的参与者中，每100人中只有不到2人出现睡眠麻痹，约1到3人报告噩梦或异常梦境。 “临床试验可以告诉你副作用发生的频率，”阿马尼奥斯说，“但它不太能告诉你这种副作用对亲身经历它的人有多重要。副作用可能很罕见，但仍然是某人中断治疗的原因。” 大多数评论者，尤其是那些停止或计划停止用药的人，使用该药的时间不到一个月。长期使用者通常给出了更高的评分，尽管研究无法解释原因。 研究人员表示，他们希望人们了解这些副作用，但不要因此感到恐慌。任何服用处方安眠药的人，在停药或改变用药前，都应咨询临床医生。 “真正突出的是人们对这种药物的体验差异之大，这凸显了设定预期和为个体找到正确治疗方案的重要性，”詹姆斯说。 本研究部分由国家药物滥用研究所（国立卫生研究院，资助号DA045765和DA063103）以及悉尼大学（地平线奖学金）资助。内容仅代表作者观点，不一定代表国立卫生研究院的官方观点。
