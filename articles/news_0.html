深度解析

本文揭示了通过利用真实世界证据（RWE）推动儿童肿瘤研究，特别是对于急性髓系白血病（AML）等罕见疾病的关键范式转变。传统的随机对照试验（RCTs）虽对常见病有效，但因患者群体小，在罕见儿童癌症中面临显著可行性挑战，常导致研究周期长、新疗法可及性有限。 “真实世界：急性髓系白血病”项目通过系统收集和分析患者电子健康记录中的真实世界数据（RWD），直接应对了这一挑战。RWD可生成RWE，从而优化临床试验设计，并可能支持外部对照臂等创新方法。外部对照臂将研究性治疗与接受标准护理的类似患者的历史数据进行比较，能够加速药物评估，增加试验中接受新型活性疗法的儿童比例，从而惠及更多患儿。 此外，PedAL主临床试验通过全面的基因组筛查，展现了精准肿瘤学的实践。识别儿童白血病的独特分子生物学特征，有助于制定个性化治疗策略，包括靶向疗法，如Menin抑制剂（扎夫曼尼布）或维奈克拉，超越了“一刀切”的治疗模式。Olivia应用程序等技术的整合，使家庭能够通过共享数据积极参与研究，同时获得个性化的健康信息和教育资源。这种以患者为中心的模式，结合同情用药计划，不仅加速了治疗开发，也为儿科罕见病研究构建了一个更具包容性、数据驱动的生态系统，为其他复杂治疗领域树立了应用典范。

Original
Blood Cancer United® today announced the launch of Dare to Dream REALworld: AML, a patient-centered initiative co-founded with Rally Foundation for Childhood Cancer Research. The initiative will engage current and former pediatric acute myeloid leukemia (AML) patients and their families to build real-world evidence that can inform research and future clinical trials, helping accelerate the development of new treatments. For children with rare diseases like AML, time matters. Because patient populations are small, clinical trials can be difficult to conduct, and research can move more slowly than families can afford to wait. REALworld: AML gives children and families an opportunity to contribute directly to research while helping us build the evidence researchers need to design more efficient studies and advance better treatment options." Gwen Nichols, M.D., chief medical officer, Blood Cancer United REAL stands for Real-world Evidence Accelerating New Treatments. REALworld: AML was co-founded by Blood Cancer United and Rally Foundation for Childhood Cancer Research, whose early support helped make the initiative possible. Tempus (NASDAQ: TEM), is providing technology to support the initiative, including Olivia, its personal health concierge app. "Families confronting pediatric AML urgently need research approaches designed around the realities of rare childhood cancers," said Dean Crowe, Founder and CEO, Rally Foundation for Childhood Cancer Research. "We are proud to have helped co-found REALworld: AML and support an initiative that puts families at the center of research. By building high-quality evidence from the experiences of children with AML, we have an opportunity to advance treatment development while creating a model that could ultimately benefit children facing other rare cancers." Families who participate can contribute directly to research while gaining access to Olivia, which brings a patient's health information together in one place, helping families access, organize and better understand their records. Through Olivia, participants will also receive educational information from Blood Cancer United based on their child's diagnosis, treatment, and support needs. "The promise of Olivia is to ensure every patient receives the full benefit of what medicine offers, while also accelerating scientific discovery to improve those benefits," said Chris Scotto DiVetta, SVP of Olivia at Tempus. "Olivia puts meaningful health and educational resources directly into the hands of families, while creating new opportunities for them to play an active role in advancing pediatric AML research. We're thrilled to be part of the Dare to Dream Project and contribute to an initiative that has the potential to make a meaningful difference for children and families." Building evidence to advance pediatric treatment and care REALworld: AML aims to create a rigorous dataset that researchers can use to better understand pediatric AML and improve the design of future clinical trials. Over time, the data may also support external control arms, allowing researchers, when scientifically and regulatorily appropriate, to compare patients receiving an investigational treatment with carefully validated information from similar patients who previously received standard treatment. This approach could allow more children in a trial to receive investigational therapy and help researchers evaluate promising treatments more efficiently. The initiative comes as the U.S. Food and Drug Administration continues to advance guidance on the use of real-world data in clinical research and regulatory decision-making. The approach has particular potential in pediatric cancers and other rare diseases, where small patient populations can make traditional randomized trials difficult. Helping the next family For Abby Breyfogle, the promise of REALworld: AML is deeply personal. Her twin daughters, Kenedi and Kendal, were both diagnosed with AML shortly after birth. After about six months in the hospital undergoing chemotherapy, both girls went into remission. Kendal later relapsed and died in September 2017. Kenedi remains in remission today. Abby is turning her family's experience into an opportunity to help others. She has shared Kenedi's health records and plans to share Kendal's, so both of her daughters' experiences can contribute to pediatric AML research. Families can currently contribute to this research by sharing electronic health records from children who are living with or in remission from pediatric AML. The project also recognizes the importance of learning from children who have died from the disease and expects to be able to include their records in the near future. "When you've lived through pediatric AML, you understand how urgently families need better options. Knowing that researchers can learn from both Kenedi's and Kendal's experiences means so much to our family. Our hope is that what we've been through can help the next family who hears the words 'your child has cancer.'" Families interested in participating in REALworld: AML can learn more here: Dare to Dream REALworld: AML | Blood Cancer United Transforming treatment and care REALworld: AML is part of Dare to Dream, Blood Cancer United's initiative to transform treatment and care for children with blood cancer through biomedical research, patient support, advocacy, and clinical research innovation. At the heart of Dare to Dream is PedAL, the first pediatric acute leukemia Master Clinical Trial. Through the PedAL screening trial, children receive comprehensive genomic screening to identify the unique biology of their leukemia and help determine whether they may be eligible for a PedAL treatment subtrial or another appropriate clinical trial. To date, PedAL has screened more than 660 children at more than 100 medical centers worldwide and currently includes two treatment arms: one evaluating the use of venetoclax and another evaluating ziftomenib, a menin inhibitor being studied for certain forms of acute leukemia. Through Dare to Dream, Blood Cancer United also recently preserved access to an investigational therapy for children with a rare and aggressive form of AML by acquiring the existing drug supply and making the treatment available to eligible patients through a compassionate use program. Dare to Dream also expands access to education, personalized support, financial assistance resources, and supports advocacy efforts for families facing blood cancer.

对于艾比·布雷福格尔而言，“真实世界：急性髓系白血病”项目的承诺具有深刻的个人意义。她的双胞胎女儿凯内迪和肯达尔在出生后不久均被诊断出患有急性髓系白血病。在医院接受了大约六个月的化疗后，两姐妹都进入了缓解期。肯达尔后来复发并于2017年9月去世。凯内迪至今仍处于缓解期。 艾比正在将她家庭的经历转化为帮助他人的机会。她已经分享了凯内迪的健康记录，并计划分享肯达尔的记录，以便她两个女儿的经验都能为儿童急性髓系白血病研究做出贡献。 目前，家庭可以通过分享患有儿童急性髓系白血病或处于缓解期的儿童的电子健康记录来为这项研究做出贡献。该项目也认识到从因该疾病去世的儿童那里获取经验的重要性，并预计在不久的将来能够纳入他们的记录。 “当你亲身经历过儿童急性髓系白血病时，你就会明白家庭是多么迫切地需要更好的选择。得知研究人员可以从凯内迪和肯达尔的经历中汲取经验，这对我们家庭意义重大。我们希望我们的经历能帮助下一个听到‘你的孩子得了癌症’这句话的家庭。” 有兴趣参与“真实世界：急性髓系白血病”项目的家庭可在此了解更多信息：敢于梦想真实世界：急性髓系白血病 | 血癌联合 变革治疗与护理 “真实世界：急性髓系白血病”是“敢于梦想”项目的一部分，该项目是血癌联合通过生物医学研究、患者支持、倡导和临床研究创新，变革儿童血癌治疗和护理的倡议。 “敢于梦想”项目的核心是PedAL，这是首个儿童急性白血病主临床试验。通过PedAL筛查试验，儿童接受全面的基因组筛查，以识别其白血病的独特生物学特征，并帮助确定他们是否符合PedAL治疗子试验或其他适当临床试验的条件。迄今为止，PedAL已在全球100多个医疗中心对660多名儿童进行了筛查，目前包括两个治疗组：一个评估维奈克拉（venetoclax）的使用，另一个评估扎夫曼尼布（ziftomenib），后者是一种正在研究用于治疗某些急性白血病形式的Menin抑制剂。 通过“敢于梦想”项目，血癌联合近期还通过收购现有药物供应，并通过一项同情用药计划向符合条件的患者提供治疗，从而为患有罕见且侵袭性急性髓系白血病的儿童保留了获得研究性疗法的机会。“敢于梦想”项目还扩大了教育、个性化支持、经济援助资源的可及性，并支持面临血癌的家庭的倡导工作。
