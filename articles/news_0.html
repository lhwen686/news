Scientists identify metabolic pathway driving lung cancer lymph node metastasis

深度解析

这项研究通过整合单细胞RNA测序和空间转录组学，揭示了肺癌淋巴结转移中肿瘤细胞代谢重编程与免疫微环境重塑的关键机制。核心专业概念在于MOCCI介导的氧化磷酸化（OXPHOS）增强，促进转移性肺癌细胞（PLMCs）分泌生长分化因子15（GDF15）。GDF15通过作用于Tfh细胞表面的转化生长因子β受体2（TGFBR2），导致Tfh细胞功能耗竭，进而抑制邻近B细胞的活化，形成有利于肿瘤进展的免疫抑制微环境。 技术原理上，空间转录组学弥补了传统单细胞测序缺乏细胞空间定位的缺陷，使得研究人员能精准分析淋巴结内不同细胞类型间的局部相互作用，特别是GDF15阳性PLMC与Tfh细胞之间的联系。这种多组学结合的方法为理解复杂的肿瘤免疫微环境提供了前所未有的深度。 其行业影响深远：首先，MOCCI和OXPHOS途径成为潜在的治疗靶点，为开发新型抗转移药物提供了新思路。通过抑制MOCCI或OXPHOS，有望逆转GDF15驱动的免疫抑制，恢复抗肿瘤免疫应答。其次，MOCCI可作为肺癌淋巴结转移和预后的生物标志物，有助于临床患者的风险分层和个体化治疗决策。尽管研究队列规模较小，但其提供的多组学图谱为精准肿瘤学研究奠定了基础，未来在更大规模临床队列中的验证将加速这些发现向临床实践的转化。

英文原文

Original
Metabolic reprogramming plays a critical role in shaping the adaptive capabilities of malignant cells during dissemination. Metastatic lymph nodes have an immunosuppressive microenvironment, but the characteristics and underlying mechanisms of immune-cell dysfunction remain poorly characterized. MOCCI, short for Modulator of Cytochrome C Oxidase during Inflammation, functions as a component of mitochondrial complex IV under inflammatory conditions. The absence of spatial context in single-cell RNA sequencing has also limited the analysis of cell–cell interactions, particularly within lymph nodes. Previous single-cell studies have inferred metabolic reprogramming without clarifying how metabolically rewired tumor cells engage specific immune cells within lymph nodes in a localized manner. Researchers at Fudan University Shanghai Cancer Center, with collaborators at the Shanghai Institute of Materia Medica, Chinese Academy of Sciences, published (DOI: 10.20892/j.issn.2095-3941.2025.0587)the study in Cancer Biology & Medicine. The team, led by corresponding authors Haiquan Chen, Yang Zhang, and Xiaoming Zhang, analyzed matched primary tumors, metastatic and uninvolved lymph nodes, and peripheral blood from 10 patients with lung cancer. They profiled 37 samples by single-cell RNA sequencing and 27 tissue samples by spatial transcriptomics, capturing 671,467 cells across the two datasets. They also used T-cell and B-cell receptor sequencing, functional assays, mouse models and an independent clinical cohort. PLMCs showed significant enrichment of the OXPHOS pathway, and MOCCI expression correlated with OXPHOS activity. MOCCI knockdown in PC9 and A549 lung cancer cells reduced migration and invasion by more than 50% compared with controls. MOCCI overexpression increased mitochondrial respiration and reactive oxygen species generation, and promoted lymph node metastasis in mice. MOCCI-overexpressing PC9 and A549 cells secreted more growth differentiation factor 15 (GDF15); OXPHOS inhibition reduced GDF15 secretion. Tfh cells were more abundant in metastatic lymph nodes than in primary tumors and exhibited dysfunctional antigen-presentation programs. Naive and memory B cells were also enriched, with greater clonal diversity but blunted clonal expansion. Spatial analyses showed that Tfh cells expressing transforming growth factor beta receptor 2 (TGFBR2) within 40 micrometers of GDF15-positive PLMCs had increased inhibitory signatures and reduced MHC-II signatures compared with those near GDF15-negative PLMCs. In an independent cohort of 875 lung cancer cases, MOCCI expression was higher in patients with lymph node metastasis and correlated with higher nodal status. In lung adenocarcinoma cohorts, higher MOCCI expression was associated with poorer overall survival. The authors regard MOCCI as a key driver of PLMC fate. In their proposed model, MOCCI-mediated OXPHOS reprogramming enhances GDF15 secretion, which acts through TGFBR2 to promote Tfh cell exhaustion and suppress activation of neighboring B cells. They conclude that these findings link metabolic adaptation to immune remodeling during lung cancer dissemination. The study points to MOCCI-driven OXPHOS reprogramming as a potential target for intercepting lymph node metastasis. The integrated single-cell and spatial multi-omics atlas provides a resource for further exploration of the cellular states and spatial interactions underlying lymph node metastasis. The authors note that the multi-omics cohort included only 10 patients and that spatial heterogeneity among samples limited the identification of shared characteristics. They call for validation in larger cohorts and further experimental studies of the mechanisms underlying metastasis establishment and microenvironment remodeling. 肿瘤细胞在播散过程中，代谢重编程对其适应能力起着关键作用。转移性淋巴结具有免疫抑制性微环境，但免疫细胞功能障碍的特征及其潜在机制仍不明确。MOCCI，即炎症时细胞色素c氧化酶调节剂，在炎症条件下作为线粒体复合体IV的一个组分发挥作用。单细胞RNA测序缺乏空间背景，也限制了细胞间相互作用的分析，尤其是在淋巴结内。此前的单细胞研究推断了代谢重编程，但并未阐明代谢重编程的肿瘤细胞如何在淋巴结内局部地与特定免疫细胞发生作用。 复旦大学附属肿瘤医院的研究人员与中国科学院上海药物研究所的合作者在《癌症生物学与医学》杂志上发表了这项研究 (DOI: 10.20892/j.issn.2095-3941.2025.0587)。该团队由通讯作者陈海泉、张杨和张晓明领导，分析了10名肺癌患者的配对原发肿瘤、转移性和未受累淋巴结以及外周血样本。他们通过单细胞RNA测序对37个样本进行了分析，并通过空间转录组学对27个组织样本进行了分析，总共从这两个数据集中捕获了671,467个细胞。他们还使用了T细胞和B细胞受体测序、功能性实验、小鼠模型和一个独立的临床队列。 转移性淋巴结内的肺癌细胞（PLMCs）显示出氧化磷酸化（OXPHOS）途径的显著富集，并且MOCCI的表达与OXPHOS活性相关。在PC9和A549肺癌细胞中，MOCCI的敲低使细胞迁移和侵袭能力较对照组降低了50%以上。MOCCI过表达增加了线粒体呼吸和活性氧生成，并促进了小鼠的淋巴结转移。MOCCI过表达的PC9和A549细胞分泌了更多的生长分化因子15 (GDF15)；OXPHOS抑制则减少了GDF15的分泌。转移性淋巴结中Tfh细胞比原发肿瘤中更丰富，并表现出功能失调的抗原提呈程序。幼稚和记忆B细胞也富集，具有更大的克隆多样性，但克隆扩增受损。空间分析显示，在距GDF15阳性PLMC 40微米范围内的表达转化生长因子β受体2 (TGFBR2) 的Tfh细胞，与靠近GDF15阴性PLMC的Tfh细胞相比，抑制性特征增加，MHC-II特征减少。在一项包含875例肺癌病例的独立队列中，淋巴结转移患者的MOCCI表达较高，并与更高的淋巴结状态相关。在肺腺癌队列中，MOCCI高表达与较差的总体生存率相关。 作者认为MOCCI是PLMC命运的关键驱动因素。在他们提出的模型中，MOCCI介导的OXPHOS重编程增强了GDF15的分泌，GDF15通过TGFBR2作用，促进Tfh细胞耗竭并抑制邻近B细胞的活化。他们总结道，这些发现将肺癌播散过程中的代谢适应与免疫重塑联系起来。 该研究指出MOCCI驱动的氧化磷酸化重编程是阻断淋巴结转移的潜在靶点。整合的单细胞和空间多组学图谱为进一步探索淋巴结转移的细胞状态和空间相互作用提供了资源。作者指出，该多组学队列仅包含10名患者，并且样本间的空间异质性限制了共享特征的识别。他们呼吁在更大的队列中进行验证，并对转移建立和微环境重塑的潜在机制进行进一步的实验研究。 来源：中国科学院 期刊参考：Zhang, Y., et al.
