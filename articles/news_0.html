Phase 3 EMT2 trial demonstrates survival benefit for high-dose EPA

深度解析

该新闻报道了3期EMT2试验的一项重要发现：在结直肠癌肝转移术前术后使用高剂量二十碳五烯酸（EPA）可显著延长患者的总生存期，但对无进展生存期（PFS）没有显著影响。这一结果在医学上具有深远的专业意义。EPA作为一种omega-3脂肪酸，其机制可能并非直接的肿瘤杀伤，而是通过调控机体对癌症的全身性反应。文章中提及的可能机制包括改善恶病质、减轻炎症反应以及调节免疫功能，这与免疫治疗中常见的PFS和OS不一致现象有异曲同工之处——即治疗可能不直接缩小肿瘤，但能增强宿主抗肿瘤免疫或改善全身状况，从而延长生命。 这一发现对行业产生了多方面影响。首先，若该结果得到进一步证实，EPA将为结直肠癌肝转移患者提供一种安全、耐受性好且成本相对较低的辅助治疗选择，填补了现有治疗的空白。其次，它将推动癌症领域对营养干预，特别是omega-3脂肪酸在肿瘤免疫调节和宿主全身状态改善方面的深入研究。这种PFS与OS分离的模式也提示研究者，在特定临床场景下，OS可能更能反映患者的真实临床获益，并促使人们重新审视肿瘤治疗的临床终点评估标准。未来，可能会有更多研究探索EPA在不同癌种中的作用及其与现有疗法的联合应用，为患者带来新的希望。

英文原文

Original
Patients assigned to receive high-dose eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA), an omega-3 fatty acid, before and after surgery for colorectal cancer liver metastases had longer overall survival than those assigned to receive placebo, according to results of the phase 3 EMT2 trial presented at the ESMO Congress 2026. Surgery to remove colorectal cancer that has spread to the liver offers the possibility of long-term survival, but recurrence remains common. Preclinical and observational studies have suggested that EPA may have activity against colorectal cancer, while an earlier phase 2 trial indicated a possible survival benefit. The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled EMT2 trial enrolled 418 patients scheduled for liver resection with curative intent at 13 UK hepatobiliary surgery centers. Patients were assigned to 4g daily of EPA, as high-purity prescription icosapent ethyl, or mineral oil placebo. Treatment began at least one day before surgery and was planned to continue for two to four years, even after cancer progression. Other cancer treatments were broadly comparable, with 20% in each group receiving adjuvant chemotherapy. Estimated five-year overall survival, the trial's key secondary endpoint, was 62.0% with EPA versus 45.1% with placebo. EPA was associated with a 32% lower hazard of death (hazard ratio [HR] 0.68; 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.48-0.96; p = 0.03). Median overall survival had not been reached with EPA versus 59.0 months with placebo. The survival benefit was consistent in the per-protocol analysis (HR 0.60; 95% CI 0.36-0.99). It also remained consistent after adjustment for diabetes, which was more common at baseline in the EPA group (16% versus 8%), and in other sensitivity analyses. However, median progression-free survival (PFS) was similar in the two groups (13.5 versus 12.9 months; HR 0.89; 95% CI 0.70-1.13; p = 0.33). EPA was well tolerated, with no excess adverse events, bleeding or atrial fibrillation compared with placebo, and no negative impact on quality of life. "The overall survival benefit seen with EPA in EMT2, if confirmed in other studies, is clinically meaningful for the group of patients who have had surgery for bowel cancer spread to the liver and in whom the cancer frequently comes back," said lead author Professor Mark Hull, Leeds Institute of Medical Research, University of Leeds, UK. "This is particularly the case as EPA is well-tolerated and safe, even when other cancer treatments are needed." Hull suggested the survival benefit might reflect effects on the body's response to cancer, including cachexia, which can involve weight loss, reduced appetite and fatigue. Longer overall survival but no significant improvement in progression-free survival has also been seen in trials of cancer immunotherapy, and more research is needed to work out whether EPA works by improving the body's immune response to cancer." Mark Hull, Professor, Leeds Institute of Medical Research, University of Leeds, UK "The results are definitely intriguing, from both a biological and a clinical point of view," said Dr Chiara Cremolini, University of Pisa, Italy, who was not involved in the study. "The lack of concordance between progression-free survival and overall survival results requires careful interpretation." She noted that PFS is a less established indicator of overall survival after surgery for liver metastases than after surgery for early-stage bowel cancer, and that additional data would help, including the treatments patients received after their cancer came back and the pattern of spread at the time of progression. "Confirmatory results are likely needed before incorporating this treatment option into daily clinical practice," said Cremolini, noting that patients having surgery for liver metastases vary widely in the biological and molecular characteristics of their cancer and in their previous treatment, which future trials could take into account. "Acknowledging the difficulty of conducting clinical trials, and especially academic research, in this setting, the authors should be highly congratulated for their successful effort," she concluded.

根据在2026年ESMO大会上公布的3期EMT2试验结果，接受高剂量二十碳五烯酸（EPA，一种omega-3脂肪酸）的结直肠癌肝转移患者，在术前和术后与接受安慰剂的患者相比，其总生存期更长。 手术切除已扩散至肝脏的结直肠癌为患者提供了长期生存的可能性，但复发仍然常见。临床前和观察性研究表明，EPA可能对结直肠癌具有活性，而早期一项2期试验则提示可能存在生存获益。 这项随机、双盲、安慰剂对照的EMT2试验招募了418名计划在英国13家肝胆外科中心接受根治性肝切除术的患者。患者被分配到每日4克EPA（以高纯度处方药二十碳五烯酸乙酯形式），或矿物油安慰剂组。治疗在手术前至少一天开始，并计划持续二至四年，即使在癌症进展后也继续。其他癌症治疗大致相当，两组中均有20%的患者接受辅助化疗。 五年总生存率是该试验的关键次要终点，EPA组为62.0%，而安慰剂组为45.1%。EPA与死亡风险降低32%相关（风险比[HR] 0.68；95%置信区间[CI] 0.48-0.96；p = 0.03）。EPA组的中位总生存期尚未达到，而安慰剂组为59.0个月。在符合方案分析中，生存获益保持一致（HR 0.60；95% CI 0.36-0.99）。在调整了糖尿病（EPA组基线时更常见，16%对8%）后，以及在其他敏感性分析中，生存获益也保持一致。然而，两组的中位无进展生存期（PFS）相似（13.5个月对12.9个月；HR 0.89；95% CI 0.70-1.13；p = 0.33）。 EPA耐受性良好，与安慰剂相比，没有出现额外的不良事件、出血或心房颤动，对生活质量也没有负面影响。 “如果EMT2试验中观察到的EPA带来的总生存获益能在其他研究中得到证实，那么对于那些因肠癌扩散至肝脏而接受手术且癌症常复发的患者群体而言，这将具有重要的临床意义，”英国利兹大学利兹医学研究所的首席作者Mark Hull教授说。“鉴于EPA的良好耐受性和安全性，即使在需要其他癌症治疗时也是如此，这一点尤为重要。” Hull教授提出，生存获益可能反映了EPA对身体抗癌反应的影响，包括恶病质，其可能涉及体重减轻、食欲下降和疲劳。 “在癌症免疫疗法试验中也观察到总生存期延长但无进展生存期无显著改善的现象，需要更多研究来阐明EPA是否通过改善身体对癌症的免疫反应来发挥作用。”英国利兹大学利兹医学研究所教授Mark Hull表示。 “从生物学和临床角度来看，这些结果无疑是引人入胜的，”未参与这项研究的意大利比萨大学Chiara Cremolini博士说。“无进展生存期和总生存期结果之间缺乏一致性，需要仔细解读。”她指出，与早期肠癌手术后相比，PFS作为肝转移手术后总生存期的指标尚不那么成熟，并且需要更多数据，包括患者在癌症复发后接受的治疗以及进展时的扩散模式。 “在将这种治疗方案纳入日常临床实践之前，可能还需要进一步的验证性结果，”Cremolini说，她指出接受肝转移手术的患者，其癌症的生物学和分子特征以及既往治疗史差异很大，未来的试验可以考虑这些因素。“鉴于在这种背景下开展临床试验，特别是学术研究的难度，作者们为他们的成功努力应获得高度赞扬，”她总结道。
