New AI e-nose device brings digitized smell to wearable technology

深度解析

本文介绍了一项在数字嗅觉领域的重大突破：结合了人工智能电子鼻和Scentronic可穿戴气味释放系统的创新。这项技术通过整合常被忽视的嗅觉，超越了主要依赖视觉和听觉线索的传统人机界面。从技术原理上看，人工智能电子鼻通过检测和分析构成气味的挥发性有机化合物（VOCs），并利用先进的模式识别算法来识别特定气味。随后，Scentronic利用机器学习实时分类这些嗅觉数据，并精确输送预设气味，实现了受控的嗅觉刺激。这项技术在医疗健康领域具有深远影响。鉴于嗅觉丧失与帕金森病、阿尔茨海默病等多种神经系统疾病的密切关联，这些设备有望成为关键的诊断工具，或用于监测疾病进展。此外，它们预示着个性化治疗干预的可能，通过定制化剂量的特定气味来提升患者舒适度，并有望减少频繁的临床就诊需求。在医学之外，这项重建和输送数字气味的能力将显著丰富扩展现实（XR）体验和虚拟训练环境，为真正沉浸式的多感官交互和人机通信的新时代铺平道路。

英文原文

Original
Our sense of smell shapes how we navigate our environment, but it's a sensation that's notoriously difficult to recreate in the digital world. To overcome this challenge, researchers have developed a device that uses an artificial intelligence e-nose to detect and analyze odors. Combined with a wearable release system called Scentronic, which uses machine learning algorithms to classify data in real time, the system passed the smell test in the lab by encouraging better interactions between humans and machines. This work combines innovations in hardware and software to bring new solutions to societal challenges. Our long-term goal here is to incorporate these systems into compact and personalized devices." Jinghua Li, senior author of the study and assistant professor of materials science and engineering, The Ohio State University Used in everything from medical research to online game entertainment, human-to-machine interfaces serve as digital conduits that connect people across incredible distances. Yet the majority of these systems focus on biophysical signals, such as haptic (touch-related), audio and visual cues, rather than smell, a sense critical for perception, flavor and emotional experiences. However, this study establishes a framework for an accurate, hands-free controlled olfactory stimulation device. After being trained on five distinct food scents - coffee, beer, red wine, orange and chocolate cake - field testing revealed that participants could identify individual odors about 73% of the time, and paired scents about 71% of the time. The team also found that even when Scentronic was used in tandem with interfaces that could generate taste, their results demonstrated the feasibility of programmable, cost-effective odor delivery systems to create engaging multisensory experiences, said Li. "We did see that some people are more sensitive to certain odorants than others," she said. "Despite being easy to distinguish, there was still lots of variation in intensity among individuals." The study was recently published in Science Advances. Despite being a prototype, Scentronic's design is so flexible that researchers created multiple versions to serve potential consumers. These include a wristband-type device that would allow users to carry it and lift it to the nose when they want to catch a whiff of a scent, and a necklace version where five odor channels align like pearls on a string, opening only after a signal is received. Although initially fitted with the user's comfort and functionality in mind, the ease with which these sensors were adapted to further demonstrations highlights the system's seamless ability to enhance extended reality experiences beyond mere sight and sound, said Li. Such versatility suggests Scentronic has many potential real-world applications, from supporting future assistive technologies and enhancing virtual training environments to becoming sensory aids in medical contexts, including as a way to reduce the need for frequent clinic visits, improve patient comfort and enable timely, personalized care, said Li. "The loss of olfaction is related to many neurological diseases and infections," she said. "As a result, it's important that we create technologies to first decouple the secrets within our olfactory systems and then try to reconstruct them based on that information." Still, while it'll be a long time before the internet is fully scratch-and-sniff capable, next steps involve advancing the work by testing other ways to incorporate the team's devices into people's daily accessories, including wearable electronics like watches or other smart jewelry. "There are many potential opportunities for these technologies to shine," she said. "The initial demonstrations are promising, so digitized smells may arrive sooner than you think." Other Ohio State co-authors include Chunyu Yang, Qi Wang, Antonia Akoto-Yeboah, Shaoru Cheng, Billings Amoah, Yizhen Jia, Shulin Chen, Akhil Canumalla, Kai Zhao and Christopher Simons, as well as Chen Wang from Zhejiang Laboratory in China. This work was supported by the National Science Foundation, the Center for Brain Injury Recovery & Discovery Pilot Award at Ohio State, and the Ohio State Alumni Grant for Graduate Research and Scholarship.

AI Translation
我们的嗅觉塑造着我们感知环境的方式，但它是一种在数字世界中臭名昭著地难以重现的感觉。 为克服这一挑战，研究人员开发了一种设备，它利用人工智能电子鼻来检测和分析气味。该系统与名为Scentronic的可穿戴气味释放系统相结合，后者利用机器学习算法实时对数据进行分类，通过促进人机之间更好的交互，成功通过了实验室的气味测试。 俄亥俄州立大学材料科学与工程助理教授、该研究的资深作者Jinghua Li表示：“这项工作结合了硬件和软件方面的创新，为社会挑战带来了新的解决方案。我们的长期目标是将这些系统整合到紧凑型和个性化的设备中。” 人机界面被广泛应用于医疗研究到在线游戏娱乐等各个领域，它们作为数字通道连接着相隔遥远的人们。然而，这些系统中的大多数都侧重于生物物理信号，例如触觉、听觉和视觉线索，而不是嗅觉——一种对感知、风味和情感体验至关重要的感官。 然而，这项研究建立了一个用于精确、无需动手操作的受控嗅觉刺激设备的框架。在对咖啡、啤酒、红酒、橙子和巧克力蛋糕这五种不同食物气味进行训练后，现场测试显示，参与者识别单一气味的准确率约为73%，识别成对气味的准确率约为71%。 Li表示，研究团队还发现，即使Scentronic与能够产生味觉的界面结合使用，其结果也证明了可编程、经济高效的气味传输系统在创造引人入胜的多感官体验方面的可行性。 她说道：“我们确实观察到，有些人对某些气味物质比其他人更敏感。尽管这些气味易于区分，但个体之间的感知强度仍存在很大差异。” 这项研究最近发表在《科学进展》杂志上。 尽管Scentronic仍是原型产品，但其设计极其灵活，研究人员为此创造了多个版本以服务潜在消费者。其中包括腕带式设备，用户可以随身携带，并在需要嗅闻气味时将其举到鼻前；以及项链式版本，其中五个气味通道像串珠一样排列，只在接收到信号后才打开。 Li表示，尽管最初的设计考虑到了用户的舒适度和功能性，但这些传感器易于适应进一步演示的特点，突显了该系统超越单纯视觉和听觉，无缝增强扩展现实体验的能力。 Li指出，这种多功能性表明Scentronic具有许多潜在的现实世界应用，从支持未来的辅助技术和增强虚拟训练环境，到成为医疗环境中的感官辅助工具，包括作为减少频繁就诊需求、提高患者舒适度和实现及时个性化护理的一种方式。 她表示：“嗅觉丧失与许多神经系统疾病和感染有关。因此，重要的是我们要首先创造技术来揭示我们嗅觉系统中的奥秘，然后尝试基于这些信息进行重建。” 然而，尽管互联网要实现完全的“刮擦即闻”功能尚需时日，但下一步的工作将通过测试其他方式，将团队的设备整合到人们的日常配饰中，包括手表或其他智能首饰等可穿戴电子产品。 她说道：“这些技术有许多潜在的机会大放异彩。最初的演示结果令人鼓舞，因此数字化的气味可能比你想象的更早到来。” 俄亥俄州立大学的其他合著者包括Chunyu Yang、Qi Wang、Antonia Akoto-Yeboah、Shaoru Cheng、Billings Amoah、Yizhen Jia、Shulin Chen、Akhil Canumalla、Kai Zhao和Christopher Simons，以及来自中国浙江实验室的Chen Wang。 这项工作得到了美国国家科学基金会、俄亥俄州立大学脑损伤恢复与发现中心试点奖，以及俄亥俄州立大学校友研究生研究与奖学金基金的资助。
