深度解析

本研究揭示了新冠疫情后持续存在的睡眠障碍与疲劳问题，即便在解封之后，且独立于SARS-CoV-2感染状态，凸显了疫情本身所带来的精神压力对公众健康的深远影响。从医学机制上看，慢性压力和焦虑可导致下丘脑垂体肾上腺（HPA）轴功能失调、神经递质失衡及炎症反应升高，进而破坏正常的睡眠结构与昼夜节律，形成恶性循环。文章强调，无序睡眠不仅降低免疫力、加剧炎症，还严重影响情绪、认知功能及抑郁风险，这些生理改变与长新冠（PCS）患者常见的疲劳症状密切相关。 这一发现对医疗健康行业具有重要影响。它预示着对睡眠医学专科、睡眠障碍诊断（如多导睡眠图）和治疗方案（如认知行为疗法失眠症CBT-I）的需求将显著增加。临床实践中，将常规睡眠评估纳入对持续性疫后疲劳及长新冠患者的诊疗流程变得至关重要。此外，这也推动了针对睡眠与疲劳的创新疗法研发，包括药物和非药物干预，以及数字健康技术在睡眠监测与管理中的应用。从公共卫生角度看，提升公众对睡眠健康的认知及提供干预措施，是应对后疫情时代健康挑战的关键一环。

英文原文

Original
Disrupted sleep and anxiety may be an ongoing issue for many people after the pandemic, whether due to COVID-19 directly or to the stress and anxiety caused by the global pandemic, and may contribute to the fatigue commonly reported by people with Post-COVID-19 Syndrome (PCS, or 'long COVID'). Based on UK COVID Symptom Study Biobank data collected in the early years of the pandemic, experts from King's College London and Flinders University in Australia have carried out one of the first large-scale longitudinal studies to assess the extent of residual or continuing sleep and related mental health problems, including people with PCS. The high levels of both poor sleep and fatigue established during lockdowns persisted after restrictions were lifted – independent of SARS-CoV-2 infection, or whether individuals reported having Post-COVID Syndrome. Our data fit with results from around the world – that the experience of the pandemic itself was associated with sleep disturbance globally." Emma Duncan, co-senior author, Professor of Clinical Endocrinology at King's College London and co-founder of the COVID Symptom Study Biobank Flinders University co-senior author Professor Sutapa Mukherjee, Professor in Respiratory and Sleep Medicine at the Flinders Health and Medical Research Institute (FHMRI) Sleep Health research group says: "We knew from pre-pandemic clinical experience that once sleep disturbance is present, it is unlikely to improve without intervention. "Given the high prevalence of both sleep disturbance and fatigue in some people after the COVID shutdowns, addressing sleep disturbance may be part of a patient's long-term recovery and wellbeing. "We recommend routine assessment of sleep for people presenting with persistent post-pandemic fatigue, including those with the persistent symptoms associated with PCS (Long COVID)," says Professor Mukherjee. The authors had previously assessed sleep disturbance and fatigue following the 2021 UK lockdown, showing extremely high levels of poor sleep and fatigue across the whole community during the pandemic. In the current study, the same individuals were reassessed after lockdowns were lifted in 2022 – with questionnaire responses from 2390 people regarding their sleep, fatigue, depression and anxiety. Adjusted for other factors, the new study published in Journal of Sleep Research found that high levels of poor sleep and fatigue persisted after lockdowns were lifted – regardless of whether respondents had contracted COVID-19. Disordered sleep has a range of health consequences, including reduced immunity, increased inflammation and worse mental health such as mood, cognition and depression. Another Flinders University co-author, research fellow Barbara Toson, adds: "Many people assume sleep returns to normal once major life disruptions end. However, our study found that sleep disturbance and fatigue often persisted after lockdown restrictions were lifted. "Our findings suggest that sleep disturbance and fatigue can persist long after major pandemic disruptions have ended, reinforcing the need for greater attention to sleep health and post-pandemic recovery," says Ms Toson, from the Flinders Health and Medical Research Institute Sleep Health research group. Researchers conclude that "Fatigue is the commonest symptom reported by individuals with PCS. If sleep disturbance is contributing to fatigue in an individual with PCS, then using proven effective therapeutic approaches may contribute meaningfully to their symptom recovery." The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic continues, with current infections leading to serious illness and hospitalization and an estimated 1.9 million people in the UK experiencing self-reported long COVID-19 symptoms of more than four weeks after confirmed or suspected infection.

疫情过后，许多人可能会持续面临睡眠障碍和焦虑问题，这既可能直接由COVID-19引起，也可能源于全球疫情造成的压力和焦虑，并可能导致新冠后综合征（PCS，或称“长新冠”）患者普遍报告的疲劳。 根据英国新冠症状研究生物样本库在疫情初期收集的数据，伦敦国王学院和澳大利亚弗林德斯大学的专家们进行了一项首批大规模纵向研究，旨在评估残留或持续性睡眠及相关心理健康问题的程度，其中包括PCS患者。 “在封锁期间出现的严重睡眠不足和疲劳，在限制解除后依然持续存在——这与SARS-CoV-2感染无关，也与个体是否报告患有新冠后综合征无关。我们的数据与世界各地的研究结果相符——疫情本身的经历与全球范围内的睡眠障碍有关。”伦敦国王学院临床内分泌学教授、新冠症状研究生物样本库联合创始人艾玛·邓肯（Emma Duncan）说，她是该研究的共同资深作者。 弗林德斯大学的共同资深作者、弗林德斯健康与医学研究所（FHMRI）睡眠健康研究小组的呼吸与睡眠医学教授苏塔帕·穆克吉（Sutapa Mukherjee）教授表示：“我们从疫情前的临床经验中了解到，一旦出现睡眠障碍，如果没有干预措施，它就不太可能自行改善。” “鉴于新冠疫情停摆后，一些人的睡眠障碍和疲劳普遍存在，解决睡眠障碍可能成为患者长期康复和福祉的一部分。” 穆克吉教授说：“我们建议对出现持续性疫后疲劳的人群进行常规睡眠评估，包括那些伴有与PCS（长新冠）相关持续症状的患者。” 研究作者此前曾评估了2021年英国封锁后的睡眠障碍和疲劳情况，结果显示在疫情期间，整个社区的睡眠不足和疲劳水平极高。在当前研究中，同样一批受试者在2022年封锁解除后再次接受评估——通过对2390人的问卷调查，了解他们的睡眠、疲劳、抑郁和焦虑情况。 这项发表在《睡眠研究杂志》上的新研究发现，在调整其他因素后，严重睡眠不足和疲劳在封锁解除后依然持续存在，无论受访者是否感染过COVID-19。 睡眠紊乱会导致一系列健康后果，包括免疫力下降、炎症增加以及情绪、认知和抑郁等心理健康状况恶化。 弗林德斯大学的另一位共同作者、研究员芭芭拉·托森（Barbara Toson）补充道：“许多人认为一旦重大的生活干扰结束，睡眠就会恢复正常。然而，我们的研究发现，在封锁限制解除后，睡眠障碍和疲劳常常持续存在。” 来自弗林德斯健康与医学研究所睡眠健康研究小组的托森女士说：“我们的发现表明，睡眠障碍和疲劳在重大疫情干扰结束后仍能持续很长时间，这强调了需要更加关注睡眠健康和疫情后恢复。” 研究人员总结道：“疲劳是PCS患者报告的最常见症状。如果睡眠障碍导致了个体PCS患者的疲劳，那么采用经验证有效的治疗方法可能对他们的症状恢复做出有意义的贡献。” COVID-19大流行的影响仍在持续，目前的感染导致重症和住院，英国估计有190万人报告在确诊或疑似感染后四周以上出现长新冠症状。
