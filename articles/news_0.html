科学家发现70种现有药物能阻断致命性汉坦病毒感染

Scientists discover 70 existing drugs that block deadly hantavirus infections

本研究通过大规模药物重定向筛选，为奥尔索汉坦病毒感染治疗带来了突破性进展。其核心专业概念在于“高通量表型药物筛选”，该技术利用活病毒在人细胞系中直接评估化合物的抗病毒效应，而非基于预设靶点的传统方法，从而能发现意想不到的作用机制。研究成功识别了70种“宿主靶向疗法”，这些疗法通过干扰病毒复制所需的细胞通路来抑制感染。最引人瞩目的发现是β-内酰胺类抗生素表现出抗病毒活性，这为药物重定向提供了新的视角，因其安全性特征明确且易于获得。行业影响深远：这些“临床就绪型候选药物”可跳过早期药物研发的漫长阶段，直接进入动物模型验证，极大加速了治疗方案的开发进程。对于汉坦病毒这类“被忽视的全球威胁”，此前缺乏特效药，此次发现有望提供首个获批的治疗方案，改善全球公共卫生状况。
Original Text
A massive drug-repurposing screen of over 5,000 compounds has uncovered unexpected antiviral powers in common antibiotics, offering a fast-tracked pipeline to treat life-threatening orthohantavirus infections.
Study: A drug repurposing screen identifies antiviral compounds against Puumala Orthohantavirus. Image Credit: Kateryna Kon / Shutterstock
In a recent study published in the journal Scientific Reports, researchers conducted a high-throughput phenotypic drug screening using live Puumala virus (PUUV) to fast-track therapeutic discovery against disease caused by orthohantaviruses.
The study screened 5,256 compounds and pinpointed 70 validated host-directed therapies that successfully suppressed viral infection across human lung and endothelial cells. Study findings further mapped key cellular pathways essential for viral replication and highlighted immediate candidates (including hitherto unexpected antibiotics) for clinical exploration against potentially lethal, orthohantavirus-caused diseases such as hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) and hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS).
Orthohantaviruses are a genus of rodent-borne viruses that are known to cause life-threatening zoonotic infections in humans through the latter’s inhalation of aerosols contaminated with rodents’ excretions (urine, feces, or saliva). Intensive medical research has revealed that specific orthohantaviral strains are responsible for one of two severe viral diseases: 1. Hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS), a disease endemic to the Americas with a documented mortality rate between 30% to 40%, and 2. Hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) is a less severe but still potentially lethal (~1% mortality rate) disease prevalent across Europe and Asia.
The Puumala virus (PUUV) was discovered in 1980 as the primary pathogen responsible for HFRS in Europe. The virus was found to trigger severe flu-like symptoms in infected individuals, alongside intense abdominal pain and sudden renal failure. Despite decades of subsequent research aimed at identifying pharmacological interventions against PUUV, no European Medicines Agency (EMA)- or Food and Drug Administration (FDA)- approved vaccines or specific treatments have yet been identified, thereby restricting clinical interventions against the disease to supportive symptom management.
The present study aimed to overcome the methodological and computational limitations of previous anti-orthohantavirus drug discovery attempts by developing an automated, microscopy-based platform to observe live PUUV interacting with human host cells.
Study data were obtained from the Drug Repurposing Hub library, which comprises an extensive collection of 5,256 FDA-approved, clinical, and pre-clinical small molecules. These molecules were screened for their anti-PUUV potential using a two-step process, which initially leveraged A549 human lung adenocarcinoma epithelial cells for primary screening purposes, and subsequently utilized human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs; primary human vascular cells) to validate molecules that passed the initial screening to replicate the virus's real-world tendency to attack human blood vessels.
The automation component of the study’s methodological approach was implemented by incubating the human cell lines with the selected small molecules (“drugs”) for 6 hours, followed by the addition of live PUUV at a multiplicity of infection (MOI) of 3. After 24 hours, cells were fixed and labeled by immunofluorescence with convalescent patient sera to detect viral proteins.
Simultaneously, the blue fluorescent dye “Hoechst 33342” and “CellTracker” were used to stain cell nuclei and cell bodies, respectively. Following successful staining, high-content automated microscopes were used to scan the plates, and quantitative analysis (in the Cell Profiler program) measured the precise ratio of infected cells to total viable cells.
The study’s primary screening platform (A549 cell line) demonstrated an initial development Z-prime of 0.7 and an average Z-prime of 0.41 across the full-scale screen. This framework was statistically validated to detect shifts in viral replication, with control treatments (such as the antiviral drug Ribavirin) significantly reducing baseline infection rates (p < 0.0001 for the example provided).
Encouragingly, of the 5,256 molecules tested in the primary screen, 151 were recognized as candidate antiviral drugs, each able to decrease the infection rate at least 2-fold compared to controls. These analyses also revealed 23 proviral candidates (e.g., histone deacetylase [HDAC] inhibitors such as CI994) that significantly increased (up to 400%) the number of infected cells, thereby informing future PUUV-intervention policy.
Subsequent multi-dose validation trials successfully confirmed 70 of the primary antiviral hits across both cell systems. Among these validated antivirals, 34 compounds demonstrated robust activity in both cell types, while 25 were specifically active in A549 lung cells and 11 showed a preference for HUVEC vascular cells. For the flagged proviral candidates, 21 of 23 primary hits were confirmed, demonstrating a clinically relevant effect predominantly in the A549 cell line.
These 70 validated antiviral hits were found to cluster tightly into definitive biological mechanisms: 1. Nucleotide biosynthesis inhibitors (e.g., mycophenolic acid and related inosine monophosphate dehydrogenase (IMPDH) blockers), 2. Rapamycin (mTOR) pathway and heat shock protein (HSP90) inhibitors, and 3. Beta-lactam antibiotics (e.g., cefodizime).

This milestone drug-repurposing study provides the scientific knowledge required to transition hantavirus research from narrow, hypothesis-driven exploration to a systematic blueprint of host-virus dependencies. The discovery of the antiviral properties of beta-lactam antibiotics introduces an unexpected and low-toxicity therapeutic angle. Moving forward, these 70 clinical-ready candidates provide an immediate, prioritized pipeline for animal model validation, bringing science closer to the first authorized treatment for this neglected global threat.

AI 机器翻译
一项对超过5000种化合物进行的大规模药物重定向筛选，意外地揭示了常用抗生素的抗病毒能力，为治疗危及生命的奥尔索汉坦病毒感染提供了一条快速通道。

研究：药物重定向筛选发现针对普马拉奥尔索汉坦病毒的抗病毒化合物。图片来源：Kateryna Kon / Shutterstock

在《科学报告》杂志近期发表的一项研究中，研究人员利用活体普马拉病毒（PUUV）进行了高通量表型药物筛选，以加速发现针对奥尔索汉坦病毒所致疾病的治疗方法。

该研究筛选了5256种化合物，并确定了70种经过验证的宿主靶向疗法，这些疗法在人肺细胞和内皮细胞中成功抑制了病毒感染。研究结果进一步描绘了病毒复制所必需的关键细胞通路，并指出了用于临床探索的即时候选药物（包括此前意想不到的抗生素），以对抗可能致命的奥尔索汉坦病毒引起的疾病，例如肾综合征出血热（HFRS）和汉坦病毒肺综合征（HPS）。

奥尔索汉坦病毒是一类由啮齿动物传播的病毒，已知可通过人类吸入受啮齿动物排泄物（尿液、粪便或唾液）污染的气溶胶而导致危及人生命的动物源性感染。深入的医学研究表明，特定的奥尔索汉坦病毒株导致两种严重的病毒性疾病之一：1. 汉坦病毒肺综合征（HPS），一种美洲地方性疾病，记录的死亡率在30%至40%之间；2. 肾综合征出血热（HFRS），一种严重程度较低但仍可能致命（约1%死亡率）的疾病，在欧洲和亚洲普遍存在。

普马拉病毒（PUUV）于1980年被发现，是欧洲肾综合征出血热的主要病原体。研究发现，该病毒会引发感染者出现严重的流感样症状，伴随剧烈腹痛和突发性肾衰竭。尽管在随后的数十年中进行了旨在识别针对PUUV的药理学干预措施的研究，但欧洲药品管理局（EMA）或食品药品监督管理局（FDA）批准的疫苗或特异性治疗方法尚未被确定，因此临床上对该疾病的干预措施仅限于支持性症状管理。

本研究旨在通过开发一个基于自动化显微镜的平台，观察活体PUUV与人类宿主细胞的相互作用，以克服以往抗奥尔索汉坦病毒药物发现尝试中存在的方法学和计算限制。

研究数据来源于药物重定向中心库，该库包含5256种FDA批准、处于临床阶段和临床前阶段的小分子。这些分子通过两步法筛选其抗PUUV潜力，首先利用A549人肺腺癌上皮细胞进行初步筛选，随后利用人脐静脉内皮细胞（HUVECs；原代人血管细胞）验证通过初步筛选的分子，以模拟病毒在真实世界中攻击人体血管的倾向。

该研究方法学中的自动化部分通过将人类细胞系与选定的小分子（“药物”）共同孵育6小时实现，随后加入感染复数（MOI）为3的活体PUUV。24小时后，细胞经固定并通过免疫荧光法与恢复期患者血清共同标记，以检测病毒蛋白。

同时，蓝色荧光染料“Hoechst 33342”和“CellTracker”分别用于细胞核和细胞体的染色。成功染色后，使用高内涵自动化显微镜扫描培养板，并通过定量分析（在Cell Profiler程序中）测量感染细胞与总活细胞的精确比例。

该研究的初步筛选平台（A549细胞系）显示出0.7的初始开发Z-prime值，并在全规模筛选中平均Z-prime值为0.41。该框架经统计学验证，可检测病毒复制的变化，其中对照治疗（例如抗病毒药物利巴韦林）显著降低了基线感染率（所提供的示例p < 0.0001）。

令人鼓舞的是，在初步筛选中测试的5256种分子中，有151种被认定为候选抗病毒药物，每种药物都能将感染率比对照组降低至少2倍。这些分析还揭示了23种促病毒候选药物（例如组蛋白去乙酰化酶[HDAC]抑制剂，如CI994），它们显著增加了（高达400%）感染细胞的数量，从而为未来的PUUV干预策略提供了信息。

随后的多剂量验证试验成功地在两种细胞系统中证实了70个初步抗病毒“命中”。在这些经过验证的抗病毒药物中，34种化合物在两种细胞类型中均显示出强大的活性，而25种专门在A549肺细胞中活跃，11种则对HUVEC血管细胞表现出偏好。对于被标记的促病毒候选药物，23个初步命中中有21个得到确认，主要在A549细胞系中显示出临床相关效应。

这70种经过验证的抗病毒“命中”被发现紧密聚集在明确的生物学机制中：1. 核苷酸生物合成抑制剂（例如霉酚酸及相关的肌苷单磷酸脱氢酶（IMPDH）阻滞剂），2. 雷帕霉素（mTOR）通路和热休克蛋白（HSP90）抑制剂，以及3. β-内酰胺类抗生素（例如头孢地秦）。

这项具有里程碑意义的药物重定向研究提供了将汉坦病毒研究从狭窄的、假设驱动的探索转变为宿主-病毒依赖性系统蓝图所需的科学知识。β-内酰胺类抗生素抗病毒特性的发现引入了一个出人意料且低毒性的治疗角度。展望未来，这70个已准备好进入临床的候选药物为动物模型验证提供了即时且优先的管线，使科学更接近于为这种被忽视的全球威胁提供首个获批的治疗方案。
