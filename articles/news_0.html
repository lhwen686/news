逆转癌症中辐射诱导的免疫抑制

Reversing radiation-activated immune suppression in cancer

深度解析

本研究揭示了放射治疗对肿瘤免疫微环境的双重影响，并提出了克服放射抵抗的新策略。核心机制在于TET2蛋白在骨髓免疫细胞中的作用。放射治疗可激活TET2，进而从染色质相关RNA（特别是5-甲基胞嘧啶，m5C）上移除化学标记。这一表观遗传修饰导致染色质压实，沉默I型干扰素基因，从而促使髓系细胞转化为髓源性抑制细胞（MDSCs），这些MDSCs会抑制CD8⁺ T细胞的抗肿瘤功能，进而促进肿瘤生长和转移。 关键突破是发现阻断TET2能逆转这一过程。缺乏TET2时，RNA上的m5C标记得以保留，并吸引MBD6蛋白，帮助打开染色质，激活I型干扰素通路。这使得髓系细胞分化为新型的髓源性活化细胞（MDACs），MDACs能够呈递肿瘤抗原并活化CD8⁺ T细胞，从而增强抗肿瘤免疫反应。这一发现为癌症治疗带来了深远影响。TET2抑制剂可能与放射治疗和免疫疗法联用，有望将免疫抑制性的MDSCs转化为免疫激活性的MDACs，从而提高治疗效果并有效抑制癌症转移，为临床开发新型组合疗法提供了明确的靶点和理论依据。

DOI: 10.1016/j.ccell.2026.08.017 Researchers at the University of Chicago have identified a mechanism to make immune cells reprogrammed by radiation therapy attack tumors rather than protect them. The study, published in Cancer Cell, shows that radiation activates a protein called TET2 on immune cells in bone marrow, where it removes chemical tags from RNA and locks these cells into an immune-suppressing state. When the researchers blocked TET2, the same cells began fighting cancer instead, helping radiation and immunotherapy shrink tumors and control metastasis. When radiation helps the tumor Radiation therapy, given to 50% to 60% of cancer patients, kills tumor cells by damaging their DNA. In some cases, however, it also triggers the expansion of myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs), immune cells made in the bone marrow that dampen the body's defenses against cancer. MDSCs travel to tumors and block CD8⁺ T cells, the immune system's main tumor-killing cells. In patients, a rise in MDSCs after radiation is linked to shorter survival and a higher risk of metastasis. To understand what drives this response, the team analyzed blood samples collected before and after treatment from 33 patients with stage IV non-small cell lung cancer. The patients were enrolled in a clinical trial at UChicago Medicine, combining high-dose, precisely targeted radiation with the immunotherapy drugs nivolumab and ipilimumab. Among all immune cell types, MDSCs had the highest levels of TET2, an enzyme that removes chemical tags called methyl groups from genetic material. TET2 levels rose further after radiation, most sharply in patients whose cancer did not respond. "We noticed in a couple of clinical trials that patients who had elevated TET2 following radiation did worse," said senior author Ralph Weichselbaum, M.D., Daniel K. Ludwig Distinguished Service Professor and chair of radiation and cellular oncology at UChicago. This observation fit well with earlier reports that showed better responses to immunotherapy in patients with TET2 mutations in their blood cells, suggesting that TET2 may act as a barrier to effective treatment. Reprogramming myeloid cells To test this hypothesis directly, the researchers studied mouse models in which TET2 was deleted only in myeloid cells. In models of colon cancer, melanoma and lung cancer, these mice showed slower tumor growth after radiation, longer survival and fewer, smaller lung metastases than normal mice. The team found that radiation activates p53, a protein that senses cellular damage, which in turn switches on TET2. This happens in the bone marrow within a day of radiation, before the reprogrammed cells reach the tumor. TET2 is best known for removing methyl groups from DNA, but earlier work by co-senior author Chuan He, Ph.D., the John T. Wilson Distinguished Service Professor of Chemistry and of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at UChicago and a Howard Hughes Medical Institute investigator, showed that it also acts on RNA. In the current study, TET2 removed a mark called 5-methylcytosine (m5C) from chromatin-associated RNAs, which sit on the tightly packaged DNA inside the nucleus. Losing these marks caused chromatin to compact, silencing genes for type I interferon, a signal that alerts the immune system to threats such as cancer. "Our previous work showed that an RNA methylcytosine-based pathway is the main mechanism underlying the tumor suppressor function of TET2. It is gratifying to see that the same RNA pathway also accounts for TET2-mediated immune modulation," He said. "It has been a pleasure to collaborate with Weichselbaum's team." Without TET2, the RNA marks stayed in place and attracted a protein called MBD6, which helps open chromatin and keep the interferon pathway active. The myeloid cells then developed into a previously undescribed cell type named myeloid-derived activating cells (MDACs). Unlike MDSCs, MDACs present pieces of tumor proteins to CD8⁺ T cells and prime them to attack cancer. Transferring MDACs into tumor-bearing mice slowed tumor growth, and a genetic signature of MDACs was linked to better survival in patients across multiple cancer types. Restoring antitumor immunity Because gene deletion is not an option in patients, the team tested an experimental TET2-blocking drug and observed slow tumor growth, a reduced number and size of lung metastases when the drug was combined with radiation, and an improved response to immunotherapy. "If you block TET2, you change these immunosuppressive myeloid cells into an immunostimulant, antigen-presenting myeloid compartment that enhances immunotherapy and radiotherapy responses in mice and markedly decreases distant metastasis," Weichselbaum said. Therapeutic implications The findings suggest that radiation resistance is not fixed but a reversible program that can be redirected by targeting the epigenetic machinery of immune cells. The chromatin-associated RNAs that control this switch could also serve as drug targets. The team is now exploring how TET2 inhibitors might be tested with radiation therapy in patients. "It points the way toward not only targeting TET2, but targeting these chromatin-associated RNAs," Weichselbaum said. "I think it's a way maybe to prevent or treat metastasis." 芝加哥大学的研究人员发现了一种机制，可以使经放射治疗重编程的免疫细胞攻击肿瘤，而非保护肿瘤。这项发表在《癌细胞》杂志上的研究表明，放射线会激活骨髓免疫细胞上一种名为TET2的蛋白质，该蛋白质会从RNA上移除化学标记，从而将这些细胞锁定在免疫抑制状态。 当研究人员阻断TET2时，同样的细胞反而开始对抗癌症，帮助放射治疗和免疫疗法缩小肿瘤并控制转移。 当放射治疗帮助肿瘤时 放射治疗应用于50%到60%的癌症患者，通过损伤肿瘤细胞的DNA来杀死它们。然而，在某些情况下，它也会触发髓源性抑制细胞（MDSCs）的扩张，这些在骨髓中产生的免疫细胞会抑制身体对抗癌症的防御机制。 MDSCs会迁移到肿瘤处，并阻断CD8⁺ T细胞，即免疫系统主要的肿瘤杀伤细胞。在患者中，放射治疗后MDSCs的升高与较短的生存期和较高的转移风险相关。 为了理解这种反应的驱动因素，研究团队分析了33名IV期非小细胞肺癌患者在治疗前后收集的血液样本。这些患者参加了芝加哥大学医学中心的一项临床试验，该试验将高剂量、精准靶向的放射治疗与免疫治疗药物纳武利尤单抗和伊匹木单抗结合使用。 在所有免疫细胞类型中，MDSCs的TET2水平最高。TET2是一种酶，可以从遗传物质中移除名为甲基的化学标记。放射治疗后，TET2水平进一步升高，在癌症未产生反应的患者中升幅最为显著。 “我们在几项临床试验中注意到，放射治疗后TET2升高的患者预后更差，”资深作者、芝加哥大学放射与细胞肿瘤学系主任兼丹尼尔·K·路德维希杰出服务教授Ralph Weichselbaum医学博士说。 这一观察结果与早期报告吻合，即血液细胞中存在TET2突变的患者对免疫治疗反应更好，这表明TET2可能作为有效治疗的障碍。 重编程髓系细胞 为了直接验证这一假设，研究人员在小鼠模型中进行了研究，其中TET2仅在髓系细胞中被删除。在结肠癌、黑色素瘤和肺癌模型中，这些小鼠在放射治疗后表现出更慢的肿瘤生长、更长的生存期以及比正常小鼠更少、更小的肺转移灶。 研究团队发现，放射线会激活p53，这是一种感知细胞损伤的蛋白质，继而启动TET2。这发生在放射线照射后一天内，在重编程的细胞到达肿瘤之前。 TET2最广为人知的功能是去除DNA中的甲基，但资深共同作者、芝加哥大学化学和生物化学与分子生物学系的约翰·T·威尔逊杰出服务教授兼霍华德·休斯医学研究所研究员Chuan He博士的早期工作表明，它也作用于RNA。 在当前的研究中，TET2从染色质相关RNA上移除了名为5-甲基胞嘧啶（m5C）的标记，这些RNA附着在细胞核内紧密包装的DNA上。失去这些标记会导致染色质压实，从而使I型干扰素基因沉默，I型干扰素是一种向免疫系统发出癌症等威胁警报的信号。 “我们之前的工作表明，基于RNA甲基胞嘧啶的通路是TET2肿瘤抑制功能的主要机制。很高兴看到相同的RNA通路也解释了TET2介导的免疫调节，”He博士说。“能与Weichselbaum的团队合作非常愉快。” 如果没有TET2，RNA标记会保留在原位并吸引一种名为MBD6的蛋白质，MBD6有助于打开染色质并保持干扰素通路的活性。随后，髓系细胞发展成一种以前未被描述过的细胞类型，命名为髓源性活化细胞（MDACs）。与MDSCs不同，MDACs将肿瘤蛋白质片段呈递给CD8⁺ T细胞，并促使其攻击癌症。 将MDACs转移到荷瘤小鼠体内减缓了肿瘤生长，并且MDACs的基因特征与多种癌症患者的更好生存期相关。 恢复抗肿瘤免疫力 由于基因删除在患者中不可行，研究团队测试了一种实验性的TET2阻断药物，并观察到当该药物与放射治疗结合使用时，肿瘤生长减缓，肺转移灶的数量和大小减少，并且对免疫治疗的反应有所改善。 “如果你阻断TET2，你就能将这些免疫抑制性髓系细胞转变为免疫刺激性、抗原呈递性髓系细胞群，从而增强小鼠对免疫治疗和放射治疗的反应，并显著减少远处转移，”Weichselbaum说。 治疗意义 这些发现表明，放射抵抗并非固定不变，而是一种可逆的程序，可以通过靶向免疫细胞的表观遗传机制来重新定向。控制这一开关的染色质相关RNA也可以作为药物靶点。研究团队目前正在探索如何将TET2抑制剂与放射治疗结合应用于患者进行测试。 “这不仅指明了靶向TET2的方向，也指明了靶向这些染色质相关RNA的方向，”Weichselbaum说。“我认为这可能是一种预防或治疗转移的方法。” 出版详情 Fei Ji
