Study identifies potential new ways to treat the most common type of bowel cancer

深度解析

这篇新闻报道揭示了针对微卫星稳定型（MSS）结直肠癌治疗的突破性进展。MSS结直肠癌约占所有结直肠癌的80-85%，但对现有免疫疗法反应不佳，主要原因是其肿瘤突变负荷较低，免疫原性差，难以引发有效的抗肿瘤免疫反应。该研究的核心在于利用人工智能（AI）方法iPhenMap对2000份患者多组学数据（基因、代谢蛋白、临床数据）进行深度整合分析，构建肿瘤的生物学图谱。这种整合性的分析方法能够识别传统单一组学分析中被忽视的肿瘤内在脆弱性或治疗依赖性，例如癌细胞分裂、生长和适应的全新机制。 通过AI发现的脆弱性，研究人员得以确定能有效靶向这些弱点的药物组合。以MEK抑制剂曲美替尼为例，它通过抑制RAS/MAPK信号通路发挥作用，该通路在多种癌症中异常激活。研究表明，在实验室模型中，曲美替尼与其他药物联用能显著抑制肿瘤生长，延长生存期。这种“老药新用”（drug repurposing）的策略具有巨大优势：已获批药物的安全性、药代动力学和药效学特性已知，能大大缩短临床开发周期和成本。这一研究范式有望加速MSS结直肠癌等难治性癌症的个性化治疗方案的临床转化，为大量患者带来新的希望，同时也为其他肿瘤类型的新药发现和旧药新用提供了通用且高效的AI驱动路径。

英文原文

Original
Major new research published today identifies potential new ways to treat the most common type of bowel cancer in Ireland. The study conducted by an international research team led by RCSI University of Medicine and Health Sciences and The Institute of Cancer Research, London, provides a clear roadmap for repurposing existing drugs and developing new ones, to create more effective, personalized treatments for thousands of patients who currently have limited options. Of the 2,500 new bowel cancer cases diagnosed annually in the Ireland, Microsatellite stable (MSS) colorectal cancer accounts for 80-85% of these, but remains challenging to treat, with limited response to many newer therapies. Trials combining chemotherapy with newer targeted drugs or immunotherapies have delivered only modest improvements over current standard treatments. Now, researchers have discovered vulnerabilities in these tumors and have identified the drug combinations – including drugs already approved to treat other tumor types – that could be used to target them, leading to more effective treatments for bowel cancer. Using 2,000 patient tumor samples, the researchers analysed genetic, metabolic protein and clinical data with the help of artificial intelligence (AI) tools, revealing previously unrecognized ways in which the cancer cells divide, grow, adapt and interact with their environment. To do this, the team developed an AI approach called iPhenMap, which can create a biological map of the tumor. Using this map, they were able to spot connections between the different elements of the tumor – such as the genetic and metabolic features – that have been missed in previous studies that looked at the data in isolation. Importantly, they found previously unidentified vulnerabilities in the tumors. They then tested these vulnerabilities in laboratory models and showed that certain drug combinations could work well to target them and treat the cancer. For example, the targeted drug trametinib – approved to treat various cancers including melanoma, lung cancer, and thyroid cancer, with a specific mutation – was shown to significantly reduce tumor growth and extend survival in the laboratory models when combined with other drugs. The researchers hope this work will accelerate the path to clinical testing of new treatment strategies for bowel cancer, using drugs which have already been developed and approved. In addition, the team believe their AI approach could be used to identify more effective drugs for other tumor types. The research published in leading oncology journal Cancer Cell was jointly led by Professor Annette Byrne, Head of the RCSI Precision Cancer Medicine Group, Department of Physiology and Medical Physics and Professor Anguraj Sadanandam, Group Leader, Systems and Precision Cancer Medicine Group, The Institute of Cancer Research, London. Dr Rodrigo Dienstmann, Vall d'Hebron Institute of Oncology, Barcelona and University of Vic–Central University of Catalonia was co- senior author of the study. Patients with MSS RAS-mutant metastatic colorectal cancer urgently need better treatment options, particularly when their disease no longer responds to standard therapy. While these findings remain preclinical, they highlight promising therapeutic opportunities and provide a strong foundation for further development. The next step will be to identify the biological features that predict response and determine how these approaches can be advanced safely towards clinical evaluation." Professor Annette Byrne, Head of the RCSI Precision Cancer Medicine Group Professor Sadanandam added: "A major strength of this research is the scale and diversity of the dataset, which was only possible through collaboration with European partners. By bringing together data from patients across multiple countries and integrating DNA, RNA, epigenetic, protein, metabolic, single-cell and clinical data at this scale, we were able to expose connections between tumor biology and therapeutic dependency that would otherwise be missed. "AI and machine learning will increasingly help cancer researchers to bring these complex data together, not just to classify tumors, but to understand how they behave, identify their vulnerabilities and guide the development and repurposing of treatments, so that patients can live longer and healthier lives." Ensuring the patient voice was embedded throughout the project and that the findings were disseminated in an accessible format was a priority. This research was funded by the European Union's Horizon 2020 Research and Innovation Programme through funding awarded to the COLOSSUS project (www.colossusproject.eu), and by a Research Ireland Project Award to Professor Byrne. COLOSSUS, led by Professor Byrne, brings together leading European and international academics, clinicians, private sector and not-for-profit partners.

AI Translation
今天发表的一项重要新研究确定了爱尔兰最常见大肠癌类型的潜在新治疗方法。这项研究由爱尔兰皇家外科学院（RCSI）医学与健康科学大学和伦敦癌症研究所共同领导的国际研究团队进行，为重新利用现有药物和开发新药提供了一条清晰的路线图，旨在为目前选择有限的数千名患者创造更有效、更个性化的治疗方案。 在爱尔兰每年诊断出的2,500例新增大肠癌病例中，微卫星稳定型（MSS）结直肠癌占80-85%，但其治疗仍然充满挑战，对许多新型疗法反应有限。结合化疗与新型靶向药物或免疫疗法的临床试验，与目前的标准治疗相比，仅取得了适度的改善。 现在，研究人员发现了这些肿瘤的弱点，并确定了可用于靶向这些弱点的药物组合——包括已获批用于治疗其他肿瘤类型的药物——从而为大肠癌带来更有效的治疗方法。 研究人员利用2,000份患者肿瘤样本，借助人工智能（AI）工具分析了基因、代谢蛋白和临床数据，揭示了此前未被识别的癌细胞分裂、生长、适应以及与环境相互作用的方式。 为此，该团队开发了一种名为iPhenMap的AI方法，可以创建肿瘤的生物学图谱。利用这个图谱，他们能够发现肿瘤不同元素之间的联系——例如遗传和代谢特征——而这些联系在以往孤立分析数据的研究中被忽视了。 重要的是，他们发现了肿瘤中此前未识别的弱点。随后，他们在实验室模型中验证了这些弱点，并表明某些药物组合可以很好地靶向这些弱点并治疗癌症。 例如，靶向药物曲美替尼（已获批用于治疗多种癌症，包括黑色素瘤、肺癌和甲状腺癌的特定突变型），在与其它药物联合使用时，在实验室模型中显示出能显著抑制肿瘤生长并延长生存期。 研究人员希望这项工作能够加速大肠癌新治疗策略的临床试验进程，这些策略利用了已开发并获批的药物。此外，该团队相信他们的AI方法也可用于识别其他肿瘤类型更有效的药物。 这项研究发表在领先的肿瘤学期刊《癌细胞》（Cancer Cell）上，由爱尔兰皇家外科学院生理学与医学物理系精准癌症医学组主任Annette Byrne教授和伦敦癌症研究所系统与精准癌症医学组组长Anguraj Sadanandam教授共同领导。巴塞罗那瓦尔德希布伦肿瘤研究所和维克-加泰罗尼亚中央大学的Rodrigo Dienstmann博士是该研究的共同资深作者。 “MSS RAS突变型转移性结直肠癌患者迫切需要更好的治疗方案，特别是当他们的疾病对标准化治疗不再响应时。尽管这些发现仍处于临床前阶段，但它们突出了有前景的治疗机会，并为进一步开发奠定了坚实基础。下一步将是确定预测治疗反应的生物学特征，并明确如何安全地将这些方法推进临床评估。”爱尔兰皇家外科学院精准癌症医学组主任Annette Byrne教授表示。 Sadanandam教授补充说：“这项研究的一个主要优势是数据集的规模和多样性，这只有通过与欧洲合作伙伴的协作才能实现。通过整合来自多个国家的患者数据，并在此规模上整合DNA、RNA、表观遗传学、蛋白质、代谢、单细胞和临床数据，我们得以揭示肿瘤生物学与治疗依赖性之间此前可能被忽视的联系。” “AI和机器学习将越来越多地帮助癌症研究人员整合这些复杂数据，不仅用于肿瘤分类，更重要的是了解肿瘤行为，识别其弱点，并指导治疗方案的开发和重新利用，从而让患者能够更长寿、更健康地生活。” 确保患者声音贯穿整个项目，并以易于理解的形式传播研究结果，是该项目的一个优先事项。 这项研究由欧盟“地平线2020”研究与创新计划通过COLOSSUS项目（www.colossusproject.eu）的资助以及Byrne教授获得的爱尔兰研究项目奖提供资金。COLOSSUS项目由Byrne教授领导，汇集了欧洲和国际领先的学者、临床医生、私营部门和非营利合作伙伴。
