深度解析

FDA批准首款自平衡个人外骨骼Eve，标志着脊髓损伤（SCI）患者康复技术的一项重大突破。Eve的核心技术在于其先进的机器人辅助步态训练系统，通过精密传感器、电机和控制算法，为下肢功能障碍患者提供稳定的躯干和下肢支撑，实现自主站立和手部自由的步态。这与传统的固定式步态训练器或需要手扶的辅助行走设备截然不同，它极大地提升了用户的独立性和功能性。从医学机制来看，这种外骨骼通过模拟正常的生理步态，可以促进神经可塑性，并有助于改善脊髓损伤患者的运动功能。临床试验报告的改善，如降低下肢痉挛、提高坐姿平衡、改善睡眠质量以及膀胱和肠道功能，都反映了活动量增加对全身健康（特别是心血管、骨骼和消化系统）的积极影响。对行业而言，Eve的上市不仅拓宽了康复医疗器械的市场，更预示着一种全新的康复范式。它将康复训练从机构转向家庭环境，赋予患者更大的自主权，并可能减少长期护理的社会和经济负担。这项创新无疑将激励更多研发投入，推动可穿戴机器人技术在康复医学领域的深度应用，最终惠及更广泛的功能障碍人群。

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has cleared Eve (Wandercraft), the first self-balancing personal exoskeleton for eligible adult wheelchair users living with spinal cord injury. Eve enables eligible users who can operate the device's remote control to walk and perform certain activities of daily living hands-free. The device is intended for use on level indoor surfaces and adjacent outdoor areas under the supervision of a specially trained companion. A trial at three clinical sites showed that participants and companions could learn to operate Eve and met key functional and usability endpoints, including measures of walking performance and activities of daily living. Participants reported daily life improvements, including health status, psychological well-being, endurance during daily activities, lower-limb spasticity and sitting balance. Improvements in sleep quality and bladder and bowel function were also reported by some participants. "What makes these findings meaningful is that the technology was evaluated not only on engineering performance but also on how users and companions could safely integrate it into real-world activities," Gail Forrest, Ph.D., director of the Tim and Caroline Reynolds Center for Spinal Stimulation at the Kessler Foundation, one of the clinical trial sites, said in a statement.
