科学家更精准地可视化细胞生命

Scientists visualize cellular life with greater precision

💡 AI 深度解析 (AI Insight)

本研究介绍的“可见光谱抗原稳定荧光纳米抗体”（VIS-Fbs）技术，是细胞成像领域的一项重大突破。其核心原理在于实现了“按需”荧光，即只有当纳米抗体探针结合到其特定靶点时才变得稳定并发出荧光。这有效解决了传统荧光探针因未结合探针产生的背景噪声问题，将背景荧光降低了约一百倍，从而显著提高了成像的清晰度和特异性。此外，VIS-Fbs提供了多色荧光选项和光控开关功能，使得科学家能够同时、高精度地追踪活体系统中多个分子或细胞过程的动态变化。

这项技术对生命科学研究产生了深远影响。它为理解细胞信号传导、发育生物学和疾病进展（如神经元和星形胶质细胞的钙活动，以及肿瘤生长机制）提供了前所未有的清晰视角。其在多种活体模型（如小鼠和斑马鱼）中的成功应用，彰显了其广泛的通用性。未来，VIS-Fbs有望加速药物发现和靶点验证，通过更精确地可视化药物-靶点相互作用，为开发新型诊断工具和治疗策略奠定基础，尤其在神经科学和肿瘤学等领域具有巨大潜力。

Axel Nimmerjahn helped develop a new technology for imaging cells, called visible-spectrum antigen-stabilizable fluorescent nanobodies, which provides a versatile platform for imaging and studying cellular processes in living systems. Credit: Salk Institute

Fluorescent proteins have revolutionized science, enabling researchers to tag and visualize individual molecules in living cells, tissues, and animals. Using these tools, researchers have watched viruses infect cells in real time, observed cellular trash collection, and tracked the signaling that spurs tumor growth.

Salk scientists and collaborators at Albert Einstein College of Medicine have advanced this visualization technology. The new technology, called visible-spectrum antigen-stabilizable fluorescent nanobodies (VIS-Fbs), was validated in multiple mammalian cell types and provides a powerful tool for a wide range of life science research applications.

The study was published in Nature Methods.

"This work establishes a versatile platform for imaging proteins with high specificity and minimal background," says co-corresponding author Axel Nimmerjahn, Ph.D., professor and Françoise Gilot-Salk Chair at Salk. "It opens new opportunities to study how molecular and cellular processes unfold in real time across diverse biological systems."

How can current cellular imaging technology be optimized?

The innovation began with tiny protein fragments called nanobodies, which can be engineered to bind specific protein targets in living cells. When fused to fluorescent proteins, these nanobody-based probes can reveal where target proteins are located and how they behave. However, conventional versions can still generate a signal even when unbound, creating background fluorescence that can obscure fine details.

Mouse brain tissue showing inhibitory neurons labeled with a red fluorescent VIS-Fb that binds to the green calcium biosensor. Neurons are highlighted in blue. Credit: Barykina et al., Nature Methods

The Salk and Einstein team designed a new type of probe that retains the targeting power of nanobodies while greatly reducing background fluorescence. VIS-Fbs become stable and fluorescent only when bound to their intended target. This binding-dependent ("on-demand") fluorescence reduces background noise by up to about a hundredfold, enabling much sharper visualization of protein location and dynamics.

In addition, the researchers developed multiple versions of this new probe that fluoresce across nearly the entire visible spectrum, from blue to far red. With this many color options, multiple cellular targets can be tracked simultaneously.

Certain VIS-Fb variants can also be switched "on" and "off" with light, making it possible to follow protein behavior over time with high spatial and temporal precision. The researchers also established a modular design framework, enabling rapid adaptation of VIS-Fb probes to different targets and functional readouts.

What do scientists study with light probes?

The new technology will allow scientists to gain more accurate, timely insight into cellular activity—even in complex environments like living brain tissue. The researchers demonstrated VIS-Fbs' capabilities across a range of living models.

In mouse models, VIS-Fb probes enabled selective labeling and ratiometric imaging of calcium activity in neurons and astrocytes during behavior. In zebrafish, the technology allowed real-time tracking of dynamic changes during early development and in response to drugs that alter signaling pathways.

Mouse brain tissue showing astrocytes labeled with a red fluorescent VIS-Fb that binds to the green calcium biosensor. Astrocytes are highlighted in purple, while neurons are shown in blue, illustrating cell-type-specific labeling. Credit: Barykina et al., Nature Methods

"Our results show that this imaging platform offers a much clearer and more precise view of how proteins behave inside living systems," says co-corresponding author of the study Vladislav Verkhusha, Ph.D., professor and co-director of the Gruss Lipper Biophotonics Center at Albert Einstein College of Medicine.

"It opens the door to studying complex biological processes, such as cell signaling, development, and disease progression, in new ways."

当前的细胞成像技术如何优化？

这项创新始于被称为纳米抗体的小型蛋白质片段，它们可以被设计成结合活细胞中的特定蛋白质靶点。当与荧光蛋白融合时，这些基于纳米抗体的探针可以揭示靶蛋白的位置和行为。然而，传统版本即使在未结合时也可能产生信号，从而产生背景荧光，遮盖精细细节。

小鼠脑组织，显示抑制性神经元被结合绿色钙生物传感器的红色荧光VIS-Fb标记。神经元以蓝色突出显示。图片来源：Barykina 等人，《自然·方法》

索尔克研究所和爱因斯坦医学院的团队设计了一种新型探针，它保留了纳米抗体的靶向能力，同时大大降低了背景荧光。VIS-Fbs只有在与其预期靶点结合时才变得稳定并发出荧光。这种结合依赖性（“按需”）荧光将背景噪声降低了约一百倍，从而能够更清晰地可视化蛋白质的位置和动态。

此外，研究人员开发了该新型探针的多个版本，其荧光覆盖了几乎整个可见光谱，从蓝色到远红色。有了如此多的颜色选择，可以同时追踪多个细胞靶点。

某些VIS-Fb变体还可以通过光照进行“开启”和“关闭”，从而能够以高空间和时间精度跟踪蛋白质随时间变化的行为。研究人员还建立了一个模块化设计框架，使得VIS-Fb探针能够快速适应不同的靶点和功能读数。

科学家们用光探针研究什么？

这项新技术将使科学家们能够更准确、及时地了解细胞活动——即使是在活脑组织等复杂环境中。研究人员在一系列活体模型中展示了VIS-Fbs的能力。

在小鼠模型中，VIS-Fb探针能够在行为过程中选择性标记和比率成像神经元和星形胶质细胞中的钙活动。在斑马鱼中，该技术允许实时追踪早期发育过程中和响应改变信号通路的药物时的动态变化。

小鼠脑组织，显示星形胶质细胞被结合绿色钙生物传感器的红色荧光VIS-Fb标记。星形胶质细胞以紫色突出显示，而神经元显示为蓝色，说明了细胞类型特异性标记。图片来源：Barykina 等人，《自然·方法》

“我们的结果表明，这个成像平台为蛋白质在活体系统中的行为提供了更清晰、更精确的视角，”该研究的共同通讯作者、阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦医学院格鲁斯·利珀生物光子学中心教授兼联席主任Vladislav Verkhusha博士说。

“它为以新方式研究复杂的生物过程，如细胞信号传导、发育和疾病进展，打开了大门。”
