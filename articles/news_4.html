大豆溶血卵磷脂对抗高盐饮食诱导的高血压和认知障碍

Soy lysolecithin counters high-salt diet-induced hypertension and cognitive impairment

深度解析

本研究深入探讨了溶血卵磷脂LPC70通过调节前列腺素（PGs）信号通路，改善高盐饮食（HSD）引起的高血压和认知功能障碍的医学机制。核心专业概念包括：前列腺素作为局部激素，其在大脑中具有神经保护或神经毒性双重作用；以及LPC70对特定前列腺素受体（如肾脏中的EP3和大脑中的DP1）的差异化调控。研究发现，HSD可增加肾脏中COX-2和EP3的表达，导致血压升高，LPC70则能抑制这些适应不良的变化。同时，HSD降低前额叶皮层DP1（神经保护性受体）表达，LPC70特异性恢复DP1，但不影响DP2（神经毒性受体），从而将PGD2信号转向神经保护。此外，LPC70还通过提高花生四烯酸（AA）的利用率，促进前列腺素生成。这些发现提示LPC70有望成为一种功能性食品成分，在公共卫生领域，可作为膳食干预策略，有效缓解高盐摄入带来的心血管和神经系统损害，对高血压相关认知衰退的预防及促进全球健康老龄化具有重要的临床应用前景和行业影响力。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: The study explores how a soy-derived lysolecithin, LPC70, mitigates HSD-induced hypertension and cognitive impairment, by regulating prostaglandin production and signaling mechanisms. Credit: Professor Akihiro Mouri, Fujita Health University, Japan High-salt (HS) intake is a major global health concern, closely linked to hypertension and growing evidence of cognitive and emotional decline. Although salt is a routine part of daily diets, long-term overconsumption can negatively affect both the cardiovascular system and the central nervous system, underscoring the need for practical dietary strategies to mitigate these adverse health effects. Prostaglandins (PGs) are lipid compounds derived from arachidonic acid (AA) that act as potent local hormones. They maintain cardiovascular homeostasis through a delicate balance of vasodilation and vasoconstriction. In the brain, depending on the receptor involved, PGs can promote either neuroprotective or neurotoxic signaling, thereby influencing emotional and cognitive function. LPC70, a soy lysolecithin in which lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC) comprises more than 70% of phospholipids, has previously been shown to attenuate high-salt diet (HSD)-induced hypertension and cognitive impairment. However, the biological mechanisms, specifically the role of LPC70 in regulating PG signaling, remain unclear. To address this gap, a team of researchers led by Professor Akihiro Mouri and Dr. Hisayoshi Kubota, along with Dr. Kazuhiro Kagotani, studied how LPC70 regulates PG signaling and how this attenuates hypertension and behavioral impairments in a mouse model. Credit: Professor Akihiro Mouri, Fujita Health University, Japan Kubota, the study's first author, made major contributions to the project's execution, including evaluating blood pressure, behavioral phenotypes and PG-related molecular changes. His work helped reveal the organ-specific effects of LPC70 on HSD-induced cardiovascular and cognitive abnormalities. Mouri and Kubota are affiliated with the Department of Regulatory Science for Evaluation and Development of Pharmaceuticals & Devices, Fujita Health University Graduate School of Medical Science, and the International Center for Brain Science (ICBS), Fujita Health University, Japan. Kagotani is affiliated with Tsuji Oil Mills Co., Ltd., Japan, and Tsuji Health and Beauty Science Laboratory, Mie University, Japan. The results were published in Neurochemistry International. "We wanted to understand the role of LPC70 in coordinating changes in PG signaling across organ systems," Mouri said. "Our study originated from a collaborative research project with Tsuji Oil Mills Co., Ltd., a company that develops naturally derived food ingredients," he added. As anticipated, mice fed the HSD developed elevated blood pressure. They also showed reduced social interaction and impaired object-recognition memory, despite no major changes in locomotor activity or anxiety-like behavior. This suggests that HSD selectively affected higher-order behavioral and cognitive functions. LPC70 supplementation attenuated the increased blood pressure and improved both social behavior and object-recognition memory in HSD mice. In the kidney, HSD increased the expression of cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) and the PGE 2 receptor EP3, both of which are involved in PG signaling and may contribute to HSD-induced hypertension. LPC70 reduced these HSD-associated changes, suggesting that it can help normalize maladaptive PG signaling in the kidney. In the prefrontal cortex, a region of the brain involved in cognition and social behavior, HSD reduced expression of the PGD 2 receptor DP1, which mediates neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory signaling. LPC70 restored DP1 expression without affecting DP2, a receptor associated with neurotoxic signaling. This receptor-specific response suggests that LPC70 may help shift PGD 2 signaling toward a more neuroprotective profile, leading to attenuation of behavioral and cognitive impairments. Another key finding involved changes in circulating AA levels, the fatty acid precursor for PG production. Despite reduced circulating AA levels in HSD mice, LPC70 increased AA-derived PGs, including PGE 2 and PGD 2 . This suggests that LPC70 enhances AA utilization, thereby promoting AA-derived PG production under HSD conditions. Together, these findings indicate that LPC70 promotes organ-specific, receptor-dependent PG signaling, suppressing maladaptive pathways in the kidney while restoring neuroprotective signaling in the brain. LPC70 shows promise as a functional food ingredient for mitigating the negative health effects of HS intake. It may support cardiovascular and cognitive health, especially in populations with high dietary salt intake. "Our study elucidates LPC70's role in PG signaling, laying the groundwork for future clinical research and dietary interventions aimed at reducing hypertension-related cognitive decline and promoting healthy aging globally," Mouri concluded. 高盐（HS）摄入是一个主要的全球健康问题，与高血压密切相关，并且越来越多的证据表明其与认知和情感衰退有关。尽管盐是日常饮食的常规组成部分，但长期过量摄入会对心血管系统和中枢神经系统产生负面影响，这凸显了制定实用饮食策略以减轻这些不良健康影响的必要性。 前列腺素（PGs）是花生四烯酸（AA）衍生的脂质化合物，作为有效的局部激素发挥作用。它们通过血管舒张和血管收缩的精妙平衡来维持心血管稳态。在大脑中，根据所涉及的受体不同，前列腺素可以促进神经保护或神经毒性信号传导，从而影响情感和认知功能。 LPC70是一种豆源溶血卵磷脂，其中溶血磷脂酰胆碱（LPC）占磷脂的70%以上，此前已被证明可以减轻高盐饮食（HSD）引起的高血压和认知障碍。然而，其生物学机制，特别是LPC70在调节前列腺素信号传导中的作用，仍不清楚。 为了解决这一空白，由Mouri Akihiro教授和Kubota Hisayoshi博士领导的研究团队，与Kagotani Kazuhiro博士一起，研究了LPC70如何调节前列腺素信号传导，以及这如何在一个小鼠模型中减轻高血压和行为障碍。 图片来源：日本藤田医科大学Mouri Akihiro教授。 该研究的第一作者Kubota对项目执行做出了重大贡献，包括评估血压、行为表型和前列腺素相关的分子变化。他的工作有助于揭示LPC70对高盐饮食引起的心血管和认知异常的器官特异性影响。 Mouri和Kubota隶属于日本藤田医科大学研究生院医药品开发与评价规制科学系，以及藤田医科大学国际脑科学中心（ICBS）。Kagotani隶属于日本辻油磨股份有限公司和日本三重大学辻健康美容科学实验室。 研究结果发表在《神经化学国际》杂志上。 Mouri表示：“我们想了解LPC70在协调跨器官系统的前列腺素信号变化方面的作用。”他补充说：“我们的研究源于与辻油磨股份有限公司的合作研究项目，该公司开发天然来源的食品成分。” 正如预期，接受高盐饮食的小鼠出现了血压升高。它们还表现出社交互动减少和物体识别记忆受损，尽管运动活动或焦虑样行为没有重大变化。这表明高盐饮食选择性地影响了高级行为和认知功能。补充LPC70减轻了高盐饮食小鼠的血压升高，并改善了其社交行为和物体识别记忆。 在肾脏中，高盐饮食增加了环氧合酶-2（COX-2）和PGE2受体EP3的表达，两者都参与前列腺素信号传导，并可能导致高盐饮食引起的高血压。LPC70减少了这些与高盐饮食相关的变化，表明它有助于使肾脏中适应不良的前列腺素信号传导正常化。 在前额叶皮层，一个涉及认知和社交行为的大脑区域，高盐饮食降低了介导神经保护和抗炎信号传导的PGD2受体DP1的表达。LPC70恢复了DP1的表达，而没有影响与神经毒性信号传导相关的DP2。这种受体特异性反应表明LPC70可能有助于将PGD2信号传导转向更具神经保护性的模式，从而减轻行为和认知障碍。 另一个关键发现涉及循环花生四烯酸（AA）水平的变化，花生四烯酸是前列腺素生产的脂肪酸前体。尽管高盐饮食小鼠的循环花生四烯酸水平降低，LPC70却增加了花生四烯酸衍生的前列腺素，包括PGE2和PGD2。这表明LPC70增强了花生四烯酸的利用，从而在高盐饮食条件下促进了花生四烯酸衍生的前列腺素的产生。 总而言之，这些发现表明LPC70促进器官特异性、受体依赖性的前列腺素信号传导，在肾脏中抑制适应不良的通路，同时在大脑中恢复神经保护性信号传导。 LPC70有望作为一种功能性食品成分，用于减轻高盐摄入对健康的负面影响。它可能支持心血管和认知健康，特别是在饮食盐摄入量高的人群中。
