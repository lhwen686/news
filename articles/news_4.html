银屑病患者白内障及白内障手术风险增高

Patients with psoriasis have increased risks for cataracts, cataract surgery

深度解析

这项研究明确揭示了银屑病与白内障及白内障手术风险增加的关联，突显了银屑病作为一种慢性全身性炎症性疾病的系统性影响。从医学机制来看，银屑病持续的炎症反应可能通过全身途径影响晶状体，导致氧化应激，从而增加白内障风险。这强调了银屑病并发症的复杂性。 研究进一步指出，治疗方式对白内障风险影响显著。光疗（PUVA和UVB）与白内障风险增加相关，可能源于紫外线对晶状体的损伤。相反，生物制剂、雅努斯激酶抑制剂及传统疾病修饰抗风湿药等全身治疗则与白内障风险降低相关。这表明有效控制银屑病全身炎症反应，不仅能改善皮肤症状，还能对眼部等并发症产生保护作用。 对行业而言，该研究具有重要临床指导意义。皮肤科医生在选择治疗方案时，应充分考虑白内障风险，特别是对光疗患者需加强眼科监测。全身炎症控制的重要性得到强化，有望推动靶向治疗在银屑病管理中的更广泛应用。同时，也促使皮肤科与眼科专家加强多学科协作，为患者提供更全面的综合管理。

Patients with psoriasis have an increased risk for cataracts and cataract surgery, with the risks for these outcomes varying by treatment modality, according to a study published online Oct. 26 in the Journal of Dermatology. Yan-Jie Su, from Taipei Veterans General Hospital in Taiwan, and colleagues examined the risks for cataract and cataract surgery in patients with psoriasis in a retrospective cohort study. Patients with newly diagnosed psoriasis were matched to nonpsoriasis controls by age, sex, and index year (61,622 patients with psoriasis and 61,622 controls). A nested case-control analysis was then performed among patients with psoriasis who developed cataract or underwent cataract surgery, matched to other patients with psoriasis who did not have these outcomes. The researchers observed associations for psoriasis with increased risks for cataract and cataract surgery (adjusted hazard ratios, 1.32 and 1.41, respectively). Across subgroups stratified by age, sex, and psoriatic arthritis status, these associations were generally consistent. In the nested case-control analysis, increased odds of cataract were seen in association with both psoralen plus ultraviolet A and ultraviolet B phototherapy (adjusted odds ratios, 1.88 and 1.22, respectively), with no clear dose-response relationship. Reduced odds of cataract were seen in association with systemic treatments, including biologics/Janus kinase inhibitors and conventional disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (adjusted odds ratios, 0.49 and 0.74, respectively). For cataract surgery, findings were similar. "This study demonstrated that psoriasis patients had increased risks of cataract and cataract surgery, regardless of age, sex, or the presence of psoriatic arthritis," the authors write. "The risks of these outcomes also appeared to vary according to treatment modality." More information Yan‐Jie Su et al., Risk of Cataract and Its Association With Phototherapy Among Patients With Psoriasis, The Journal of Dermatology (2026).
