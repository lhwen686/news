深度解析

文章深入阐释了寻常痤疮日益严峻的全球公共卫生负担，其核心在于采用“伤残调整生命年”（DALYs）这一关键流行病学指标进行量化。DALYs综合了因疾病导致的健康生命年损失，全面评估了疾病对人群健康和生活质量的整体影响。研究发现，痤疮的DALY负担超越基底细胞癌，并与鳞状细胞癌、黑色素瘤及银屑病等更严重的皮肤病相匹敌，这凸显了痤疮并非仅是美容问题，而是对患者心理健康和社会功能具有显著影响的慢性疾病。 全球发病率和患病率的持续增长，以及至2050年的未来预测，预示着皮肤病学领域将面临巨大挑战。这可能与人口结构、环境暴露、生活方式（如饮食、压力）及诊断意识提高等多种因素有关。对医药行业而言，这强烈驱动着对新型局部和系统性治疗药物、更精准的诊断技术以及先进皮肤护理产品的研发需求。公共卫生部门应加强痤疮的健康教育，促进早期干预，并关注其伴随的心理社会问题。尽管长期预测存在不确定性，但数据明确指出，将寻常痤疮视为一个重要的全球健康问题，并加大研发和管理投入，已成为当务之急。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain The global burden of acne increased from 1990 to 2023 and is set to continue increasing through 2050, according to a study published online in JAMA Dermatology. Pierina Parraga, from Brigham and Women's Hospital in Boston, and colleagues conducted a cross-sectional study to examine the global burden of acne vulgaris using data from the Global Burden of Disease (GBD) Study 2023. The researchers identified an estimated 54.4 million prevalent acne cases globally using the GBD 2023 case definition (674.1 per 100,000 persons); 56% and 44% of cases were among females and males, respectively. There were an estimated 27.8 million incident cases (344.4 per 100,000 persons), with acne accounting for 1.17 million disability-adjusted life years (DALYs; 14.5 per 100,000 persons). No association was seen for acne with mortality, but it contributed to global skin disease burden, with a DALY burden exceeding that of basal cell carcinoma (2,612 DALYs globally) and of similar magnitude to that of squamous cell carcinoma, melanoma, and psoriasis (1.33, 1.82, and 2.65 million DALYs, respectively). Global age-standardized incidence risks, prevalence risks, and DALY rates increased between 1990 and 2023 (67.3%, 63.6%, and 63.5% increases, respectively). By 2050, demography-plus-autoregressive integrated moving average projections estimated 37.5 million incident cases, 77 million prevalent cases, and 1.65 million DALYs globally. "Long-term forecasts remain uncertain, despite incorporating projected demographic changes; they do not propagate the uncertainty in GBD estimates or account for future changes in health care utilization or diagnoses," the authors write. Publication details Pierina Parraga et al., Global Burden of Acne Vulgaris From 1990 - 2023 and Forecasts to 2050, JAMA Dermatology (2026).
