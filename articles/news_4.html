Gut bacteria metabolites directly extend the lifespan of host flies

深度解析

这项研究揭示了肠道微生物群在宿主寿命调节中的关键作用，核心概念是肠道细菌产生的多胺（如腐胺和亚精胺）能够直接延长宿主寿命，且这种作用独立于饮食摄入的多胺。研究团队通过构建精密的已知菌群果蝇模型，并利用基因工程大肠杆菌，成功将细菌产生的多胺与饮食多胺进行区分，从而精确地量化了肠道微生物对宿主健康的贡献。 从医学机制来看，多胺是生物体内广泛存在的小分子有机胺，对细胞生长、分化及应激反应至关重要。本研究初步探索了其延长寿命的分子通路，发现宿主与产多胺细菌的相互作用与TotM和Halo基因表达的下调相关。TotM与JAK/STAT免疫/应激反应通路有关，该通路通常随年龄增长而激活。因此，TotM的下调可能有助于抑制年龄相关的炎症和应激反应，从而促进寿命延长，尽管其精确机制尚需进一步阐明。 这项发现对生物医药行业具有深远影响。首先，它为开发新型功能性食品和益生菌提供了科学依据，未来可设计能优化肠道多胺生产的产品，以促进健康和延长寿命。其次，它为抗衰老研究开辟了新路径，通过靶向肠道微生物代谢产物或相关分子通路，有望开发出延缓衰老进程的干预策略。此外，该研究建立的严谨方法学框架，也为探究其他微生物群来源分子对宿主长期健康的影响提供了蓝图，推动了对宿主-微生物互作复杂性的深入理解，为个性化营养和精准医疗奠定基础。

英文原文

Original
Gut microbiota influences multiple aspects of host physiology, including metabolic and immune functions. Many metabolites produced by gut bacteria can cross the intestinal epithelium (which the bacteria may not be able to cross) and enter host tissues, providing a pathway through which microbial activity can influence host physiology. However, determining the specific contribution of individual gut bacterial metabolites to host lifespan has remained challenging. To address this gap, a research team led by Associate Professor Shin Kurihara from the Faculty of Biology-Oriented Science and Technology, Kindai University, Japan, and Professor Takayuki Kuraishi of Kanazawa University, Japan, used gnotobiotic flies colonized with genetically engineered Escherichia coli (E. coli). This experimental system enabled the researchers to compare flies carrying bacteria that could produce polyamines with those carrying bacteria genetically modified to lack polyamine production. "Polyamines, including putrescine and spermidine, are known gut microbial metabolites associated with improved biological functions and lifespan extension. We wanted to understand whether polyamines produced specifically by gut bacteria can directly influence the lifespan of a host, independent of polyamines obtained from the diet," says Dr. Kurihara. The study findings were published online in the journal mBio on September 10, 2026. The researchers first examined the effect of dietary polyamines using germ-free flies maintained on a chemically defined, polyamine-free diet supplemented with either putrescine or spermidine. Both supplemented groups showed significantly longer lifespans than flies maintained on a diet without polyamines. The mean lifespan was 20.8 days for flies receiving putrescine and 21.1 days for those receiving spermidine, compared to just 18.2 days for the polyamine-deficient control group. The team then investigated whether polyamines produced specifically by gut bacteria could contribute to lifespan extension. The researchers colonized the flies with one of the three genetically distinct E. coli strains: SK929, a wild-type strain capable of producing putrescine; SK930, a mutant strain in which three genes involved in putrescine biosynthesis had been deleted; and SK931, a complemented strain which was capable of producing putrescine. All groups were maintained on a modified polyamine-free diet, allowing the researchers to assess the contribution of bacterial polyamine production. Putrescine was detected in whole-body homogenates of flies colonized with SK929 and SK931, at approximately 1.2 nmol/mg and 1.3 nmol/mg, respectively. However, it was below the detection limit in flies colonized with SK930. The researchers also detected spermidine in all three groups, consistent with the ability of flies to synthesize polyamines themselves. The higher putrescine levels in flies carrying the polyamine-producing strains suggested the uptake of bacterial-derived putrescine by the host and its conversion to spermidine. The lifespan results showed sex-dependent differences. In male flies, those colonized with the putrescine-producing SK929 and SK931 strains achieved average lifespans of 13.8 and 12.6 days, respectively, compared with 11.0 days for flies colonized with the polyamine-deficient SK930 strain. Both polyamine-producing groups showed significantly longer lifespans than the SK930 group. The effect in female flies was less consistent between strains. While females colonized with SK929 had a mean lifespan of 14.9 days, significantly longer than the 11.0 days of the control group carrying the SK930 strain, the complemented SK931 strain yielded an average lifespan of only 10.7 days. The researchers also investigated the underlying molecular mechanisms that influence the extension of their lifespan. Compared to the polyamine-deficient control cohort, host interaction with polyamine-producing bacteria correlated with a significant downregulation of TotM and Halo expression. Because TotM is associated with the JAK/STAT immune/stress-response pathway, which becomes activated with age, its downregulation may contribute to lifespan extension. However, the precise mechanism remains to be elucidated. "This experimental approach allows us to examine how individual metabolites produced by gut bacteria influence the lifespan of its host," explained Dr. Kurihara. "Although we focused on polyamines in this study, the same strategy could help us investigate the long-term effects of other bacterial metabolites. Ultimately, this insight could drive the development of functional foods and probiotics that optimize polyamine production, offering novel, diet-based strategies to regulate gut microbial metabolism for health maintenance." Overall, the study provides a controlled experimental demonstration that gut bacteria-derived polyamines actively modulate host lifespan, while establishing a robust methodological framework to investigate how other microbiota-derived molecules impact long-term host health.

AI Translation
肠道微生物群影响宿主生理的多个方面，包括代谢和免疫功能。肠道细菌产生的许多代谢产物可以穿过肠上皮（而细菌本身可能无法穿过）并进入宿主组织，这为微生物活动影响宿主生理提供了一条途径。然而，确定个体肠道细菌代谢产物对宿主寿命的具体贡献一直具有挑战性。 为了弥补这一空白，由日本近畿大学生物导向科学与技术学院的栗原慎副教授和金泽大学的仓石隆幸教授领导的研究团队，使用了接种了基因工程大肠杆菌（E. coli）的已知菌群果蝇。该实验系统使研究人员能够比较携带能产生多胺的细菌的果蝇与携带基因改造后无法产生多胺的细菌的果蝇。“多胺，包括腐胺和亚精胺，是已知的与改善生物功能和延长寿命相关的肠道微生物代谢产物。我们希望了解肠道细菌特异性产生的多胺是否能直接影响宿主寿命，而与从饮食中获得的多胺无关，”栗原博士说。该研究结果于2026年9月10日在线发表在mBio杂志上。 研究人员首先使用无菌果蝇，将其饲喂化学成分明确的、无多胺饮食，并补充腐胺或亚精胺，以检测饮食中多胺的作用。结果显示，两个补充组的果蝇寿命均显著长于饲喂无多胺饮食的果蝇。接受腐胺的果蝇平均寿命为20.8天，接受亚精胺的果蝇为21.1天，而多胺缺乏对照组的果蝇平均寿命仅为18.2天。 随后，研究团队调查了肠道细菌特异性产生的多胺是否能促进寿命延长。研究人员用三种基因上不同的大肠杆菌菌株之一定植果蝇：SK929，一种能够产生腐胺的野生型菌株；SK930，一种腐胺生物合成相关的三个基因已被删除的突变株；以及SK931，一种能够产生腐胺的互补菌株。所有组均饲喂改进的无多胺饮食，以便研究人员评估细菌多胺产量的贡献。 在用SK929和SK931定植的果蝇全身匀浆中检测到腐胺，浓度分别约为1.2 nmol/mg和1.3 nmol/mg。然而，在用SK930定植的果蝇中，腐胺水平低于检测限。研究人员还在所有三组中检测到亚精胺，这与果蝇自身合成多胺的能力一致。携带产生多胺菌株的果蝇中腐胺水平较高，这表明宿主摄取了细菌来源的腐胺并将其转化为亚精胺。 寿命结果显示出性别依赖性差异。在雄性果蝇中，用产生腐胺的SK929和SK931菌株定植的果蝇平均寿命分别为13.8天和12.6天，而用多胺缺陷型SK930菌株定植的果蝇平均寿命为11.0天。两个产生多胺的组的寿命均显著长于SK930组。 在雌性果蝇中，不同菌株间的效果一致性较低。虽然用SK929定植的雌性果蝇平均寿命为14.9天，显著长于携带SK930菌株的对照组的11.0天，但互补菌株SK931仅产生了10.7天的平均寿命。 研究人员还调查了影响其寿命延长的潜在分子机制。与多胺缺陷对照组相比，宿主与产生多胺的细菌的相互作用与TotM和Halo表达的显著下调相关。由于TotM与JAK/STAT免疫/应激反应通路相关，而该通路会随年龄增长而被激活，因此其下调可能有助于寿命延长。然而，精确机制仍有待阐明。 “这种实验方法使我们能够研究肠道细菌产生的个体代谢产物如何影响宿主寿命，”栗原博士解释道。“尽管本研究侧重于多胺，但同样的策略可以帮助我们研究其他细菌代谢产物的长期影响。最终，这一见解可以推动功能性食品和益生菌的开发，以优化多胺生产，提供新颖的、基于饮食的策略来调节肠道微生物代谢以维持健康。” 总而言之，这项研究提供了一个受控的实验证明，即肠道细菌来源的多胺积极调节宿主寿命，同时建立了一个稳健的方法学框架，以研究其他微生物群来源的分子如何影响宿主长期健康。
