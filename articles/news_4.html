Immunotherapy found effective against a subtype of difficult-to-treat ovarian cancer

深度解析

这篇医学新闻报道揭示了IL-17在透明细胞卵巢癌免疫疗法响应中的关键作用，为理解这种难治性癌症的机制和开发个性化治疗提供了新视角。核心专业概念在于IL-17（白细胞介素-17）如何将免疫学上的“冷肿瘤”转化为“免疫许可”的微环境。研究发现，IL-17直接作用于癌细胞，而非免疫细胞，激活其内部的NF-κB炎症通路，促使癌细胞释放趋化因子等物质，从而大量招募并激活免疫细胞（如CD4阳性T细胞）进入肿瘤。这种“内源性”炎症编程使得肿瘤微环境从抑制免疫反应变为有利于免疫系统攻击癌细胞，进而显著提高免疫检查点抑制剂（如抗PD-L1抗体）的治疗效果和患者生存期。 其行业影响深远。首先，IL-17有望成为预测透明细胞卵巢癌患者对免疫疗法敏感性的新型生物标志物，独立于传统的MSI和TMB。这将使临床医生能更精准地筛选出可能受益的患者，避免无效治疗，优化医疗资源。其次，这一发现为开发靶向IL-17或其下游通路的新型联合疗法提供了理论基础，可能突破现有免疫疗法在卵巢癌领域应用受限的瓶颈。最终，它推动了肿瘤个性化医疗的实现，确保每位患者获得最适合其肿瘤分子特征的精准治疗，尤其对于透明细胞卵巢癌这种预后较差、治疗选择有限的疾病，具有重要的临床转化价值。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: IL-17 acts directly on the cancer cells and creates an immune-permissive tumor microenvironment by recruiting immune cells. Credit: Dr. Kosuke Murakami, Kindai University A research group has revealed through studies in humans and mice that a subset of clear cell ovarian cancers, which have been considered difficult to treat with anticancer drugs and immunotherapy, responds to immunotherapy. They also identified the inflammation-related protein IL-17 as a key factor underlying this response. The study found that IL-17 acts directly on cancer cells and creates an immune-permissive tumor microenvironment by recruiting immune cells. Harnessing this mechanism may enable prediction of immunotherapy efficacy in individual patients, contributing to the development of personalized medicine. The work is published in the journal Molecular Cancer. The research was led by Kosuke Murakami, a lecturer in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at Kindai University Faculty of Medicine in Sakai, Osaka Prefecture, and Professor Noriomi Matsumura, department head, together with collaborators including Shiki Takamura, team director of the Laboratory for Immunological Memory at the RIKEN Center for Integrative Medical Sciences in Wako, Saitama Prefecture, and the Department of Immunology at Kindai University Faculty of Medicine. Ovarian cancer is one of the most difficult gynecological cancers to treat. Clear cell ovarian cancer accounts for about one-quarter of all ovarian cancers in Japan and is known to be more common there than in Western countries. Because clear cell ovarian cancer is resistant to anticancer drugs and remains challenging to manage after recurrence, new treatment strategies have been urgently needed. In recent years, cancer immunotherapy (e.g., immune checkpoint inhibitors), which harnesses the body's immune system to attack cancer, has shown promising results in treating various types of cancer. However, large-scale clinical trials have not demonstrated a clear benefit in ovarian cancer. In particular, clear cell ovarian cancer has long been considered an immunologically cold tumor because it contains few surrounding immune cells. Nevertheless, previous studies have reported cases in which immunotherapy was highly effective in some patients with clear cell ovarian cancer, highlighting the challenges of understanding why only certain patients respond and how to identify those likely to benefit. A small responsive subgroup First, the research group analyzed tissue samples and genetic data from 180 cases of human clear cell ovarian cancer. The results revealed that, although the number of immune cells in clear cell ovarian cancer is low overall, a very small proportion (approximately 5%) exhibits a subtype in which the inflammation-related protein IL-17 is highly active. This type of cancer showed an inflammatory signature characterized by immune cell infiltration and activation within the tumor microenvironment. Crucially, this characteristic emerged independently of markers traditionally used to predict the efficacy of immunotherapy, suggesting that IL-17 could serve as a new biomarker. How IL-17 changes tumors Second, the researchers verified the mechanism by which IL-17 functions using a mouse model that replicates human clear cell ovarian cancer and cultured cells. The results revealed that IL-17 acts directly on cancer cells, triggering the inflammatory switch known as NF-κB within the cells and causing them to release substances that attract and activate immune cells. When an environment conducive to IL-17 action was established in mice, increased infiltration and activation of immune cells were observed within tumors, leading to enhanced efficacy of immunotherapy (anti-PD-L1 antibody) and prolonged survival. These findings indicate that IL-17 acts as a trigger that transforms immunologically cold tumors into a state in which the immune system can effectively attack cancer cells. Identifying tumors with high IL-17 activity may therefore provide a new biomarker for selecting patients with clear cell ovarian cancer who are likely to benefit from immunotherapy. Evidence beyond standard markers Ovarian clear cell carcinoma is characterized by a slightly higher number of CD4-positive T cells, a feature that caught the research group's attention. Analysis of large-scale genomic data sets revealed that a subset of cancers with high IL-17 activity develops an inflammatory state characterized by the recruitment and activation of immune cells. This state emerged independently of conventional biomarkers for predicting treatment response, such as microsatellite instability (MSI) and high tumor mutational burden (TMB). Furthermore, the study confirmed that IL-17 acts directly on cancer cells without involving immune cells to activate NF-κB, thereby inducing the production of substances such as chemokines that recruit immune cells. In mouse models, tumors exposed to IL-17 showed increased recruitment and activation of immune cells within the tumor microenvironment. Additionally, single-cell-level analysis revealed that these immune cells were not dysfunctional but retained their ability to attack the tumor. Furthermore, mice with an inflammatory tumor microenvironment showed prolonged survival following treatment with an anti-PD-L1 antibody, whereas no survival difference was observed in the absence of immunotherapy. These results indicate that IL-17 is not a marker of prognosis but rather a biomarker for predicting the response to immunotherapy. A route to patient selection These findings provide new insights that may apply not only to clear cell ovarian cancer but also to a wide range of cancer types, suggesting that the inflammatory environment created by cancer cells themselves influences the efficacy of immunotherapy. "Clear cell ovarian cancer is resistant to anticancer drugs, and immunotherapy has not been accessible to many patients," says Murakami. "Although it represents only a small subset, we have demonstrated that there are cancer types that are highly responsive to immunotherapy and that inflammation induced by IL-17 is the key factor underlying this response. This achievement was made possible through close collaboration between RIKEN and the Faculty of Medicine at our university. We hope to further advance our research toward the realization of personalized medicine by identifying patients who are likely to benefit from treatment and delivering the optimal therapy for each individual." 他们还发现，炎症相关蛋白IL-17是这种反应的关键因素。研究发现，IL-17直接作用于癌细胞，并通过招募免疫细胞创建一个免疫许可的肿瘤微环境。利用这一机制，有望预测个体患者的免疫疗法疗效，从而促进个性化医疗的发展。 该研究成果发表在《分子癌症》杂志上。这项研究由大阪府堺市近畿大学医学部妇产科学讲师村上浩介和科主任松村纪臣教授共同领导，合作者包括埼玉县和光市理化学研究所生命医科学研究中心免疫记忆实验室团队负责人高村志树，以及近畿大学医学部免疫学系。 卵巢癌是妇科癌症中最难治疗的癌症之一。透明细胞卵巢癌约占日本所有卵巢癌的四分之一，并且已知在日本比西方国家更常见。由于透明细胞卵巢癌对抗癌药物具有耐药性，且复发后仍难以管理，因此迫切需要新的治疗策略。 近年来，癌症免疫疗法（例如免疫检查点抑制剂）通过利用身体自身的免疫系统攻击癌症，在治疗多种癌症方面显示出有希望的结果。然而，大规模临床试验尚未在卵巢癌中显示出明确的益处。 特别是，透明细胞卵巢癌长期以来被认为是免疫学上的冷肿瘤，因为它周围的免疫细胞很少。尽管如此，之前的研究报告了一些透明细胞卵巢癌患者中免疫疗法非常有效的病例，这凸显了理解为何只有某些患者有反应以及如何识别可能受益的患者所面临的挑战。 一小部分有反应的亚组 首先，研究小组分析了180例人透明细胞卵巢癌的组织样本和基因数据。结果显示，尽管透明细胞卵巢癌中的免疫细胞总体数量较低，但有极小一部分（约5%）表现出炎症相关蛋白IL-17高度活跃的亚型。 这种类型的癌症表现出以免疫细胞浸润和肿瘤微环境内活化为特征的炎症信号。至关重要的是，这一特征的出现独立于传统上用于预测免疫疗法疗效的标志物，这表明IL-17可能作为一种新的生物标志物。 IL-17如何改变肿瘤 其次，研究人员使用复制人透明细胞卵巢癌的小鼠模型和培养细胞验证了IL-17发挥作用的机制。结果显示，IL-17直接作用于癌细胞，触发细胞内名为NF-κB的炎症开关，并使其释放吸引和激活免疫细胞的物质。 当在小鼠体内建立有利于IL-17作用的环境时，观察到肿瘤内免疫细胞的浸润和活化增加，从而增强了免疫疗法（抗PD-L1抗体）的疗效并延长了生存期。这些发现表明，IL-17作为一种触发器，将免疫学上的冷肿瘤转化为免疫系统能够有效攻击癌细胞的状态。 因此，识别IL-17活性高的肿瘤可以为选择可能受益于免疫疗法的透明细胞卵巢癌患者提供新的生物标志物。 超出标准标志物的证据 卵巢透明细胞癌的特点是CD4阳性T细胞数量略高，这一特征引起了研究小组的注意。对大规模基因组数据集的分析显示，一部分IL-17活性高的癌症发展出一种以免疫细胞招募和活化为特征的炎症状态。这种状态的出现独立于预测治疗反应的传统生物标志物，如微卫星不稳定性（MSI）和高肿瘤突变负荷（TMB）。 此外，研究证实IL-17在不涉及免疫细胞的情况下，直接作用于癌细胞以激活NF-κB，从而诱导趋化因子等物质的产生，这些物质可以招募免疫细胞。在小鼠模型中，暴露于IL-17的肿瘤显示肿瘤微环境内的免疫细胞招募和活化增加。 此外，单细胞水平分析显示，这些免疫细胞并未功能失调，而是保留了攻击肿瘤的能力。再者，具有炎症性肿瘤微环境的小鼠在接受抗PD-L1抗体治疗后生存期延长，而在没有免疫疗法的情况下未观察到生存差异。 这些结果表明，IL-17不是预后标志物，而是预测免疫疗法反应的生物标志物。 选择患者的途径 这些发现提供了新的见解，可能不仅适用于透明细胞卵巢癌，也适用于广泛的癌症类型，表明癌细胞自身产生的炎症环境影响免疫疗法的疗效。 村上表示：“透明细胞卵巢癌对抗癌药物具有耐药性，免疫疗法对许多患者来说难以获得。” “尽管只代表一小部分亚组，但我们已经证明存在对免疫疗法高度敏感的癌症类型，并且由IL-17诱导的炎症是这种反应的关键因素。这一成就得益于理化学研究所和我们大学医学部之间的紧密合作。我们希望通过识别可能受益于治疗的患者并为每个人提供最佳疗法，进一步推进我们的研究，以实现个性化医疗。” 出版详情：Kosuke Murakami et al, IL-17–driven tumor
