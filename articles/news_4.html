Electronic health record algorithm can identify drug-resistant epilepsy

深度解析

这项研究展示了电子健康记录（EHR）算法在识别耐药性癫痫（DRE）患者方面的巨大潜力，这是一种颠覆性的技术原理。DRE的临床诊断通常耗时且复杂，需要医生仔细回顾患者的用药史和治疗反应。EHR算法通过自动化分析海量的结构化（如诊断代码、药物处方）和非结构化（如临床笔记）数据，能够高效地筛选出符合DRE表型特征的患者。这种“可计算表型算法”的核心优势在于其大规模、低成本的筛选能力，极大地提高了DRE患者的早期识别率。 从行业影响来看，该技术不仅能优化医疗资源的分配，使更多符合条件的患者及时接受专业评估和治疗，还能为癫痫手术等高级干预提供患者队列。文章指出，超过一半的DRE患者尚未启动术前评估，这揭示了当前临床路径中存在的重大缺口。DRE患者的术前评估是决定能否通过手术获得治愈的关键步骤，涉及多学科专家团队的精细定位和风险评估。EHR算法的应用将有助于系统性地追踪患者，加强护理协调，并简化诊断流程，从而显著提升DRE患者的治疗依从性和预后，推动精准医疗在癫痫领域的实践。这对于改善患者生活质量和降低长期医疗负担具有深远意义。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Pixabay/CC0 Public Domain An electronic health record (EHR) algorithm can identify people with drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE), according to a study published in the December issue of Epilepsy & Behavior. Grace B. Simmons, from Columbia University Medical Center in New York City, and colleagues identified people with probable DRE from EHR data using a computable phenotypic algorithm. Two hundred people were randomly sampled for manual chart abstraction. A total of 3,027 people with probable DRE were identified by the algorithm. The researchers found that 175 (87.5%) of the 200 randomly sampled people had epilepsy; 42% and 11.5% had DRE and epilepsy with undefined drug responsiveness, respectively. More than half of those with DRE (48 of 84; 57.1%) had not initiated presurgical evaluation. There were associations seen for presurgical evaluation with comorbid mood disorder (odds ratio, 3.88), shorter median time since last epilepsy-related visit and second-to-last epilepsy visit (2.40 versus 6.96 months and 8.76 versus 13.08 months, respectively), and tracking by a surgical coordinator (odds ratio, 46.00). Lower odds of evaluation were seen in association with unknown magnetic resonance imaging classification (odds ratio, 0.04) and generalized seizures (odds ratio, 0.04). "Over half of people with DRE have not had their candidacy for epilepsy surgery determined or begun presurgical evaluation, and substantial numbers continue to have undefined drug responsiveness despite uncontrolled seizures," the authors write. "Our findings suggest that systematic patient tracking, enhanced care coordination, and streamlined diagnostic pathways should be further investigated." More information Grace B. Simmons et al., Status of presurgical evaluation among patients with drug-resistant epilepsy identified with a computable electronic health record algorithm, Epilepsy & Behavior (2026).
