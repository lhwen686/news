深度解析

EloraTZP的显著疗效源于其独特的多靶点作用机制。该药物结合了依托林肽（一种选择性胰淀素受体激动剂）和替尔泊肽（一种双重GIP/GLP-1受体激动剂）。胰淀素、GIP和GLP-1是人体内自然存在的肠道激素和胰腺激素，它们在能量代谢和血糖稳态中发挥关键作用。胰淀素通过延缓胃排空、促进饱腹感和抑制餐后胰高血糖素分泌来调节血糖和体重。GIP和GLP-1作为肠促胰素，能够葡萄糖依赖性地刺激胰岛素分泌，并抑制胰高血糖素释放，同时GLP-1还能延缓胃排空并增加饱腹感。EloraTZP通过同时激活胰淀素、GIP和GLP-1这三种受体，实现了对食欲、能量摄入、胃排空速度和胰岛素分泌的协同调控。这种多重机制策略解释了其在肥胖症和2型糖尿病患者中观察到的显著体重减轻和糖化血红蛋白降低。在临床实践中，肥胖症和2型糖尿病的共病管理一直面临挑战，传统的单一靶点疗法往往难以达到理想的体重和血糖控制目标。EloraTZP的数据预示着一种变革性的治疗选择，能够为患者带来更全面的代谢改善。其行业影响在于推动了糖尿病和肥胖症治疗从单一靶点向多靶点联合干预的范式转变，有望显著提升临床疗效，改善患者生活质量，并巩固礼来公司在此治疗领域的领导地位。

英文原文

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Pixabay from Pexels New data from a phase 2b study being presented at the 2026 annual meeting of the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD) in Milan, Italy (Sept. 28–Oct. 2), show that, for people living with obesity and type 2 diabetes, treatment with Eli Lilly's new investigational medication, EloraTZP, a combination of eloralintide and tirzepatide (TZP), resulted in weight loss of up to 23.3% at the maximum dose and accompanying substantial improvements in blood sugar control. The study evaluated the safety and efficacy of eloralintide in combination with TZP. Eloralintide is a selective amylin receptor agonist, and tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. The study examined 367 adults with obesity or overweight and type 2 diabetes—a population in which it has been difficult to achieve substantial and sustained weight loss. Amylin, released from pancreatic beta cells, and the gut hormones GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide) and GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) are released naturally by the body after eating, each acting through its own distinct receptor to help regulate hunger and blood sugar. At 48 weeks, all four dosing combinations of eloraTZP (administered as two separate pen injections) met the primary and secondary endpoints, delivering substantial weight loss and reductions in glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c—a measure of blood sugar control). From an average starting weight of 105.4 kg (232.4 pounds), participants given the smallest dose of eloraTZP (Elora 3 mg + TZP 5 mg) lost a mean 13.2% of body weight (13.9 kg, or 30.6 pounds) and saw their HbA1c drop by a mean 2.2% from an average of 8.1%. At the highest dose of eloraTZP (Elora 9 mg + TZP 15 mg), the mean weight loss was 23.3% (24.6 kg, or 54.2 pounds), and the average decrease in HbA1c was 2.9%. The most common adverse events were gastrointestinal and generally mild or moderate, occurring primarily during dose escalation. These events were seen more frequently in the combination arms than with eloralintide or tirzepatide alone. "Obesity and type 2 diabetes are interconnected, and we are seeing the potential benefit of targeting multiple hormonal pathways to address both," said Dr. Liana K. Billings, director of clinical and genetics research in diabetes and cardiometabolic disease at Endeavor Health in Evanston, Illinois, and lead author. "Across the dose combinations studied with eloralintide and tirzepatide, participants had substantial weight loss alongside meaningful reductions in HbA1c. These are outcomes that matter to patients. "We need to continue building on these findings so that, in the future, we can offer patients more treatment options that address both weight and blood sugar control and better reflect the complexity of living with type 2 diabetes and obesity." Provided by European Association for the Study of Diabetes
