Steatotic liver disease risk scores compared for predicting cirrhosis

深度解析

本研究强调了脂肪性肝病（SLD）患者风险评估工具的关键作用。SLD是一种以肝脏脂肪异常堆积为特征的慢性肝病，若不加以控制，可逐步发展为肝纤维化、肝硬化，甚至肝细胞癌（HCC），严重威胁患者生命。文章核心在于比较多种非侵入性风险评分在预测这些疾病进展中的临床实用性。其中，脂肪变性相关纤维化评估（SAFE）评分被证明在预测肝硬化方面具有最大的“净获益”，这不仅考量了预测的准确性（区分度），更综合权衡了正确识别高风险患者的临床益处与误判可能带来的弊端，从而衡量其真正的临床价值。 此类非侵入性评分的广泛应用，能显著优化SLD患者的管理。它有助于早期识别出进展为肝硬化或HCC的高风险个体，从而能及时介入生活方式干预、药物治疗或更频繁的影像学监测，有效阻止或延缓疾病进程，减轻患者痛苦。从行业影响来看，这减少了对肝活检等侵入性检查的依赖，降低了医疗成本和患者负担。同时，它也为制药行业的新药研发提供了更精准的临床试验目标人群，并促进了基层医疗机构对SLD的早期筛查和风险分层，对公共卫生具有深远意义。

Original
This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies . Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: For patients with steatotic liver disease (SLD), the steatosis-associated fibrosis estimator (SAFE) score demonstrated the greatest net benefit for predicting cirrhosis, according to a study published online Sept. 21 in JAMA Internal Medicine. Catherine Mezzacappa, M.D., Ph.D., M.P.H., from the Yale School of Medicine in New Haven, Connecticut, and colleagues compared the clinical utility of SLD risk scores for predicting cirrhosis and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) in patients with noncirrhotic SLD. The cohort study used data from adults with imaging-confirmed SLD in the national U.S. Veterans Affairs health system. Nine clinical risk scores were evaluated for their ability to predict cirrhosis or HCC within 10 years. The analysis included 853,131 patients, of whom 3.96% developed cirrhosis and 0.35% developed HCC within 10 years. The fibrosis-4 index (FIB-4), the aspartate aminotransferase-to-platelet ratio and SAFE scores showed the greatest discrimination of cirrhosis risk; the SAFE, Tate and FIB-4 scores showed the greatest discrimination of HCC risk. The SAFE score had the greatest net benefit for predicting cirrhosis. A score of 29.5 corresponded to a 10-year cirrhosis risk of 2.5% and a net benefit of 0.019 (1.9 additional individuals who develop cirrhosis per 100 classified as at risk). The SAFE score had a net benefit of 0.0016 at a 0.25% 10-year risk for HCC. "This comparative validation study highlights the importance of assessing test performance at relevant thresholds for clinical practice," the authors write.
