诺林治疗有助于维持糖尿病大鼠血管健康并保护视力

Treatment with norrin helps maintain healthy blood vessels and preserve vision in diabetic rats

深度解析

糖尿病视网膜病变是糖尿病严重的微血管并发症，其核心病理机制在于高血糖导致视网膜血管内皮细胞损伤，促使血管内皮生长因子（VEGF）过表达。VEGF不仅刺激异常新生血管生成，更关键的是增加血管通透性，破坏血视网膜屏障（BRB），引发渗漏、水肿及缺血，最终导致视网膜病变和视力丧失。当前主流的抗VEGF疗法通过抑制VEGF作用，能在一定程度上控制病变，但其局限性在于需频繁眼内注射，且对部分患者效果不佳，未能全面解决BRB功能障碍和神经退行性病变。本研究揭示了norrin作为一种内源性分子，在糖尿病状态下水平下降，其治疗作用机制是多方面的。Norrin能够稳定并修复受损的血视网膜屏障，从而减少血管渗漏和水肿。更重要的是，它展现出对视网膜神经细胞的保护作用，能减少神经细胞损失、抑制视网膜细胞死亡并减轻炎症反应，这超越了单纯抗VEGF治疗的范畴。这种多靶点、综合性的保护机制，有望为糖尿病视网膜病变患者提供更长效、更全面的视力保护，特别是在BRB功能障碍和神经退行性病变方面填补了现有疗法的空白。行业影响巨大，若临床试验成功，norrin有望成为16年来首个突破性新药，显著改善患者预后，并为未来糖尿病眼病治疗开辟新思路。

Credit: Antonetti lab Millions of people in the U.S. are affected by diabetic retinopathy, a complication of diabetes that affects the eyes. The condition is caused by damage to the blood vessels in the retina, the light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye. At first, diabetic retinopathy might not cause any symptoms, or the problems might be mild. However, if the underlying diabetes remains poorly managed, it can lead to blindness. In a new study, University of Michigan researchers showed that treatment with the molecule norrin can prevent vision loss in diabetic rats. The study is published in the journal Diabetes. Diabetic retinopathy treatment Current therapies for diabetic retinopathy target vascular endothelial growth factor, or VEGF, a protein that causes new blood vessels to form. VEGF also increases blood vessel permeability, or the capacity to allow water and nutrients to move in and out of the bloodstream into tissues. In patients with diabetic retinopathy, excess VEGF causes abnormal blood vessel growth and leakage, both of which lead to vision loss. Although anti-VEGF agents are commonly used to treat diabetic retinopathy, treatment requires several injections and fails to fully manage the condition in half the treated patients. "There is an urgent need for new therapies that can help preserve vision in patients with diabetic retinopathy," said David Antonetti, Ph.D., Roger W. Kittendorf research professor of ophthalmology and visual sciences at Kellogg Eye Center. "We have been working on the effects of norrin for several years and these results will help develop new therapies that can prevent vision loss." Preventing vision loss In previous studies, the team showed that norrin can reverse the effects of VEGF on the blood vessel barrier in cells. In the current study, they found that after rats developed diabetes, their levels of norrin decreased and this was accompanied by decreased vision. When they injected these rats with norrin, the researchers could prevent vision loss. "We are excited that norrin treatment can also reduce the loss of nerve cells involved in vision in diabetic rats," said Laura González-González, postdoctoral fellow in the Antonetti lab. Treatment with norrin also prevented retinal cell death and inflammation, both in early and late stages of diabetes. Starting norrin treatment even after vision loss prevented further loss in the diabetic rats. These results suggest that norrin can potentially help prevent vision loss in patients with diabetes. The team identified several genes that were affected by norrin treatment, and they are working on understanding how they contribute to barrier restoration. Clinical trials and retinal blood vessels The work done by the Antonetti lab has helped identify targets for a new diabetic retinopathy treatment method. To this end, their work helped EyeBio and Merck develop clinical trials for drugs that can increase the activity of cell receptors that respond to norrin. Antonetti was also involved in helping Merck develop a drug for treating adults with macular edema, a major driver of vision loss in patients with diabetic retinopathy. If successful, this will be the first new therapy for the condition in 16 years. Publication details Laura González-González et al., Norrin Treatment Prevents Vascular Permeability and Neural Cell Loss While Preserving Vision in Diabetic Rats, Diabetes (2026).
