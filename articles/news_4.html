深度解析

本文深入剖析了美国呼吸道疾病的流行现状。核心在于“流感样疾病（ILI）”的监测，作为一种综合症候群定义，它使得公共卫生机构能迅速识别并追踪急性呼吸道感染趋势，即便病原体未确诊。ILI门诊就诊比例高于全国基线，预示流感季开启，突显了早期预警在疾病控制中的关键作用。 “废水监测”是另一重要流行病学工具。通过分析废水中特定病毒（如COVID-19、流感、RSV）的核酸浓度，卫生部门能获得社区层面的病毒活动数据，评估无症状传播及感染强度。这作为早期预警指标，对指导公共卫生干预措施具有独特优势。 文章还揭示了包括呼吸道合胞病毒、甲型流感等多种病原体的共存流行。这种多病原体情况增加了临床诊断复杂性，并对疫苗策略提出挑战。CDC指出，早期流感活动度高不必然预示更高的季节性高峰或更严重的整体病程，体现了流行病学预测的严谨性。面对多重呼吸道威胁，持续监测和提升疫苗覆盖率是维护公众健康的核心策略。

Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Credit: Unsplash/CC0 Public Domain Although U.S. cases of respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) and COVID-19 are low, the proportion of outpatient visits for influenza-like illness (ILI) is 3.2%, above the 2.9% national baseline, indicating the beginning of flu season in earnest. "This early-season influenza activity is currently higher than typically observed at this time of year, but an earlier increase in activity does not necessarily lead to a higher seasonal peak or greater overall severity," the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) said today in its weekly FluView update. The CDC also noted two child deaths from flu this week, for a total of five so far this season. In total, 9,584 people were hospitalized for flu. This season, there have been at least 2.6 million flu cases, 36,000 hospital admissions and 2,100 deaths. Flu test positivity was 11.3% this week, up from 9.1% the week before, primarily from influenza A. While infections are minimal or low in most of the country, flu is in the high or very high category in California, Louisiana, Oregon, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas and Washington state and moderate in Colorado, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York state and Puerto Rico. The preliminary estimate of season severity is low across all ages, and no novel influenza virus has been confirmed in people. "CDC expects the 2026–2027 respiratory illness season to be much like last season," the agency said. "It is too early to know what viruses may predominate or to say whether there might be implications on flu vaccine effectiveness this season." Low but rising overall respiratory illness activity The amount of acute respiratory illness such as COVID-19 and flu causing people to seek medical care is low but rising, the CDC said in its respiratory illness update today. Levels of rhinovirus/enterovirus and respiratory adenovirus are elevated across the country. The CDC also noted cases of group A Streptococcus (strep), walking pneumonia and pertussis (whooping cough). Wastewater levels of COVID are moderate in most of the country, while flu and RSV are low and very low, respectively. Wastewater concentrations are a measure of community viral activity.
