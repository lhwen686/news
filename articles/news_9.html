World项目推出的核心专业概念是“零知识证明认证”（Zero-Knowledge Proof, ZKP）与生物识别技术（虹膜扫描）结合的“人类证明”机制。ZKP允许一方（证明者）向另一方（验证者）证明某个陈述是真实的，而无需透露除该陈述本身以外的任何信息。World利用Orb扫描用户虹膜生成匿名World ID，并结合ZKP确保在不泄露个人身份信息的前提下，验证数字互动背后是“真实的人类”而非AI机器人或自动化程序。

这一技术及其行业影响深远。在AI内容爆炸式增长的背景下，“人类证明”成为数字信任的基石。在约会应用（如Tinder），它能有效打击虚假档案和骗子，提升用户交友体验的真实性；在票务行业（如Concert Kit），它能对抗黄牛机器人，确保真粉丝能买到票；在商业领域，它能防范深度伪造威胁（Zoom）和确保数字签名（DocuSign）的真实性。World通过推出多级验证方案（Orb、NFC政府ID、自拍）来平衡安全性和用户便利性，试图解决规模化推广的难题。尽管面临隐私和安全挑战，但其目标是重塑数字世界的信任体系，为未来人工智能与人类共存的网络环境提供必要的身份验证保障。
Original Text
At a trendy venue near the San Francisco pier, Sam Altman’s verification project World celebrated its next evolution and rapid expansion of its ambitions. And it’s starting with Tinder.

Tools for Humanity, the company behind the World project, announced Friday plans to integrate its verification tech into dating apps, event and concert ticketing systems, business organizations, email, and other arenas of public life.

“The world is getting close to very powerful AI, and this is doing a lot of wonderful things,” said Altman, speaking before a packed crowd at The Midway. “We are also heading to a world now where there’s going to be more stuff generated by AI than by humans,” he added. “I’m sure many of you where you’re like, ‘Am I interacting with an AI or a person, or how much of each, and how do I know?”

World (formerly Worldcoin) distinguishes itself from many of its ID verification peers by offering the ability to verify that a real, living human is using a digital service while still protecting that person’s anonymity. There is some complex cryptographic alchemy behind this (something called “zero-knowledge proof-based authentication” is involved), but the long and the short of it is that the company is creating what it calls “proof of human” tools — mechanisms that can verify human activity in a world rife with AI agents and bots.

Its chief tool for verification is the Orb — a spherical digital reader — that scans a user’s eyes, converting their iris into a unique but anonymous cryptographic identifier (known as a verified World ID), which can then be used to access World’s services, although users can also access World’s app without one.

Altman kept his remarks brief on Friday (TFH’s co-founder and CEO, Alex Blania, was absent due to a last-minute hand surgery, Altman said). He then turned much of the presentation over to World’s chief product officer Tiago Sada and his team.

Sada explained that World was launching the newest version of its app (the last version was launched at an event in December), along with a plethora of new integrations for its technology.

World has been preparing, for some time, to deploy a verification service for dating apps — most notably, Tinder. Last year, Tinder launched a World ID pilot program in Japan. That pilot was apparently a success because World announced that Tinder would be launching its verification integration in global markets —including the U.S. The program integrates a World ID emblem into the profiles of users who have gone through its verification processes, thus authenticating them as a real person.

Image Credits:World

World is also courting the entertainment industry by launching a new feature called Concert Kit, where musical artists can reserve a certain number of concert tickets for World ID-verified humans. This is designed to ensure that fans are safe from scalpers who often use automated ticket-buying bots to scarf up seats. Concert Kit is compatible with major ticketing systems, including Ticketmaster and Eventbrite, and the company is promoting it via partnerships with 30 Seconds to Mars and Bruno Mars—both of whom plan to use it for their upcoming tours.

The event was full of many other announcements, including some aimed at businesses. A Zoom/World ID verification integration seeks to battle a supposed deepfake threat to business calls, and a DocuSign partnership is designed to ensure signatures come from authentic users.

The company is also working on a number of features in anticipation of the Wild West of the agentic web, including one called “agent delegation,” in which a person can delegate their World ID to an agent to carry out online activities on their behalf. A partnership with authentication firm Okta has also created a system (currently in beta) that verifies that an agent is acting on behalf of a human. The system is set up so that a World ID can be tied to a specific agent and then, when the agent goes out into the web to operate on that person’s behalf, websites will know a verified person is behind the behavior, said Okta’s chief product officer, Gareth Davies, at the event.

So far, it’s been difficult for World to scale, due largely to the verification process itself. For much of the company’s history, to get its gold standard, you had to travel to one of its offices and have your eyeballs scanned by an Orb—a fairly inconvenient (not to mention weird) experience.

Image Credits:World

However, World has continually made moves to increase the ease and incentive structure for verification. In the past, it offered its crypto asset, Worldcoin, to some members who signed up, and has also distributed its Orbs into big retail chains so that users can verify themselves while they’re out shopping or getting a coffee. Now, the company is announcing that it is significantly expanding its Orb saturation in New York, Los Angeles, and San Francisco. The company also promoted a service where interested users could have World bring an Orb to their location for remote verification.

In a conversation with TechCrunch, Sada also shared that World has attempted to solve the scaling problem by creating different tiers of verification. The highest tier is Orb verification, but below that, World has previously offered a mid-level tier, which uses an anonymized scan of an official government ID via the card’s NFC chip.

The company also introduced a low-level tier, or what Sada called “low friction”— meaning low effort, I guess, but also “low security” — which involves merely taking a selfie.

Selfie Check, which Sada’s team presented during the event, is designed to maintain user privacy.

“Selfie is private by design,” said Daniel Shorr, one of TFH’s executives, during the presentation. “That means that we maximize the local processing that’s happening on your device, on your phone, which means that your images are yours.”

Selfie verification obviously isn’t new, and fraudsters have long managed to spoof it. “Obviously, we do our best, and it’s like one of the best systems that you’ll see for this. But it has limits,” Sada told TechCrunch. Developers looking to integrate World’s services can choose from the three different verification tiers depending on the level of security that’s important to them, he noted.

在旧金山码头附近一家时尚的场所，萨姆·奥特曼的验证项目World庆祝了其下一次演进和抱负的迅速扩张。而这一切都从Tinder开始。

World项目背后的公司Tools for Humanity周五宣布，计划将其验证技术整合到达交友应用、活动和音乐会票务系统、商业机构、电子邮件以及公共生活的其他领域。

奥特曼在The Midway挤满人群的会场上发言说：“世界正越来越接近非常强大的人工智能，这将带来许多奇妙的事物。”他补充道：“我们现在也正走向一个由人工智能生成的内容将多于人类生成内容的世界。我确信你们中的许多人都会想，‘我是在与人工智能还是一个人互动，或者两者各有多少，我怎么知道？’”

World（前身为Worldcoin）通过提供验证一个真实活生生的人正在使用数字服务同时仍保护其匿名性的能力，使其有别于许多同行身份验证项目。这背后涉及一些复杂的密码学魔法（其中包含一种称为“零知识证明认证”的技术），但简而言之，该公司正在创建其所谓的“人类证明”工具——这些机制能够在一个充满人工智能代理和机器人的世界中验证人类活动。

其主要的验证工具是Orb——一个球形数字读取器——它扫描用户的眼睛，将虹膜转换为一个独特但匿名的加密标识符（称为已验证的World ID），然后可用于访问World的服务，尽管用户也可以在没有World ID的情况下访问World的应用程序。

奥特曼周五的讲话很简短（TFH的联合创始人兼首席执行官亚历克斯·布拉尼亚因临时手部手术缺席，奥特曼表示）。随后他将大部分演示交给了World的首席产品官蒂亚戈·萨达及其团队。

萨达解释说，World正在推出其最新版本的应用程序（上一个版本在去年12月的一次活动中推出），同时还推出了大量新的技术集成。

World已经准备了一段时间，为交友应用部署验证服务——最值得注意的是Tinder。去年，Tinder在日本启动了World ID试点项目。该试点显然很成功，因为World宣布Tinder将在包括美国在内的全球市场推出其验证集成。该项目将World ID徽章整合到已通过其验证过程的用户资料中，从而将他们认证为真人。

图片来源：World

World还通过推出名为Concert Kit的新功能来争取娱乐行业，通过该功能，音乐艺术家可以为World ID验证的人预留一定数量的音乐会门票。这旨在确保粉丝免受黄牛的侵害，黄牛经常使用自动购票机器人抢购座位。Concert Kit兼容包括Ticketmaster和Eventbrite在内的主要票务系统，该公司正通过与30 Seconds to Mars和Bruno Mars的合作来推广它——两者都计划在即将到来的巡演中使用它。

这次活动还发布了许多其他公告，其中一些针对企业。Zoom/World ID验证集成旨在打击所谓的商业电话深度伪造威胁，而与DocuSign的合作旨在确保签名来自真实用户。

该公司还在开发许多功能，以应对代理网络（agentic web）的“狂野西部”时代，其中包括一个名为“代理委托”的功能，即一个人可以将其World ID委托给一个代理，由其代表执行在线活动。与身份验证公司Okta的合作也创建了一个系统（目前处于测试阶段），该系统验证代理是否代表人类行事。Okta首席产品官加雷思·戴维斯在活动中表示，该系统设置后，World ID可以绑定到特定代理，当代理代表该人进行网络操作时，网站将知道行为背后是一个经过验证的人。

到目前为止，由于验证过程本身，World一直难以扩展。在该公司的大部分历史中，要获得其“金标准”验证，你必须前往其办公室，并让Orb扫描你的眼球——这是一种相当不便（更不用说奇怪）的体验。

图片来源：World

然而，World一直在采取措施，以提高验证的便捷性和激励结构。过去，它向一些注册会员提供了其加密资产Worldcoin，并且还将Orb分发到大型零售连锁店，以便用户在购物或喝咖啡时进行自我验证。现在，该公司宣布将显著扩大Orb在纽约、洛杉矶和旧金山的普及度。该公司还推广了一项服务，感兴趣的用户可以让World将Orb带到他们的所在地进行远程验证。

在与TechCrunch的对话中，萨达还分享说，World试图通过创建不同级别的验证来解决扩展问题。最高级别是Orb验证，但在此之下，World之前提供了一个中等级别，它通过卡的NFC芯片匿名扫描官方政府ID。

该公司还引入了一个低等级别，或者萨达所说的“低摩擦”——我猜这意味着低投入，但也意味着“低安全性”——这仅涉及自拍。

萨达的团队在活动中展示的Selfie Check旨在维护用户隐私。

“自拍在设计上就是私密的，”TFH的一位高管丹尼尔·肖尔在演示中说。“这意味着我们最大限度地利用您设备（您的手机）上发生的本地处理，这意味着您的图像属于您自己。”

自拍验证显然不新鲜，欺诈者长期以来一直设法伪造它。“显然，我们尽力做到最好，这是您将看到的最好的系统之一。但它有局限性，”萨达告诉TechCrunch。他指出，寻求集成World服务的开发人员可以根据对他们而言重要的安全级别，从三种不同的验证级别中进行选择。
