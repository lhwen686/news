How Notable Capital, GIC, and Baillie Gifford bet big on Anthropic before its IPO

深度解析

本文深入揭示了顶级风险投资机构在AI领域高风险、高回报的投资策略。Notable Capital、GIC和Baillie Gifford等机构对Anthropic的巨额投资，最初在一些有限合伙人看来可能令人费解，因为Anthropic的估值已远超历史水平，且面临激烈竞争。然而，这些投资者独到的商业逻辑在于，他们识别并押注了AI市场中的核心驱动力：开发者生态和企业级应用。 通过深入分析开发者对Claude而非ChatGPT的偏好，以及模型改进对企业采用率的推动，投资者看到了Anthropic的巨大增长潜力。同时，对于“关键任务”工作需要美国本土闭源模型的判断，也为其商业模式增添了战略价值。这种以开发者和企业需求为中心的投资视角，加上Anthropic自身在愿景执行、营收增长和毛利率方面的出色表现，共同促成了公司估值从数百亿美元飙升至1.5万亿美元的惊人成就，为早期投资者带来了15倍甚至更高的账面回报。 这不仅验证了AI基础模型市场的巨大潜力，也预示着全球投资格局的深刻变化。AI正吸引前所未有的资本，并以创纪录的估值重塑科技公司的生命周期，强调了在快速变化的技术浪潮中，识别核心技术优势、用户粘性及战略定位对投资成功的决定性作用。

英文原文

Original
When Notable Capital poured roughly 10% of the U.S. portion of its $1 billion-plus fund into Anthropic’s Series E, many limited partners were perplexed by the massive bet. Claude’s maker was still trailing its biggest rival, OpenAI, and DeepSeek had released its R1 model just weeks earlier, threatening to overtake closed-source competitors. Moreover, at $61.5 billion, Anthropic’s valuation was by far the highest that the predominantly early-stage venture firm had invested at in its 25-year history. But investors at Notable, formerly known as GGV Capital before its 2024 split from its China operations, were confident in Anthropic’s future. A team that included managing partners Hans Tung and Glenn Solomon and principal Dan Cahana crunched the numbers and found that developers were increasingly favoring Claude over ChatGPT, using Claude 70% of the time, a reversal from the previous year. “We just knew that for AI, the developer is at the center because they’re the early adopters,” Tung, a co-founder of the firm, told TechCrunch. Having navigated the complexities of U.S.-China relations firsthand, Notable was also convinced that “mission-critical” work would always require U.S.-based, closed-source models, Tung said. Less than two years later, Notable’s LPs are no longer questioning the outsized investment. On Wednesday, Anthropic raised $81.3 billion in the largest IPO in history, reaching a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion before its shares began trading the next day, a paper return of roughly 15 times. Notable is, of course, not the only firm sitting on a massive paper windfall from Anthropic. Even traditional late-stage investors, who rarely see venture-scale multiples, are set for a big win. Singaporean sovereign wealth fund GIC had been researching Anthropic throughout 2024, but it didn’t make its first investment until after Chris Emanuel, head of the fund’s tech investments, met with Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao at Che Fico, an Italian restaurant in San Francisco, in early 2025. Rao helped pique Emanuel’s interest in the company by showing that model improvements were driving rising enterprise adoption. GIC then made its first investment in Anthropic’s Series F, a round announced in September 2025 at a $183 billion valuation. “Claude Code was just released then,” Emanuel told TechCrunch. “It was very early, but even then, we could see signs that it was really popular with developers.” GIC’s conviction in the AI lab was so high that the fund later led Anthropic’s Series G alongside Coatue at a $380 billion valuation, and returned as one of the co-leads for the pre-IPO round at $965 billion. Across the three rounds, GIC invested more than $4.5 billion in Anthropic, amassing a stake equal to about 1% of the company after its IPO, according to a person familiar with GIC’s financials. Lilian Li, who leads U.S. growth investments at Baillie Gifford, began studying Anthropic and speaking with its team nearly two years before first investing in the company’s Series F. Baillie Gifford invested in the Claude maker once it became clear that the company was “executing consistently” on its vision while generating strong revenue and “compelling gross margins,” Li told TechCrunch. After Baillie Gifford’s initial investment, Anthropic continued to deliver on its promises, expanding into industries such as legal services and life sciences, as well as broader applications like Cowork and cybersecurity, Li said. “It’s actually quite incredible how consistent they have been with how quickly everything’s been changing,” she added. Baillie Gifford went on to invest a total of $1.5 billion across three funding rounds, a bet that has generated substantial paper gains for the investment manager, as well as for dozens of other backers who invested at what once seemed like astronomical valuations.

当Notable Capital将其逾10亿美元基金中约10%的美国部分投入Anthropic的E轮融资时，许多有限合伙人对这笔巨额投资感到困惑。Claude的开发商当时仍落后于其最大竞争对手OpenAI，而DeepSeek也在数周前发布了其R1模型，威胁要超越闭源竞争对手。此外，Anthropic高达615亿美元的估值，是这家主要专注于早期投资的风险投资公司在其25年历史上投资的最高估值。 但Notable（其前身为GGV Capital，在2024年与中国业务分拆）的投资者对Anthropic的未来充满信心。一个由管理合伙人童士豪（Hans Tung）和格伦·所罗门（Glenn Solomon）以及负责人丹·卡哈纳（Dan Cahana）组成的团队对数据进行了分析，发现开发者越来越倾向于使用Claude而非ChatGPT，使用Claude的时间占比达到70%，这与前一年相比是一个逆转。 该公司的联合创始人童士豪告诉TechCrunch：“我们只是知道，对于人工智能而言，开发者是核心，因为他们是早期采用者。” 童士豪表示，由于Notable亲身经历了中美关系的复杂性，他们也坚信“关键任务”工作将始终需要基于美国的闭源模型。 不到两年后，Notable的有限合伙人不再质疑这笔超乎寻常的投资。周三，Anthropic通过历史上最大规模的首次公开募股（IPO）筹集了813亿美元，在其股票次日开始交易前，其完全稀释估值达到约1.5万亿美元，账面回报率约为15倍。 当然，Notable并非唯一一家因Anthropic获得巨额账面财富的公司。即使是那些传统上很少看到风险投资规模倍数回报的后期投资者，也即将迎来一场大胜。 新加坡主权财富基金GIC在2024年全年都在研究Anthropic，但直到2025年初，该基金科技投资主管克里斯·伊曼纽尔（Chris Emanuel）在旧金山的意大利餐厅Che Fico与Anthropic首席财务官克里希纳·拉奥（Krishna Rao）会面后，GIC才进行了首次投资。拉奥通过展示模型改进如何推动企业采用率上升，成功激发了伊曼纽尔对该公司的兴趣。随后，GIC首次投资了Anthropic的F轮融资，该轮融资于2025年9月公布，估值为1830亿美元。 伊曼纽尔告诉TechCrunch：“当时Claude Code刚刚发布。那是一个非常早期的阶段，但即便如此，我们也能看到它在开发者中非常受欢迎的迹象。”GIC对这家人工智能实验室的信心如此之高，以至于该基金后来与Coatue一起领投了Anthropic估值3800亿美元的G轮融资，并在估值9650亿美元的IPO前融资中再次作为联合领投方之一。据一位熟悉GIC财务状况的人士透露，GIC在三轮融资中共向Anthropic投资了超过45亿美元，在IPO后持有该公司约1%的股权。 贝利吉福德（Baillie Gifford）美国增长投资主管李丽莲（Lilian Li）在首次投资Anthropic的F轮融资前，已对该公司进行了近两年的研究并与其团队进行了交流。李丽莲告诉TechCrunch，一旦贝利吉福德明确Anthropic“始终如一地执行其愿景”，同时创造强劲收入和“令人信服的毛利率”时，便投资了Claude的开发商。 李丽莲表示，在贝利吉福德首次投资后，Anthropic继续兑现其承诺，业务扩展到法律服务和生命科学等行业，以及像Cowork和网络安全等更广泛的应用领域。 她补充说：“令人难以置信的是，尽管一切都在迅速变化，但他们始终保持着高度的一致性。”贝利吉福德在三轮融资中共计投资15亿美元，这笔投资为该投资管理公司以及数十家在曾经看似天文数字般估值下进行投资的其他支持者带来了可观的账面收益。
