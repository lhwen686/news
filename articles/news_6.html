英文原文

Original
After a tumultuous week, which saw WordPress founder Matt Mullenweg ousted from his position as CEO of Automattic, WordPress.com’s parent company, by way of a board vote, the company has now issued a statement confirming that Mullenweg has returned to his position. “Matt Mullenweg is the chairman and CEO of Automattic, with full support of the board and if you search online you can see many top executives and Automatticians supporting him as well,” a company spokesperson shared with TechCrunch via email just after 5 PM ET on Saturday evening. (The mention of online support appears to refer to supportive posts on X that Mullenweg has been reposting from his X account.) Automattic’s board had voted earlier this week to put Mullenweg on a paid leave of absence for unknown reasons. The move seemingly came as a surprise to Mullenweg, who posted on Automattic’s Slack, accusing the board members of “conspiring” against him. Automattic confirmed Mullenweg’s removal to TechCrunch on Wednesday, saying that Mullenweg was “currently on leave” and that Automattic’s Chief Financial Officer, Mark Davies, would lead as interim CEO with “full confidence” of the board. However, the board’s plan did not go smoothly. Seemingly declining to depart, Mullenweg booted other admins out of the company Slack and told employees everything had been worked out and that he was back in control of Automattic, multiple sources told TechCrunch. At one point, he also posted to Slack, “I’m a pirate now” and cursed, which is something Mullenweg famously did not do. “If this is an HR problem, please wrangle me in since my normal wranglers are with Mark Davies,” he wrote. When TechCrunch asked Mullenweg if his comments about being back as CEO were legitimate, he promised a blog post was coming. When it arrived, however, it was about him buying a houseboat. When we asked if his comments about being back were also him trolling, he replied, “I’m not a troll I’m a pirate, obviously.” Mullenweg never provided any official comment about his return, but noted on X that this was likely the fifth time he’s faced a “coup.” Automattic also did not respond to repeated requests for comment on Friday, nor to reports we heard about board member Toni Schneider stepping down. Schneider, a founding CEO of Automattic, now leads Bluesky. He did not return requests for comment at his personal email or via requests sent to Bluesky. We have since asked Automattic again about this and other changes to the board’s composition, which we’re hearing still may be in flux.

经历了一周的动荡，WordPress创始人马特·穆伦维格（Matt Mullenweg）在一次董事会投票中被罢免了WordPress.com母公司Automattic的首席执行官职务。该公司现已发表声明，确认穆伦维格已重返其职位。 “马特·穆伦维格是Automattic的董事长兼首席执行官，获得了董事会的全力支持，如果你在网上搜索，也会看到许多高级管理人员和Automattic员工支持他，”公司发言人于周六晚上东部时间下午5点过后不久通过电子邮件告知TechCrunch。（提及的在线支持似乎是指穆伦维格在其X账户上转发的支持性帖子。） 本周早些时候，Automattic董事会投票决定让穆伦维格带薪休假，原因不明。此举似乎让穆伦维格感到意外，他在Automattic的Slack上发帖，指控董事会成员“密谋”反对他。 周三，Automattic向TechCrunch证实了穆伦维格被罢免的消息，称穆伦维格“目前正在休假”，Automattic的首席财务官马克·戴维斯（Mark Davies）将以董事会“充分信任”的身份担任临时首席执行官。 然而，董事会的计划并不顺利。据多方消息人士告诉TechCrunch，穆伦维格似乎拒绝离职，反而将其他管理员踢出了公司Slack，并告诉员工一切都已解决，他已重新掌控Automattic。他一度还在Slack上发帖说：“我现在是个海盗了”，并且还爆了粗口，而穆伦维格是出了名的不骂脏话。“如果这是个人力资源问题，请把我管好，因为我的常规管理员都和马克·戴维斯在一起，”他写道。 当TechCrunch询问穆伦维格关于他重返CEO职位的言论是否属实时，他承诺会发布一篇博客文章。然而，当文章发布时，却是关于他购买一艘船屋的事情。当我们问他关于回归的言论是否也是在开玩笑时，他回答说：“我不是一个喷子，我显然是一个海盗。”穆伦维格从未就他的回归提供任何官方评论，但他在X上指出，这很可能是他第五次面临“政变”。 Automattic周五也没有回应多次置评请求，也没有回应我们听到的关于董事会成员托尼·施耐德（Toni Schneider）辞职的报道。施耐德是Automattic的创始首席执行官之一，现在领导Bluesky。他没有通过私人电子邮件或发送给Bluesky的请求回应置评请求。 此后，我们再次就此以及董事会构成中的其他变动向Automattic进行了询问，我们听说这些变动可能仍在进行中。
