深度解析

这篇文章揭示了人工智能虚拟形象在高端商业教育领域的创新应用，以哈佛商学院的 Foundry 创业训练营为例。其核心专业概念在于利用生成式AI技术（如 HeyGen 创建的虚拟形象）模拟人类导师，为学员提供个性化的实践反馈。技术原理涵盖了AI的面部表情生成、语音合成以及基于输入内容（如创业路演或董事会发言）进行实时分析和反馈的自然语言处理能力。其商业逻辑在于，哈佛商学院旨在通过AI扩大教育覆盖范围，提供传统上难以大规模复制的“一对一”指导体验，从而提高教学效率和可及性。 这种模式对行业产生了多方面影响。首先，在教育领域，它预示着个性化、可扩展反馈的新时代，有助于优质教育资源触达更广泛受众，补充而非完全替代人类导师的角色。其次，对于人工智能技术本身，它展示了AI在专业发展和技能培训中的强大潜力，并强调用户对AI体验从“聊天机器人”向“更具指导性”方向发展的需求。再者，对于未来商业，这种“令人毛骨悚然但有用”的AI工具的接受度，反映了企业和个人对于效率与体验平衡的新认知，预示着AI将日益渗透到专业反馈、辅导和决策支持中，改变人才培养和工作方式。

英文原文

Original
In Brief As Harvard Business School seeks to expand its reach, it’s leaning on AI avatars to provide individual feedback. These avatars were created by a startup called HeyGen and are included in the eight-week, $699 HBS Foundry bootcamp for entrepreneurs. The program offers live sessions with instructors every week, but the AI avatars are the ones providing feedback during practice pitches and board meetings. New York Times reporter Sarah Kessler actually tried this out herself by pitching an AI-generated copy of Flybridge Capital co-founder Jeff Bussgang. Apparently, both the real Bussgang and his simulacra were unimpressed by her plan to build “Uber for bananas,” but Kessler said the virtual version offered a noticeably frozen smile during her pitch. Project director Katharina Rings said she initially envisioned the AI component as something closer to a chatbot. However, after HBS released a trial version, students said they wanted a more guided experience. And while some college students haven’t been shy about expressing their negative feelings towards AI, Foundry participants told Kessler they like the avatars. As for Bussgang, he acknowledged his digital copy is a little “creepy,” but he said, “My students love it.”
