深度解析

这篇文章揭示了Mobileye从核心技术供应商向全面解决方案提供商转型的商业逻辑和技术演进。其核心技术基础是计算机视觉芯片和人工智能算法，使车辆能够实现环境感知和决策，支撑了从高级驾驶辅助系统（ADAS）到完全自动驾驶（L4/L5）的发展。最初，Mobileye作为芯片供应商，为汽车制造商提供关键组件。然而，随着自动驾驶技术日益成熟，公司看到了将感知技术扩展到整个自动驾驶堆栈的潜力。因此，它开始构建自己的自动驾驶系统，并进一步向出行服务领域进军，例如计划推出机器人出租车服务。收购类人机器人公司Mentee Robotics并提出“Mobileye 3.0”战略，则标志着公司将其AI和视觉技术从汽车扩展到更广泛的机器人领域，旨在创造能在物理世界中执行复杂任务的智能机器人。这种战略性转变不仅拓宽了Mobileye的市场边界，从传统汽车行业延伸至新兴的机器人和出行服务市场，也预示着AI技术在未来交通、物流和劳动力自动化方面将产生深远而颠覆性的影响，加剧了行业内对全栈AI解决方案和自动驾驶服务的竞争。

英文原文

Original
In Brief Mobileye founder and CEO Amnon Shashua plans to step down from the top leadership post after nearly three decades, just as the company pushes into robotaxis and humanoid robots. Shashua will remain CEO until Mobileye hires a replacement, according to a regulatory filing Thursday. Mobileye got its start making computer vision chips based on Shashua’s academic research at Hebrew University in Israel, and grew into a major supplier of the chips that power automotive safety and driver-assistance features. It had the largest IPO in Israel’s history, was acquired in 2017 by Intel for $15.3 billion, then spun back out as a publicly traded company in 2022, though Intel remains its largest shareholder. Under Shashua, Mobileye also moved beyond selling chips to automakers and began building its own systems that handle autonomous driving, which it now supplies to Volkswagen and its MOIA subsidiary. In January, the company acquired Shashua’s humanoid robotics startup Mentee Robotics for $900 million, which Shashua called part of “Mobileye 3.0,” the next phase of the business focused on robotics and automotive AI. Mobileye also said in June it would expand beyond its supplier status to launch its own robotaxi service in a U.S. city in 2027.
