深度解析

Inherent公司推出的AI代理Faraday，以其独特的“研究品味”和在更小模型体量下超越大型AI模型的成就，为人工智能领域带来了新的思考。其核心专业概念在于，Inherent通过强化学习（Reinforcement Learning）来训练AI，而非传统地预设大量规则或依赖庞大的参数规模。强化学习允许AI通过与环境交互并根据结果获得奖励来学习，从而逐步培养出一种类似人类科学家对实验设计和问题选择的“品味”或直觉。这种方法使得Faraday能够独立复现科学论文，并有潜力去发现新的科学知识，而非仅仅验证已知结果。 这一成果对行业影响深远。它挑战了当前AI领域普遍存在的“大模型即更好”的范式，即认为参数越多、模型越大，AI能力越强。Inherent的成功表明，更高效、更智能的训练方法——例如结合强化学习培养AI的“研究品味”——可以在较小的模型（如270亿参数的Qwen 3.6）上实现与数百亿甚至数万亿参数模型相媲美甚至更优异的性能。这预示着AI研究可能从对计算资源和数据量的无限追求，转向对算法效率和智能训练策略的深度探索，有望降低AI研发门槛，加速科学发现进程，并在科学研究等特定领域催生更具针对性和专业性的AI代理。

英文原文

Original
Inherent, a London AI lab founded by Google DeepMind alumni, says its AI agent just outperformed much larger models from Anthropic and OpenAI using a fraction of the size. Of all the startups launched by Google DeepMind alumni, Inherent has gotten relatively little attention. But while better-funded rivals have yet to show the world anything concrete, the London-based team is starting to share what it’s been building. Just weeks after emerging from stealth with a $50 million seed round, the British startup says its newly released AI agent, Faraday, has outperformed larger, better-known models at a specific task: independently reproducing the findings of published scientific papers without being told the answer in advance. That may sound like a mere party trick given Inherent’s much loftier goal — building AI that can discover new scientific knowledge and not just verify old results. But paper replication is a standard training exercise for human scientists, too, cofounder and chief scientist Edward Hughes said. “Many PhD students actually start by doing this.” Beating other AI systems at the task wasn’t the point, Hughes told TechCrunch; how they got there was. “What was most interesting to us about this was not so much the result of beating those frontier agents — which of course we liked — but was actually the way we went about building this.” Here’s the part that should catch an investor’s eye: measured against Anthropic’s Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI’s GPT-5.5 — both much larger, frontier-scale systems — Faraday runs on a comparatively tiny model called Qwen 3.6 that has just 27 billion parameters. (Roughly speaking, “parameters” is a proxy for a model’s size and, typically, its training costs, as well.) Inherent’s bar for success was also higher than simply accuracy. Beyond replicating results, it wanted Faraday to demonstrate “research taste” — an instinct for what experiments are worth running and how to design them well. Teaching something as intangible as taste is hard, which is where reinforcement learning comes in. It’s a training method that rewards an AI system for good outcomes rather than spelling out rules for it to follow. Rather than training its agents primarily on the study of how science itself is conducted, Inherent leans on this reward-based approach, betting it will generalize better to its longer-term goal of agents capable of contributing across many scientific fields. “We’re always guided by that north star of building an AI scientist agent and imbuing our agents with taste,” Hughes said. That focus has also shaped what Inherent chooses not to build. Rather than developing its own coding tool, it had Faraday use OpenAI’s GPT-5.5 Codex instead, much the way human scientists lean on existing software rather than building everything themselves, according to the company. Inherent is also trying to avoid building agents that simply tell users what they want to hear. Instead, Hughes said, the goal is modeled on his favorite kind of teammate — the kind who comes back and says: “I got curious about this, and I went off and I did these experiments. What do you think of these results?” That collaborative instinct extends to how Inherent operates as a company. Its dozen employees all work in person out of an office in King’s Cross — the once-rundown London neighborhood that Google DeepMind’s presence helped turn into one of the world’s top AI hubs. “We believe that London is the place to be,” Hughes said. Hughes is bullish on London’s density of AI talent, but he has also added his voice to calls to end “garden leave” — the practice, common in the U.K., of barring departing employees from joining or starting a rival company for months after they resign. It’s a restriction American researchers generally don’t face, giving U.S. startups a head start on hiring talent who’ve left a prior role. “This is a personal view rather than a company view, but I was affected by the garden leave problem,” he told TechCrunch. Hughes eventually got around that constraint and started Inherent alongside two other DeepMind alumni and a fourth cofounder. The startup isn’t slowing down either. It plans to grow its headcount to “about 20 to 25” by the end of the year. Given its ambitions in world models as well, and with Demis Hassabis’s new role leaving some DeepMind staff unsettled, Inherent’s hiring push could make it an appealing landing spot for DeepMind employees weighing a move. 中文翻译

AI Translation
Inherent，一家由谷歌DeepMind前员工创立的伦敦人工智能实验室，宣称其AI代理刚刚以远小于对方模型体量的方式，超越了Anthropic和OpenAI更大型的模型。 在谷歌DeepMind前员工创办的所有初创公司中，Inherent受到的关注相对较少。但当那些资金更雄厚的竞争对手尚未向世界展示任何具体成果时，这家伦敦的团队正开始分享他们一直在构建的东西。 在完成5000万美元的种子轮融资并走出隐身状态仅仅几周后，这家英国初创公司表示，其最新发布的AI代理“法拉第”（Faraday）在特定任务中表现优于更大、更知名的模型：无需预先告知答案，即可独立复现已发表科学论文的研究结果。 考虑到Inherent更为宏大的目标——构建能够发现新科学知识而不仅仅是验证旧结果的AI，这听起来可能只是一种“派对把戏”。但联合创始人兼首席科学家爱德华·休斯（Edward Hughes）表示，论文复现对于人类科学家来说也是一种标准的训练练习。“许多博士生实际上都是从做这个开始的。” 休斯告诉TechCrunch，击败其他AI系统并非关键；他们是如何实现这一目标的才是。“我们对此最感兴趣的不是击败那些前沿代理的结果——当然我们也喜欢这个结果——而是我们构建它的方式。” 以下是应该引起投资者关注的部分：与Anthropic的Claude Opus 4.8和OpenAI的GPT-5.5相比，法拉第运行在一个相对微小的模型上，该模型名为Qwen 3.6，仅有270亿个参数。（粗略地说，“参数”是衡量模型大小以及通常其训练成本的一个指标。）Inherent的成功标准也高于单纯的准确性。除了复现结果，它还希望法拉第能展现出“研究品味”——一种判断哪些实验值得进行以及如何良好设计它们的直觉。 教授“品味”这种无形之物是困难的，而这正是强化学习发挥作用的地方。这是一种训练方法，它奖励AI系统取得良好成果，而不是明确规定其应遵循的规则。Inherent不主要通过研究科学本身是如何进行的来训练其代理，而是倾向于这种基于奖励的方法，押注它将更好地推广到其更长期的目标：即代理能够在众多科学领域做出贡献。 “我们始终以构建AI科学家代理并赋予我们的代理‘品味’为指引。”休斯说。这种专注也塑造了Inherent选择不构建什么。该公司表示，Inherent没有开发自己的编码工具，而是让法拉第使用OpenAI的GPT-5.5 Codex，这就像人类科学家依赖现有软件而不是自己构建一切一样。 Inherent还在努力避免构建那些只会告诉用户他们想听什么内容的代理。相反，休斯说，他们的目标是效仿他最喜欢的那种队友——那种会回来告诉你：“我对这个产生了好奇，然后我做了这些实验。你觉得这些结果怎么样？” 这种协作本能也延伸到Inherent作为一家公司的运作方式。它的十几名员工都在国王十字街（King’s Cross）的办公室里全职工作——这个曾经破败的伦敦街区，因谷歌DeepMind的存在而变成了世界顶级AI中心之一。“我们相信伦敦是最佳选择。”休斯说。 休斯对伦敦AI人才的密集度感到乐观，但他同时也加入了呼吁终止“花园式休假”（garden leave）的行列——这是英国常见的做法，即禁止离职员工在辞职后数月内加入或创办竞争对手公司。这是美国研究人员通常不会面临的限制，这让美国初创公司在聘用离职人才方面占据先机。“这是个人观点而非公司观点，但我受到了花园式休假问题的影响。”他告诉TechCrunch。 休斯最终绕过了这一限制，与其他两位DeepMind前员工和一位第四位联合创始人共同创立了Inherent。这家初创公司也没有放缓脚步。它计划到今年年底将员工人数增至“约20到25人”。考虑到其在世界模型方面的雄心，以及德米斯·哈萨比斯（Demis Hassabis）新角色导致一些DeepMind员工感到不安，Inherent的招聘攻势可能会使其成为正在考虑跳槽的DeepMind员工一个有吸引力的落脚点。 图为Inherent联合创始人路易斯·基尔希（Louis Kirsch）、卡洛扬·阿列克谢夫（Kaloyan Aleksiev）、坦图姆·柯林斯（Tantum Collins）和爱德华·休斯（Edward Hughes），从左到右。
