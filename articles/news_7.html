深度解析

Prentis的核心专业概念在于开发“AI代理”，使其能够像人类办公室职员一样理解并操作计算机界面，从而自动化复杂的日常工作流程。这超越了传统RPA（机器人流程自动化）的局限，后者通常依赖于预设的固定脚本。Prentis的AI代理具备识别屏幕元素、导航多个应用程序和系统、以及执行端到端任务的能力，本质上是一种“认知自动化”，让AI理解任务上下文并适应动态环境。其Hive-32B模型在特定计算机使用基准上表现优异，并声称以更小的模型实现显著的成本优势。这项技术预示着办公自动化将成为AI最主要的用例之一，有望大幅提升白领工作效率，降低运营成本，并为企业创造巨大价值。然而，这也意味着AI领域将面临激烈竞争，众多巨头正竞相开发通用型AI代理，共同塑造未来的工作方式。

英文原文

Original
Prentis, a new AI research lab focused on computer use models, co-founded by serial entrepreneur Ritankar Das, and tech heavyweights Reid Hoffman and Marc Pincus, is in talks to raise $100 million at a $1 billion valuation, according to two people familiar with the discussions. Launched in April, Prentis is training models to learn how office workers navigate routine workflows across documents and systems, with the goal of building AI agents that can control computers to automate those tasks. Prentis will ostensibly develop agents tailored to these customers’ needs, such as handling insurance claims and automating customs duty refund exceptions without needing a human to hunt down paperwork. The startup has already signed contracts worth up to $50 million with several customers, including healthcare management service organization, a manufacturer, and goods and clothing manufactures, the two people familiar with the discussions tell TechCrunch, echoing investor materials obtained by TechCrunch that predict an estimated $75 million annualized run rate by the third quarter of this year. (Prentis’s pitch deck notes those figures reflect estimated annualized value based on a contracted fee equal to 20% of savings realized, not recognized revenue, and are “performance-dependent and subject to final execution.”) By its own account, Prentis says its Hive-32B model outperforms rivals, including OpenAI’s GPT-5.4 and Anthropic’s Claude Opus 4.6, on two computer-use benchmarks: WindowsAgentArena, which measures end-to-end task completion on real Windows applications; and ScreenSpot-v2, which tests a model’s ability to locate the right on-screen control. In its pitch deck, the company argues its edge comes from running a much smaller, cheaper model. In fact, it claims roughly 10 times lower cost per task than frontier APIs, saying it’s more economical to deploy across everyday workflows. TechCrunch hasn’t independently verified the company’s benchmark results. The startup is betting that automating everyday office tasks will soon outpace coding as AI’s biggest use case, but it’s a crowded market. Anthropic, Open AI, and Mira Murati’s Thinking Machines are also working on developing AI agents for computer use, one of sources said. Anthropic has also been acquiring talent in the category directly — it bought the Seattle computer-use startup Vercept earlier this year, folding in its founders and shutting down its product. Prentis didn’t respond to TechCrunch’s request for comment. Ritankar Das, CEO of Prantis, is also the founder of Titan, a holding company that builds and operates AI companies. Das, now 31, was UC Berkeley’s youngest University Medalist in more than a century, graduating at 18 with a double major in bioengineering and chemical biology before earning a master’s in biomedical engineering at Oxford. He founded Titan in 2014 after dropping out of an AI PhD program at Cambridge, where he’d been a Gates Cambridge Scholar. Das has described Titan as an intentional throwback to an old-fashioned holding-company model like Berkshire Hathaway, one that’s funded by its own exits rather than outside limited partners. Other businesses launched and operated by Titan include AI-powered virtual care provider Tala Health, which raised a $100 million seed round last year, and Forta Health, an autism care startup that raised $55 million led by Insight Partners in 2024. Titan-founded disease prediction company Dascena was acquired by CirrusDx in 2022. Prentis is a side project of sorts for its two other co-founders. Hoffman, the LinkedIn co-founder and Greylock partner, said last month that he was stepping down from Microsoft’s board after nearly a decade to go “founder mode” on Manas AI, an AI drug-discovery startup he’s also backing; he was an early OpenAI investor and co-founded Inflection AI with Mustafa Suleyman before Microsoft absorbed most of that team in 2024. Pincus, the Zynga founder, now runs the investment firm Reinvent Capital with Hoffman as a senior adviser, and published a memoir, “Life at the Speed of Play,” last month. Prentis has already hired more than 25 employees, including researchers who previously worked at OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, Tencent and Alibaba, according to its website.

AI Translation
Prentis，一家由连续创业家Ritankar Das以及科技巨头Reid Hoffman和Marc Pincus共同创立的、专注于计算机使用模型的新AI研究实验室，正洽谈以10亿美元估值筹集1亿美元资金，两位熟悉谈判的人士透露。 Prentis于四月成立，正在训练模型学习办公室工作人员如何在文档和系统之间处理日常工作流程，目标是构建能够控制计算机以自动化这些任务的AI代理。 Prentis将表面上开发针对这些客户需求的代理，例如处理保险索赔和自动化关税退款例外情况，而无需人工查找文书工作。 两位熟悉谈判的人士告诉TechCrunch，该初创公司已与多家客户签署了价值高达5000万美元的合同，其中包括一家医疗管理服务机构、一家制造商以及商品和服装制造商。这与TechCrunch获得的投资者材料相符，这些材料预测到今年第三季度，该公司年化收入将达到7500万美元。（Prentis的募资演示文稿指出，这些数字反映的是基于合同费用等于所实现节省的20%的估计年化价值，而非已确认收入，并且“依赖于绩效并受最终执行情况影响”。） Prentis声称，其Hive-32B模型在两项计算机使用基准测试中优于竞争对手，包括OpenAI的GPT-5.4和Anthropic的Claude Opus 4.6：一项是WindowsAgentArena，它衡量在真实Windows应用程序上端到端任务完成情况；另一项是ScreenSpot-v2，它测试模型定位正确屏幕控件的能力。 在其募资演示文稿中，该公司认为其优势来自于运行一个更小、更便宜的模型。事实上，它声称每项任务的成本比前沿API低约10倍，称其在日常工作流程中部署更经济。TechCrunch尚未独立核实该公司的基准测试结果。 该初创公司认为，自动化日常办公任务很快将超越编码，成为AI最大的用例，但这是一个竞争激烈的市场。一位消息人士称，Anthropic、OpenAI和Mira Murati的Thinking Machines也正在开发用于计算机使用的AI代理。Anthropic也直接在该领域招聘人才——它在今年早些时候收购了西雅图的计算机使用初创公司Vercept，将其创始人纳入团队并关闭了其产品。 Prentis未回应TechCrunch的置评请求。 Prantis的首席执行官Ritankar Das也是Titan的创始人，Titan是一家构建和运营AI公司的控股公司。现年31岁的Das是加州大学伯克利分校一个多世纪以来最年轻的大学奖章获得者，18岁时以生物工程和化学生物学双学位毕业，随后在牛津大学获得生物医学工程硕士学位。 他于2014年从剑桥大学AI博士项目辍学后创立了Titan，他曾是该项目的盖茨剑桥学者。Das将Titan描述为一种有意回归老式控股公司模式（如伯克希尔哈撒韦）的企业，其资金来源于自身的退出而非外部有限合伙人。 Titan旗下其他已推出和运营的业务包括：AI驱动的虚拟护理提供商Tala Health，该公司去年筹集了1亿美元种子轮资金；以及自闭症护理初创公司Forta Health，该公司于2024年在Insight Partners领投下筹集了5500万美元。Titan创立的疾病预测公司Dascena于2022年被CirrusDx收购。 Prentis对另外两位联合创始人来说，某种程度上是一个“副业”。LinkedIn联合创始人兼Greylock合伙人Hoffman上个月表示，他将辞去微软董事会职务（任职近十年），进入他也在支持的AI药物发现初创公司Manas AI的“创始人模式”；他曾是OpenAI的早期投资者，并于微软在2024年吸收了大部分团队之前与Mustafa Suleyman共同创立了Inflection AI。 Zynga创始人Pincus现在与Hoffman（担任高级顾问）一起经营投资公司Reinvent Capital，并于上个月出版回忆录《游戏速度的人生》（Life at the Speed of Play）。 根据Prentis的网站，该公司已雇佣了超过25名员工，其中包括曾在OpenAI、谷歌DeepMind、Meta、腾讯和阿里巴巴工作的研究人员。
