Anthropic shares more details about how Claude’s new watermarks will work

深度解析

这篇文章深入探讨了Anthropic为其AI聊天机器人Claude实施文本水印的举措，这主要是受欧盟《人工智能法案》透明度准则的推动。其核心专业概念是AI水印，这是一种技术原理，即AI模型在生成文本时嵌入一个微妙的、统计上可检测的模式，同时不影响人类阅读体验或内容质量。这通过在文本生成过程中，以非随机、形成模式的方式进行特定的“低风险选择”（如同义词选择）来实现。与通过分析写作“线索”进行传统AI检测不同，水印技术创建了一种内在的、隐藏的信号。此举对行业影响深远：它标志着AI生成内容在可追溯性和问责制方面的转变，旨在打击虚假信息并建立信任。Google DeepMind的SynthID-Text等方法的采纳以及其他主要开发商的承诺，预示着AI输出识别方面正在形成行业标准。然而，用户间的争议以及彻底重写可能去除水印的局限性，凸显了在快速演进的AI领域中，平衡法规遵从、技术效率和用户接受度所面临的持续挑战。

英文原文

Original
Anthropic published a blog post Friday seeking to answer some basic questions about how it will watermark the text generated by its chatbot Claude. Such as: How will the watermarking actually work? Can it be hidden with editing? And how does this affect code? Claude users have been debating the move since the company revealed earlier this week that it would be doing this watermarking to comply with the EU AI Act’s Transparency Code, which requires AI companies to use systems that make it possible to identify AI-generated content. On Reddit, for example, one poster characterized this as a conspiracy against innocent Claude users, while another claimed, “The only reason you wouldn’t want this is to lie to people.” And Business Insider reports that “dozens” of users on X have claimed to cancel their Claude subscriptions as a result. Anthropic’s new post starts with a general overview of the watermarking concept, explaining that when making “low-stakes choices” — like choosing between the words “overcast” and “grey” to describe the weather — Claude can create a pattern in its responses that is “undetectable to the reader, but is detectable to anyone who has a key that encodes it.” “Watermarking does not impact the quality of Claude’s output,” the company said. “To a reader, a watermarked response is indistinguishable from an unwatermarked one.” More specifically, Anthropic said it will be using the SynthID-Text approach that the Google DeepMind team outlined in 2024, and that it plans to release a watermark detection API. It also noted that watermarking is distinct from the AI detection approaches offered by companies like Pangram that look for “tells” in the writing (like the construction “his isn’t [X], it’s [Y]”) to reveal AI usage: “Picking up on these patterns is fundamentally different from checking for a watermark.” Could someone just rewrite the text to hide the watermark? Anthropic said it’s possible, but “light editing probably won’t remove the watermark completely,” while “a complete rewrite where every word is replaced will.” “In the latter case, of course, it’s arguable whether the text can any longer be described as AI-generated,” the company said. As for whether the watermark will be detectable in text that was only proofread or edited by Claude, Anthropic said that will depend on “the length of the text and how heavily Claude has edited it.” If it’s only been lightly edited, “nearly all the words” will have been written by the human author and “there’s very little (if anything) for the watermark to attach to.” Code, meanwhile, should have less of a watermark than other text, because the model will need to create working code and won’t have the freedom to choose between a variety of equally valid options. “Having said that, in areas where there is an arbitrary choice between particular words or terms within the code, the watermark can be used, such as comments within code,” Anthropic said. “But by definition, it will have a negligible effect on the actual code produced.” Anthropic also said that Claude won’t be the only AI chatbot to generate watermarked text, as “other major model developers have signed the same Code of Practice and will be implementing their own watermarks.”

AI Translation
Anthropic于周五发布了一篇博客文章，旨在回答一些关于其如何为其聊天机器人Claude生成的文本添加水印的基本问题。例如：水印将如何实际运作？能否通过编辑隐藏水印？以及这如何影响代码？ 自该公司本周早些时候透露将进行水印处理以遵守欧盟《人工智能法案》的透明度准则以来，Claude用户一直在就此举展开辩论。该准则要求人工智能公司使用能够识别由人工智能生成内容g的系统。 例如，在Reddit上，一位发帖人将此描述为针对无辜Claude用户的阴谋，而另一位则声称：“你不想这样做，唯一的理由就是想欺骗他人。”《商业内幕》报道称，X平台上有“数十名”用户因此声称取消了他们的Claude订阅。 Anthropic的新文章首先概述了水印概念，解释说当进行“低风险选择”时——例如在选择“阴天”和“灰色”这两个词来描述天气时——Claude可以在其回复中创建一个模式，该模式“对读者来说是不可见的，但对于拥有编码密钥的任何人来说都是可检测的”。 该公司表示：“水印不会影响Claude输出的质量。”“对读者而言，带有水印的回复与没有水印的回复是无法区分的。” 更具体地说，Anthropic表示它将采用Google DeepMind团队在2024年概述的SynthID-Text方法，并计划发布一个水印检测API。它还指出，水印与Pangram等公司提供的通过寻找写作中的“线索”（例如“这不是[X]，而是[Y]”的结构）来揭示人工智能使用情况的人工智能检测方法是不同的：“识别这些模式与检查水印有着根本区别。” 有人能否通过重写文本来隐藏水印？Anthropic表示这有可能，但“轻微的编辑可能无法完全去除水印”，而“对每个词都进行替换的彻底重写则会去除”。 该公司表示：“当然，在后一种情况下，文本是否还能被称为人工智能生成的内容，是值得商榷的。” 至于水印是否能在仅由Claude校对或编辑过的文本中被检测到，Anthropic表示这将取决于“文本的长度以及Claude编辑的程度”。如果只是进行了轻微编辑，“几乎所有的词语”都将由人类作者撰写，并且“水印几乎没有什么（或者说根本没有什么）可以附着的地方”。 同时，代码应该比其他文本具有更少的水印，因为模型需要创建可运行的代码，并且没有自由在各种同样有效的选项中进行选择。 Anthropic表示：“话虽如此，在代码中特定词语或术语存在任意选择的领域，例如代码中的注释，水印仍然可以使用。”“但从定义上讲，它对实际生成的代码影响微乎其微。” Anthropic还表示，Claude不会是唯一生成带有水印文本的人工智能聊天机器人，因为“其他主要模型开发商已签署了相同的行为准则，并将实施他们自己的水印”。
