深度解析

这篇文章揭示了AI编程平台Replit在大型语言模型（LLM）供应商选择上的战略转变，核心在于OpenAI与Anthropic之间的激烈竞争。Replit首席执行官马萨德的言论凸显了性能、成本效益和合作关系强度是企业选择AI模型提供商的关键考量。当平台将某个模型设为“默认”时，它能显著影响用户采纳度及平台支出，这对于LLM供应商而言是至关重要的市场份额争夺点。尽管Replit强调其“模型无关”的立场，但优先选择OpenAI作为Replit Agent的驱动者，无疑是对OpenAI技术实力和市场地位的认可。文章也揭示了AI行业内部动态的复杂性，马萨德的公开声明与内部人士所说的持续合作存在出入，这反映出AI公司在对外沟通和实际业务操作之间可能存在微妙的平衡。同时，Replit企业收入的显著增长预示着AI开发工具正加速向B2B市场渗透，企业和政府客户成为其未来增长的主要动力。最后，马萨德对AI安全“栖息地”的描述，强调了AI部署不仅是技术问题，更需要完善的编排、错误恢复、安全沙盒等生态系统保障，这代表了行业对负责任AI发展日益增长的关注。

英文原文

Original
Replit, the AI coding platform that has become a favorite of startup founders and developers, is no longer working with Anthropic, the company’s CEO and co-founder Amjad Masad said Thursday while onstage at TechCrunch Disrupt. Masad said the decision was made for a number of different reasons, including cost. Replit announced a partnership with OpenAI in August, but there was never any indication that it was no longer partnering with Anthropic. “I think ChatGPT is better now,” Masad said, adding that the company uses an internal benchmark to help assess metrics like performance. “We publish a public benchmark as well, and consistently, Sol and Astra perform better on building vibe-coded applications from scratch. It was a decision based on both cost, partnership, and performance. And for all these reasons, we just felt like they were a better partner for now.” It seems, however, that Anthropic hasn’t been completely cut out of Replit, which sells a cloud-based software development platform where users can develop and deploy apps directly from inside their browser. After Disrupt, Masad clarified that customers can still access Anthropic models on the platform, but that OpenAI’s ChatGPT is now the default. This shift could significantly reduce Replit’s spend with Anthropic since most customers stick with the default rather than selecting a model themselves. Prior to striking a deal with OpenAI, Replit relied on Anthropic’s Claude models to run its platform. That partnership was said to have helped the company grow its revenue significantly, and Masad noted on stage that Replit had been, until recently, a top-five customer of Anthropic’s. “Replit remains model agnostic,” Masad told TechCrunch over the weekend. “Our users can choose Anthropic’s Claude or other supported models…OpenAI is now our preferred partner for powering Replit Agent, based on performance, economics, and the strength of our partnership.” Masad’s comments on stage were rejected by insiders familiar with the partnership between the Anthropic and Replit, however. One person familiar, who asked not to be named, said the partnership was still in place. There is evidence on the company’s website, blog posts, and social media posts that Replit was promoting Anthropic products as recently as a few weeks ago. For instance, a blog post in late September cites Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 and Claude Fable 5.1 as models running in Replit Agent’s production environment. Around the same time, one of Replit’s product employees posted on X about using Anthropic’s flagship model Opus 5.5 and promoting its capabilities. Masad also shared insights about the company’s growth while onstage at Disrupt, noting that enterprise is starting to provide a larger slice of revenue. “We’re not sharing ARR numbers just yet, but in terms of growth, like, I would say, enterprise was like 2% of revenue last year,” Masad said. “This year, I would say like 15% of our revenue is enterprise contracts, and more than 50% of our revenue is B2B.” Backstage, Masad told TechCrunch that he expects more than half of Replit’s revenue to come from enterprise and government customers in 2027. Masad also commented on the necessity for good AI safety measures. “In the same way that us humans need a certain environment to work — you need a nice office, maybe nice good food in the office, air-conditioning, computer — agents need a habitat for them to exist and do good work,” he said. “So that habitat has good orchestration, good error recovery, good safety and security and sandboxing, good networking, good identity and authorization. And once you figure all of that out, then you can deploy AI safely.” This article was originally published on October 16 at 2:22 pm PT. It has been updated to include more context around Replit’s partnerships with Anthropic and OpenAI. Its title has been updated, as well.

作为备受创业公司创始人和开发者青睐的AI编程平台Replit，已不再与Anthropic合作，该公司首席执行官兼联合创始人阿姆贾德·马萨德周四在TechCrunch Disrupt大会上表示。 马萨德表示，做出这一决定是出于多种原因，其中包括成本。Replit在八月份宣布与OpenAI建立合作关系，但当时没有任何迹象表明它将不再与Anthropic合作。 “我认为ChatGPT现在更好了，”马萨德说，并补充道，公司使用内部基准来评估性能等指标。“我们也会发布公开基准，而Sol和Astra在从零开始构建‘vibe-coded’应用程序方面表现持续更佳。这是一个基于成本、合作关系和性能的决定。出于所有这些原因，我们认为他们目前是一个更好的合作伙伴。” 然而，Anthropic似乎并未完全被Replit切断联系，Replit销售的是一个基于云的软件开发平台，用户可以直接在浏览器内部开发和部署应用程序。 在Disrupt大会后，马萨德澄清说，客户仍然可以在平台上访问Anthropic模型，但OpenAI的ChatGPT现在是默认选项。由于大多数客户会坚持使用默认选项而非自行选择模型，这一转变可能会显著减少Replit在Anthropic上的开支。 在与OpenAI达成协议之前，Replit曾依赖Anthropic的Claude模型来运行其平台。据说那次合作帮助公司显著增加了收入，马萨德在台上指出，直到最近，Replit一直是Anthropic的前五大客户之一。 马萨德周末告诉TechCrunch：“Replit仍然是模型无关的。”“我们的用户可以选择Anthropic的Claude或其他支持的模型……基于性能、经济效益和我们合作关系的强度，OpenAI现在是我们为Replit Agent提供支持的首选合作伙伴。” 然而，熟悉Anthropic与Replit合作关系的内部人士驳斥了马萨德在台上的言论。一位不愿透露姓名的知情人士表示，合作关系仍在进行中。 该公司的网站、博客文章和社交媒体帖子都有证据表明，Replit在几周前还在推广Anthropic的产品。例如，九月下旬的一篇博客文章提到Anthropic的Claude Fable 5和Claude Fable 5.1作为Replit Agent生产环境中运行的模型。大约在同一时间，Replit的一名产品员工在X上发布了关于使用Anthropic旗舰模型Opus 5.5并推广其能力的帖子。 马萨德还在Disrupt大会上分享了公司增长的见解，指出企业业务开始贡献更大比例的收入。 马萨德说：“我们暂未公布年度经常性收入（ARR）数据，但就增长而言，我想说，去年企业业务约占收入的2%。今年，企业合同约占我们收入的15%，而超过50%的收入来自B2B。” 在后台，马萨德告诉TechCrunch，他预计到2027年，Replit超过一半的收入将来自企业和政府客户。 马萨德还评论了良好AI安全措施的必要性。 他说：“就像我们人类需要一个特定的工作环境一样——你需要一个舒适的办公室，办公室里可能有美味的食物、空调、电脑——AI代理也需要一个‘栖息地’才能存在并高效工作。”“所以，这个栖息地需要良好的编排、良好的错误恢复、良好的安全性和沙盒机制、良好的网络、良好的身份识别和授权。一旦解决了所有这些问题，你就可以安全地部署AI。” 本文最初于太平洋时间10月16日下午2:22发布。现已更新，以提供更多关于Replit与Anthropic和OpenAI合作关系的背景信息。文章标题也已更新。
