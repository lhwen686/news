深度解析

这篇新闻揭示了生成式AI（Gen-AI）在游戏产业掀起的一场深刻变革。核心专业概念在于“AI优先”的生产流程和“AI加速开发”。这意味着Epic Games不再将AI视为辅助工具，而是将其提升为开发模式的核心驱动力，具体表现为“不再有手写代码”和“通过完整的AI工作流程生成”艺术作品。Anthropic的Claude等工具被用于自动化代码编写和内容生成，极大地改变了传统的手动开发模式。 这种范式转变对行业产生了深远影响。首先，它旨在提高效率和开发速度，以应对项目延期等挑战。其次，虽然CEO斯威尼强调目标是“创造更多更好内容”，但结合此前的大规模裁员，也暗示了通过AI优化资源配置和潜在的成本控制。再次，它预示着未来游戏开发人员的角色将从纯粹的编码和美术制作，转向AI工具的监督、提示工程和创意指导，成为“愿景者和创意驱动者”。最后，Epic作为行业巨头采用此策略，将促使其他开发商重新评估其工作流程，加速整个行业的AI整合，从而影响内容生产的规模、质量和开发成本结构。

英文原文

Original
Epic Games has reportedly turned to gen-AI to finish up development on a shooter that crosses over Star Wars and Marvel, which has been internally delayed. That's according to a Bloomberg report from journalist Jason Schreier, citing anonymous sources familiar with the unannounced game. Epic's in-development project - a collaboration with Disney - is said to have hit a speed bump, and the Fortnite maker is apparently telling its developers to embrace more gen-AI tools for the rest of the game's production. The new shooter purportedly features characters from both the Marvel and Star Wars series, and the company expected to have it out the door in March 2027. Now that the crossover project has slipped, Epic reportedly told its staff to adopt an "AI first" production pipeline and finish the game with tech such as Anthropic's Claude. In an internal message seen by Bloomberg, one game director told developers there would be "no more hand-written code" and art work should be "generated with full AI workflows" - much to the surprise of many workers. "We're embracing AI-accelerated development, led by Epic developers who are the visionaries and creative drivers for everything we do," Epic's communications director, Jake Jones, said in a statement to Bloomberg. "We have a huge pivot underway here at Epic, moving developers around to retool for maximum AI acceleration," CEO Tim Sweeney tweeted. "The aim isn't to do the same stuff with fewer people, but to enable everyone here to build MORE and BETTER stuff, and to bring the benefits to all teams using Unreal." The Walt Disney company acquired a $1.5b stake in Epic Games a few years back, as the two entertainment giants agreed to partner on a "games and entertainment universe" somehow connected to Fortnite. There weren't any concrete details on the collaboration at the time, though the duo said players would be able to "create their own stories and experiences, express their fandom in a distinctly Disney way, and share content with each other in ways that they love". More recently, it was reported that senior Disney executives were interested in buying Epic Games outright. Epic's push for gen-AI to infiltrate almost all levels of game development comes less than a year after it laid off over 1,000 humans. At the time, CEO Tim Sweeney told staff "we're spending significantly more than we're making, and we have to make major cuts to keep the company funded."
